## Graduation eligibility

Use the year that best matches the role so the resume passes screens:

- **Sophomore-targeted roles** (e.g. "class of 2028", "sophomore", "second-year"): **Expected graduation: 2028**
- **Junior-targeted roles** (e.g. "class of 2027", "junior", "third-year"): **Expected graduation: 2027**

When the JD is ambiguous, pick the graduation year that fits the posting (e.g. if they say "rising junior" use 2027).
