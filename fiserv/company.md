# Fiserv

## What They Do

Fiserv is a global B2B provider of financial technology serving banks, credit unions, investment firms, retailers, and merchants. It operates in two main segments: Payments and Industry Products, and Financial Institution Services. The company provides core banking systems, merchant acquiring, point-of-sale technology, digital payments, and integrated software and services—with account processing for more than a third of U.S. financial institutions. Growth is driven by software licensing, transaction fees, professional services, and strategic acquisitions.

## Why This Company

I want to work at Fiserv because their scale—touching a huge portion of U.S. financial institutions and merchant payments—means every feature and service has to be reliable, secure, and performant. Their move toward microservices and cloud-native core banking aligns with my interest in distributed systems and backend engineering. I’m drawn to the chance to contribute to coding, testing, and code reviews on systems that power real financial operations, and to learn from senior analysts and teammates in a regulated, high-stakes environment. Fintech is where strong software engineering directly impacts millions of users and institutions.

## Engineering Culture

Fiserv has modernized around microservices and open APIs, enabling incremental upgrades and third-party integrations without full core replacements. They use cloud-native, multi-cloud infrastructure for scalability and resiliency. Teams work with componentized platforms (e.g., Signature) for core banking—customer info, deposits, loans, card management—with an emphasis on deployment with minimal downtime and rapid configuration. The internship JD calls out Java, Spring Boot, Kubernetes, Docker, GitLab, RabbitMQ, and related backend/infra tooling, reflecting a stack focused on application development, testing, and quality.
