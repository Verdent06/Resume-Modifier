# Skechers

## What They Do
Skechers is a global footwear, apparel, and accessories brand (5,001–10,000 employees) selling through wholesale and direct-to-consumer channels. They run e-commerce on Shopify Plus, use Aptos ONE microservices for POS and omnichannel retail, and have adopted o9’s AI platform for retail planning and inventory. Engineering supports digital retail, internal tools, and GenAI use cases from their El Segundo HQ.
