# Software Engineering Intern — Arch Capital Group

## Quick Facts
- **Type:** Internship
- **Location:** New York, NY, USA — Hybrid (two days on-site, three days remote)
- **Compensation:** $26–$30/hr
- **Template Used:** full-stack

## ATS Keywords
Java, Spring Boot, Angular, REST APIs, microservices, SQL, databases, data modeling, RAG, GenAI, AI/ML, prompt engineering, vector search, Agentic AI, AWS, Azure, GCP, Git, GitHub, JIRA, Agile, CI/CD

## Role Summary
Interns on Reinsurance IT partner with IT and business stakeholders to gather requirements for underwriting and reinsurance workflows, then translate those needs into technical solutions using Java, Spring Boot, and Angular. Day-to-day work includes designing and building tools, services, and web applications used across the Reinsurance IT landscape; developing and integrating REST APIs and microservices; and collaborating on end-to-end feature delivery from UI to backend. A major focus is exploring and prototyping AI/GenAI capabilities—summarization, Q&A over documents, workflow guidance, and agentic AI with guardrails—to improve user experience and decisioning efficiency while maintaining compliance. Success at three months means shipping at least one end-to-end feature, contributing to Agile delivery (JIRA, GitHub, standups, sprint planning), and clearly documenting and presenting work to technical and non-technical audiences.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Java/Spring + Angular as the daily stack** — Not "some backend and front-end"; they want hands-on Spring Boot services and Angular (components, services, routing, state management). Evidence of building services and UIs with these is critical.
- **REST and microservices fluency** — Ability to design, build, and integrate RESTful services and understand microservices and integration patterns, not just consume APIs.
- **AI/GenAI as applied integration** — Exposure through coursework, projects, or hackathons is enough; they care that you can prototype RAG, prompt engineering, or agentic behaviors and think about guardrails and compliance, not that you train models.
- **Stakeholder translation** — Comfort turning business and underwriting needs into technical solutions and communicating clearly in both directions (design notes, demos, readmes).
- **Self-directed but collaborative** — "Self-starter" and "work independently" plus Agile (JIRA, standups, sprint planning). They want someone who takes initiative but fits into their delivery rhythm.
- **Cloud and tooling as plus** — AWS, Azure, or GCP and Git/GitHub/CI/CD are preferred, not mandatory; mention them where you have them.

## Required Skills
- Actively completing area of study in Computer Science or relevant program; minimum 3.0 GPA; Junior, Senior, or graduate with graduation Dec 2026 – May 2027
- Curiosity, creativity, willingness to experiment with new technologies (including AI/GenAI); strong analytical and problem-solving skills; self-starter; excellent written and verbal communication
- **Core Engineering:** Programming proficiency in Java with hands-on experience building services using Spring Boot; front-end development experience with Angular (components, services, routing, state management); understanding of REST APIs, microservices concepts, and integration patterns; experience with databases (SQL preferred) and basic data modeling concepts
- **AI/GenAI:** Exposure to AI/ML concepts and modern GenAI patterns; familiarity with one or more of: prompt engineering and evaluation; RAG for Q&A over enterprise documents; embedding/vector search concepts; interest or experience in Agentic AI (tool-using agents, function calling, workflow orchestration, guardrails, human-in-the-loop), including auditable, safe, and compliant agent behaviors
- **Platform & Delivery:** Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) is a plus; exposure to Agile and JIRA; experience with version control (Git/GitHub) and basic CI/CD concepts is a plus

## Preferred Skills
- Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
- Experience with version control (Git/GitHub) and basic CI/CD concepts
- Agile methodologies and tools like JIRA
