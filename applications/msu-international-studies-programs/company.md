# MSU International Studies & Programs (ISP)

## What They Do

International Studies & Programs is a university-level office at Michigan State University that coordinates international research, teaching, and outreach. It houses area and thematic studies centers, student and scholar mobility programs, travel safety and funding resources, and public-facing web properties that explain programs, deadlines, and compliance to diverse audiences. ISP is currently running a large-scale website redesign and migration, which is why they are hiring web operations support.

## Why This Company

I am already on campus as a CS undergrad, and ISP’s work sits at the intersection of public information, inclusive digital spaces, and the messy reality of migrating many legacy sites into one coherent web presence. The Website Intern role maps cleanly to work I have already shipped: production customer-facing sites, design-to-code iteration with stakeholders, and disciplined workflows where small mistakes show up as broken links or wrong dates for real people. Supporting ISP’s migration is a chance to apply that same rigor to content that helps international students and faculty navigate MSU.

## Engineering Culture

ISP is a communications- and operations-heavy organization, not a product engineering org. The bar is reliable publishing: correct copy, accessible markup, consistent navigation, and CMS workflows that other staff can follow without breaking production. Success is measured in fewer content defects, smoother launches during migration waves, and analytics or search signals that show users can complete tasks (find a form, confirm a deadline, understand a policy). Technical depth shows up as attention to information architecture, accessibility, and quality assurance across many subsites.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs

The **ISP web team** supports International Studies & Programs and affiliated units during the redesign and migration. The intern works alongside that team on content review, migration into the new CMS, QA passes on new pages, and light support for UX, analytics, or SEO as needed.
