# L&L Products

## What They Do
L&L Products is a technology-driven, family-owned manufacturing company founded in 1958 that specializes in applied engineering and materials science. They design and produce customized solutions for static sealing, acoustic improvement, vibration reduction, structural reinforcement, and composite components for automotive, aerospace, commercial vehicle, rail, and industrial sectors. They employ 1,200+ people across production facilities in the USA, Europe, China, India, Turkey, and Brazil and ship to 30+ countries.

## Why This Company
I want to support enterprise IT and HR technology transformation at a company where engineering and operations scale globally. L&L’s HCM rollout across the Americas is a concrete chance to work on project coordination, requirements gathering, system configuration, and testing—exactly the blend of process and technology I’m building in CS and in leading technical teams. Contributing to a deployment that touches Payroll, Timekeeping, and Benefits for U.S. employees would give me hands-on experience with real business impact at a manufacturer that invests in both R&D and how people work.

## Engineering Culture
L&L emphasizes customized engineering over one-size-fits-all solutions and invests in R&D across material science, CAD, and manufacturing technology. Their IT organization supports global operations and is currently driving a major HCM implementation, which implies a focus on enterprise systems, integration, and cross-functional project delivery rather than product software development.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs
Global IT (reporting to Global IT Business Architect) is running the HCM implementation. The intern role sits within IT/HCM Technology & Testing Support, working with HR, IT, and project teams on coordination, requirements, configuration support, data migration, SIT/UAT, and training for the new HCM solution across the Americas.
