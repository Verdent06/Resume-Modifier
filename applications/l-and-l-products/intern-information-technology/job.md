## Role Summary
The IT/HCM Technology & Testing Support Intern supports the implementation of a new HCM (Human Capital Management) solution across the Americas. Day-to-day work includes coordinating project activities with HR, IT, and project teams; gathering and documenting business requirements; assisting with configuration workbooks and data validation; supporting data migration and mapping legacy to new system fields; and participating in System Integration Testing (SIT) and User Acceptance Testing (UAT). Success at three months means the intern has become a reliable coordinator, has contributed to requirement templates and test execution, and has helped keep documentation and action items current for a successful U.S. deployment of Payroll, Timekeeping, and Benefits.

## What They're Really Looking For
- Someone who can own meeting logistics, minutes, and action-item tracking without dropping balls—operational reliability.
- Ability to sit in workshops, capture HR/IT needs, and turn them into structured, reusable requirement artifacts (not just notes).
- Comfort doing data cleanup, mapping, and validation with attention to detail; willing to execute scripted test cases and log defects clearly.
- Cross-functional comfort: working with HR, IT, and consultants without needing to be the technical expert.
- Proficiency in Office tools (Excel, Word, PowerPoint) and PM tools; quick to learn new systems.
- Interest in enterprise IT and HR technology transformation, not just coding—this is implementation and support, not product development.
