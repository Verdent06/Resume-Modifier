# Smiths Group (Smiths Detection)

## What They Do

Smiths Detection is the security and threat-detection division of Smiths Group, a global leader in technology for aviation, ports and borders, defence, and urban security. Their systems screen people, baggage, and cargo to protect infrastructure and freedom of movement. The division operates 8 R&D centres and 5 manufacturing sites (France, Germany, Malaysia, UK, USA) with ~3,200 employees and 40+ years at the frontline of safety and security technology.

## Why This Company

I want to work where software directly supports safety-critical systems and where automation and tooling improve how security and engineering teams operate. Smiths Detection’s focus on integrating vulnerability scanning (e.g. Nessus) with ticketing (Jira), automating reporting, and experimenting with applied AI (e.g. Ollama) for categorisation aligns with my experience building automation pipelines, CI/CD, and API-driven workflows at Claude Builder Club and MindMosaic. I’m drawn to the chance to contribute to continuous monitoring and remediation workflows in a regulated, high-impact environment.

## Engineering Culture

Smiths Group emphasises a 175-year heritage of innovation and technical excellence, with a stated purpose to “engineer a better future” and a culture that encourages new ideas and execution. The group invests ~4% of revenue in R&D and holds 2,700+ granted and pending patents. Smiths Excellence (lean six sigma–style problem-solving) is used from shop floor to board level. The internship sits at the intersection of software engineering, security, and DevOps—tool integration, automation, and applied AI—rather than pure product feature work.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs

The role supports **Software Engineering and Security** teams: integrating vulnerability scanning platforms (e.g. Nessus) with Jira, developing automation workflows, and enhancing continuous monitoring. Work spans design and prototyping with dev, test, infrastructure, and security; scripting and reporting; and optional experimentation with open-source AI (Ollama) for scan-result summarisation or categorisation. No specific product team is named; the intern works across these functions.
