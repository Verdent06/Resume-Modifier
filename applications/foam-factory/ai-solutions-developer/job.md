## Role Summary
The AI Solutions Developer builds and maintains software powered by LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT-style systems) to automate quoting, customer email responses, order logging, and internal workflows across sales, customer service, order processing, quoting, accounting, and administration. Beyond AI, the role owns internal systems support, website development and maintenance, CAD-based machine programming for manufacturing equipment, and day-to-day technical and IT support. Success means accurate, policy-compliant, and efficient systems with strong data integrity and security. The developer works cross-functionally with customer service, accounting, operations, IT, and manufacturing and serves as the main technical resource for turning business needs into reliable tools.
