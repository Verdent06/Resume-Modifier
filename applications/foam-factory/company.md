# Foam Factory

## What They Do

Foam Factory is a family-owned manufacturer and distributor of foam, rubber, sponge, and upholstery products, operating from a 310,000-square-foot facility in Southeast Michigan. They combine manufacturing with direct retail to offer customized foam solutions at low cost, serving both commercial/industrial applications and consumers. The company uses computer-controlled machinery and has invested in advanced manufacturing processes; they are now building AI-driven systems to streamline quoting, customer service, order processing, and internal operations.

## Why This Company

I want to work at Foam Factory because the role sits at the intersection of AI and real operations — building LLM-powered tools that directly affect customer quotes, email support, and workflow accuracy, not abstract demos. My experience with agentic systems (Mira’s intent resolution and task dispatch), API integrations (Claude Builder Club’s provisioning and check-in), and full-stack delivery (MindMosaic) maps well to implementing and refining AI solutions that have to be reliable and policy-compliant. I’m based in Michigan (MSU, East Lansing) and am interested in seeing how AI can improve day-to-day workflows in manufacturing and customer-facing systems. The mix of AI development, internal tooling, and light IT/website support is a fit for someone who likes breadth and shipping end-to-end.

## Engineering Culture

The company is adopting LLMs (e.g., ChatGPT-style systems) for quoting, customer communication, and operational automation while maintaining existing internal systems, websites, and CAD-based machine programming. Engineering culture is practical and cross-functional: the AI Solutions Developer works with sales, customer service, order processing, accounting, and production. Success means systems that are accurate, compliant, and efficient — with strong emphasis on data integrity, validation, and secure development. Stack likely involves OpenAI or Azure AI APIs, prompt engineering, and integrations with existing databases and possibly ERP/accounting systems.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs

The role reports into a central technology function that supports the whole organization. There is no separate “AI team” — the AI Solutions Developer is the primary builder of AI-driven solutions and works alongside customer service, accounting, operations, IT, and manufacturing to gather requirements and deploy tools. Close collaboration with order data entry, quoting, and customer service is explicit in the JD.
