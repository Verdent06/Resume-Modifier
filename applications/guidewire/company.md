# Guidewire

## What They Do
Guidewire provides a cloud-based SaaS platform for the property and casualty (P&C) insurance industry. Its suite runs core operations—underwriting, policy administration, billing, claims—with analytics and AI, serving ~1,600 implementations and a strong position in Gartner's Magic Quadrant for P&C Core Platforms. Customers subscribe for cloud access, implementation, and support. The company differentiates through deep P&C specialization, an integrated set of digital, core, analytics, and AI tools, and products like the Jutro design system (React-based) for building consistent digital experiences.

## Why This Company
I want to work on real customer-facing digital products in a domain where UX and reliability directly affect how insurers serve policyholders. Guidewire's Jutro platform and Professional Services implementation work mean I'd be building reusable React components and integrating with RESTful backends on live projects, not internal tools. The explicit focus on AI-assisted development (Copilot, Claude) and data-driven improvement matches how I already work. Toronto hybrid gives me a chance to learn from senior consultants and see how enterprise front-end and design systems scale.

## Engineering Culture
React-centric front-end (Jutro is React-based with SDKs, CLI, and metadata-driven components). Emphasis on design systems, accessibility, responsive design, and integration with Guidewire Cloud and core P&C apps. Professional Services does customer implementations with cross-functional teams (UX, BAs, implementation). Culture emphasizes curiosity, AI-augmented development, and repeatable patterns for scaling digital solutions. Developer docs, Jutro documentation, and hackathons (e.g., Jutro Hackathon) signal investment in developer experience.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs
Professional Services / Digital implementation teams—where the intern role lives. These teams use Jutro and Guidewire Digital products to deliver customer implementation projects, work with UX Designers and Business Analysts, and integrate front-ends with RESTful APIs and InsuranceSuite backends. Success means contributing features to real customer projects, pairing with senior consultants, and using AI-assisted tools to reduce implementation effort.
