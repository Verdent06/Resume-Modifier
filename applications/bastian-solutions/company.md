# Bastian Solutions

## What They Do

Bastian Solutions, a Toyota Automated Logistics company, is an independent material handling and robotics system integrator. They design and deliver automated solutions for distribution, manufacturing, and order fulfillment—conveyors, ASRS, AGVs, palletizing automation, and warehouse management software. Their proprietary software Exacta and viadat are developed in-house for warehouse and intralogistics control. The company has 20+ U.S. offices and international presence; they integrate equipment, software, and consulting for end-to-end automation.

## Why This Company

I want to build software that controls real-world automation—warehouse systems, pallet handling, and logistics—where code directly affects physical operations. Bastian’s Pallet Automation group and in-house Exacta/viadat stack are a strong fit: I’m looking for a co-op where I can learn C#, SQL Server, and enterprise dev practices (Visual Studio, validation, bug tracking) while contributing to software that ships to customer sites. Grand Rapids is in-state for me (MSU), and the three-rotation structure gives time to own features and see them through deployment. I’m eager to grow from specs and requirements into validation, test plans, and root-cause debugging with senior developers.

## Engineering Culture

In-house development of Exacta and viadat; stack centers on C#, C++, Visual Studio, SQL Server, and Oracle. Workflow emphasizes specifications, functional validation, design documents, and end-user requirements. Strong focus on bug tracking, log analysis, root-cause investigation, and test environments. Engineers deploy and test software manually and work closely with implementation teams; some travel to customer sites for rollout. Quality control and attention to detail are explicit values.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs

**Pallet Automation group** — The team hiring for this co-op. Develops and maintains warehouse management and pallet automation software (Exacta/viadat). Day-to-day work includes implementing features from specs, software validation, test plan design, bug triage and fix, and maintaining test environments. Co-ops work with senior developers and participate in the full development cycle from requirements through validation and deployment.
