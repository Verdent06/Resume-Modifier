## Role Summary
This is the Engagement Office’s operational backbone during summer: you keep records straight for the office and General Assembly, answer phones and route people correctly, handle mail and inventory basics, and run structured programs that depend on accuracy—especially the short-term student loan workflow (eligibility checks, issuing/collecting payments, compiling applications and reports, updating records). Success at three months means the Business Manager trusts your cash drawer reconciliation, your loan paperwork is complete the first time, room reservations and key logs are current, and students get clear, courteous answers about ASMSU and MSU.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Trust with money and records:** loan documentation, deposits, and end-of-day cash handling signal integrity and attention to detail.
- **Front-desk professionalism:** phone etiquette, triaging questions, and patient service for students and the public.
- **Office software fluency:** databases/spreadsheets/word processing are explicit requirements—not optional “nice skills.”
- **Reliability under repetition:** filing, mail prep, inventory counts, and maintaining contracts/logs reward consistency more than creativity.
- **Comfort being the face of ASMSU:** the JD explicitly calls out customer service to MSU students and the public.
- **Judgment on eligibility/rules:** loan eligibility isn’t engineering; it’s following policy carefully and documenting decisions.
