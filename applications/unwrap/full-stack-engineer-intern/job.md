## Role Summary

The Full Stack Engineer Intern owns and delivers major customer-facing components end-to-end on a TypeScript/GraphQL/Node backend and React frontend. Day-to-day work centers on understanding customer needs, helping compile sprint requirements, and shipping core product features, with primary focus on designing and building user-facing tools. The company expects a builder who spends 80%+ time actively developing, owns what they build, ships robust high-quality software, and is comfortable with ambiguous product requirements and meeting with customers. Success at three months looks like shipping at least one significant customer-facing feature from design through deploy and participating in sprint planning and customer context.

## What They're Really Looking For

- **Builder-first:** Someone who wants to code most of the time, not only spec or support—80%+ developing is explicit.
- **End-to-end ownership:** Can take a feature from fuzzy requirements to shipped, not just implement tickets.
- **Full-stack fluency:** Real work in both frontend (React) and backend (Node/GraphQL), not one side only.
- **Product and customer comfort:** Willing to meet with customers and tolerate ambiguous requirements—startup-style product discovery.
- **Quality and pride in ship:** “Robust high-quality software” and “take pride in shipping” signal they care about tests, clarity, and maintainability, not just speed.
