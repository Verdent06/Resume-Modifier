# Draper

## What They Do

Draper is an independent, nonprofit R&D company headquartered in Cambridge, MA, with 2,000+ employees. They deliver engineering solutions for national challenges across defense, space, and biomedical domains—including next-generation resilient and fault-tolerant embedded software for undersea, terrestrial, air, hypersonics, and space systems. Work spans low-level software for real-time embedded systems, device drivers, and the full product development lifecycle from requirements through Verification and Validation.

## Why This Company

I want to work on low-level software for real-time embedded systems and learn engineering processes that matter for mission-critical systems. Draper’s focus on C/C++, real-time operating systems, and embedded Linux aligns with my interest in systems and computer organization; I’ve taken Computer Organization and Architecture and built real-time pipelines (e.g., 60 FPS gesture recognition with sub-20ms latency). I’m drawn to the chance to contribute to requirements-through-V&V workflows and to strengthen my testing and documentation habits in an environment where correctness and traceability matter.

## Engineering Culture

Known for multidisciplinary, collaborative teams and Agile delivery from customer requirements to field-deployed solutions. Stack emphasis: C/C++, real-time systems (VxWorks, FreeRTOS, RTEMS), ARM/RISC-V/low-power processors, embedded Linux, device drivers, and protocol work (CAN, I2C, SPI, etc.). Engineers use in-circuit emulators, JTAG, version control, and full lifecycle visibility from design through deployment. Workplace flexibility, clubs, and off-site events; many roles require security clearance.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs

The Software Engineering Intern role sits in the Software Engineering Division, focused on low-level software for real-time embedded systems. Day-to-day work involves design and development of engineering solutions, implementation and testing, analysis, documentation, and test reports (plots, tables, evaluation criteria). No specific sub-team named in the JD; the division is the relevant org.
