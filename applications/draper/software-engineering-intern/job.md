## Role Summary
The Software Engineering Intern works in Draper’s Software Engineering Division on low-level software for real-time embedded systems. Day-to-day work includes designing and developing engineering solutions for hard problems, implementing and testing code, performing analysis, and providing documentation. You’ll generate and present test reports (plots, tables, evaluation criteria, conclusions) and complete assignments that build practical knowledge of processes, procedures, equipment, and systems across engineering disciplines. Success at three months means delivering tested, documented work that fits into the division’s requirements-through-V&V lifecycle and showing you can work independently or on a team in a changing environment.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **C/C++ competency** — Not just “used in a class”; they want someone who can write and test low-level or systems-oriented code. Any project or coursework that shows C/C++ with measurable outcomes helps.
- **Real-time / systems mindset** — Understanding of timing, determinism, or resource constraints. Real-time pipelines (e.g., 60 FPS, sub-20ms latency), performance tuning, or low-level optimizations are strong signals.
- **Testing and documentation discipline** — Test reports, coverage, and clear documentation matter for V&V. CI/CD with coverage gates and structured test reporting are relevant.
- **Product lifecycle awareness** — Requirements → design → implementation → test → V&V. Experience with branch protection, code review, and traceable changes is a plus.
- **Adaptability and ownership** — “Ability to adapt to ever-changing environments” and “work independently or in a team” mean they want someone who can take an assignment and run with it without constant hand-holding.
- **Clear communication** — Written and oral; test reports and conclusions from data must be presentable. Strong computer skills (Word, PowerPoint, Excel) support reporting.
