## Role Summary
Co-op software engineers contribute to initiatives that improve operational efficiency and decision-making through Agentic AI. Day-to-day work includes developing and implementing solutions that process and analyze diverse data sources, automate workflows, and deliver insights so business groups can use data-driven intelligence. You’ll apply foundational programming (Python, Java, C/C++, or Go), data structures and algorithms, and collaboration tools (Confluence, JIRA, Jenkins, GitHub) to build and maintain high-quality, reliable platforms. Success at three months looks like shipping features that automate or accelerate real workflows and demonstrating comfort with the team’s stack and processes.
