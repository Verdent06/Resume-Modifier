# SpotHero

## What They Do

SpotHero is a technology-driven parking reservation and payment platform. Drivers use the SpotHero app or website to find, compare, and book parking in advance at 13,000+ locations across 400+ cities in the U.S. and Canada; parking operators use SpotHero to reach customers and optimize operations. The company processes payments, provides APIs for checkout and operator tools, and has white-labeled integrations (e.g., Uber post-acquisition). Revenue is commission-based on completed bookings. SpotHero has raised $120M+ and was acquired by Uber in early 2026 to power in-app parking reservations.

I want to work on backend systems that power real transactions and scale—payment flows, APIs, and data reliability—at a company where engineering quality directly affects both drivers and partners. SpotHero’s stack (Kotlin, Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, Kafka, gRPC) and squad model (e.g., Money Squad for payments, Platform for latency and reliability) line up with my experience in APIs, databases, and CI/CD. ## Engineering Culture

Backend is built with Kotlin, Python (Django), PostgreSQL, Kafka (async), and gRPC (service-to-service). They use a modular-monolith style with domain-driven design. Deployment runs on Docker, Kubernetes, and Concourse; observability uses Sentry, SumoLogic, and Grafana. Engineering is organized into Leagues and Squads (e.g., Money Squad for payments and refunds, Platform Engineering, Integrations). They emphasize outcome-focused work, mentorship, experimentation, and psychological safety. Notable wins include cutting inventory data latency from 5s to 50ms and reducing race-condition errors by 98% (Platform), and resilience work like the Integration Circuit Breaker (Integrations Squad).
