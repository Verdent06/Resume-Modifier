# Engineering Intern — SpotHero

## Quick Facts
- **Type:** Internship (Summer 2026)
- **Location:** Chicago, IL — Hybrid (1–2 days/week in office)
- **Compensation:** $25/hr, 40 hrs/week; lunch stipend, parking stipend
- **Template Used:** full-stack

## ATS Keywords

## Role Summary
Backend-focused engineering intern on a squad (e.g., Money Squad for payments/APIs or general backend) building and supporting systems that power SpotHero’s parking reservation and payment platform. You’ll participate in the full product lifecycle—concept to deployment—troubleshoot and debug issues, and collaborate with engineering, product, and design. Success looks like shipping small features or fixes, learning their stack (Kotlin, Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, Kafka, gRPC), and demonstrating strong communication and teamwork. The JD asks for GitHub, LinkedIn, and a project you’re proud of; application window is short (March 9–13, 2026).

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Backend fundamentals:** SQL and at least one of Python/Java/Kotlin in real projects, not just coursework.
- **Full lifecycle exposure:** Evidence you’ve touched design, development, testing, and deployment (CI/CD, Docker, or similar).
- **Collaboration signal:** Cross-functional work, code review, or team ownership (e.g., lead, sprint planning).
- **Quality and reliability:** Testing, coverage, or debugging with measurable impact (latency, errors, throughput).
- **Fit for growth-stage:** Comfort with ambiguity, willingness to learn their stack, and “give drivers the right of way” — quality and user impact matter.
- **No visa sponsorship:** Must be authorized to work in the US; candidate is U.S. citizen, so no gap.

## Required Skills
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment
- Basic proficiency in SQL, a programming language (e.g., Python, Java, Kotlin), and engineering tools
- A foundation in engineering principles
- Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, or a related technical field, or a recent graduate
