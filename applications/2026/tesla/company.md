# Tesla

Tesla builds electric vehicles, energy storage, solar products, and the software platforms that operate them at fleet scale. For this Commercial UI Software Engineer internship, the technical center is Tesla Energy: production web systems that monitor, aggregate, and control real-time data from millions of IoT devices. The company brand is strong, but the engineering culture is known for speed, ownership, and a high bar for production discipline.
