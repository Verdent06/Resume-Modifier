# Recruiter Persona — Applied AI Engineer, AI Hardware (Fall 2026)

## Role Summary

Tesla's AI Hardware team builds AI-powered applications that accelerate the chip development lifecycle — from static timing analysis and physical synthesis optimization to novel placement algorithms and RTL-level experimentation. The intern designs production AI tooling, maintains LLM-agent workflows for root-cause analysis and coverage scripting, and bridges software models with silicon reality alongside cross-functional hardware and software teams. Surface JD reads as applied ML + agentic LLM deployment on geometric/netlist-style data; company identity is Tesla's in-house AI silicon cadence (AI5/AI6 and beyond), not generic web or EV software.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** `ai-ml` — requirements test Python, PyTorch/TensorFlow/JAX, graph ML (PyG/DGL) for netlists/geometric data, LLM-agent deployment and workflows, and data pipelines for circuit/simulation datasets.
- **differentiator:** `ai-hardware` / silicon-EDA — chip development context (STA, physical synthesis, placement, Cadence/Synopsys tooling familiarity, closing the loop between models and silicon).
- **track_divergence:** `false` — ai-hardware is a domain lens on top of the ai-ml spine; the resume leads with applied ML and agentic LLM evidence, with close-to-metal systems depth as a secondary credibility signal where the page supports it.

## Team & Bar

Tesla screens fast: human + ATS pass, then HackerRank OA (medium), then ~3 technical rounds where tech depth is the bottleneck (~5–8% estimated offer rate at B-tier). The recruiter is a hardware-adjacent applied-ML hiring manager or senior IC — not a pure research scientist. They want someone who has shipped Python ML code, understands model training and evaluation beyond notebooks, can reason about deploying LLM agents into real engineering workflows, and shows curiosity about EDA/silicon tooling even without prior chip experience. Bias for action, experimentation, and picking up Cadence/Synopsys-class tools quickly. Intern bar: demonstrated framework fluency and end-to-end ML workflow, not production EDA tenure.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**
- Python and at least one major ML framework evidenced in bullets, not Skills alone.
- LLM-agent or agentic-workflow delivery with eval, orchestration, or deployment mechanics — not a single API-call demo.
- Model training or fine-tuning with held-out evaluation and a quantified lift — not inference-only API wrappers.
- Data-pipeline or dataset engineering at scale (ingestion, normalization, aggregation) relevant to simulation or structured engineering data.
- Close-to-metal or low-latency systems work (C++, real-time constraints) as credibility for "closing the loop between AI models and silicon reality."
- Coursework surfacing math/stats foundations (linear algebra, calculus, probability, discrete math).
- One-page, dense, metric-backed bullets; shipped systems over coursework-only ML.

**Anti-patterns:**
- Notebook ML or Kaggle-style projects with no delivery or evaluation rigor.
- Skills-list keyword stuffing (PyTorch, JAX, PyG) with zero bullet proof.
- Pure full-stack or frontend resumes with no training/inference depth.
- Club-ops, outreach, or community growth framing without engineering substance.
- Generic "built an AI app" without workflow, eval, or data-pipeline detail.
- Missing Python in demonstrated work when the JD treats it as a floor.
