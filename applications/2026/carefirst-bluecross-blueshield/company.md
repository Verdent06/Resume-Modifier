# CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield

CareFirst BlueCross BlueShield is a not-for-profit regional health insurer serving roughly 3.5 million members across Maryland, Washington, D.C., Northern Virginia, and beyond. The technical signal for this application is healthcare analytics: extracting and integrating data from multiple systems, building dashboards and reports, and translating claims/member/provider data into decisions that improve engagement, outcomes, cost of care, and operations.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated
- **HQ / offices:** Baltimore, MD and Washington, D.C.; remote role limited to MD/DC/VA
- **Valuation / signal:** One of the largest not-for-profit health insurers in the Mid-Atlantic; 3.5M members
- **Product focus:** Health insurance products and analytics supporting care quality, provider performance, clinical outcomes, and cost-of-care decisions
- **Intern comp (2026 data analytics):** $18,720 - $26,000 annualized intern pay range, paid hourly
- **Work model:** Year-round internship; 15-20 hrs/week during Fall/Spring, 35-40 hrs/week during summer; remote MD/DC/VA
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Must be eligible to work in the U.S. without sponsorship; junior/senior undergraduate or graduate student; GPA 3.0+

## Interview Process

| Stage | Format | Notes |
| ----- | ------ | ----- |
| Resume screen | Human + ATS | Looks for junior/senior/grad eligibility, GPA gate, analytics coursework, SQL/Python/R, visualization, and communication |
| Recruiter screen | Phone/video | Likely confirms work authorization, year-round availability, location eligibility, GPA, and interest in healthcare analytics |
| Hiring manager / team interview | Behavioral + analytics scenario | Expect dashboard/reporting, stakeholder communication, SQL/data integration, and healthcare/business-context questions |
| Behavioral | Culture and collaboration | Mission alignment, iterative learning, documentation, and cross-functional communication matter |

**Estimated funnel:** 2-3 rounds · Easy/practical analytics · No known OA · No system design · Bottleneck: resume eligibility + analytics fit · Moderate for eligible, local candidates

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** SQL, Python, R
- **Domains:** Data analytics, reporting, dashboards, relational databases, healthcare operations, quality/cost-of-care analytics
- **What wins:** Evidence of extracting, integrating, and explaining data; SQL/database work shown in bullets rather than skills alone; dashboards or stakeholder-facing analysis; clear documentation and communication.
