# Arcfield

Arcfield is a U.S. defense and national-security systems integrator — spun from Peraton's space and engineering businesses — that delivers systems engineering, digital engineering, modeling and simulation, space/launch support, and test-range services for DoD and allied customers. The Middletown, RI site sits in the Naval Undersea Warfare Center (NUWC) ecosystem, where adjacent full-time roles emphasize unmanned-vehicle software, Linux/Windows polyglot development, integration/test discipline, and Agile delivery under government contract rigor.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated (not in reference/companies.md; defense integrator comparable to B/C-tier primes)
- **HQ / offices:** Chantilly, VA (HQ); Middletown, RI (this posting); multi-state presence
- **Valuation / signal:** 68+ year legacy via predecessor firms; federal contractor; mission-critical defense/space work
- **Product focus:** Systems engineering & integration, digital engineering, modeling/simulation, unmanned systems software support
- **Intern comp (2026 SWE):** Not stated in JD
