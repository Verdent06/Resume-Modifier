## Role Summary
Interns are placed in one of Visa's technology organizations based on skills, interest, and team needs. Day-to-day work involves one or more hands-on projects—examples from past summers include self-serve Kafka deployment with Docker/Kubernetes and auto-scale, NL chatbots for internal knowledge bases, payment automation prototypes, low-latency duplicate-account removal, Visa Checkout improvements, internal testing tools, transaction monitoring, digital currency routing applications, and mobile banking app factories. Success at three months means delivering a concrete project outcome, participating in case challenges, and engaging in networking and workshops. The bar is generalist SWE: code, test, document, collaborate, and show willingness to learn.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Generalist SWE with one strong language** — Java, Python, or JavaScript preferred; ability to write clean, testable code and follow secure coding practices.
- **Exposure to modern infra** — Kubernetes, Docker, or CI/CD (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, ArgoCD) even from class projects or labs signals readiness for platform-style work.
- **Testing and quality mindset** — unit testing, test design, root cause analysis, defect resolution; "document process, designs, test results."
- **Collaboration and communication** — clear written and spoken communication, no repeated grammatical/typo errors; team player, interpersonal skills, lead through ambiguity.
- **Optional but valued** — RDBMS/NoSQL (MySQL, Cassandra), observability (Prometheus, Splunk, OpenSearch), agentic/ML solutions; not required but differentiators.

- Strong ability to collaborate; highly driven, resourceful and results oriented; good team player and excellent interpersonal skills; good analytical and problem-solving skills; demonstrated ability to lead and navigate through ambiguity
