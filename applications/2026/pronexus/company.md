# ProNexus

ProNexus builds an AI-powered expert sourcing and workflow platform for diligence and market research teams — a system of record for sourcing, vetting, scheduling, and outreach across primary research projects. Founded in October 2024 by former EY-Parthenon strategy consultant Tom Myers (CEO) and Erica Dippold (CTO), the company pivoted from an expert-network aggregator model to an integrated expert network plus platform in early 2026. The product automates enrichment, classification, routing, and CRM-style project execution for human-intelligence workflows — the technical bet is agentic GenAI over brittle manual sourcing, not model training.
