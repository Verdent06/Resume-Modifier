## Role Summary
Interns build and support full-stack applications (frontend UIs and backend services), create or improve REST APIs and backend services with senior guidance, and contribute to GenAI use cases such as chatbots, internal tools, and AI-assisted workflows. Day-to-day work includes prompt engineering and experimentation with LLMs, learning RAG and vector search, researching emerging AI tools and frameworks, and collaborating with engineering, QA, UX, and product. Success at three months means shipping maintainable code, solid unit testing, clear documentation, and active participation in Agile ceremonies and demos.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Full-stack comfort**: Ability to touch both React/Vue frontends and Node.js or Python backends without hand-holding.
- **API and backend literacy**: REST APIs and backend services are central; they want someone who can own a small service or endpoint.
- **GenAI curiosity over expertise**: Hands-on LLM experience not required; they want someone willing to learn prompt engineering, RAG, and AI workflows.
- **Cloud and tooling exposure**: AWS (Lambda, S3, EC2) and Git/GitHub matter; they prefer candidates who have used them in projects or coursework.
- **Collaboration and process**: Agile (stand-ups, sprint planning, retrospectives), documentation, and demos matter as much as code.
- **Clean, testable code**: Unit testing and basic validation are explicit expectations.
