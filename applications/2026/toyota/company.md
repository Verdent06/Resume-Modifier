# Toyota

## What They Do
Toyota is one of the world's largest automakers and is shifting toward a mobility company—connected vehicles, software-defined features, and services beyond traditional manufacturing. Toyota's OneTech division is the internal IT organization that powers core business operations: thousands of applications and services supporting consumer-facing systems, dealer tools, corporate functions, supply chain and logistics, R&D solutions, and manufacturing technology. OneTech enables in-vehicle technology, cybersecurity, manufacturing operations, and business operations across the company and has been recognized for its DevOps and engineering practices.

I want to apply my experience in automation, CI/CD, and full-stack delivery to real business and manufacturing systems at scale. OneTech's focus on Agile and DevSecOps, alignment with business units (Consumer, Dealer, Supply Chain, R&D, Manufacturing), and the chance to work on architecture, data science, and analytics—or software development that supports the "Toyota Way"—aligns with what I've built: automated onboarding and event systems (Claude Builder Club), dual-database and CI/CD delivery (MindMosaic), and distributed voice/ML pipelines (Mira). I'm drawn to contributing to innovative solutions for the auto industry's challenges while strengthening the link between IT and business outcomes. Saline's R&D headquarters is a strong fit for someone who wants to see how enterprise IT supports both product and manufacturing.

## Engineering Culture
OneTech is known for investing in DevOps practices and industry recognition in that space, diversity and lifelong learning, and recruiting from varied backgrounds. The organization supports thousands of applications and services and emphasizes alignment between IT and business units. Stack and practices include Agile, DevSecOps, architecture and data management, and work that spans software development, business process re-engineering, cybersecurity, and R&D. Toyota's broader technical footprint includes research divisions (e.g., InfoTech Labs) focused on connected mobility, data/ML platforms, and digital twin technology.
