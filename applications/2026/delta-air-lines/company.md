# Delta Air Lines

Delta Air Lines is a Fortune 500 global carrier headquartered in Atlanta, GA, and one of the largest airlines in the world by revenue and fleet. Its Innovation & AI Engineering group builds machine-learning and AI products that reshape customer- and employee-facing experiences — personalization and recommendation for travelers, NLP and computer-vision tooling, operational anomaly detection, and decision-support for business units across the airline. The intern role is explicitly framed as "not a typical engineering role": interns own projects from day one, engage business stakeholders directly, and present to leaders across the company. This is an applied-ML product team inside a legacy enterprise, so the win is shipping defensible models against real business problems, not research novelty.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated (not in `reference/companies.md`) — Fortune 500 airline, strong consumer brand, Atlanta prestige employer
- **HQ / offices:** Atlanta, GA (HQ); this role is Atlanta, onsite
- **Valuation / signal:** ~$40B+ revenue global carrier; established enterprise, not a startup — AI/Innovation is a strategic internal investment area
- **Product focus:** ML/AI products for customer and employee experience — personalization/recommendation, NLP, computer vision, anomaly detection, operations
- **Intern comp (2026 AI/ML):** Not stated in JD (research gap); Delta pays competitive intern hourly for tech co-ops/internships
- **Work model:** Onsite, Atlanta GA; Fall 2026 term; internship (co-op rotations run spring/summer/fall)
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Actively enrolled undergraduate during the program; 18+; HS diploma/GED; authorization to work in the U.S. (undergraduate posting — distinct from the M.S./MBA/PhD graduate posting)

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python (primary for ML); TensorFlow / PyTorch / other ML platforms; SQL for data
- **Domains:** applied machine learning — NLP, computer vision, personalization/recommendation, anomaly detection; neural networks, deep reinforcement learning, gradient-boosted trees; feature engineering, model selection, training/serving
- **What wins:** A defensible end-to-end ML project narrated decision-by-decision (data → features → model selection → evaluation → deployment), Python + PyTorch fluency shown *through use* in bullets, and evidence the candidate can talk to non-technical stakeholders and present results — Delta screens hard on communication, ownership, and STAR-style behavioral fit, not just LeetCode. Applied/GenAI delivery (agents, eval harnesses, deployed pipelines) reads as exactly the "AI-based products that enhance customer/employee experience" the JD asks for.
