# Global Relay

## What They Do
Global Relay is a Vancouver-headquartered leader in enterprise information archiving and compliance, serving over 20,000 customers in 90+ countries including 22 of the top 25 banks. They capture, preserve, and make searchable the communications data of highly regulated firms (email, collaboration tools, voice, social, financial messaging) via a single cloud-native platform with eDiscovery, surveillance, and analytics. The company builds proprietary technology in-house rather than acquiring it and has been bootstrapped and profitable for 25+ years.

## Why This Company
I want to work on large-scale data pipelines and analytics at a company where the stack is the product—archiving and querying massive, heterogeneous communication datasets at compliance-grade reliability. Global Relay’s use of Hadoop, Spark, and NoSQL (HBase) for analytics and search aligns with my interest in distributed systems and backend data engineering. The co-op’s focus on implementing a data analytics engine with senior guidance is a concrete way to grow in Java, big-data tooling, and requirements/architecture tradeoffs with product and architects. Vancouver’s tech scene and Global Relay’s long-term, engineering-first culture make it a place where I can contribute to real systems while learning how regulated, high-scale data platforms are built.

## Engineering Culture
Engineering is emphasized as patient, collaborative, and problem-focused; they “love solving complex problems as a team, using the latest technology and methodologies.” The platform is built from the ground up as a single integrated system, with early adoption of cloud and open-source foundations. Day-to-day stack includes Hadoop (MapReduce, HDFS), HBase, Spark, Kafka, Flume, Zookeeper, Thrift/Protocol Buffers, Jenkins, Ansible, Oozie, ElasticSearch, and dependency-injection frameworks (Guice, Spring). Security (SSL, OAuth) and CI/CD are part of the standard workflow.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs
The Co-op Java Developer role sits within Global Relay’s software development team, building a data analytics engine and application on a Hadoop software stack. The team works with product management on requirements and with architects on implementation options and technology tradeoffs. No specific org name is given; the role is the primary team context for this position.
