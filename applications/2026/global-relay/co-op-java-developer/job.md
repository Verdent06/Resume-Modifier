## Role Summary
The co-op implements a data analytics engine and application on a Hadoop-based software stack under senior developers and architects. Day-to-day work includes refining requirements with product management, researching implementation options, and presenting viable options plus impact on requirements to architects and PM. Success at three months looks like a working analytics component or pipeline, clear documentation of technology choices, and familiarity with the team’s stack: HBase, Zookeeper, Thrift/Protocol Buffers, Hadoop, Flume, Kafka, Spark, Jenkins, Ansible, Oozie, ElasticSearch, and Spring/Guice. The role is backend/data-engineering focused within a regulated, large-scale archiving and eDiscovery context.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Java fluency** — coursework or work experience with Java; the rest of the stack is “asset” or learn-on-the-job.
- **Co-op experience** — they want someone who has done a co-op before (or is in a co-op program), i.e. proven in a professional dev environment.
- **Ability to work with requirements** — comfortable refining specs with PM and translating technical tradeoffs for non-engineers.
- **Research and present options** — can evaluate implementation choices and articulate how tech choices affect requirements; not just coding.
- **Comfort with big-data ecosystem** — Hadoop, Spark, NoSQL (HBase) are daily-use technologies; prior exposure is a plus but not strictly required for the role.
- **CI/CD and quality practices** — Jenkins, Agile, TDD, continuous integration are listed; they want someone who can slot into their pipeline and quality bar.
