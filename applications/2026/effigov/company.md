# EffiGov

EffiGov builds AI voice assistants and modern software for local governments — starting with 24/7 AI phone agents that handle 311 and departmental constituent lines (permits, animal control, service requests, routing). The platform integrates with municipal CRM, ticketing, and telephony stacks so residents get answers and action in one call without cities staffing every desk around the clock. Y Combinator–backed (2025 cohort); live deployments in multiple jurisdictions (e.g., Sumter County, FL — ~160K residents). HQ in San Francisco; engineering culture is early-stage startup: ship features fast, debug production AI systems, own scoped product problems with founders.
