EffiGov hires a Fall 2026 Software Engineer Intern to onboard at SF HQ, ship a first feature on Python/FastAPI or TypeScript/Next.js, trace and fix real customer issues through app, integrations, and infrastructure, improve deployments or internal tooling, and own scoped product problems with founders and engineers — including debugging production AI voice systems and launching capabilities for government customers. ## Screen Criteria

- Full-stack evidence through bullets: backend services (FastAPI/Flask or Node), APIs/REST, client or dashboard work, cloud deployment (AWS, Docker, CI) — not Skills-only keyword stuffing.
- Python and TypeScript should appear in project or experience context; React/Next.js client depth supports the JD; FastAPI or equivalent async API layer is a floor signal.
- Production AI / voice-AI signals: inference pipelines, cost/latency wins, agentic workflows, eval/hardening, debugging live systems — preferred in JD and core to company identity.
- Strong signals: self-built products with paying users or institutional delivery, sole-engineer ownership, measurable impact metrics, initiative without being asked, civic/public-sector adjacency as a plus.
- Anti-patterns: generic AI buzzwords without architectural decisions; single-bullet thin entries; club-growth framing without ship depth; Skills-line MongoDB/Next.js with zero bullet support.
- When track diverges, first read should say "ships full-stack product software" while a parallel read registers voice-AI and production LLM/agent depth in the top half of the page.
