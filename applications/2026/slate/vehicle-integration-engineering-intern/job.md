## Role Summary
The intern supports development, testing, and optimization of vehicle performance and functionality across multiple systems and components. Day-to-day work includes installing and configuring CAN instrumentation on prototype vehicles, working with cloud-transmitted vehicle data to identify issues and conditions that lead to root cause, and building analytics dashboards for fleet performance, efficiency, usage metrics, open issues, and requirements validation. You will collaborate with Chassis, Electrical, and Powertrain engineers to define signals and triggers for data collection, help engineers and managers use reporting tools and dashboards for KPIs, and support vehicle-level testing and validation via the requirement management system. Success at three months looks like owning at least one dashboard or data pipeline in production use and contributing to at least one root-cause finding from instrumented data.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **CS/EE/ME hybrid:** Someone who can wire up CAN and also write Python and build dashboards—bridge between vehicle hardware and data tooling.
- **Data-to-decision:** Comfort mining messy vehicle/telemetry data to find patterns and conditions that explain failures or behavior.
- **Dashboard and reporting literacy:** Build things management and engineers will use daily (Grafana, Excel, or similar); clarity and KPIs matter.
- **Cross-functional comfort:** You work with Chassis, Electrical, Powertrain; no silos—signals and triggers come from those teams.
- **Hands-on and lab/plant capable:** Willing to be on vehicles and in the plant, not only at a desk.
- **Start-up fit:** Adaptable, proactive, scrappy; OK with ambiguity and changing priorities.
