# Website Intern — MSU International Studies & Programs

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Part-time internship (20 hrs/wk typical; 15–20 minimum)
- **Location:** Hybrid, East Lansing, MI (on campus / in office part of the week)
- **Compensation:** $15/hr
- **Template Used:** full-stack

## ATS Keywords

International Studies & Programs, website redesign, migration, web content, content management system, CMS, WordPress, Drupal, Sitecore, digital accessibility, user experience, UX, quality assurance, website analytics, SEO, content strategy, digital communications, hybrid, undergraduate, legacy sites, content migration

## Role Summary

This intern backs ISP’s web team while multiple unit sites move through a redesign and CMS migration. Day to day, that means reviewing and organizing web content, uploading and formatting pages in the new system, fixing links, and running structured checks so new pages behave correctly on desktop and mobile. Over a summer, success looks like a migration queue that stays unblocked: content owners get clear feedback, defects are logged and retested, and analytics or SEO tasks are handled as one-off requests rather than as recurring fire drills.

## What They're Really Looking For

- Someone who treats the website as operational infrastructure: accurate, timely, and navigable for non-expert visitors.
- Evidence you can follow a checklist without hiding behind it—you still notice broken states and ask questions.
- Comfort with stakeholder-driven iteration (edit cycles, approvals) more than greenfield coding.
- Enough digital fluency to learn a CMS quickly even if the logo on the login screen changes.
- Availability through summer 2026 (and fall 2026 is a plus), with predictable weekly hours during business hours.

## Required Skills

- Currently enrolled as an undergraduate student at MSU for the duration of the internship
- One to two years of demonstrated experience with web content and content management systems
- College coursework in web content management, digital accessibility, communications, or user experience

## Preferred Skills

- Familiarity with website content management systems (CMS), such as WordPress, Drupal, or Sitecore (training will be provided)
- Basic understanding of digital accessibility standards or willingness to learn
- Ability to follow defined workflows and documentation but also take initiative and work independently
- Excellent organizational skills and the ability to handle multiple tasks with deliverables and deadlines
- Ability to be actively involved in weekly staff meetings, as well as other regular inter-departmental meetings
