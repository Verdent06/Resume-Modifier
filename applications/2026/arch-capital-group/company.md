# Arch Capital Group

## What They Do
Arch Capital Group Ltd. operates globally in three segments: Insurance, Reinsurance, and Mortgage. The Insurance segment covers specialty lines (construction, environmental, offshore energy, marine); the Reinsurance segment handles property and casualty risk transfer; the Mortgage segment provides mortgage insurance and reinsurance. Arch underwrites policies, sets premiums to match risk, and earns investment income from premiums and investments. IT supports underwriting and reinsurance workflows with in-house and third-party systems—services-oriented architecture, J2EE/Java back-ends, Oracle/SQL Server, and modern integrations—while exploring GenAI and agentic AI to improve underwriting efficiency and decisioning.

## Why This Company
I want to work at Arch because the Reinsurance IT team sits at the intersection of full-stack product engineering and applied AI in a regulated, high-stakes domain. Building tools and services that underwriting and business stakeholders actually use—and prototyping GenAI assistants (summarization, Q&A over documents, workflow guidance) with guardrails and compliance in mind—is exactly the kind of problem I find compelling: real impact on decision quality and operational efficiency, not just demos. Arch’s focus on integrating AI into underwriting and operational applications, with explicit interest in RAG and agentic AI, aligns with my experience on Mira (RAG, async pipelines, multi-step workflows) and my interest in systems that are auditable and safe. I’m drawn to the technical challenge of making AI useful inside enterprise workflows rather than in isolation.

## Engineering Culture
Arch’s IT organization uses a hybrid stack: J2EE/Java back-ends, Oracle and SQL Server, .NET and SharePoint on the front-end in some areas, with a push toward flexible platforms that support changing underwriting needs. The company develops custom systems where off-the-shelf solutions don’t fit and is investing in GenAI and agentic AI for underwriting and operations. Engineering emphasizes collaboration with business stakeholders, Agile delivery (JIRA, GitHub), and clear documentation and demos for both technical and non-technical audiences. Security, compliance, and vendor management are first-class concerns.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs
Reinsurance IT is the team implied by this JD: they own application and system requirements across underwriting and reinsurance workflows, build and enhance tools and web applications used across the Reinsurance IT landscape, and integrate AI/GenAI capabilities (summarization, Q&A over documents, workflow guidance, agentic AI with guardrails) into underwriting and operational applications. Success means delivering end-to-end features from UI (Angular) to backend (Java/Spring Boot), integrating APIs and microservices, and prototyping AI use cases that improve UX and decisioning efficiency while staying compliant.
