# Cadence Design Systems

## What They Do
Cadence Design Systems provides software, hardware, and IP for electronic design automation (EDA) so customers can design advanced semiconductor chips and electronic systems. Their tools cover system-level design and verification, digital and analog IC design, and custom design flows; IP offerings include Tensilica processors and DSP cores. They differentiate with an end-to-end EDA portfolio and deep collaboration with industry leaders across implementation, verification, and IP, helping customers bring silicon and electronics to market faster while reducing risk in AI, automotive, and other domains.

## Why This Company
I want to work at Cadence because the SVG team’s mix of data mining, analysis tools, and machine learning applications sits exactly where I’ve been building—ML pipelines, intent classification, and real-time systems—but applied to hardware and EDA tooling. Contributing to tools that semiconductor teams use daily, and learning Cadence’s core products and workflows, would ground my software and ML skills in a domain that has clear impact on what gets built. The chance to own a summer project end-to-end (requirements, implementation, debugging, documentation) and present results aligns with how I’ve led projects and shipped features on timelines.

## Engineering Culture
Cadence emphasizes "One Cadence—One Team" with values around innovation, quality, agility, and integrity. Technically, the stack is built on computational software and algorithms powering synthesis, placement, routing, timing, and power analysis. They are investing in GPU-accelerated computing and a five-level AI approach (optimization AI through conversational LLMs to autonomy) to reduce SoC engineering time. Engineering work centers on C/C++, Python, performance, and correctness in tools used by chip designers worldwide.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs
The **SVG (Systems Verification Group or similar)** team is the one hiring for this role. The JD calls out contributing to SVG projects: data mining and analysis tools, machine learning applications, C++ development, and open source exploration. Success means defining requirements, planning, coding, debugging, testing, documenting, training on Cadence core products, working with a mentor, and presenting results at the end of the summer.
