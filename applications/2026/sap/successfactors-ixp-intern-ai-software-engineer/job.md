## Role Summary
The intern designs and builds full-stack AI-driven solutions for automated performance analysis across SAP SuccessFactors. Day-to-day work includes building tools with Agentic AI and GenAI to detect, report, and resolve product performance issues; analyzing AI and product workflows to identify performance problems and anti-patterns; and creating AI-powered solutions for automated MUPT performance testing scripts. The role sits at the intersection of AI/ML tooling, cloud-native development, and performance engineering. Success at three months means shipping tools that improve how the team finds and fixes performance issues, and adopting open-source tech in the SAP context.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Full-stack + AI in one package** — Someone who can ship both the ML/AI logic and the surrounding services and tooling (APIs, frontends, pipelines).
- **Performance mindset** — Not just "used Docker" but "optimized/analyzed a component or API for performance, reliability, or scalability." They want evidence you've measured and improved latency, throughput, or resource use.
- **GenAI/Agentic AI literacy** — Ability to assess benefits and shortcomings of AI/ML in an end-to-end workflow; bonus for adopting AI for automated analysis of real-world or software tasks.
- **Cloud-native execution** — Comfort with at least one major cloud (Azure, GCP, AWS) and containerization (Docker, Kubernetes). SAP runs at scale; they want interns who won't be lost in that environment.
- **Language depth** — Java, Python, or Go with enough depth to understand execution behavior and its impact on a software product (not just syntax).
- **Well-architected software** — Passion for elegant, maintainable tools and services that incorporate recent AI and software tech; debugging and defect resolution as table stakes.
