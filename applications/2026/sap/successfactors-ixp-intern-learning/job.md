## Role Summary
Full-stack software development on SAP SuccessFactors Learning — the HR learning/training product used by 100M+ users with 1B+ peak daily transactions. You will design, develop, test, and enhance features; participate in lean/agile ceremonies (stand-ups, sprint planning, reviews, retrospectives); and collaborate with Product, Design, and other engineering teams. The role offers exposure to Gen AI, foundation models, and LLMs. Success at 3 months means shipping tested features, owning a slice of the product, and demonstrating ownership through the development and deployment lifecycle.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Strong CS fundamentals** — Algorithms, data structures, OOP, design patterns. They want someone who can reason about design, not just implement tickets.
- **Full-stack or backend-leaning** — Backend (REST, microservices, Java/Spring) and frontend (React/Angular/Vue) both matter; backend + DB is table stakes.
- **DevOps mindset** — Ownership from development through deployment; comfort with Docker, CI/CD, automated tests. Coverage and quality gates are valued.
- **Agile collaboration** — Comfort in stand-ups, sprint planning, and cross-team work across geographies. "Fast-paced, multiple teams" means you ship with others.
- **Quality and craftsmanship** — Automated tests, resolving issues, "go the extra mile" for customer experience. No throw-over-the-wall code.
- **Initiative and curiosity** — "Takes initiative, perseveres, stays curious." They want self-directed learners who will grow into Gen AI/LLM work.
