# Blackstone LaunchPad — HealthTech Partner

## What They Do

This role is with a health-tech startup in the Blackstone LaunchPad Internship Program, administered through DivInc. LaunchPad places students from partner universities into paid internships at portfolio startups; DivInc is the nonprofit partner that connects first-generation and untapped talent with rapidly growing companies. The specific employer is a startup in HealthTech Engineering building software that integrates with electronic health record (EHR) systems and healthcare interoperability standards. Interns work on real product development—full-stack features, EHR integrations (Epic, Cerner, Meditech), and HL7/FHIR implementation—at 35 hours/week for 10 weeks (June–August), remote.

## Why This Company

I want to ship production-ready software in a domain where correctness and interoperability directly affect patient care. This role is a direct path to hands-on full-stack and integration work: building features that talk to Epic, Cerner, and Meditech, and implementing HL7 and FHIR so systems actually exchange data. My background is full-stack and backend—dual-database persistence, OAuth, CI/CD, and async pipelines—so I’m aligned with building scalable backend and frontend components and supporting EHR integrations from day one. I’m drawn to the constraint set of healthcare: strict standards, clear interfaces, and the chance to learn health-tech and data interoperability in a real product environment rather than in theory.

## Engineering Culture

Startups in the LaunchPad/DivInc portfolio tend to be early-stage and engineering-heavy; interns get real ownership and ship to production. The stack for this role centers on full-stack development plus healthcare interoperability: EHR integrations (Epic, Cerner, Meditech), HL7, FHIR, and scalable backend/frontend components. Culture is likely scrappy and standards-aware—health-tech demands both speed and compliance with interoperability and data-exchange requirements.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs

HealthTech Engineering is the stated focus: full-stack feature development, EHR integration support, and implementation of healthcare interoperability standards. The role sits at the intersection of product engineering and healthcare data—building the services and UIs that connect to major EHRs and speak HL7/FHIR.
