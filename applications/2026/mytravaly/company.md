# MyTravaly

MyTravaly is an India-based travel technology company building a hotel-led travel platform for independent hotels, homestays, resorts, and travelers. Current public coverage frames its Hotel Business Centre as a hospitality SaaS suite with a property management system, booking engine, payment/guest tools, and AI-assisted business health checks for small and mid-sized properties.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated
- **HQ / offices:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India; remote role

- **Product focus:** Hotel Business Centre, PMS, direct booking, marketplace, travel/hospitality SaaS, AI-assisted operations
- **Intern comp (2026 technology / AI / software):** Not stated in JD
