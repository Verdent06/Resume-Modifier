# American Heart Association

The American Heart Association is a national nonprofit focused on cardiovascular and brain health research, policy, and care delivery. This internship sits on the Data Science team and supports clinical and biomedical research through healthcare data integration, statistical modeling, AI/ML evaluation, and scientific communication. Technically, the role is less a generic software internship and more a healthcare data science / applied AI research support seat.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated
- **HQ / offices:** National Center in Dallas, TX; internship listed as remote in the United States
- **Valuation / signal:** Large national voluntary health organization; strong mission and research credibility, lower pure-SWE brand signal
- **Product focus:** Data science, data engineering, statistical modeling, and AI/ML workflows for clinical and population-health research
- **Intern comp (2026 data science):** $23.00/hr
- **Work model:** Remote, part-time 20-25 hrs/week, Fall 2026 term (2026-09-08 to 2026-12-11)
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Must be legally authorized to work in the United States without sponsorship; remote work must be performed inside the United States; JD asks for current MS or PhD pursuit

## Interview Process

| Stage | Format | Notes |
| ----- | ------ | ----- |
| Resume screen | Human + ATS | Likely filters for graduate-level data science, Python/R/SQL, healthcare or biomedical research, and reproducible analytical workflows |
| OA | Unknown / likely none | Nonprofit research internship posting does not name a coding assessment |
| Technical screen | Data science / research discussion | Expect questions on Python/R/SQL, data processing, model evaluation, documentation, and collaboration with clinical/research stakeholders |
| Behavioral | Mission and collaboration screen | Cross-functional research communication and nonprofit mission alignment matter |

**Estimated funnel:** 2-3 rounds · practical data/ML discussion · OA unknown · no system design · Bottleneck: MS/PhD and healthcare research fit · selective for undergrad applicants

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python, R, SQL; Git/version control
- **Domains:** Healthcare analytics, biomedical informatics, clinical research data, data engineering, ML/AI evaluation, LLM/agentic workflows, cloud/Snowflake/AWS
- **What wins:** Demonstrated end-to-end ML/data workflows, reproducible pipelines, Python/R/SQL evidence in bullets, scientific communication, and credible healthcare or research context. For an undergraduate, the binding concern is not raw engineering ability but whether the resume overcomes the JD's graduate/research-healthcare framing.
