## Role Summary

Applications Intern is a backend/software engineering internship in CWAN’s New York office. You’ll learn the team’s development tools and best practices with mentor guidance, collaborate with peers on software development, and independently complete simple coding tasks. You’re expected to incorporate feedback from team leads, engage in design discussions, and document, implement, and test one or more features with peer and mentor support. You’ll participate in code reviews and submit your own code for review. Success means mastering business-domain basics within about a month and detailed domain knowledge within three months while delivering quality, well-documented code.

## What They're Really Looking For

- **Java-first backend candidate** — Java is the only explicitly named language and is highlighted as preferred; they want someone who can be productive in a Java-based stack quickly.
- **Mentor-driven learner** — “Learns … quickly and accurately with mentor guidance” and “with peer and mentor assistance” signal they expect you to absorb feedback and ramp with support, not solo.
- **Team player over lone coder** — Collaboration, code reviews, and “constructively participates” matter as much as individual output.
- **Domain curiosity** — “Master’s the team’s business domain basics within 1 month and detailed knowledge within 3” means they want someone who will learn the investment-management context, not just write code in a vacuum.
- **Quality and process** — Documentation, testing, and high-quality features are called out; they care about sustainable practices.
- **Current undergrad in CS or related** — Four-year degree in Computer Science or related field; fits sophomore/junior intern profile.
