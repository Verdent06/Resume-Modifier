## Role Summary
The intern supports Software Engineering and Security teams at Smiths Detection by integrating vulnerability scanning (e.g. Nessus) with Jira for automated ticket creation and tracking, building automation scripts (Python) to turn scan results into reports and tickets, and helping stand up continuous vulnerability monitoring. Day-to-day work includes installing and configuring scanning tools, evaluating findings for severity and remediation, and optionally building lightweight GUI frontends or experimenting with open-source AI (Ollama) for summarising or categorising scan results. Success at 3 months looks like working integrations, documented workflows, and clearer handoff from scans to remediation.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Automation-first mindset** — They want someone who turns manual, repetitive security workflows into scripts and integrations (Nessus → Jira, reports, tickets).
- **Python + APIs** — Scripting and REST API integration (JSON/CSV/XML parsing) are core; comfort with tool APIs and data transformation matters more than web app UI.
- **Cybersecurity curiosity** — Understanding of vulnerability management (CVEs, severity, remediation) and willingness to learn; prior exposure is preferred but not mandatory.
- **DevOps-adjacent experience** — CI/CD, pipelines, or tool integration (e.g. GitHub Actions, GitLab) signal ability to own automation and monitoring.
- **Applied AI as a plus** — Experimentation with Ollama or similar for summarisation/categorisation is optional but aligns with their “applied AI” angle.
- **U.S. Person** — Role requires ITAR/EAR access; citizenship or permanent residency is a hard requirement.
