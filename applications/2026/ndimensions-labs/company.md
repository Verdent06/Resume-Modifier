# Ndimensions Labs

Ndimensions Labs is a Boston-based robotics AI startup from technologists associated with MIT, the University of Waterloo, and the University of Washington. The company is building infrastructure and learning systems for next-generation embodied AI agents, with hiring focused on multimodal model training, robotics navigation, hardware, and real-platform experimentation.
