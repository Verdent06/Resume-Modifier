# Clockwork Systems

## What They Do

Clockwork Systems builds software-driven fabrics for AI infrastructure. Founded by Stanford researchers, they deliver cross-stack observability, workload fault tolerance, and performance acceleration for GPU clusters — improving utilization and reliability where traditional infrastructure struggles. Their products include FleetIQ (GPU communication and utilization across clusters/clouds) and ClockSync (sub-microsecond telemetry and time synchronization). They raised $20.6M in 2025 and treat communication as the defining constraint for scaling AI at pace.
