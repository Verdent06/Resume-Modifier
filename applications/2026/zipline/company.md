# Zipline

Zipline designs, builds, and operates the world's largest autonomous drone delivery logistics network, moving critical supplies (blood, vaccines, medicine, food, retail) across four continents. The engineering surface spans consumer mobile/web apps, backend services, and the autonomy stack that flies millions of commercial autonomous miles — the drone is only part of a global operations system.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated (not in reference/companies.md)
- **HQ / offices:** South San Francisco, CA (this role onsite)
- **Valuation / signal:** Market leader in drone delivery; 140M+ commercial autonomous miles; delivery every ~30 seconds globally
- **Product focus:** Autonomous logistics platform — consumer apps, backend services, fleet operations
- **Intern comp (2026 SWE):** $54/hour; relocation/housing stipend possible; Sept–Dec 2026 full-time onsite
- **Work model:** Paid, full-time, in-person (South San Francisco); Fall 2026 cohort
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Must have completed second year of undergraduate studies by internship start; Masters/PhD eligible
