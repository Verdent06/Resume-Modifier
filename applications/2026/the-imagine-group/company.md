# The Imagine Group

The Imagine Group (operating as Imagine Staffing) is a Buffalo, NY-based talent and workforce solutions firm founded in 2009 — a women-owned, WBE-certified staffing and RPO company spanning four brands (Imagine Staffing, CryptoPeople, Imagine-360, Mary Kraft HR), ~35 internal employees, ~$14.7M revenue, and 1,000+ contingent workers across 30+ states. This role is a staffing placement: Imagine is recruiting an AI Solutions Intern on behalf of a client — a Western New York compressed-air equipment manufacturer — to embed with that client's Sales and Proposal/Tendering team. The work is applied generative-AI enablement (Copilot/ChatGPT prompt and workflow design, use-case research, pilot testing, adoption training) aimed at proposal quality, productivity, and time-savings, not core product engineering.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated (staffing-agency placement; client is a mid-market industrial manufacturer)
- **HQ / offices:** Buffalo, NY (892 Main Street) HQ; offices in Atlanta, Fort Mill SC, Hunt Valley MD. This role is onsite in the Buffalo–Niagara Falls area at the client site.
- **Valuation / signal:** Private, ~$14.7M revenue; "Top 2% largest U.S. staffing firms"; Official Talent & Workforce Partner of the Buffalo Bills and Sabres.
- **Product focus:** Workforce/talent solutions (staffing, RPO, MSP). Client-facing deliverable here: GenAI workflows and prompt libraries for a manufacturer's sales/tendering team.
- **Intern comp (2026 AI Solutions):** $25/hr, 10–20 hrs/week, contract through Fall 2026.
- **Work model:** Paid contract, part-time, onsite Buffalo NY, flexible around school.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Must be currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's (CS, Data Science, Information Systems, Business Analytics, Engineering, AI, or related). No sponsorship/clearance gate stated.
