# AI Full Stack Engineer Intern at Gesture

## Role Summary

Gesture hires an onsite Denver AI Full Stack Engineer Intern to build and ship features front-to-back across its consumer app and Reach360 platform: work the API layer, databases, and front-end frameworks for end-to-end functionality, design and prototype agentic workflows with LangChain/LangGraph, debug across the stack from UI to database, and collaborate with product and design through code reviews and sprint planning. JD requirements test full-stack/backend app building (React front end; Node.js/Python back end; REST APIs; databases; Git; Fern stack — Firebase, Express, React, Node.js — and GCP strongly preferred) plus hands-on agentic-AI framework experience. Company identity is an AI-driven connection company shipping agentic AI across a live consumer app and a B2B engagement engine — not generic CRUD behind an "AI" label.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack
- **differentiator:** agentic-ai (LangChain/LangGraph agentic workflows, LLM pipelines, production hardening)
- **track_divergence:** true

The resume spine must pass a full-stack SWE screen: genuinely shipped applications, backend APIs (REST), a real database, a client/front-end surface (React), cloud/containerized deployment, and the JD's languages demonstrated in bullets rather than listed alone. Because Gesture's engineering identity — and one of its explicit requirements — is agentic AI, the page also needs prominent, deep agentic-workflow signal (LangChain/LangGraph orchestration, eval/consensus gating, production debugging of an AI pipeline) in the top half, without letting notebook/research-only ML or a thin keyword list displace the shipping spine.

## Team & Bar

The reader is an early-stage startup engineer-founder or hiring engineer screening for builders who move fast through unfamiliar problems, own scoped work end-to-end, and communicate clearly. Funnel is likely a human resume screen → practical technical conversation or take-home → onsite founder loop (`recruiting.md` §5 startups: a human reads the PDF early; `recruiting.md` §11 full-stack: the signal that matters is one or more genuinely engineered, shippable products you can narrate decision-by-decision). Bar: evidence of independently built software with real users, polyglot full-stack depth in at least one layer, and credible agentic-AI/LLM pipeline work with architectural decisions and metrics — not buzzwords, not club ops without engineering substance. The low stipend and small team reward self-starters who ship.

## Screen Criteria

- Full-stack evidence through bullets: backend services (Node/Express or FastAPI/Flask), REST APIs, a client/dashboard surface (React), a real database, and cloud/Docker/CI deployment — not Skills-line keyword stuffing.
- JD languages should appear in project/experience context, not only in Skills: Python and TypeScript/JavaScript demonstrated in real work; React front-end depth; an async API layer is a floor signal.
- Agentic-AI signal is core to company fit: LangChain/LangGraph orchestration, multi-node agent pipelines, eval/consensus/hardening, and debugging a live AI system — preferred in the JD and central to Gesture's identity.
- Strong signals: self-built products with paying/real users, sole-engineer or founder ownership, measurable impact metrics (latency, cost, throughput, precision), initiative without being asked.
- Anti-patterns: generic AI buzzwords with no architectural decision; single-bullet thin entries; club-growth framing without ship depth; Firebase/GCP/Express listed in Skills with zero bullet support (JD-preferred keywords, but weak if wholly unsupported).
- When the track diverges, the first read should register "ships full-stack product software end-to-end" while a parallel read registers agentic-AI / production-LLM depth near the top of the page.
