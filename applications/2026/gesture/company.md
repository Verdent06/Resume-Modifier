# Gesture

Gesture is an AI-driven "connection" company (founded 2017) that pairs a consumer gifting app with a B2B engagement platform, Reach360, to turn digital touchpoints into real-world, tangible experiences ("no address needed" gifting). Reach360 unifies and enriches first-party data for hospitality and higher-ed brands, then triggers personalized physical deliveries and measures each touchpoint from send to ROI. The stack blends AI/ML, agentic automation, and intelligent logistics across a mobile consumer app and the Reach360 web platform. Small, fast-moving team (~20 people, ~$4.1M raised over 5 rounds); HQ in New York with a growing Denver office where this onsite internship sits.
