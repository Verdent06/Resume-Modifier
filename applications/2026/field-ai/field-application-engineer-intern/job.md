## Role Summary
Field Application Engineer interns support customers and partners as they integrate Field AI's hardware-agnostic AI platform and Field Foundation Models (FFMs) into their robots and workflows. Day-to-day work involves technical demos, integration support, troubleshooting deployment and API issues, and traveling to customer sites. Success at three months means reliably supporting at least one vertical (e.g., construction or industrial) with demos and basic integration guidance, and feeding back clear use cases and pain points to product and engineering. The role is part-time (20+ hrs/week), Detroit-based, with frequent nationwide travel.

## What They're Really Looking For
- Someone who can explain technical concepts clearly to non-experts (demos, training).
- Comfort with travel and onsite work—this is a field role, not remote.
- Practical software skills: APIs, integration, maybe Python or similar for scripts/demos.
- Interest in robotics and AI so you can speak credibly about perception, planning, control.
- Local or Detroit-area preference—they want availability and willingness to be onsite.
- Part-time flexibility that still allows 20+ hours and travel without conflicting with school.
