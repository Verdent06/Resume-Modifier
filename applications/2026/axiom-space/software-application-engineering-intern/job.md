## Role Summary

Intern partners with Mission Integration & Operations (MI&O) and cross-functional teams to gather and understand software requirements, then design, develop, test, and maintain solutions that interact with relational databases and connect to external programs via APIs. Day-to-day work includes writing and optimizing database queries, participating in the full SDLC from concept through deployment and maintenance, following SDL methodologies for quality and timelines, and supporting/troubleshooting applications. Success at 3 months looks like delivering shippable features on schedule and providing reliable support for existing applications in a mission-critical, enterprise environment.

## What They're Really Looking For

- **Relational DB fluency** — Not just "used a database"; they want query optimization, efficient retrieval/storage, and comfort with Oracle/SQL Server/MySQL/PostgreSQL.
- **API integration** — Software that "connects to external programs through APIs"; REST experience and integration patterns matter.
- **Enterprise delivery** — Experience delivering software in an enterprise setting; SDLC discipline and timeline awareness.
- **Collaboration** — Partnering with multiple teams (MI&O, cross-functional); strong communication and teamwork.
- **Problem-solving and ownership** — Proactive, resourceful; can troubleshoot bugs, issues, and enhancements.
- **Agile familiarity** — Preferred; signals they work in sprints and iterative delivery.
