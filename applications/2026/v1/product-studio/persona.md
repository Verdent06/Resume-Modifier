# Product Studio at V1 (V1 Michigan)

## Role Summary

Product Studio is an 8-week UMich student-org cohort (~10–20 people) for builders who take a product from ideation to shipped software to customer discovery. There is no assigned intern stack and no idea requirement. The FAQ surface prefers technical strength, with exceptions for entrepreneurial track record. V1’s identity is a high-autonomy builder community (guidance on execution, not idea-picking; demo day for teams that show progress). This is not a corporate ATS internship.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack
- **differentiator:** founder-agency / shipped-product execution (customer discovery, iteration, ownership)
- **track_divergence:** true

The literal screen is “can this person build a product end-to-end.” The memorable fit signal is agency: already shipping, not waiting for a program to start. The resume leads with full-stack shipping and keeps founder/product-ownership evidence prominent — it does not collapse into a campus-club ops packet or a notebook-ML packet.

## Team & Bar

Reader is V1 Core / Product Studio mentors, ~30 seconds on a PDF plus a Tally form. Funnel is human resume screen (`recruiting.md` §5 human-read PDF), no OA. Bar: evidence of autonomous technical execution and product ownership — paying users, shipped APIs, or a live product beat club titles. Cohort is small; they filter for people who will put in ≥10 hours/week without being managed.

## Screen Criteria

- Pass signals: a live product with users or revenue; end-to-end technical delivery (API + data + deploy); founder or sole-engineer ownership; iteration from a real defect or user/stakeholder constraint.
- Technical proof in bullets, not Skills alone. Languages and frameworks appear inside architectural decisions.
- Anti-patterns: club-ops without shipping; thin single-bullet filler; keyword stuffing; tools the candidate does not have; notebook ML with no product; presenting a campus consulting club as if it were a full-time employer.
- Metric-free leadership lines are weaker than shipped-product metrics on this screen — agency is proven by products, not titles.
