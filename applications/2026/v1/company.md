# V1 (V1 Michigan)

V1 is a University of Michigan student builder community. Product Studio is its semester cohort for student founders executing from ideation through product building and customer discovery — not a corporate internship. Mentors coach execution (technical resources, user feedback, iteration) rather than picking the idea. The program is high-autonomy: ~10 hours/week, one all-hands plus mentor check-ins, ~8 weeks, cohort of 10–20, with demo day for teams that show consistent progress.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated (student org; not in `reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, MI
- **Valuation / signal:** Campus org; alumni network includes YC founders and early-stage engineers; demo day has produced student funding
- **Product focus:** Student-founded products shipped in-cohort; no assigned company stack
- **Intern comp (2026 Product Studio):** Unpaid student-org program (FAQ does not list pay)
- **Work model:** ~8 weeks, one semester; ≥10 hours/week; Ann Arbor / campus; high autonomy
- **Clearance / eligibility:** No graduation-year cap; mostly first-years and sophomores historically, some juniors; no idea required

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** FAQ names none. Prefer strong technical builders; exceptions for entrepreneurial track record.
- **Domains:** Product execution, customer discovery, iteration on user feedback, shipping a product in ~8 weeks
- **What wins:** Evidence the applicant already builds (shipped product, founder ownership, technical delivery) — FAQ: “people who will build regardless.” Club membership without shipping is a miss.
