# Cloudflare

Cloudflare operates one of the world's largest global edge networks, sitting in front of a large share of internet traffic to deliver CDN, DNS (the 1.1.1.1 resolver), DDoS mitigation, and Zero Trust security. Its developer platform — Cloudflare Workers and the surrounding Workers ecosystem (KV, Durable Objects, R2, D1) — pushes serverless compute to hundreds of edge locations for single-digit-millisecond latency close to users. The engineering identity is low-latency systems at the edge: performance-critical proxy, networking, and security software where Go and Rust are first-class and C++/systems fluency is valued. Publicly traded (NYSE: NET); this internship is an Austin, TX hybrid role.
