# Associated Students of Michigan State University (ASMSU)

## What They Do
ASMSU is MSU’s undergraduate student government, funded by a per-credit student tax and organized around elected leadership and a General Assembly. It runs programs and services students interact with directly—including advocacy, Safe Ride, legal services, and short-term student loans—while also administering budgets, contracts, and day-to-day business operations for the student association.

## Why This Company
I spend most of my time on campus building systems that have to work for real students under real deadlines—check-in, membership records, and reporting for a large student org. ASMSU sits at the same intersection: high-trust student services, careful record-keeping, and front-desk professionalism when students walk in with questions. I want that work to be part of how I contribute on campus, not only through engineering clubs, but by helping the Engagement Office run smoothly during a busy summer session.

## Engineering Culture
ASMSU is not a software company; its “stack” is fiscal discipline, policy process, and student-facing operations. Recent public reporting around the organization emphasizes rebuilding administrative systems, updating governance documents, and expanding engagement infrastructure—exactly the kind of environment where reliable filing, loan documentation, room scheduling, and courteous service matter more than shipping code.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs
The **Engagement Office** supports ASMSU’s business operations alongside the Business Office Manager and Controller: records for the Engagement Office and General Assembly, student loan program administration, room reservations, mail, inventory, and day-to-day student questions. The role is the operational front line between ASMSU’s internal processes and the MSU community.
