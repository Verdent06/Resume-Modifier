# Neo4j

## What They Do

Neo4j is the graph intelligence platform that turns connected data into knowledge for intelligent applications and AI systems. They provide enterprise knowledge graphs for explainable AI, graph database and analytics (Aura, AuraDB, Infinigraph), and a large ecosystem—84 of the Fortune 100 use Neo4j. The platform powers use cases from fraud detection and recommendation engines to agentic AI and GraphRAG. HQ in San Mateo; 1,001–5,000 employees; $200M+ ARR; $325M Series F (2024).

I’ve already built on Neo4j and Cypher at MindMosaic (graph persistence, query optimization, p95 latency cuts), and I care about how APIs and services are designed, tested, and documented. ## Engineering Culture

Neo4j runs a Silicon Valley company with a Swedish soul: collaboration, empowerment, and putting an innovative stamp on projects. Security is first-class: ISO 27001, SOC 2/3, HIPAA, GDPR; Trust Center, responsible disclosure, and application security content (e.g. ASVS). Engineering spans graph algorithms, cloud (Aura), distributed systems (Infinigraph), and agentic AI (GraphRAG). The Application Security team works with platform and product teams on how APIs and MCP servers are built and exposed.
