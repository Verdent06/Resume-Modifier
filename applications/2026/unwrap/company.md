# Unwrap

## What They Do

Unwrap is an AI-powered customer intelligence platform based in Santa Barbara that helps product and customer experience teams surface actionable insights from customer feedback automatically. The platform integrates feedback from thousands of sources (app store reviews, support tickets, surveys, social media) and uses natural language processing to categorize and analyze it without manual tagging. They serve companies like Lyft, Stripe, Oura, Microsoft, Perplexity, and GitHub. Founded by ex-Amazon Alexa PMs, the company raised $12M Series A (Scale VP) in early 2025 and has roughly 25 employees with strong YoY growth.

## Why This Company

I want to work at Unwrap because they sit at the intersection of full-stack product engineering and applied AI—building the tools that let teams understand and act on customer feedback at scale. Their stack (TypeScript, GraphQL, Node, React) is exactly where I’ve been building: MindMosaic’s graph-backed UI and Mira’s real-time client are both end-to-end ownership of user-facing features. I’m drawn to the “builder” culture and the chance to own customer-facing components from requirements through ship, and to work in a small team where my code directly affects how Lyft or Stripe-style customers use the product.
