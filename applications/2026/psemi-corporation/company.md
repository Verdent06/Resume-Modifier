p# pSemi Corporation

pSemi is a Murata company building high-performance semiconductors on Peregrine Semiconductor's RF integration legacy. Its portfolio spans power management, connected sensors, optical transceivers, antenna tuning, and RF frontends for smartphones, base stations, PCs, EVs, data centers, IoT, and healthcare. The San Diego engineering org is applying GenAI — LLMs, agent frameworks, and RAG — to automate RFIC design workflows from simulation setup through verification and deployment.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated (Murata subsidiary; semiconductor/RF niche, not in tier list)
- **HQ / offices:** San Diego, CA (HQ); Murata global offices
- **Valuation / signal:** Murata Manufacturing parent (~$30B+ market cap); Peregrine Semiconductor acquisition heritage
- **Product focus:** RF semiconductors and intelligent analog/mixed-signal ICs for mobile, infrastructure, and automotive
- **Intern comp (2026 AI/ML):** $32.20–$58.32/hr (JD); paid via third-party staffing agency
- **Work model:** Summer 2026 internship; onsite San Diego; standard intern term
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Must be completing 3+ years of Bachelor's/Master's in CS, SWE, Data Science, or related; current student; employed via staffing agency
