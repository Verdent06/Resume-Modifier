# Xaira Therapeutics

Xaira is an AI-first drug-discovery biotech that pairs generative AI with large-scale in-house data generation to design protein and antibody therapeutics against historically undruggable targets, and to build foundation models for biology and disease. It launched out of stealth in April 2024 with ~$1B in committed capital — one of the largest initial raises in biotech history — incubated by ARCH Venture Partners and Foresite Labs, led by CEO Marc Tessier-Lavigne (ex-Genentech CSO, ex-Stanford president) and co-founded with protein-design pioneer David Baker (RFdiffusion / RFantibody lineage). This role sits in a newer "Physical AI" effort: a next-generation Vision AI platform that lets autonomous robotic lab systems perceive, reason about, and act on unseen instruments and consumables — combining computer vision, multimodal/LLM reasoning, and workflow orchestration to scale lab autonomy rather than training a bespoke model per instrument.
