# Recruiter Persona — Lab Automation · Vision AI Engineer Intern @ Xaira Therapeutics

The shared recruiter lens for the writer and grader. Describes who is screening, what the role tests, and what bar signals look like in the abstract. It names no candidate entries and prescribes no include/omit table.

## Role Summary

The intern joins Xaira's "Physical AI" effort building a next-generation Vision AI platform for autonomous laboratory robots: systems that perceive, reason about, and act on previously unseen lab instruments and consumables without per-object model training. The work spans modern computer vision, vision-language / multimodal models, and an AI reasoning layer that orchestrates perception → reasoning → planning → task execution (a state-machine architecture), deployed on real robotic platforms in a cutting-edge biotech lab.

- **JD surface (what the requirements literally test):** strong SWE in Python/C++ (C# acceptable), deep-learning fluency in PyTorch/TensorFlow, and demonstrated computer-vision / multimodal-AI project work — object detection, segmentation, tracking, vision-language models, scene understanding — plus experience evaluating, training, or fine-tuning models.
- **Company identity (what makes a candidate memorable here):** embodied / "Physical AI" for science — generalized perception and spatial reasoning on real robots, agentic LLM orchestration with a real action boundary, sim-based synthetic data, and edge-GPU deployment. The mission is autonomy in the physical world, not notebook ML.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** `ai-ml` (computer-vision flavored) — the requirements gate on DL frameworks, CV/multimodal project depth, and model eval/train/fine-tune.
- **differentiator:** embodied-AI / robotics-perception — the autonomous-lab, real-robot, spatial-reasoning, LLM-to-action identity that defines this team.
- **track_divergence:** **true.** The literal screen is an ai-ml/CV bar, but the company's distinguishing signal is robotics/embodied perception + agentic orchestration. The page must lead with a CV/ML spine **and** keep the robotics/embodied-AI and LLM-agent evidence prominent and deep — not collapse to one pole. A pure full-stack or pure notebook-ML reading both miss.

## Team & Bar

- **Funnel:** resume-first startup (Greenhouse, human/HM read early — no standard OA); 3–4 rounds of practical CV/ML + project deep-dive + team fit; tiny cohort; very low acceptance.
- **Bar:** frontier AI/biotech with elite founders and funding — expects demonstrated, defensible perception/multimodal work, real embodied-AI or robotics build experience (sim + hardware is a maturity signal), and the discipline to benchmark/evaluate models, not just call `model.fit()`.
- **Recruiter voice:** an AI/CV/robotics/automation engineer screening for someone who can ship perception into a real robotic loop and reason about trade-offs (accuracy vs latency vs deployment cost, per-step vs whole-plan, sim-to-real gap). Embodied-AI curiosity and a live GitHub/portfolio register strongly.
- **Evidence that wins:** shipped/deployed systems with a real-world action boundary; computer-vision demonstrated through use with a defensible pipeline; LLM-agent orchestration (state machines, tool-calling, approval/safety seams); model training/eval with honest metrics; C++/Python polyglot depth shown in bullets.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**
- JD languages (Python, C++) demonstrated *in bullets*, not in Skills alone.
- Computer vision / multimodal perception shown end-to-end and defensibly (real-time inference, detection/tracking, on-device or edge constraints), not as a buzzword.
- Real embodied-AI or robotics-perception evidence: sim + hardware, ROS2/perception pipelines, sensor fusion, spatial reasoning — the differentiator must be visibly present near the top.
- Agentic / LLM-to-action orchestration with a real action or safety boundary (state machine, tool-calling, approval gate) — directly mirrors the role's reasoning layer.
- Model training/fine-tuning/evaluation with honest impact metrics and trade-off reasoning.

**Anti-patterns (no-pile):**
- Notebook ML with no delivery, or `model.fit()` with no end-to-end workflow.
- CV/robotics claimed only in a skills list with no backing bullet ("familiar with OpenCV/ROS").
- A pure full-stack/web spine that buries the perception and embodied-AI signal at the bottom.
- Club-ops or generic CRUD leading the page over robotics/CV/ML evidence.
- Unsupportable claims that collapse under "walk me through this."
