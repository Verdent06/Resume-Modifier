## Role Summary
Insight Day is a one-day, in-person recruiting event in London where first- or second-year undergraduates explore Visa's technology org through deep-dive labs, mini-projects, Q&A with leaders, and a CV/application masterclass. Success means demonstrating technical curiosity and backend-relevant skills (AWS, Python, Java, SQL, REST) in collaborative exercises, then using the day to position for a full-time 11-week internship starting June/July 2027. The event doubles as prep for internship applications and as a signal to recruiters that you're serious about payments and technology.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Eligibility first:** First-year on a 3-year degree or second-year on a 4-year degree (Graduate 2028); they're filtering for pipeline into 2027 internships.
- **Backend alignment:** The role is tagged Software Engineering + Backend; they want to see Java, Python, AWS, SQL, REST APIs, and Linux/Unix so the resume survives keyword screens and fits their stack.
- **Curiosity and fit:** "Highly motivated and curious" and "career in payments and technology" mean they want candidates who can articulate why Visa and why payments—not generic "interested in tech."
- **Collaborative and presentable:** Deep-dive labs and networking mean they're assessing how you work in groups and how you talk about your experience; the CV masterclass implies they care how you frame your projects and experience on paper.
- **Pathway signal:** Participation positions you for internship applications; the resume should look like someone they'd want in a backend or platform internship, not just someone attending an event.
