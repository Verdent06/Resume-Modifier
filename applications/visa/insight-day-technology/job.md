## Role Summary
Insight Day is a one-day event for first- or second-year undergraduates (graduating 2028) to explore technology and payments at Visa. Candidates choose Deep-Dive Labs by function, work through scenarios and mini-projects, do collaborative exercises, and attend Q&A and networking with leaders and alumni. The day includes a CV/application masterclass to strengthen internship applications. Success means standing out in labs and networking so that recruiters remember you for 2027 internship roles. The underlying bar is backend/software engineering: AWS, Python, Java, SQL, REST APIs, Git, Linux/Unix.

## What They're Really Looking For
- Evidence you can code in the stack they use daily: Java, Python, SQL, REST, AWS, Git, Linux.
- Someone who can own a small scenario or mini-project in a lab—build something that works, not just describe it.
- Curiosity about payments and scale (transactions/sec, reliability, security) even if you haven’t worked in fintech.
- Strong fundamentals (data structures, APIs, databases) so you can contribute to real backend work in a future internship.
- Fit for a global, in-person event: communication, collaboration, and genuine interest in Visa’s technology and products.
