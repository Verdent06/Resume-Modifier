# Visa

## What They Do
Visa operates the global digital payment network that connects consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and governments. The company processes 76,000+ transactions per second and $16+ trillion in payment volume annually across 4.6 billion credentials and 150+ million merchant locations. Revenue comes from transaction fees, data processing, and value-added services; the business model is built on scale, reliability (99.9999% uptime), and security (120+ cyber controls, AI-driven fraud prevention).

## Engineering Culture
Visa emphasizes scale, reliability, speed, and security. They run proprietary data centers (500PB+, 99.9999% uptime) and process transactions in milliseconds. Stack includes virtual GPUs, Ray for ML workflows, and heavy use of AI for fraud (e.g., $40B+ prevented annually). Recent work includes MCP Server and Acceptance Agent Toolkit for developer-facing AI/API integration. Engineering blogs and corporate perspectives focus on security, trust, and “engine of global commerce.”
