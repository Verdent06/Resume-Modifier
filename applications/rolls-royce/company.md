# Rolls-Royce Motor Cars

## What They Do

Rolls-Royce Motor Cars is a British luxury automobile manufacturer and a wholly-owned subsidiary of the BMW Group. It designs, manufactures, and distributes highly personalized, handcrafted luxury vehicles and parts worldwide through an exclusive global dealership network. The company is expanding into electrification; the BMW Group's Spartanburg, SC operations (where this role is based) produce BMW X series and are building out high-voltage battery assembly and EV production, with heavy use of digital twin and simulation (e.g., FactoryVerse/Omniverse, Unreal Engine) for plant planning and training.

## Why This Company

I want to work on real-time 3D and simulation tooling that directly affects how a factory runs—Unreal Engine 5 for the battery plant project, VR/AR for training or layout validation, and 3D data pipelines that connect digital twins to live systems. Rolls-Royce/BMW Group’s use of Unreal and Omniverse for manufacturing digitalization is exactly the kind of applied software work I’m interested in: performance-sensitive C++, clear product impact, and cross-functional teams. I’m drawn to the mix of gameplay-style mechanics (for training/simulation), database and dashboard support, and Agile delivery in a manufacturing context rather than pure game dev.

## Engineering Culture

The innovations team works in Unreal Engine 5, with exposure to Nvidia Omniverse/FactoryVerse for digital twin and logistics planning. Stack includes C/C++, version control (e.g., Perforce), VR/AR tooling, and integration with plant systems (e.g., PlantCon, IPS). Emphasis on Agile, documentation, coding standards, and 5S/safety. Technical work centers on 3D data pipelines, gameplay-style mechanics for simulation, database development, and department dashboards—blending game-engine skills with industrial software.
