# Trillium

Trillium (Trillium Labs) is a New York-based proprietary trading firm active in US Equities, US Options, Canadian Equities, and OTC Equities, with additional trading floors in Chicago and Miami. Its trading technology is built, tested, and optimized in-house: engineers and technologists collaborate directly with traders to ship the tools and functionality the desks need. Trillium Labs — the engineering arm behind this internship — builds high-performance solutions for complex financial trading and data-analysis applications, so the work sits at the intersection of low-latency systems, quantitative/market data, and production software the firm runs on its own capital.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated in `reference/companies.md`; sits in the proprietary-trading / market-making peer set (funnel family near DV Trading, CTC, IMC — OA-gated, C++/Python heavy, low-single-digit acceptance, brutal-early cycle).
- **HQ / offices:** New York City (HQ, this role); trading floors in Chicago and Miami.
- **Valuation / signal:** Established proprietary trading firm; in-house trading technology across multiple US/Canadian equity and options markets. Prestige is finance/prop-trading eng, not consumer-tech brand.
- **Product focus:** In-house trading systems and data-analysis applications for equities/options market-making and proprietary trading.
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** $25/hour, 12–13 week internship (Summer 2027) — below top-tier quant headline rates.
- **Work model:** In-person, New York, NY. No remote.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Prefers a recently completed CS / CIS / Mathematics (or related) degree; will consider candidates currently pursuing one. No stated citizenship gate.
