# Software Engineer Intern (Summer 2027) at C3 AI

## Role Summary

Summer 2027 Software Engineer Intern at C3 AI (NYSE: AI), Redwood City HQ, onsite 5 days/week, $8–10k/mo. Interns embed into **Platform or Forward Deployed Engineering** and ship on the C3 AI Suite from day one. JD surface is generic product SWE: design/maintain performant scalable applications, internal tools for large-scale data analysis, product+engineering feedback, end-to-end application development; qualifications are BS/MS CS-or-related, JavaScript/Java/**or other OOP**, DS&A, profiling & optimization. Company identity is an **enterprise AI application platform** (C3 Agentic AI Platform, industry SaaS, C3 Generative AI) — product/platform engineering at an enterprise-AI company, **not** an ML-research lab and **not** an FDE-only consulting seat. Form role to optimize for: Software Engineer, Platform (foundational components/frameworks/tools for distributed applications; CS fundamentals; Java *or similar*). Placement may still be FDE; the page stays Platform-primary product SWE.

## Screen Criteria

- JD OOP languages demonstrated in bullets, not Skills alone — JavaScript/TypeScript family, Python, and/or C++ through use. Java is **not** a knockout; "or other OOP" / "Java or similar" is the floor.
- Full-stack shipping: APIs or services, data stores, a deploy path, and some client or tool UI (`resume.md` §12 keywords-through-use).
- Performant, scalable applications with profiling/optimization evidence a recruiter can size (latency, cost, failure rate, throughput) — JD names profiling & optimization as a qualification.
- End-to-end application ownership (define → build → maintain), not a single-layer tutorial.
- Differentiator visible in the top half: production systems and data tools/pipelines that would transfer to an enterprise AI *application platform* — not research notebooks and not "I used Copilot."
- Cross-functional communication implied by stakeholder or product+engineering delivery, without turning the page into consulting-only FDE marketing.
- Class-year on page: `Expected May 2028` matches the Greenhouse window for Summer 2027.

## Anti-Patterns

- Inventing Java, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, or C3 Suite / C3 Agentic AI Platform experience — **emergency**.
- ML-research packaging: LoRA/notebook training as the page identity when this is product SWE intern (Platform-primary).
- FDE-only consulting narrative with no platform, API, or systems engineering the Platform form tests.
- Club-ops or GTM/deal-flow work with no engineering the role tests.
- Skills-only polyglot stuffing (especially listing Java without inventory or bullets).
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a production + data-tools + optimization spine.
- Metric-free architecture claims the candidate cannot defend after a HackerRank OA in a coding/project deep-dive.
