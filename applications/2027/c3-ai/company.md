# C3 AI

C3 AI (NYSE: AI) sells enterprise AI application software: the C3 Agentic AI Platform for developing, deploying, and operating enterprise AI applications, industry-specific C3 AI SaaS apps, and C3 Generative AI for domain-specific enterprise genAI. Interns embed into Platform or Forward Deployed Engineering and ship on the C3 AI Suite from day one — this req is **product SWE intern work**, not an ML-research rotation. `reference/companies.md` B-TIER Notes flag the public enterprise-AI identity, HQ 5-days/week onsite in Redwood City, and intern comp of $8–10k/mo.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD floor is JavaScript, Java, **or other OOP**. Platform form text says "Java or similar." Java on the page is **not** a knockout; inventing Java (or Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, C3 Suite) is an emergency. TypeScript / JavaScript-family, Python, and C++ satisfy the OOP clause.
- **Domains:** Product/platform engineering on an enterprise AI application suite — foundational components, frameworks, and tools for distributed applications (Platform) or full-stack AI-driven web apps (FDE). Interns may land on either team; the screen is still product SWE, not ML training and not FDE-only consulting.
- **What wins:** A shippable full-stack spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) with OOP languages through use, DS&A/profiling/optimization visible as engineered systems, end-to-end apps, and data tools/pipelines for large-scale analysis. Differentiator is enterprise-AI *platform product engineering*, not notebook ML. After the PDF, HackerRank OA is the binding filter (`companies.md`).
