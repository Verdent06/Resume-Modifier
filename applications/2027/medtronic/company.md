# Medtronic

Medtronic is the world's largest medical-device company: cardiac, diabetes, surgical, and neuromodulation products that reach tens of millions of patients a year. Engineering identity is regulated healthcare technology — software that has to be correct next to devices, cloud, quality systems, and firmware — not a consumer web product. Intern SWE work is routed from a catch-all early-career posting into software development, AI/data science, cloud, quality, DevOps, systems, test, or firmware teams, not a single greenfield app team.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-tier (`reference/companies.md` — Fortune-class medical-device peer to Intel/WD/Dell for intern brand; solid program, not FAANG SWE prestige)
- **HQ / offices:** Fridley / greater Minneapolis, MN (this req). Other intern hubs include Tempe AZ, Eatontown NJ, Santa Rosa CA, Lafayette/Boulder CO, Plymouth/Mounds View/Brooklyn Center/Brooklyn Park/Minneapolis MN, Irvine/Santa Ana CA, North Haven CT, Memphis TN, Newton/Mansfield/Boston MA, Dexter MI, Fort Worth TX, Jacksonville FL.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: MDT); ~95,000 employees; ~$32B revenue class; ~400 interns/year; Vault/Handshake/Forbes early-talent awards (Extern 2026 guide, directional)
- **Product focus:** Implantable and connected medical devices plus the software, cloud, quality, and firmware stack around them
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** $26.50–$45.25/hr (posted national intern range); housing assistance and relocation for eligible interns
- **Work model:** Paid 10–11 weeks, 40 hours/week; Summer 2027 cohort June 1–August 13 (semester) or June 14–August 20 (quarter); onsite minimum 4 days/week; location preference considered, flexibility increases placement odds
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Bachelor's or master's in CE / SWE / CS or related; graduation Winter 2027, Spring 2028, Winter 2028, or Spring 2029. Must be authorized to work in the U.S. without sponsorship — E, F-1 (CPT/OPT), H-1, H-2, L, B, J, and TN are ineligible. GPA 3.0 preferred. Anticipated close 16 Oct 2026; rolling.

| Stage | Format | Notes |

**Estimated funnel:** 2–3 rounds · Easy · Aon SJT (not coding OA) · No intern system design · Bottleneck: resume + Aon · ~10–15%

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** C, C++, C#/.NET, SQL, JavaScript, Java, Python, Swift, HTML/CSS (JD: one or more via coursework, internships, research, or personal projects). Visual Studio, Jenkins, GitLab listed as tools.
- **Domains:** Application/software development; cloud architecture; software quality (requirements, V&V); DevOps/SDLC; systems software and tools; test engineering; AI/data science algorithms; firmware as a *placement option*, not the screen bar for this catch-all.
- **What wins:** Shipped software in a listed language (C++/Python/SQL/JS-family), plus evidence of design/test/debug/implement — APIs, data pipelines, cloud, CI — without inventing medical-device internships, Java, C#, or firmware. Recruiter routes the catch-all toward software/cloud/quality/test/AI for a full-stack + C++ candidate; firmware only if the page shows real embedded work. Resume is the gate; Aon is the campus behavioral filter (`recruiting.md`: intern eligibility knockouts first, then screen).
