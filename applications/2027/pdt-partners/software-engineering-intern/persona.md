# Summer 2027 Software Engineering Intern at PDT Partners

## Role Summary

SWE intern in New York on an individual project with a senior developer, contributing to the technology that enables PDT's trading life cycle: large handling of financial data, real-time events, and batch processing. Depending on the project, work may include infrastructure-management tooling and large-scale dataset maintenance. The JD surface is generalist, high-bar SWE — strong programming, data structures and algorithms, program correctness (resource management, exception safety, reentrancy/thread safety), maintainability (unit testing, coding standards), and efficiency (optimal data structures/algorithms, parallelization). Company identity is a systematic quantitative investment manager (30+ year track record, Morgan Stanley spin-out): engineers ship the systems under the desk, not a customer-facing product. PDT does not run a quantitative-research internship; this is an engineering role that may *learn* markets on the job. C++ and Python are the load-bearing languages for trading-lifecycle SWE here (FT infra listings also name Java). Prior trading-desk experience is not required.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (general SWE — the JD requirements literally test programming, DS&A, design/debug/test, correctness, and efficiency)
- **differentiator:** quant trading-lifecycle systems — real-time events, financial-data/batch processing, C++/Python systems depth (concurrency, resource management, efficiency)
- **track_divergence:** false — the differentiator is flavor on a general-SWE spine, not a second track. The screen still reads as general SWE (`reference/recruiting.md` III §11, `reference/resume.md` III §12); the systems/data signal is what makes the candidate memorable, not a re-target to ai-ml or quant-research.

The spine is general SWE: ship real, defensible software; strong DS&A for a coding screen; breadth across the stack with depth in at least one layer. The differentiator is carried by whatever on-page work demonstrates real-time/concurrency/memory discipline and Python data/batch/infra — kept prominent and deep, but the resume does not become a research or trading-desk page.

## Team & Bar

PDT Partners is S-tier systematic quant (`reference/companies.md`) — selective SWE intern funnel (<1–2%; remote coding assessment, platform unpublished; C++/Python/Java per FT loop reports). The screener is an engineer/recruiter at a firm where programming excellence and correctness under load are floors. Funnel: Greenhouse application (GPA, why quant finance, GitHub) → rolling resume screen (~3-week feedback) → technical assessment → technical/behavioral loop. Comp is $180,000 annualized. What wins the human screen: C++ and Python proven in real, defensible work (not Skills-only); systems/performance depth that reads trading-tech-adjacent (real-time constraints, thread safety, resource management, efficiency); Python data/batch or infra evidence that maps onto financial-data handling. Prior finance is not required; inventing a trading-desk internship is worse than honest CS + systems + data. Class-year on page must show graduating Fall 2027 or later and a path to FT in 2028 or 2029 (Expected May 2028 qualifies).

## Screen Criteria

- C++ and Python demonstrated in **bullets**, not Skills alone — both are load-bearing at this firm.
- Real-time, concurrency, resource-management, or efficiency depth that reads as trading-lifecycle adjacent (thread safety, lock-free or memory-discipline, hot-path constraints, measured latency/throughput).
- Data/batch/infra signal: pipelines, large datasets, APIs, or tooling that maps onto financial-data handling and batch processing — without claiming a desk or exchange stack the page does not have.
- Exceptional programming and design in real, shipped, defensible work — the candidate can narrate trade-offs; strong CS fundamentals visible for a coding screen.
- End-to-end ownership and production impact with metrics (latency, throughput, %, scale) — soft, metric-free claims read thin at this bar.
- DS&A / OOP foundation and analytical coursework (statistics, discrete math) visible.
- Class-year on page shows Fall 2027 or later graduation and return-to-school after a Summer 2027 internship (Expected May 2028 is in window).

## Anti-Patterns

- Re-targeting to an ML-research / LoRA / agent-pipeline lead as if this were a quant-research internship — PDT does not offer that intern program.
- All-CRUD / all-web-app page with no systems-performance or data/batch signal.
- C++ or Python named in Skills with zero bullet evidence.
- Finance-jargon padding or an invented trading-desk internship — prior finance is not required, and faking it reads worse than honest strong CS.
- Soft, metric-free shipping claims where correctness and efficiency are the firm's selling point.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a strong systems + data spine.
