# Software Engineering Intern (2027 Summer Internship) at Northwood Space

## Role Summary

A paid Summer 2027 software engineering internship, in person five days per week at Northwood's Torrance, CA office ($36/hr). The intern supports design, implementation, and optimization of data movement across a global phased-array ground-station network: secure/extensible payload-data interfaces, a hybrid on-prem/cloud data plane for low-latency high-throughput distribution, data-movement pipelines at volume, and systems that are observable, fault-tolerant, and reliable enough for uptime targets. Hands-on networking devices/protocols and collaboration with software, network, and FPGA engineers are in scope; FPGA is a collaboration surface, not intern firmware work. The JD surface is generic SWE (enrolled CS/SE, Rust/C++/Go, interest in systems/networking/distributed systems, cloud and Docker/Terraform as pluses). Northwood's identity is space-infra — Portal ground stations and the data plane that moves satellite traffic — so the memorable signal is systems / networking / reliability / cloud-pipeline depth, not a consumer web app and not an embedded specialist page.

## Screen Criteria

- JD languages demonstrated through use in bullets — C++ at minimum — not Skills-only while the posting names Rust/C++/Golang. Claiming Rust, Golang, Terraform, or FPGA that the page cannot support is a credibility miss.
- Full-stack/general SWE delivery on the page: APIs or services, data movement/pipelines, cloud or on-prem deployment, some client or ownership evidence so the generic intern screen clears (`resume.md` §12).
- Differentiator visible in the top half: systems programming, networking-adjacent or concurrent/real-time constraints, distributed or pipeline reliability, observability/fault-tolerance — without turning the resume into an embedded/firmware story or a hobby DSP page.
- Metrics that size real outcomes (latency, throughput, failure recovery, scale, cost) — uptime/reliability is a listed duty, not a nice-to-have.
- Ownership and collaborative delivery via shipped systems others depend on — not club-ops or membership filler.
- Anti-patterns: Skills-only C++ with no bullet proof; inventing Rust/Go/Terraform/FPGA; collapsing this SWE intern into embedded/firmware; club-ops without engineering depth; notebook ML without delivery; a consumer-SaaS or agentic-LLM lead with zero systems/pipeline/reliability signal at a ground-station company.
