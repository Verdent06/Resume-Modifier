# Northwood Space

Northwood Space is a space-infrastructure company building a global network of electronically steered phased-array ground stations (Portal) so satellite missions — national security, connectivity, disaster response — can talk to the ground in real time instead of waiting on traditional dish passes. Software sits on the data plane: payload-data interfaces, hybrid on-prem/cloud distribution, and observable, fault-tolerant pipelines that move high-throughput traffic between ground stations and cloud. The company is vertically integrated (design, manufacture, deploy) out of a 185k sqft Torrance HQ/factory; it is not a launch provider and this SWE intern req is not the Embedded Software Engineering Intern posting.
