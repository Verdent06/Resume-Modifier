# WEC Energy Group (We Energies / Wisconsin Public Service)

WEC Energy Group (NYSE: WEC) is a Milwaukee-based Fortune 500 (#424, 2026) electric and gas holding company serving about 4.8 million customers in Wisconsin, Illinois, Michigan, and Minnesota. Principal utilities include We Energies and Wisconsin Public Service; WEC Infrastructure owns a renewable generation fleet. This intern sits on the Renewables Analytics team that turns operational fleet data (wind / solar / battery storage) into metrics, pipelines, and KPI reporting — applied data engineering and analytics, not generic utility IT or power-systems design.

- **HQ / offices:** Milwaukee, WI (HQ). This req: hybrid Milwaukee, WI or Green Bay, WI. Generation-site travel in WI, MI, MN, IA, IL, or IN.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: WEC); S&P 500; ~$9.8B revenue (Fortune 2026); ~7,000 employees; ~$51–52B assets. 4.8 million customers.
- **Product focus:** Regulated electric/gas utilities plus a renewables operating fleet; this team owns fleet data architecture, ETL, and performance/reliability analytics.
- **Intern comp (2027 Renewables Data Analytics):** $23.10–$27.55/hr; starting $23.10; paid company holidays. Housing/relocation not listed.
- **Work model:** Paid Summer 2027 intern (May/June start); full-time summer with potential part-time in the fall; hybrid (remote/in-office). Valid driver's license required.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** HS diploma; currently pursuing Bachelor's or higher in Data Science, Data Analytics, Computer Science, Engineering or related; graduation after June 2027; GPA 2.8+; valid driver's license. US work authorization required. No sponsorship; no OPT/CPT or temporary visas (E, F-1 OPT/CPT, H-1, H-2, L-1, B, J, TN). End date listed 11/15/2026; applications reviewed rolling.
