## Role Summary

A Summer 2027 **hybrid** internship on the Renewables Analytics team at We Energies / Wisconsin Public Service (WEC Energy Group subsidiaries). Locations: Milwaukee, WI or Green Bay, WI. Comp: $23.10–$27.55/hr (starting $23.10). Full-time summer with potential part-time in the fall. Req 7118. May/June 2027 start. Travel to generation sites in WI, MI, MN, IA, IL, or IN. Valid driver's license required.

The intern supports day-to-day analytics: business-requirement gathering, data preparation, analysis, visualization, and meetings. Responsibilities are operational data work for a large renewables fleet — equipment-metadata databases, data flows/pipelines that feed real-time predictive models, ETL of operational SCADA data into aggregated repositories, performance/reliability review of wind / solar / battery storage, fleet-level KPI reporting, ad hoc equipment investigations, and SME self-service access to raw and aggregated data. Collaboration is with the Renewables team, business/data analysts, and IT.

JD surface is **applied data engineering and analytics**, not general SWE, not ML research, not power-systems design. Company identity (`company.md` / `companies.md`): Fortune 500 Midwest utility holding company whose renewables operating fleet needs metrics and pipelines — industrial energy operations, not a tech-product company.

- Pipelines that feed a model, score, or decision tool — analog to "data flows that feed real-time predictive models."

- Fleet- or entity-level aggregation, scoring, or KPI-style reporting when the work is real — analog to fleet performance/reliability review. Do not invent wind/solar/storage metrics.
- Visualization or dashboard delivery only if it exists in the pool (React/report UIs count as visualization analogs). Do not invent Power BI or Tableau.
- Git / GitHub / Docker as supporting DevOps signal when tied to a pipeline, not as a Skills-only dump.
- Quantitative coursework (stats, calc) visible in Education.

**Anti-patterns:**
- Generic SWE / full-stack product resume that never shows ETL, SQL, or data pipelines — wrong job.
- Notebook ML or `model.fit()` with no delivery, no pipeline, no measured outcome.
- Skills-only Python/SQL/Power BI/SCADA with no bullet proof.
- Invented Power BI, Tableau, Snowflake, Databricks, VBA, Athena, T-SQL, SCADA, or renewable-energy operations claims (JD names several; absence in bullets is honest; stuffing them is a fabrication smell — `resume.md` §8).
