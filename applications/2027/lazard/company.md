# Lazard

Lazard is an independent financial advisory and asset management firm (NYSE **LAZ**, founded 1848) with just over 3,000 professionals (YE2025: **3,309** employees; AM AUM **$254B** per 2025 10-K). The intern sits in the **AI and Data Group** — data scientists, data engineers, AI engineers, and software engineers who take internal products from concept through deployment and performance analysis for Financial Advisory bankers and Asset Management portfolio managers. This req is a **Software Engineer** seat on that team: large-scale **data-intensive applications and data products**, applied AI/data pipelines (LLM frameworks, agentic workflows, MCP interest), not ML research and not an IBD banking-analyst rotation.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** New York, NY — **30 Rockefeller Plaza, New York, NY 10112**; this intern is NYC hybrid and must be in NYC for the term
- **Valuation / signal:** NYSE **LAZ**; elite independent FA + AM; IB-tech peer of Goldman/Blackstone campus tech (~5–8% directional)
- **Product focus:** Internal AI & Data Group — data-intensive applications and data products that enhance decision-making across Financial Advisory and Asset Management
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer Summer Internship, AI & Data):** ~$135,000 USD annualized (Built In: 135K Annually; JD: prorated salary approximately $135,000)
- **Work model:** Paid; **June 7, 2027 – August 13, 2027** (~10 weeks); Hybrid NYC; must be in New York for the duration
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Ability to start June 7 and end August 13, 2027; in NYC for the term; bachelor or advanced degree in Computer Science/Engineering or equivalent. No published GPA or exclusive class-year knockout.
