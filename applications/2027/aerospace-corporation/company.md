# The Aerospace Corporation

The Aerospace Corporation is the national nonprofit that operates the only federally funded research and development center (FFRDC) committed exclusively to the space enterprise — satellite, launch, ground, and cyber for defense, civil, and commercial customers. HQ is El Segundo, CA, with ~26 U.S. sites. Technical prestige sits above C-tier primes and next to JHU APL; it is not SpaceX. This intern (Workday **R016605**) is **2027 Aerospace Software Engineer Undergraduate Intern**, full-time onsite El Segundo, in the Vehicle Systems Software Department (**VSSD**): independent verification and validation of **flight software** embedded in avionics on launch vehicles, spacecraft, and other vehicle systems; high-fidelity closed-loop **SIL/HIL/emulator** testbeds; and real-time embedded software for CubeSats, drones, and emerging tech. Vehicle Systems Division (VSD) org copy can include mechanical/aero domains — **this seat is VSSD software, not mechanical design, CAD, or structures.**
