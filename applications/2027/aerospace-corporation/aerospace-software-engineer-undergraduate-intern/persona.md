A Summer 2027 full-time **onsite El Segundo** undergraduate software internship (paid **$25–$30/hr**, ≥10 weeks outside term, return to Bachelor’s afterward). Preferred items (GPA 3.5+, dynamics/control, spacecraft/rocket coursework, SDLC/IDEs, ML/AI, MBSE, MCUs) are stand-outs, not knockouts.
