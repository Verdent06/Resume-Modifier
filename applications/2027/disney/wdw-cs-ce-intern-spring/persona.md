# WDW Computer Science / Computer Engineering Interns, Spring 2027 at The Walt Disney Company

## Role Summary

A paid January–June 2027 onsite internship at Walt Disney World (Lake Buena Vista, FL) under Worldwide Safety, Health, Engineer & Sourcing, with Safety Assurance as the primary business. This is a Parks & Experiences Professional Internship, not Disney Streaming SWE. The posting is multidisciplinary: candidates may be presented to Quality Engineering, Worldwide Safety & Assurance, Engineering Services, Textile Engineering Services, Central Shops, Global Engineering & Technology, or Facility Asset Management. Day-to-day may include safety-related data analysis and metrics, software services for ride/show and other process-control systems, and resolving technical issues at software–hardware interfaces, using languages such as C++, C#, Python, and Node.js. The JD surface is general CS/CE: OOP, Microsoft Office, Junior/Senior/Graduate by arrival. The company's memorable identity here is parks operations software — process-control, safety analytics, and hardware–software interfaces in a guest-facing environment — not a streaming product org and not a robotics/ride-fabrication shop.

The resume leads with the full-stack spine AND keeps real-time/C++ and data-metrics evidence prominent and deep — it does not collapse into a pure web-product page, a PE-deal-sourcing intern page, an agentic-LLM product page, or a hobby DSP page with no delivery.

## Team & Bar

The reviewer is a B-tier Parks Professional Internship recruiter/engineer (`companies.md`: Workday resume → recruiter phone 20–30m → HackerRank for tech tracks, which Parks CS/CE may skip if routed to non-SDE teams → virtual onsite coding + behavioral with Guest-Centric / Why Disney; Easy–Med; light intern system design; bottleneck = resume; ~5–10% **[directional]**). With resume as the bottleneck, the PDF is the front door (`recruiting.md` §8 intern funnel: resume-first when the OA is not the binding gate). They reward candidates who look like they can sit on a safety/QE/process-control team and ship reliable software — OOP and JD languages shown inside bullets (not Skills alone), production delivery with a stakeholder or deploy path, data-metrics or ETL that could analogize to safety-trend work, and systems discipline next to hardware (real-time constraints, fault tolerance, interface debugging). Recruiter voice is practical: "can this person write, test, and maintain code other people will run in the parks?" Prestige is a tiebreaker. Guest-Centric behavioral is a later-loop filter, not a resume section. Unrestricted work authorization and class standing are apply-form knockouts, not resume lines. GPA 3.0 preferred should appear if it is ≥ 3.0. Do not claim a Disney College Program.

## Screen Criteria

- Demonstrates JD languages **through use in bullets**, especially C++ and Python, plus SQL where the work used it — not Skills-list only. C# and Node.js are "may use," not a fabrication invitation.
- Shows shipped software with a real stakeholder, paying users, or a release artifact (API, deploy, CI) so the software-services screen is obvious.
- Makes parks-adjacent discipline visible in the top half: real-time / process-control analog (deterministic constraints, concurrency, fault tolerance) and/or safety-style data analysis and metrics (ingest → aggregate → serve) — without turning the page into a hobby DSP resume or an invented ride internship.
- Backs the full-stack spine with end-to-end delivery (APIs/services, data pipelines, Git, Docker/CI, cloud) so preferred platform keywords appear through use.
- Uses impact metrics (latency, failure rate, hours saved, throughput, scale) rather than vanity counts or club-ops filler.
- Object-oriented / software-engineering fundamentals shown in built software, not claimed as coursework alone.
- Anti-patterns: inventing C#, Node.js, PowerApps/PowerAutomate, Linux-as-a-claimed-skill, TDD/BDD as practice, a Disney College Program, ride-vehicle fabrication, or ROS; leading as a PE-deal-sourcing intern or an agentic-LLM product intern; notebook ML without delivery; a DSP-hobby page with no shipped artifact; Skills stuffing with tools the bullets cannot defend.
