# The Walt Disney Company

The Walt Disney Company (NYSE: DIS) is a Fortune-class public entertainment company. Parks & Experiences Professional Internships at Walt Disney World are not Disney Streaming SWE (~$42/hr). Three Spring 2027 Lake Buena Vista reqs share this file — **do not mix legal employers, ATS sites, or pay**:

- **WDW CS/CE Interns (req 10158145)** — WSHES / Safety Assurance multidisciplinary routing (quality engineering, safety analytics, ride/show process-control, Engineering Services, Facility Asset Management).
- **Labor Systems Web Integration Intern (req 10158184)** — Park Ops / Experience Integration (DLR). Cast labor-management web apps (workload, scheduling, time, deployment). Do not mix stacks or personas across these two.
- **Decision Science Undergraduate Intern (req 10159998)** — corporate **DDSI** (Disney Decision Science and Integration). Applied analytics / modeling for yield, revenue planning, forecasting, pricing, and optimization across Disney Experiences, Disney Entertainment, and ESPN. **Not** Parks PI, **not** Labor Systems, **not** Decision Science Graduate Associate, **not** Disney Streaming SWE.
