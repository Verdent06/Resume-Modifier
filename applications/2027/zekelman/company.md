# Zekelman Industries

Zekelman Industries is a privately held family manufacturer (founded 1877; CEO/Executive Chair Barry Zekelman; formerly John Maneely / JMC) and the largest independent steel pipe and tube manufacturer in North America — **100% domestic**. Brands include Atlas Tube, Wheatland Tube, Sharon Tube, Picoma, Western Tube, **Z Modular**, and Hayes Modular. Careers copy: **2.8 million tons**/yr of steel plus millions of square feet of modular buildings across **23** North American locations. Z Modular is the fully integrated self-developer of multi-family housing; Troy hosts the Z Modular engineering team.
