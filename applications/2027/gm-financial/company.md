# GM Financial

GM Financial is the wholly owned captive auto-finance subsidiary of General Motors (founded 1992 as AmeriCredit; GM acquired 2010). HQ Fort Worth, TX; ~5,001–10,000 employees. Through GM dealer relationships it offers retail financing and lease programs plus commercial lending so dealers can finance and grow their businesses, with operations in North America, South America, and the Asia Pacific region. **Do not mix with GM (Detroit/Warren) SWE intern.** This folder is Oracle CX **260818** **Intern - Software Development Engineer** — generic SDE intern (Agile/Scrum; web, mobile, API, and data-marketplace features; assist DevOps pipelines/static scanning, vulnerability remediation, production support; capstone). **Not** Intern - Business Systems Analyst sibling.
