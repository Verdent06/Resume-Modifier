# Gordon Food Service (GFS)

Gordon Food Service is a privately held, family-managed broadline foodservice distributor (founded 1897; 5th-generation Gordon family). Headquarters is the Home Office in Wyoming, Michigan (Grand Rapids metro; 1300 Gezon Pkwy SW). Stewardship Guide 2026: **$24B** annual sales; North America's largest privately held foodservice distributor; ~175 GFS Stores (JD JSON-LD also says more than 180 retail stores). Serves restaurants, healthcare, schools, and operators across the US and Canada. Flagship digital product is **Gordon Ordering**, the customer eCommerce platform. This intern sits on the Software Engineering team that ships features, APIs, tests, and CI/CD for that platform — not the Low Code / Appian SWE intern sibling and not the MMSC Category Technology Intern.
