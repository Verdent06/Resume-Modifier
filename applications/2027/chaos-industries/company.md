# CHAOS Industries

CHAOS Industries builds Coherent Distributed Networks (CDN™) — software-defined sensor and radar networks that synchronize nodes in time so warfighters, commercial air operators, and border teams get a single operating picture earlier than traditional exquisite radars. The intern-facing work is firmware on those sensor nodes plus the software that keeps them synchronized under timing, power, thermal, and radio constraints. Founded 2022; no `reference/companies.md` row. Closest doctrine peer is Anduril (A-TIER defense tech, C++ / embedded / autonomy), but CHAOS is smaller, newer, and unlisted — do not inherit Anduril's acceptance rate or OA platform.
