# Tyler Technologies

Tyler Technologies (NYSE: **TYL**) builds integrated software for the public sector — courts, public safety, appraisal/tax, ERP, civic services, and regulatory systems. This intern sits at the **Civic Services / EnerGov** office in **Lawrenceville, GA** (2530 Sever Rd NW; Civic Services HQ moved from Duluth in 2021). EnerGov (now also marketed as Enterprise Permitting & Licensing) is municipal permitting, licensing, code compliance, and community development software — the JD’s “solutions that enable new businesses to safely build, open their doors, and stay in compliance,” including fire prevention and structural safety. Production shop at Lawrenceville is **.NET/C# + Angular** (adjacent FT: Full Stack .NET/Angular, Enterprise Permits & Licensing). **Not** a consumer product shop, **not** an ML-research intern, **not** Technical Services / IT DevOps / Professional Services PM intern **2026-9509**.
