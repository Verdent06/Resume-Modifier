# Interactive Brokers

Interactive Brokers Group (Nasdaq: IBKR) is a Greenwich-headquartered electronic broker and S&P 500 company that provides automated trade execution and custody of securities, commodities, FX, and related products across 170+ markets from a single platform. Clients are individual investors, hedge funds, prop groups, advisors, and introducing brokers — the product is a technology-first brokerage, not a traditional bank IT shop. Engineering owns the high-performance systems behind execution, custody, and back/middle/front-office tooling; intern SWE work is design/develop/maintain on those systems, with a named project under mentorship and code review.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`) — respected electronic broker; finance company with a real C++/Java platform, not a prestige quant desk
- **HQ / offices:** Greenwich, CT HQ; offices in 15+ countries; this intern role is onsite Greenwich
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NASDAQ: IBKR); S&P 500; Barron's / StockBrokers.com top-broker awards; ~2,400 employees
- **Product focus:** Unified electronic brokerage — automated execution, custody, and client/ops software at scale
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Developer Intern):** not listed on the 2027 JD; Levels.fyi Summer 2025 Greenwich SWE intern ~$50/hr plus lunch allowance, shuttle, ~$1,500 relocation
- **Work model:** Paid 9-week onsite program (2027 posting); Greenwich, CT; daily lunch allowance; free shuttle from the train station. Prior-year SWE intern posting was 10 weeks — use 9 weeks from the live JD.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Pursuing BS/MS/Ph.D. in CS or Software Engineering; GPA ≥ 3.5; programming experience in C++, Java, or Python; preferred graduation Dec 2027–May 2028; required onsite 9-week commitment. Work authorization not stated as a knockout on this posting.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** C++ and Java are the production floor for the trading platform; intern JD also accepts Python. SQL/relational concepts show up in full-time loops.
- **Domains:** High-performance brokerage software — execution, custody, back/middle/front office, large-data research platforms (prior-year intern posting).
- **What wins:** C++ or Python proven in bullets with systems or production-shipping depth; DS&A coursework visible; financial-markets interest is a plus, not a substitute for engineering. Per `reference/recruiting.md` Part III §11, this is general SWE with a human+OA front door — keyword-through-use for C++/Python and shipped software beats ML-research or CAD framing. Do not claim Java if it is not in the inventory.
