# Fable (Fable Security)

Fable Security (legal/brand: **Fable**; founded 2024) builds an AI-native human-risk cybersecurity platform: identify at-risk employees, curate hyper-personalized Gen-AI video briefings, and deploy them at the relevant moment. Founders Nicole Jiang-Gibson (CEO) and Dr. Sanny Liao (CPO) are Abnormal AI founding-team alumni; the thesis is that security-awareness training has become a checkbox while the real attack surface is human behavior. This intern sits in **R&D** and ships weekly on that platform (full-stack features, backend services, data pipelines, gen-AI video at scale) — product SWE, not an ML-research rotation. Do **not** mix with Fable (accessibility captions) or Fable (storytelling).
