# Rugged Robotics

Rugged Robotics builds construction layout robots — the **Mark I** autonomous rover prints coordinated A/E (BIM/CAD) designs onto unfinished concrete so trades install from one on-slab source of truth, sold as layout-as-a-service (self-contained; multi-robot). Founded **2018** by **Derrick J. Morse, P.E.** (construction) and **Logan Farrell** (ex-NASA robotics). Named jobs include Hensel Phelps **250K+ sq ft** hyperscale data center (company: **750+** hours of layout labor freed, framing install **+15%**), Brogoitti Construction, and early customers Consigli, Suffolk, and Brasfield & Gorrie. Competitor analog is **Dusty Robotics**. **Do not mix with Rivet Industries** (defense wearables) or **REV Robotics** (FIRST educational OEM).
