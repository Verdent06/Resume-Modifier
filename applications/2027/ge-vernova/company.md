# GE Vernova

GE Vernova is GE's 2024 energy spinoff: a Fortune-class public company (NYSE: GEV) whose Power, Wind, and Electrification businesses sell generation, grid, and storage equipment plus software to utilities and industrials. Installed base generates a large share of world electricity; 2026 demand is being pulled by data-center load. Power Conversion & Storage (PCS) builds solar and energy-storage conversion systems; this intern sits on PCS Engineering / NPI in Niskayuna and builds AI tooling for those product lines — data infra, templates, agents, and engineer-facing interfaces — not CAD or power-electronics design.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-tier (`reference/companies.md` — Fortune-class industrial energy peer to Medtronic/WD/Intel for intern brand; solid program, not FAANG SWE prestige)
- **HQ / offices:** Cambridge, MA (HQ). This req: Niskayuna, NY (onsite). Other intern hubs include Schenectady NY, Greenville SC, Atlanta, Wilmington NC.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: GEV); ~$35–38B revenue class; ~77,000 employees; intern pipeline into EEDP and other GE rotational programs (Extern 2026 guide, directional)
- **Product focus:** Power conversion and energy storage (solar / storage product lines); AI tools to accelerate NPI engineering
- **Intern comp (2027 PCS AI Tool Developer):** $1,000–$2,000/week (~$25–$50/hr); relocation assistance yes; US intern roles ineligible for GE Vernova benefits
- **Work model:** Paid Summer 2027 intern; onsite Niskayuna, NY; relocation assistance provided
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Working toward BS in CS, EE, or similar; full-time enrollment. GPA 3.0 preferred (without rounding). Legally authorized to work in the US; posting states no H1B / intern sponsorship. Drug screen as applicable. Posted 2026-08-12; listed deadline 2026-08-21; "expected to remain open at least seven days" (rolling).
