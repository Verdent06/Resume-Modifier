# Persona — Engineering Intern, Power Conversion & Storage AI Tool Developer (Summer 2027)

Recruiter lens shared by the writer and grader. Abstract bar signals only — no candidate entry names, no include/omit table.

## Role Summary

A Summer 2027 onsite internship on GE Vernova's Power Conversion & Storage (PCS) Engineering / New Product Introduction team in Niskayuna, NY. The intern is the CS/EE interface between branded AI solutions, application engineers, design engineers, and leadership: stand up data flow and infrastructure for AI deployment on solar and energy-storage products; collect, format, and templatize engineering documents for an AI tool; work with the AI Research Technology team on the data needed to tune that tool; build agents and similar interface tools that meet NPI needs; test and program the tools; write and present training materials. JD surface is applied AI tooling and data plumbing for engineers — not power-electronics design, not CAD, not notebook ML research. Company identity: industrial energy hardware (conversion/storage) whose NPI loop is being accelerated with internal AI tools.

## Team & Bar

Screener is a PCS NPI hiring manager plus recruiters reading Workday — resume-first industrial intern funnel, not a high-volume OA gauntlet (`recruiting.md` intern: OA is the binding filter at big-tech scale; here the resume carries the front-end weight). Evidence that wins: a shipped agentic/LLM workflow with orchestration and evaluation; data collection/ETL/infra that feeds a tool; interface or API work next to non-ML stakeholders; metrics that size impact. GPA 3.0 is the preferred floor; 3.66 clears it. CS or EE undergrad, full-time enrollment. Intern-stage weighting still favors engineered projects + a live GitHub (`resume.md` §9). Math/stats coursework is a genuine ai-ml signal (`resume.md` §14) but not a substitute for agent/workflow evidence.

## Screen Criteria (abstract)

Pass signals:
- Hands-on AI prompt, agent, and workflow construction (multi-step orchestration, tool/model integration, structured output or eval) — not a thin API wrapper or a notebook demo.
- Data infrastructure for AI: collection, formatting, templates, pipelines, or stores that feed a model/tool — the JD's first two responsibility bullets.
- Evaluation / validation discipline: eval harnesses, tests, faithfulness/accuracy lifts, or "test and program AI tools" analogs.
- Engineer-facing delivery: APIs, interfaces, or stakeholder-scoped tools shipped to people who are not the builder — analog to NPI application/design engineers and training materials.
- Python (and related data/AI stack) demonstrated through use in bullets, not Skills alone. JD names no languages; orphaned AI buzzwords still fail.
- Metrics that size impact (accuracy/faithfulness, latency, hours saved, throughput), landed as the closer (`resume.md` §4).

Anti-patterns:
- Notebook ML or `model.fit()` with no agent, no pipeline, no delivery.
- Off-axis lead: pure frontend, pure audio-DSP, or club-ops fronting an AI-tooling intern req.
- Agentic/LLM claims only in Skills with no supporting system.
- Mechanical/CAD or power-electronics framing this CS intern cannot defend.
- Metric-free architecture bullets the recruiter cannot size.
- No live GitHub on an intern engineering resume (`resume.md` §3).
