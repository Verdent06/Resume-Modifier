# Johnson & Johnson

Johnson & Johnson is a Fortune-class MedTech and Innovative Medicine company (Kenvue consumer spinoff 2023; ~130k employees). This co-op sits in **MD Quality and Compliance (Q&C) Digital Solutions** at **Ethicon, Inc. in Cincinnati**, building software and advanced analytics for **medical-device quality-system digital solutions** — not an R&D lab, not a consumer app team, and not a quality-engineer seat that requires prior FDA internships. Engineering identity is regulated MedTech quality software: validation, SDLC, process improvement, and analytics apps that quality engineers and Quality management actually use.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-tier (`reference/companies.md` — Fortune-class MedTech peer to AbbVie/Medtronic; solid intern brand, not FAANG SWE prestige)
- **HQ / offices:** New Brunswick, NJ (HQ). This co-op: Cincinnati, OH (Ethicon). Other US sites include Raritan/Titusville NJ, Irvine/Santa Clara CA, and many more.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: JNJ); large-cap healthcare; Credo-driven culture. Intern program ~7–12% **[directional — Extern]**
- **Product focus:** MedTech (Ethicon surgical) + Innovative Medicine; this req is MD Q&C Digital Solutions advanced analytics and quality-system software
- **Intern comp (2027 Quality Data Science Co-op):** $23.50–$52.50/hr (posted)
- **Work model:** Paid full-time Spring 2027 co-op (01/2027–04/2027); hybrid; Cincinnati, OH. Non-LDP Career Programs intern/co-op (R-095792). Posting close September 14, 2026.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Bachelor's or Master's in Mechanical / Biomedical / Chemical / Electrical / Computer/Software Engineering or related. Second year or above at start of term January 2027; graduation after April 2027. GPA 3.0+. Permanently authorized to work in the U.S. for the full co-op; must not require sponsorship now or in the future (CPT/OPT/STEM OPT usually does not meet). Workday ATS.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python (and R on the JD as preferred). SQL is implied by data analytics, not named as a hard requirement. Do not invent R, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Power BI, Copilot, or Fusion.
- **Domains:** Advanced analytics for quality digital solutions; software development and validation for medical-device quality systems; SDLC / lifecycle management; process improvement; collaboration with data scientists, analytics app developers, and quality engineers; Quality-management risk/opportunity discussions.
- **What wins:** Python + data analytics in shipped pipelines (messy Excel/regulatory data → ETL → API or scored output), plus a software-validation / SDLC analog (real-time safety discipline or eval/quality gates) — not fake FDA knowledge, medical-device internships, hospital volunteering, or quality-engineer titles. Resume is the gate (`recruiting.md` intern: eligibility knockouts first, then screen; B-tier non-tech-tech is resume-first).
