# FHLBank Chicago (Federal Home Loan Bank of Chicago)

The Federal Home Loan Bank of Chicago is a self-capitalizing cooperative in the FHLBank System — one of **11** Federal Home Loan Banks created by Congress in **1932**. Mission (live posting): partner with members in **Illinois and Wisconsin** on competitively priced funding, a reasonable return on their investment, and community-investment support. It is a **bank for banks** and other member financial institutions (commercial banks, credit unions, insurance companies, savings institutions, CDFIs), not a consumer retail bank and not the Chicago Fed. YE2025: total assets **$141.2B**, advances **$61.1B**, MPF loans held in portfolio **$14.7B**, net income **$654M**; **637** members (409 IL / 228 WI); **467** full-time and **8** part-time employees (10-K). HQ **433 W. Van Buren St., Suite 501S, Chicago, IL 60607**. This intern sits in **Information Technology** on emerging **AI, automation, and digital transformation** — agents, Copilot *exposure*, usage analytics, governance documentation, and PoCs in a regulated GSE — **not** Summer Internship - Application Development **R2600441**, **not** Finance Transformation & Systems **R2600460**, and **not** the risk/accounting/MPF intern siblings.
