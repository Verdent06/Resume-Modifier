Investor materials: **300+** Embraer 170/175, ~25M passengers/year, ~1,300 daily departures, 100+ cities in the US/Canada/Caribbean/Mexico, ~8,500 associates, 12 bases. HQ is the **$200M** Carmel, IN campus at **2 Brickyard Lane, 46032** (HQ + training center + Brickyard Hotel; 2026 move from Purdue Rd, Indianapolis). LIFT Academy is the pilot pipeline.
