# Biogen

Biogen (Nasdaq: **BIIB**) is a mid-size independent biotechnology company (JD wording) founded in 1978. It discovers and delivers medicines for serious neurological disease — MS, SMA, and Alzheimer’s (LEQEMBI collaboration) among others — from Cambridge, MA. FY2025 total revenue **$9.891B**; **7,471** employees / **7,443** FTE (official ESG 2025). The JD sidebar notes the recent **Apellis** acquisition. This req is a **6-month Data Science co-op** on SPARQ TADI (acronym not expanded on the posting): applied DS / analytics / AI over **regulatory, R&D quality/compliance, drug safety and/or non-clinical safety** data — LLM chatbots on unstructured repositories, human-in-the-loop document generation, auditor-style agents, SOP/guidance for AI oversight — **not** a 12-week summer intern, **not** a PhD Advanced Analytics intern sibling, **not** QSDO data-visualization intern, **not** a lab/R&D bench co-op.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages / tools (this JD):** Python **or** R **or** SQL (one is the floor). Foundational statistics, machine learning, data visualization, data preparation. Example work: LLM chatbots over unstructured repos, HITL document generation, auditor-style agents, SOP/guidance for AI quality/oversight. Do **not** invent R, SAS, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Power BI, Copilot, Fusion, GxP tooling, or a SPARQ acronym expansion
- **Domains:** Biopharma **R&D analytics**: regulatory, R&D quality/compliance, drug safety, non-clinical safety — structured + unstructured data → dashboards/UIs/reports → reusable pipelines and documented models
- **What wins:** End-to-end ingest → clean/integrate → statistical or ML method → ranked insight, dashboard, or NLP/retrieval analog → measured outcome (`resume.md` Part III §14; `recruiting.md` Part III §13). Python and SQL shown in bullets, not Skills alone. LLM/eval/pipeline analog is supporting evidence for SPARQ TADI’s example projects — not a substitute for messy-data prep. Workday knockouts (enrolled, 18+, US work auth, Jan–Jun 2027 remote) are absolute (`recruiting.md` §1 / §8)
