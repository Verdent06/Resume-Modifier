# Wellington Management

Wellington Management is an independent private-partnership asset manager (est. 1928; ~$1.43T AUM, Form ADV 2026-03-31) that offers comprehensive investment management across nearly all segments of global capital markets, with proprietary research and institutional clients in more than 50 countries. Technologists sit on Client, Infrastructure, and Investment platforms and partner across the firm to build software that improves decision-making for a global investment organization. This req (**R94778**, Technology Undergraduate Summer Internship) is a 10-week Boston HQ **cohort project**: interns identify, design, build, test, and deliver a software application on a real firm business challenge (software engineering + data analysis + product delivery) — **not** Technology Associate (TAP) full-time, **not** Business Associate, and **not** an investment-analyst seat. Python is the named example language; AI-tool curiosity is a look-for, not an ML-framework screen. Do not invent Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, or Kubernetes.

- **Clearance / eligibility:** Current sophomore or junior undergraduate pursuing a bachelor's; **not eligible for visa sponsorship** (FAQ: must be authorized without sponsorship, including F-1 without work auth beyond CPT/OPT). GPA unstated on this JD. No prior finance experience required.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python named as an example ("familiarity with at least one programming language such as Python"). Do not invent Java, C#, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, or Kubernetes.
- **Domains:** Software engineering, data analysis, application design, testing, iterative delivery, stakeholder feedback, cohort collaboration; curiosity about emerging technologies and AI tools as a look-for. Investment-management technology (fintech-backend for Client / Infrastructure / Investment platforms) is the company identity, not a trading-desk or quant-research intern.
- **What wins:** Because the bottleneck is the resume (`companies.md`; `recruiting.md` intern funnel at C-tier), a one-page PDF that shows a shipped software application (design → code → test → deliver), data analysis through use (Python/Pandas/SQL), stakeholder-facing delivery, and honest AI-tool curiosity from real agent/product work — without claiming AM platforms the candidate has not used. Dual CS + Economics is on-axis flavor. Club-ops without engineering, notebook ML as the lead, and invented warehouse/BI tools fail this screen.
