# Skyward

Skyward, Inc. is a family-owned K-12 software company that builds **student information systems (SIS)** and **ERP** for school districts: student records, scheduling, attendance, grades, plus payroll, purchasing, and HR on the business side. Flagship product is **Qmlativ** (SMS 2.0 is the legacy line). Founded 1980; HQ at 2601 Skyward Drive, Stevens Point, WI. LinkedIn-directional scale is ~462 employees and ~$95.5M revenue. Interns sit on real **Programming** development teams shipping applications K-12 districts use; the firm cites 159 full-time hires who started as interns and runs a UWSP on-campus intern center. Production shop is C# / .NET / MS SQL / Visual Studio, with OpenEdge ABL (Progress) a plus on entry-level reqs — intern JD still screens generic SDLC (source control, code review, testing, agile), not those languages as exclusives.
