## Role Summary

Ten-week New York **Analyst Internship Programme** intern in **Technology, Data & Innovation (TDI)** — not IB coverage, not a data-analyst dashboard seat, and not an ML-research rotation. TDI teams deliver and manage engineering solutions for the bank's global customers: modernize technology, reduce complexity and risk, and drive business growth. Rotation-dependent work includes hands-on coding (languages that **could include** Java, Python, and others), cloud and AI applied to complex financial problems, and technology solution development on an Agile team. JD surface is generic intern SWE plus training/network packaging. Company identity (`company.md` / `companies.md`) is **bank-IT modernization** — cloud computing and AI as long-term infrastructure investment at a global bank — not a consumer-app startup and not a quant desk.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**Python**). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). Java is "could include" — do not invent it.
- Shipped full-stack or backend software: REST/APIs or services, persistence, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12).
- Technology solution development: a problem → architectural decision → shipped artifact, not a course dump.
- Cloud and/or AI **through use** as modernization analogs — production inference, agentic workflows with eval/judgment, containerized pipelines — matching TDI's cloud+AI thesis without becoming an ML-research resume.
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy–Med coding screen if invited.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies 1 Dec 2027 – 31 Jul 2028.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Invented Java (Skills or bullets) — not in the honest inventory; fabrication smell at a polyglot "could include Java" shop.
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, or Tableau — banned, and not in this JD.
- Notebook-ML lead / `model.fit()` as the first-pass identity when the role tests intern SWE coding and solution delivery.
- Club-ops filler occupying prime slots.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web/API/cloud/bank-IT SWE.
- Skills-only Python/cloud/AI with no bullet proof.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + bank-modernization spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
