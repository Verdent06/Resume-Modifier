# Deutsche Bank

Deutsche Bank is a global investment bank and financial-services group (Frankfurt HQ) whose Technology, Data & Innovation (TDI) organization engineers, operates, and modernizes the systems that run trading, risk, client platforms, payments, and internal operations. TDI's stated strategy is strengthening engineering expertise, moving to an agile delivery model, and modernizing bank IT with long-term infrastructure investment — including cloud computing and AI — rather than a trading-desk or IB coverage intern path. US TDI summer interns rotate through technology teams that deliver and manage engineering solutions for global customers: hands-on coding, cloud/AI applied to financial problems, and Agile solution development.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Frankfurt HQ; this req is **New York, United States**. US TDI intern/grad inventory also includes Cary, NC (separate TDI postings on the official early-careers calendar)
- **Valuation / signal:** Global systemically important bank; TDI is the technology arm modernizing bank IT — respected finance-tech intern brand, not S-tier eng prestige
- **Product focus:** Bank-wide engineering platforms — cloud/AI modernization of IT infrastructure plus delivery of solutions that reduce complexity/risk and support global customers
- **Intern comp (2027 TDI NYC intern):** **$100,000 annualized** (JD). Posted ranges exclude incentive compensation and other remuneration
- **Work model:** Paid 10-week Analyst Internship Programme, June–August 2027, New York. Formal training, seminars, networking, rotation-dependent placements
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Complete all degree requirements between **1 December 2027 and 31 July 2028**; full-time 10-week summer; **no more than 12 months** full-time relevant work experience; BS/MS in CS, Engineering, Mathematics, or related. **No intern visa sponsorship** — presently authorized to work for any US employer, now and in the future. This TDI posting prints **no GPA floor** (some DB IB desks do 3.5 — not this req). Official US TDI summer intern window: **17 August 2026 – 1 February 2027** (careers.db.com)

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Rotation "could include Java, Python, and others." Screen for coding proof in languages the candidate actually has — Python is the honest JD hit. Do not invent Java
- **Domains:** Generic TDI intern SWE — technology solution development, Agile teams, cloud computing and AI as modernization flavor (not an ML-research seat and not a dashboard/analyst job)
- **What wins:** Shipped engineering (APIs/services, cloud deploy, Python through use) plus responsible AI-tool evidence that is real systems work (agents, on-device inference, eval), not Copilot claims. Per `reference/recruiting.md` Part III §11, this is general SWE with a resume-first intern front door; OA/HireVue follow if invited. Finance-adjacent delivery is the company differentiator, not a second named track's requirements list
