# Generac (Generac Power Systems / Generac Holdings)

Generac Holdings Inc. (NYSE: GNRC) is a Waukesha, Wisconsin energy-technology OEM founded in 1959. It pioneered affordable home standby generators and remains the #1 name in that category, with a broader portfolio of portable, commercial, and industrial generators, energy storage, energy-management devices, transfer switches, mobile power, and power tools. FY2025 net sales were $4.21B; ~9,400 employees as of 31 Dec 2025 (10-K). This intern sits in the Summer 2027 internship pool for **data science / AI & ML / data engineering / product & operational analytics** on connected-product, manufacturing, test, and operational data — applied analytics for a power-equipment manufacturer, not a CAD intern and not an ML-research lab.
