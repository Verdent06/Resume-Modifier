# Dayton Freight Lines, Inc.

Dayton Freight Lines, Inc. is a privately owned, union-free **regional LTL carrier** founded in 1981 by Thomas L. Cronin, Jr. HQ is **6450 Poe Ave, Dayton, OH 45414**. Flagship is regional less-than-truckload (also truckload). LinkedIn: **54 Midwest service centers** and **13-state 1–2 day service**, plus a Strategic Alliance Network covering US / Canada / Mexico / Puerto Rico / Guam. Company facts: Technology **API and EDI integrations**; SCAC **DAFG**; Cardinal Health Regional LTL Carrier of the Year (company site). Internal IT advertises **Experienced C# .Net Developer**. LinkedIn intern evidence (2026): ASP.NET + EF Core learning project before the production codebase; planning, code review, testing. **Not** a consumer product startup and **not** an ML-research shop.
