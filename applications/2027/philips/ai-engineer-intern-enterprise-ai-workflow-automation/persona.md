## Role Summary

A Summer 2027 paid intern on Philips' Plymouth, MN campus (Plymouth - B1) building applied enterprise AI for workflow automation — not ML research, not IGT imaging models. The intern designs, builds, tests, and optimizes AI agents and conversational AI (prompts, instructions, grounding, knowledge sources); connects those agents to enterprise data; supports RAG over internal business content; automates processes; ships dashboards for adoption, feedback, and business impact; and documents architecture, workflows, user guides, and governance/operational readiness. JD surface is applied-AI SWE: Python/JavaScript/C#/SQL or-list, data management, GenAI/LLM/agents/APIs, Git. Company identity is health-tech / Enterprise Informatics (cloud PACS, interoperability, clinical workflow) sitting on an IGT-heavy Plymouth site. Comp $26.00–$36.00/hr + OT; onsite (office-based ≥3 days/week; JD: this is an onsite role). US work auth is a precondition; no sponsorship now or later; no company relocation.

## Screen Criteria

Pass signals (abstract — the writer discovers which entries carry them):

- JD languages the candidate actually has (Python, SQL) demonstrated in bullets, not Skills alone. TypeScript/React may analog JS-family apps; do not claim JavaScript-as-claimed or C#.
- AI agents / conversational or multi-step LLM workflows with prompts, grounding, or knowledge sources — built systems, not Copilot-user claims.
- RAG as retrieval over internal/business content (embeddings, vector search, knowledge sources) — not a Skills-only buzzword.
- Enterprise data integration analog: prep, transform, validate, APIs, connecting models to stores or REST — without inventing SharePoint, Lists, Teams-as-platform, Azure, Power BI.
- Process automation / workflow evidence (pipelines, workers, recurring jobs, deterministic gates) — Power Automate is preferred on the JD; do not invent it.
- Dashboards or reporting that size adoption, feedback, or business impact.
- Eval / test / optimize discipline: harnesses, faithfulness/accuracy lifts, troubleshooting UX or pipeline defects with before/after numbers (`resume.md` §4 DRAW closer).
- Git / source control through use (CI, GitHub Actions, versioned delivery).
- Documentation/enablement analog: stakeholder delivery, user-facing APIs, operational readiness — transferable to guides and governance without a healthcare internship.
- Class-year on page: current BS CS, `Expected May 2028` (Summer 2027 junior / rising junior after sophomore year). US citizen / no sponsorship is a form field, not a resume claim.

Anti-patterns:

- ML research / LoRA-as-identity as the lead story on an applied agents/RAG/automation intern.
- Notebook `model.fit()` with no pipeline, no agent, no delivery.
- Invented Copilot Studio, Power Automate, Power Apps, Power BI, Azure, SharePoint, Microsoft Lists, Teams-as-platform, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Fusion, C#, or JavaScript-as-claimed.
- Mixing this packet with IGT-D Data AI/ML Engineering intern, Graduate Data Scientist co-op 590567, or Software System Engineering co-op.
- Club-ops or pure audio-DSP leading an enterprise-AI workflow screen.
- Skills-line AI/LLM/RAG/Git with zero bullet proof.
- Metric-free architecture bullets the recruiter cannot size.
