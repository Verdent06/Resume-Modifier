# Cox Automotive

Cox Automotive is the auto-retail technology arm of family-owned Cox Enterprises (Atlanta). It operates consumer brands **Autotrader** and **Kelley Blue Book** and dealer/wholesale platforms **Manheim**, **vAuto**, and **Dealertrack**. The company’s own copy is that it is transforming how the world buys, owns, sells, or uses cars.
