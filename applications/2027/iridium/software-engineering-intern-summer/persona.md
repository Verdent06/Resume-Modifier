# Iridium Software Engineering Internship – Summer 2027 at Iridium Communications

## Role Summary

A paid, 11-week (May 18–July 30 2027), $31/hr internship at Iridium Satellite, LLC — in person at McLean, VA and/or Tempe, AZ (JSON-LD both; employee hybrid ≥4 days/week onsite). The intern develops and maintains features, services, applications, and libraries for the satellite network, ground systems, and products: architecture, design, coding, testing, and documentation in Agile/Scrum; GUI frameworks and web applications; Python/C++ utilities for data management and analysis; scripts/simulators/tools in Python, Java, or C; simulation/emulation/performance verify of satellite devices and transceivers; unit/integration testing, code reviews, and automation frameworks. The JD *requirements* are generic SWE intern gates: 18+, currently enrolled bachelor's or graduate, interest in software/product development, OOP + data structures + algorithms, U.S. citizenship, in person at an Iridium location. SWE/CS/IT/DS/Applied Math is preferred, not a knockout. Company identity is commercial LEO satcom — the world's only truly global voice/data/PNT network, now including Aireon space-based ADS-B — mission-critical network and ground-systems software, not RF hardware design, not Iridium Orbit (the 18-month new-grad rotation).
