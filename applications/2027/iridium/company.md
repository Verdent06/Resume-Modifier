# Iridium Communications

Iridium operates the world's only truly global voice, data, and PNT satellite network — 66 operational cross-linked LEO satellites plus in-orbit spares — selling commercial satcom rather than one-off spacecraft. Parent is Iridium Communications Inc. (NASDAQ: IRDM); this req hires through Iridium Satellite, LLC. FY2025 total revenue was $871.7M (service $634.0M). In July 2026 Iridium completed the remaining 61% of Aireon (~$366.7M), folding space-based ADS-B air-traffic surveillance into the same network. This intern is a catch-all SWE seat on satellite network, ground systems, and products — features, Agile/Scrum, GUI/web plus Python/C++ utilities, scripts/simulators, device/transceiver sim+emulation, automation, and unit/integration test — not Iridium Orbit (the 18-month new-grad rotation).
