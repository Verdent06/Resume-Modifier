# Veeam Software

Veeam is a privately held data-resilience vendor (Insight Partners) that sells backup, recovery, portability, security, and data-intelligence software. Company press (11 Dec 2025) states #1 global market share in data resilience, HQ Seattle, 30+ countries, 550,000+ customers including 82% of the Fortune 500. On 11 Dec 2025 Veeam closed a $1.725B acquisition of Securiti AI (DSPM, privacy, governance, AI trust); Securiti founder Rehan Jalil joined as President of Security and AI. This intern is **Tech Team Securiti AI** in San Jose — not the Veeam Data Cloud AI Intern sibling and not a generic HQ-Seattle corp-tech intern.
