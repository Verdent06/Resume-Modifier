# Riot Games

Riot Games ships live competitive games — League of Legends, VALORANT, Teamfight Tactics — and the shared social, client, and platform services those titles run on. Engineering identity is **player-facing live-game software at global scale**: real-time data paths, backend services, SDKs/APIs for internal game teams, and craft that treats player delight as a product constraint. HQ is Los Angeles; this intern req is the **US Summer Internship Program remote track** (form knockout: 40 hours/week remote), with University Programs matching across products by interest and seat availability. Strong culture / player-empathy screen (`companies.md` B-tier). **This intern is Greenhouse 8222015 / req 6172 Software Engineering Intern - Summer 2027 (Remote) — not a Unity/Unreal gameplay seat, not an onsite-LA full-time conversion pipeline (JD: no visa sponsorship, including conversions).**

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Los Angeles, USA (Greenhouse office). This intern: **remote US**, 40h/week; University Programs also cites a studio week at LA HQ for the remote US program **[directional, riotgames.com/en/student-internships]**

- **Product focus:** Live games + shared platform (social, client, backend, real-time data, internal SDKs/APIs)
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern):** **USD $58.37/hr** (JD: Remote Only). Paid; 401(k); in-game perks; Play Fund
- **Work model:** Paid **12-week** Summer 2027 internship; **full-time / 40 hours/week remote** (required form checkbox). Applications close **November 6th, 2026**. First published **2026-10-01**
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Graduating in the **2028 calendar year**; enrolled in CS / Engineering / Technology; GPA **≥ 2.5**; OOP language (Java, C++, or C#); DS&A; legally authorized to work in the US for the program; **not eligible for visa sponsorship** (including intern conversions). Age 18+ on the form. `education_required`

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** OOP floor is **Java, C++, or C#** (JD examples). Candidate-side C++ in bullets is the honest hit; do **not** invent Java, C#, Unity, or Unreal Engine
- **Domains:** Player-facing live-game features; backend/platform services; real-time data; SDKs/APIs for internal game customers; global-scale operations
- **What wins:** Shipped systems with latency/reliability proof; OOP language demonstrated in bullets not Skills-only; backend/API ownership; CI/CD or testing as flavor; a GitHub of original work. Unity/Unreal is **desired, not required** — absence is not a knockout. Player empathy shows up in the "why Riot" essay and behavioral, not as a fake ranked-ladder line
