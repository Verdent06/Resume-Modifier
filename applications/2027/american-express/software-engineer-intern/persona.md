# Software Engineer Intern — Enterprise Technology Services (Summer 2027) at American Express

## Role Summary

Ten-week hybrid SWE intern on an Enterprise Technology Services scrum team in Sunrise, FL. Interns design, code, test, and improve applications and services; they may land on backend, frontend, cloud, mobile, AI/ML, data, or full-stack product work. JD surface is generic intern SWE: one of Java / JavaScript / Python / C# / Go / Kotlin (or similar), CS fundamentals (DS&A, OOP, debugging), version control, testing, code reviews, Agile, and interest across web/mobile/backend/cloud/API/full-stack. Company identity is finance-adjacent enterprise payments and colleague-facing platforms at Amex scale — not a trading desk, not a consumer-app startup, and not an ML-research seat. AI/ML and AI-powered developer tools are preferred curiosity plus a possible team alignment, not the screen filter.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (Python; TypeScript covering the JavaScript/TypeScript beneficial area). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). Do not invent Java, Go, Kotlin, or C#.
- Shipped full-stack or backend software: REST/APIs or services, a real frontend or client surface, a persistence decision, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12).
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy HackerRank OA.
- Modern practices through use: Git/version control, unit tests or CI, code-review or collaborative delivery, Agile/scrum-shaped shipping with stakeholders.
- Finance-adjacent or enterprise-delivery flavor on that spine: production APIs, regulated/financial data workflows, or stakeholder-scoped contracts — memorable at Amex without replacing the SWE spine.
- AI-powered tools / ML / agentic automation as a plus (preferred + possible team match), never as the lead story for a generic SWE intern req.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies the December 2027–June 2028 window.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-line Java/Go/Kotlin/C# with zero bullet evidence (fabrication smell at a polyglot floor-signal company).
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML lead as if this were an ML-research intern seat.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web/API/cloud/enterprise SWE.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + enterprise-delivery spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
