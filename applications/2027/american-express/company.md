# American Express

American Express is a publicly traded payments and consumer-finance company whose technology org — including Enterprise Technology Services (ETS) — builds the platforms that run card issuing, merchant servicing, risk, and colleague-facing enterprise systems at global scale. ETS partners across the firm on engineering, architecture, infrastructure, 24x7 operations, product management for core enterprise platforms, technology risk and information security, enterprise data governance, digital product and design, and enterprise AI platforms. Campus SWE interns join Agile scrum teams that may land on backend, frontend, cloud, mobile, AI/ML, data, or full-stack product work — finance-adjacent enterprise software, not a trading desk and not a consumer-app startup.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** NYC HQ; this req is **Sunrise, FL** (hybrid). Other US intern sites include Phoenix, Palo Alto, Salt Lake City, Atlanta, Washington, D.C.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public payments network and consumer-finance brand; large campus intern class; respected finance-adjacent tech program, not S-tier eng prestige
- **Product focus:** Enterprise platforms powering Amex growth — payments, servicing, risk, infrastructure, and colleague-facing systems
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer intern):** this JD posts "competitive base salaries" with no number. Third-party cycle guides (Extern, July 2026): undergraduate intern range ~$24–$63/hr; NYC SWE intern analog ~$54.40/hr + ~$5K housing. Sunrise FL is not separately posted — treat NYC as analog only.
- **Work model:** Paid 10-week Summer Internship Program; hybrid (Amex Flex; typically 2–3 days onsite); Sunrise, FL
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently enrolled full-time bachelor's; expected graduation **December 2027–June 2028**. Employment eligibility to work with American Express in the United States is required; company will not pursue visa sponsorship. Applications reviewed rolling; class may fill before any posted close.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Intern JD names Java, JavaScript, Python, C#, Go, Kotlin "or similar"; beneficial areas add TypeScript/React (frontend) and Java/Python/Golang (backend/cloud/AI). Screen tests polyglot proof in whatever the candidate actually ships — do not invent Java/Go/Kotlin/C#.
- **Domains:** Enterprise SWE across APIs, microservices, UIs, mobile, cloud, data, and AI-powered developer tools; payments/servicing/risk platforms; Agile scrum delivery with PMs, QE, architects
- **What wins:** Shipped full-stack or backend software with APIs, tests/code-review culture, and version control through use (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11). Finance-adjacent or enterprise-delivery framing (regulated data, stakeholder-scoped production APIs) is the company differentiator, not a second track. Resume is the binding intern gate; Easy HackerRank follows. AI/ML curiosity is preferred team-alignment flavor, not the screen filter.
