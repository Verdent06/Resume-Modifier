# Software Engineer – 2027 Internship Program (June Start) at Old Mission

## Role Summary

Chicago-onsite SWE intern at a small global proprietary trading firm. Interns sit with software engineers on market initiatives and support **C++ based trading applications and tools** that contribute to daily firm activity — electronic-trading systems, not intern-only sandboxes. The JD surface is generalist high-bar SWE: CS or CE (or similar), GPA ≥ 3.0, junior-or-higher graduating December 2027 or May 2028, comfortable with one or more of C++, Python, or TypeScript, CS fundamentals (data structures, algorithms, systems), and genuine interest in how software supports markets. No finance experience is required. The firm’s identity is Chicago prop/MM electronic trading. This is the SWE intern role, not quantitative trading or research.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (general SWE — the JD requirements literally test programming in C++/Python/TypeScript, CS fundamentals, and shipping software alongside engineers)
- **differentiator:** Chicago prop/MM identity — C++ trading applications and electronic-trading systems (low-latency / systems flavor: memory, containers, OS, concurrency)
- **track_divergence:** false — the differentiator is flavor on a general-SWE spine, not a second track. The screen still reads as general SWE (`reference/recruiting.md` III §11, `reference/resume.md` III §12); the C++ / systems-performance signal is what makes a candidate memorable here, not a re-target to ai-ml, robotics, or infra-only.

The spine is general SWE: ship real, defensible software; strong DS&A/CS fundamentals for a hard OA; breadth across the stack with depth in at least one layer. The differentiator is carried by whatever on-page work demonstrates C++ systems, real-time / low-latency / memory discipline, and production correctness — kept prominent and deep, but the resume does not become an ML-research or FPGA page.

## Team & Bar

Old Mission is S-tier quant/HFT (`reference/companies.md`) — Chicago prop/MM peer of IMC, Optiver, and Virtu, with an OA-gated funnel (~1–2% intern acceptance). The screener is an engineer/recruiter at a ~200–300 person firm where programming excellence and production discipline are floors and intern cohorts are small. Funnel: application → HackerRank OA (CS-fundamentals MCQ ~30 in 60m + 2 med–hard coding) → phone/video tech → Chicago onsite (~2h, low-level C++/systems: containers, memory, OS). Comp is $150k–$200k annualized plus housing and meals (JD). What wins the human screen: C++ proven in real systems work, not Skills-only; Python or TypeScript as supporting polyglot proof; production shipping with metrics; curiosity about markets without fake desk experience. Class-year on page must show Expected May 2028 (inside Dec 2027 or May 2028). GPA ≥ 3.0 is a knockout; 3.66 clears it. Prior finance is not required.

## Screen Criteria

- At least one JD language (C++, Python, or TypeScript) proven in bullets — not Skills-only. Day-to-day work is C++ trading applications; honest C++ + Python depth beats inventing TypeScript-only fluency.
- High-performance / systems depth surfaced on the page: real-time constraints, concurrency, memory discipline, latency/throughput, production correctness — the prop/MM signal that separates this firm from a generic SWE screen.
- End-to-end ownership and production impact with metrics (latency, throughput, %, scale) — soft, metric-free claims read thin at this bar.
- Clean, well-architected systems the candidate can walk decision-by-decision; a live GitHub backing the claims.
- DS&A / CS fundamentals visible (coursework + engineered projects) for a hard OA that also tests systems/OS.
- Markets curiosity (econ, financial-data systems) helps without requiring a trading internship; collaboration with non-engineers is a plus.
- Class-year on page shows a current student graduating December 2027 or May 2028 (Expected May 2028 is in window). GPA on page ≥ 3.0.

## Anti-Patterns

- Re-targeting to an ML-research / LoRA / agent-pipeline lead — this is a general-SWE role on C++ trading applications.
- All-CRUD / all-web-app page with no C++ or systems-performance signal — reads generic and off-differentiator at a Chicago prop shop.
- C++ named in Skills with zero bullet evidence when the intern will write trading applications in C++.
- Soft, metric-free shipping claims where latency/throughput/scale numbers are expected at a performance-obsessed firm.
- Finance-jargon padding with no engineering substance (prior finance is not required, and faking it reads worse than honest strong CS).
- Thin single-bullet filler entries that dilute a strong systems + production spine.
- Treating this as a quantitative-trading or research internship instead of SWE.
