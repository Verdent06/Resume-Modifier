# Old Mission

Old Mission is a global proprietary trading firm that uses in-house technology and research to identify and execute trading strategies across equities, bonds, currencies, and derivatives. The firm does not take outside investor capital; engineers sit with traders and researchers on a small (~200–300 person) book spanning Chicago, New York, and London. This SWE internship is Chicago-only and is scoped to **C++ trading applications and tools** that support electronic trading — day-to-day firm work, not intern-only sandboxes. Old Mission sits in the elite S-tier Chicago prop/MM band alongside IMC, Optiver, and Virtu: early cycle, OA-gated, low-single-digit intern acceptance.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** S-Tier (quant / HFT; `reference/companies.md` — Chicago prop/MM; C++ trading apps/tools; IMC / Optiver / Virtu peer)
- **HQ / offices:** Chicago, IL HQ (1 N Dearborn St). Also New York and London. This SWE internship is Chicago onsite only.
- **Valuation / signal:** Independent global prop/MM (~200–300 people); no outside LP capital; elite Chicago trading resume brand in the IMC / Optiver / Virtu peer band
- **Product focus:** Proprietary electronic-trading systems — C++ trading applications and tools that support market making and execution across multiple asset classes
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern, Chicago):** $150,000–$200,000 annualized base (JD good-faith range) + corporate housing + free breakfast/lunch on-site
- **Work model:** Paid, onsite Chicago; ~10–12 weeks; start June 1, 2027; mentored projects that contribute to daily firm activities; sessions with senior engineers
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Pursuing bachelor’s or master’s in CS, CE, or similar; minimum 3.0 GPA; at least junior standing; graduation December 2027 or May 2028; must start June 1, 2027. No finance experience required. JD does not state a sponsorship policy.

- **Languages:** C++, Python, or TypeScript (JD: comfortable with one or more). Day-to-day intern work is **C++ trading applications**; honest C++ + Python depth beats inventing TypeScript-only fluency.
- **Domains:** Electronic trading systems; C++ applications and tools around the desk; CS fundamentals (data structures, algorithms, systems); collaboration with traders
- **What wins:** C++ proven in real systems work (latency, concurrency, memory discipline, production shipping) — not Skills-only; Python/TypeScript as supporting polyglot proof; genuine markets curiosity without fake desk experience.
