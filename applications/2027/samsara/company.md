# Samsara

Samsara (NYSE: IOT) is the pioneer of the Connected Operations Cloud — an open IoT platform that ingests, aggregates, and enriches real-time data from millions of connected endpoints across trucks, equipment, and worksites, then uses AI to turn it into actionable insights for organizations that run physical operations (transportation, construction, field services, agriculture, manufacturing, utilities). Product solutions span Video-Based Safety, Vehicle Telematics, Apps and Driver Workflows, and Equipment Monitoring. The technical core is a data platform that scales ingestion and storage across 15,000+ hardware asset types and tens of thousands of customers, surfaced through web, mobile, and embedded/edge software. Founded 2015, ~$22B market cap, headquartered at 1 De Haro Street, San Francisco.
