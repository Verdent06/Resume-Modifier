# K2 Space Corporation

K2 Space builds the largest and highest-power satellites ever flown, mass-producing them on a vertically integrated line rather than as one-off spacecraft. The flagship Mega-class bus delivers 20kW+ of power for LEO through deep-space missions, with a 100kW Giga-class platform planned for 2H 2028; multiple launches are slated for 2027 with a stated goal of scaling to 100 satellites per year. Technically, the hard problems are real-time, fault-tolerant flight software for a high-power spacecraft — subsystem control (propulsion, attitude/GNC, thermal, power, comms), state machines for robust on-orbit operation, cyber-physical simulators, and the build/test infrastructure that lets a factory ship quality flight code fast. Backed by over $1B in capital and over $1B in signed commercial and U.S. government contracts, the company sits at the intersection of commercial satellite manufacturing and defense space.

- **Tier:** B
- **HQ / offices:** Torrance, CA (HQ + 180k sq ft factory, Los Angeles area); Seattle engineering office

Rowe Price); >$1B signed contracts including SES (30 satellites) and Golden Dome / Anduril work; ~300 employees; ~85% vertically integrated
- **Product focus:** Mass-produced high-power satellite platforms — Mega-class 20kW+ bus today, Giga-class 100kW planned 2H 2028 — plus the flight software and on-orbit operations that run them
- **Intern comp (2027 software engineering):** $30–$40/hour; housing and travel stipend may be available
- **Work model:** Paid; full-time in person at the Torrance HQ; 3-month term with a 12-week minimum
- **Clearance / eligibility:** ITAR — must be a U.S. Person (citizen, lawful permanent resident, refugee/asylee) or hold an export license; must be currently enrolled in a Bachelor's or Master's program in CS, CE, EE, or a related engineering discipline

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Rust, C/C++, Go (JD requirement — one is a filter); Rust is the company-level stack signal; Python appears in tooling and analysis work
- **Domains:** Spacecraft flight software; real-time and fault-tolerant control; state machines; GNC / attitude control, propulsion, thermal, power, comms subsystems; cyber-physical simulators and HITL/HOTL test systems; Linux systems programming and drivers; CI/CD and build/test infrastructure; anomaly investigation on live on-orbit operations
- **What wins:** A named JD language demonstrated inside bullets — C++ is the honest one here — carrying real-time or resource-constrained reasoning (allocation discipline, threading, latency); evidence of software shipped and depended on outside the classroom (the 6-month out-of-classroom bar is a literal requirement); debugging and anomaly-investigation stories with a before/after number; CI/CD and test/build infrastructure ownership; hardware-adjacent or sensor/actuator-adjacent work over pure web or notebook ML. Aerospace domain experience is not required, but mission-critical discipline must be visible in the top half of the page.
