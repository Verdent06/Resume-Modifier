# Geneva Trading

Geneva Trading is a Chicago principal / proprietary trading firm founded in 1999. It trades listed derivatives (futures and options) and multi-asset products on its own capital over a proprietary platform, pairing traders with technologists. The firm is a member of the FIA Principal Traders Group and sits in the Chicago prop cluster below the IMC / Jane Street prestige band — established, markets-native, and performance-sensitive rather than a consumer-product shop. **Do not mix intern reqs:** this packet is the **AI Engineer Internship – Summer 2027** (Greenhouse **5240107007**, req **2026-73**, AI Engineering) — applied AI tools, agents, and prototypes for *internal* workflows — **not** the C++ Junior SWE (Trading Systems & OS) sibling **5085231007**, and **not** an ML-research seat.
