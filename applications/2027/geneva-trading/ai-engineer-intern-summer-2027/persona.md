- Metrics that size impact (speedup, faithfulness, precision, latency, cost), landed as the closer (`resume.md` §4).
- Live GitHub on an intern engineering resume (`resume.md` §3).

**Anti-patterns:**

- Research-ML lead: publications, novel methods, notebook `model.fit()` with no agent, no eval loop, no delivery (`recruiting.md` §13 research vs applied split). Do not lead as a research-ML intern.
- Claiming Claude Code, Codex, or Grok Build (or TensorFlow-as-used, Kubernetes, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau) without evidence.
- Generic SWE with no agents / no multi-step AI workflows.
- C++-only trading-systems resume with no AI — this is not the Junior SWE (Trading Systems & OS) sibling.
- Keyword stuffing "HFT / market-microstructure" into unrelated bullets.
- Inventing buy-side or Geneva platform experience the page cannot defend.
- Off-axis lead: pure frontend, club-ops, or audio-DSP with no applied-AI spine.
- Agentic/LLM/Gemini/eval claims only in Skills with no supporting system.
- Metric-free architecture bullets the recruiter cannot size.
