# TD Bank

Toronto-Dominion Bank Group (NYSE/TSX: **TD**) is the fifth-largest bank in North America by branches, serving 27+ million households and businesses with ~95,000 colleagues. US retail is TD Bank, N.A. ("America's Most Convenient Bank"). GTS sits at the Mount Laurel, NJ Technology Center (17000 Horizon Way) — generic Software Engineer support and project work for bank technology, not a trading desk and not a data-science rotation. Two SWE packets share this campus: **R_1509773** Spring Co-op (Winter/Term 2) and **R_1510796** Summer Internship (Summer/Term 3). Neither is the Fall 2026 GTS twin (R_1481069). GTS also posts BSA, BMA, Data Engineer, Cloud/DevOps, and Quality Engineer internships on the same campus; each packet is the named SWE seat only.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Toronto HQ (TD Bank Group). This req: **Mt Laurel - Technology Center, 17000 Horizon Way, Mount Laurel, NJ**. Cherry Hill / Fort Lauderdale also US tech.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public Canadian Big Five bank with a large US retail franchise; legitimate GTS intern/co-op program; weaker national SWE brand than JPM / Amex / BNY
- **Product focus:** US retail-bank technology and internal GTS platforms — software engineering support, departmental projects, and applied-AI interest on a generic SWE co-op
- **Intern comp (2027 GTS SWE):** **$30.74/hr** USD (JD band is a single point) on both Spring co-op and Summer intern. Spring **R_1509773** also names a relocation stipend; Summer **R_1510796** JD (captured 2026-09-14) does **not** — do not copy it onto Summer.
- **Work model:** Paid Internship/Co-op, full time, **40 hours/week**, **on-site 4 days/week** in Mount Laurel. Spring: **Winter/Term 2**. Summer: **Summer/Term 3**.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** High school diploma; currently enrolled in a 4-year college; CS or related; **May 2028 graduation preferred**; **3.2 GPA preferred**; no experience required. No visa line on these GTS SWE reqs (do not copy older Platforms & Technology sponsorship language). Spring **R_1509773**: posted **2026-09-12**; apply-by **2026-10-11**. Summer **R_1510796**: posted ~**2026-09-13**; apply-by **2026-10-04**. **Not** Fall 2026 GTS R_1481069.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Java, JavaScript, and/or Jira are **preferred**, not exclusive. Screen for proof in languages the candidate actually has — TypeScript/JavaScript-family (Angular/React) and Python/REST are the honest hits. **Do not invent Java or Jira.**
- **Domains:** Generic GTS intern SWE — departmental projects, bank-IT support, SDLC, plus a stated **must-have interest in AI and Machine Learning** (interest, not an ML-framework/training/inference filter)
- **What wins:** Shipped web/API software with a deploy path; JavaScript-family through use; production or messy-data delivery as bank-IT flavor; applied-AI interest shown on a SWE spine, not as a research-ML lead. Per `reference/recruiting.md` Part III §11, this is general SWE with a resume-first intern front door at a mid-size / non-tech-tech bank. Java/Jira are plus-and-train, not fabrication targets.
