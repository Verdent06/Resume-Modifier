# Dimensional Fund Advisors

Dimensional Fund Advisors is a systematic asset manager (~$1.02T AUM, Form ADV Mar 2026) that implements academic factor research (Fama–French and related dimensions of expected return) in equity and fixed-income portfolios for advisors, institutions, and consultants. The firm is not a hedge fund and not a product-SWE shop: distribution sits in Global Client Group (GCG), whose Operations division runs business insights, technology product management, intermediary relationships, client onboarding, reporting, and admin support. This req (**2026-9003**, Internship in Global Client Group - Data and Tools) is GCG Operations **Business Data and Tools** — CRM-ecosystem accuracy, sales-enablement products, and BI for US sales channels — not a Technology SWE intern and **not** GCG Operations Insights (**2026-9002**).

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md` — systematic AM; resume + philosophy/fit gated; peer to Bloomberg / Interactive Brokers for intern brand, not S-tier trading)
- **HQ / offices:** Austin, TX (HQ). This req is **Austin**, fully in-person. US intern sites also Charlotte, NC and Santa Monica, CA (`careers.dimensional.com/internships`)
- **Valuation / signal:** Independent systematic AM; ~$1.02T AUM (Form ADV Mar 2026); academic factor investing; majority of past interns convert to full-time (`careers.dimensional.com/internships`)
- **Product focus:** Factor-based investment solutions plus GCG sales-ops data tools — internal client-asset / cash-flow / CRM systems that enable a holistic view of global client relationships
- **Intern comp (2027 GCG Data & Tools intern):** unlisted on this JD (paid). SWE intern analog ~$37/hr Austin (Levels.fyi); treat as analog only, not this req
- **Work model:** Paid nine-week Summer 2027 internship; full-time; fully in-person Austin (`#LI-Office`). Program is for students the summer before their final year. Rolling screen mid-August through December
- **Clearance / eligibility:** GPA ≥ 3.2; pursuing undergraduate or specialized Master's; expected graduation **December 2027 – June 2028**. **This req is not eligible for immigration sponsorship** (FAQ on the internships page says Dimensional *may* consider H-1B for some intern roles — do not generalize; 2026-9003 is explicit)

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** This JD names **no programming language**. Named tool is **Excel**; "global CRM system" is generic — do not invent Salesforce, Microsoft Access, Power BI, Tableau, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, or Fusion (those belong to other Dimensional intern reqs or are not in this JD). Python/SQL are candidate evidence of analytical/automation skill when they exist in real work, not JD-required languages.
- **Domains:** GCG Operations Business Data and Tools — client-asset and cash-flow data accuracy, sales-enablement products, data governance/process maps, BI for US sales channels (RIAs, broker dealers, banks, institutions, retirement plans, consultants), data automation. Systematic AM / factor investing is the firm's identity; this seat is sales-ops data tools, not PM/Research.
- **What wins:** Because the bottleneck is the resume plus philosophy/fit (`recruiting.md` intern funnel; B-tier AM), a one-page PDF that shows ingest → clean/aggregate → report or score → measured outcome, Excel through real use if the work has it, stakeholder-facing sales-ops or GTM data delivery, and honest automation of messy operational data. Do not lead as generic SWE or ML research. Club-ops, notebook ML, and invented CRM vendors fail this screen.
