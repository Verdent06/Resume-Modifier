# Blackstone

Blackstone is the world's largest alternative asset manager (~$1.3T AUM per the 2027 SWE Summer Analyst posting), with strategies across real estate, private equity, credit, infrastructure, life sciences, growth equity, secondaries, and hedge funds. Blackstone Technology & Innovations (BXTI) is the internal technology organization: it builds platforms, applications, data systems, and automation that the firm uses to operate at scale, make investment decisions, manage risk, and deliver to LPs and portfolio companies — not a PE/IB coverage seat and not a consumer-product org. BXTI software engineers collaborate with product managers to design, develop, and test internal applications, including scaling infrastructure systems and cloud-based tools. This req is **Software Engineer Summer Analyst (45021, Miami)** — distinct from Data Engineer 45022 and from Data Science Summer Analyst.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** NYC HQ; this intern is **Miami, FL**. Firm also lists Miami among global offices
- **Valuation / signal:** World's largest alt AM (~$1.3T AUM); BXTI is the technology arm powering firm operations — respected finance-tech intern brand, not S-tier eng prestige
- **Product focus:** Internal BXTI platforms — applications, APIs, cloud, data systems, and automation for investment/risk/operations workflows
- **Intern comp (2027 BXTI SWE Summer Analyst):** **$125,000 annualized** (JD expected annual base range $125,000–$125,000). Posted range excludes other compensation and benefits
- **Work model:** Paid full-time Summer Analyst, June 2027 start, Miami onsite. Official students page: summer program typically **~10 weeks**; juniors / third-year university apply for Summer Analyst
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently enrolled undergraduate; anticipated graduation **Fall 2027–Spring 2028**; resume must include **expected graduation month/year and GPA**; PDF resume. Work authorization **not stated on this posting**. Students FAQ: international students may apply; Blackstone may petition for an employment-based visa (country immigration service decides). Do not treat this SWE loop as a paper-LBO IB process
