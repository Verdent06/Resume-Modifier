## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**Python**, **TypeScript**, **SQL**). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). C#, Node.js, Java, Terraform, GitLab, Snowflake are "may work with" — do not invent them.
- Shipped full-stack or backend software: REST/APIs or services, persistence, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12).
- Technology solution development: a problem → architectural decision → shipped artifact, not a course dump. Automation of repetitive workflows is a plus on that spine.
- Cloud and/or AI **through use** as BXTI analogs — production inference, agentic workflows with eval/judgment, containerized pipelines — matching BXTI's AI-enabled/platform thesis without becoming an ML-research resume.
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy–Med HackerRank if invited **[directional]**.
- Class-year and GPA on the page: `Expected May 2028` plus GPA satisfies Fall 2027–Spring 2028 and the explicit GPA/grad-date resume requirement.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Invented C#, Node.js, Terraform, GitLab, or Java (Skills or bullets) — not in the honest inventory; fabrication smell at a polyglot "may work with" shop.
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, or Tableau — banned; Snowflake is JD "may work with" only — omit from bullets/Skills.
- Notebook-ML lead / `model.fit()` as the first-pass identity when the role tests intern SWE coding and solution delivery.
- Club-ops filler occupying prime slots.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web/API/cloud/internal-platform SWE.
- Skills-only Python/cloud/AI with no bullet proof.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + alt-AM-platform spine.
- PE/IB coverage framing or Data Engineer 45022 / Data Science packaging.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
