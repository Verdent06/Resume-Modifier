# Stoke Space

Stoke Space is a Kent, Washington aerospace company building Nova, a fully and rapidly reusable medium-lift launch vehicle designed for aircraft-like flight cadence to drive launch cost down and availability up. Founded in 2019/2020 by former Blue Origin and SpaceX engineers, the company has raised ~$1.34B to date (Series D extended to $860M in Feb 2026) and is targeting Nova's first orbital launch from Launch Complex 14 at Cape Canaveral. For a software intern, the literal screen is general/full-stack SWE, but the company identity is mission-critical, real-time aerospace systems: control software that runs on test stands and flight vehicles, and tooling that hardware-manufacturing teams depend on under operational urgency.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated in `reference/companies.md` — peer signal to SpaceX (B-TIER aerospace); white-hot "new space" brand, real launch program
- **HQ / offices:** Kent, WA (HQ + factory); test site Moses Lake, WA; launch ops at LC-14, Cape Canaveral, FL
- **Valuation / signal:** ~$1.34B raised; reported ~$2B valuation (Sep 2025); on the U.S. Space Force national-security launch provider list
- **Product focus:** Nova fully reusable launch vehicle; Boltline hardware-manufacturing SaaS; real-time vehicle/test control software; launch & post-flight data systems
- **Intern comp (2027 Software):** $28/hr fresh/soph, $33/hr junior/senior, $35/hr completed B.S., $40/hr completed M.S. (from JD)
- **Work model:** Paid, onsite at Kent, WA; Spring 2027 term (Jan–April), minimum 10–12 consecutive weeks full-time
- **Clearance / eligibility:** ITAR — must be U.S. citizen, lawful permanent resident, protected individual (8 U.S.C. 1324b(a)(3)), or eligible for U.S. Dept. of State authorization; background/reference checks

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** General req — Python, C, or Rust. Boltline — JavaScript/TypeScript (React, Next.js, Apollo, Express.js, Node.js, GraphQL, PostgreSQL, AWS). Vehicle Software — Rust / C / C++ (real-time, embedded, OS/kernel). Data — Rust (AWS, time-series, Svelte, WebGPU).
- **Domains:** Reusable launch vehicles, real-time vehicle/test-stand control, embedded/flight systems, hardware-manufacturing SaaS, launch & telemetry data pipelines.
- **What wins:** Project-level command of a real language; ability to take a complex technical project from design to execution and run it on real hardware; engineering-fundamentals depth; shipped, owned systems with operational discipline; and full-stack fluency for the Boltline path. Self-direction, urgency, and "learn on the fly" are explicit culture filters.
