# Summer 2027 IEF ASMI College Associate Intern at Gulfstream Aerospace

## Role Summary

A Summer 2027, 12–14 week, full-time onsite Collegiate Associate internship in Savannah, GA on Gulfstream's Advanced Structures and Materials (ASMI) team. The intern designs, develops, tests, and deploys automated engineering tools that encode design requirements, engineering knowledge, and established process into software used by aircraft structural disciplines — engineering applications, data processing pipelines, engineering-database integration, and (possibly) CAD/CATIA automation. The JD surface is general CS: strong programming in Python, C++, or VBA; experience building automation tools or engineering applications; CAD as a desired plus. The company's identity is a GD business-jet OEM; this seat is a CS tooling intern on ASMI, not an airframe CAD designer.

## Screen Criteria

- JD language demonstrated through use in bullets — Python and/or C++ (VBA is listed but not required if another language is proven) — not Skills-only.
- Shipped automation or engineering applications: tools that capture a process, reduce repetitive work, or integrate data — with impact metrics that size the outcome (hours saved, consistency, throughput, scale), not vanity counts.
- Data processing pipelines and/or engineering-database integration visible as real work (ETL, APIs over stored data, SQL/DAL), matching ASMI's "projects may include" list.
- Object-oriented / software-engineering fundamentals shown in built software, not claimed as coursework alone.
- CAD/CATIA is a desired plus if present through use; absence is not a knockout — do not treat it as a filter the JD did not write.
- Class-year and GPA on the page: current full-time enrollment (`Expected May 2028`) and GPA ≥ 3.0 listed (posting requires it unrounded on the resume).
- Multidisciplinary collaboration / stakeholder delivery reads well for a team that sits with engineers; sole-engineer ownership of a shipped tool is still on-axis.

## Anti-Patterns

- Screening as an ML-research, agentic-AI, or robotics intern because hobbies mention DS/AI/ML or robotics — the requirements test general CS tooling.
- A JD language named only in Skills with zero bullet evidence at a resume-gated OEM.
- Pure web-product or voice-AI framing with no automation, pipeline, or database signal for an engineering-tools seat.
- Club-ops or coursework dump in place of shipped software.
- Treating missing CATIA/SolidWorks as a hard fail, or stuffing CAD keywords with no backing work.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a strong applications + pipelines spine.
