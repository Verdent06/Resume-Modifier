# Gulfstream Aerospace Corporation (General Dynamics)

Gulfstream Aerospace, a wholly-owned subsidiary of General Dynamics (NYSE: GD), designs, develops, manufactures, markets, services, and supports large-cabin business jets from its Savannah, Georgia headquarters. This is the business-jet OEM — not GDIT (the D-tier General Dynamics "Gov IT" row). Advanced Structures and Materials (ASMI) builds software tools that encode design requirements, engineering knowledge, and established process into automation used by structures and materials engineers across the aircraft design lifecycle: engineering applications, data processing pipelines, engineering-database integration, and CAD/CATIA workflows. The Collegiate Associate intern on this req is a CS seat on that tooling team — designing, developing, testing, and deploying automation solutions alongside engineers and software developers — not an airframe CAD designer.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-tier (`reference/companies.md` C-Tier: Gulfstream (General Dynamics); moderate brand, resume-gated, no standard OA)
- **HQ / offices:** Savannah, GA (corporate headquarters; this req is onsite Savannah)
- **Valuation / signal:** GD subsidiary (NYSE: GD); premier large-cabin business-jet OEM; stable aerospace brand, limited SWE prestige vs. tech
- **Product focus:** Large-cabin business jets; ASMI specifically — automated engineering tools (apps, data pipelines, DB integration, CAD/CATIA automation) for aircraft structural disciplines
- **Intern comp (2027 Collegiate Associate / CS tooling):** Competitive pay; no numeric intern rate on the posting. PTO; housing stipend if permanent residence >50 miles from site; medical; 401(k) match; collegiate networking
- **Work model:** Paid Collegiate Associate intern, onsite Savannah (not remote), Summer 2027, 12–14 weeks; First shift; travel up to 25%; reliable transportation required
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Full-time enrollment pursuing bachelor's or higher; cumulative GPA 3.0+ (unrounded) listed on resume; freshman year complete by start; onsite only. Gulfstream does not provide work visa sponsorship unless the applicant is a currently sponsored Gulfstream employee.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python, C++, or VBA (desired; any one is the programming floor). CAD/CATIA is desired, not the job definition.
- **Domains:** Engineering applications, automation tools, data processing pipelines, engineering-database integration, CAD/CATIA workflows supporting aircraft structural design — CS applied to aerospace engineering challenges
- **What wins:** A resume that shows shipped engineering applications or automation (not club-ops), JD languages demonstrated in bullets (Python and/or C++), data-pipeline / database integration through use, and OOP/SWE fundamentals. CAD/CATIA is a plus, not a knockout. With no OA, the resume is the binding filter (`recruiting.md` intern funnel: resume-first when OA is absent). Multidisciplinary collaboration with engineers reads well; notebook-ML or hobby-robotics framing without delivery does not.
