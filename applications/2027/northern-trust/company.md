# Northern Trust

Northern Trust (Nasdaq: NTRS) is a Chicago-based wealth manager, asset servicer, and asset manager — not an IB coverage shop. Public Q2 2026 figures: assets under custody/administration **$20.0T**, assets under custody **$15.9T**, AUM **$1.97T**. Technology Interns are fully integrated into engineering teams that design, develop, deploy, and modernize applications, databases, data pipelines, APIs, microservices, and cloud platforms that run custody, wealth, and asset-management workflows. Four 2027 Chicago Technology Intern tracks — **do not mix packets; North America cap is 3 postings per recruitment season, not one-per-track:** Software Engineering **R160832**, Information Security **R160869**, Infrastructure and IT Management **R160872**, Data Science and Analytics **R160865**.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Chicago HQ (50 S. LaSalle). This intern: Chicago, IL, hybrid
- **Valuation / signal:** Public Nasdaq: NTRS; one of the largest global custodians/wealth platforms by AUC/A; Vault-class financial-services intern brand, not S-tier eng prestige
- **Product focus:** Custody / wealth / asset-servicing platforms and the engineering that modernizes them
- **Intern comp (2027 Technology Intern – Software Engineering):** **$35.00 USD per hour** (JD)
- **Work model:** Paid ten-week internship, Chicago IL, hybrid. Recruiter places onto an application or platform development team. Sample path: Technology Analyst in the Software Engineering track of the Analyst Development Program (ADP)
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Pursuing bachelor's or master's in Computer Science, Information Systems, or related; expected graduation **December 2027 through Summer 2028**; GPA **3.0+**; **no employment-based visa sponsorship** now or in the future (no H-1B, L-1, TN, O-1, E-3, H-1B1, F-1, J-1, OPT, CPT). Posted **2026-09-01**; close copy **October 9, 2026, 11:59pm Central**, rolling, may close when filled. End date on posting: 2026-10-10. NA intern cap: **maximum of 3 job postings per recruitment season**

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages (SWE R160832):** JD examples include Java, Python, SQL, Azure DevOps, and Github. Screen for coding proof in languages the candidate actually has — **Python, SQL, Git/GitHub**. Do not invent Java, Azure DevOps, Azure, Spring, Node, C#, ServiceNow, Power BI, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, or Sentry
- **Domains:** Generic intern SWE on wealth/custody platforms — apps, APIs, microservices, databases, data pipelines, cloud, Agile, CI/CD, TDD. Automation and generative-AI capabilities are "likely involves" flavor across every technology internship, not an ML-research seat and not an InfoSec/Infra/DS packet
- **What wins:** Resume is the front door (`recruiting.md` intern funnel; OA unpublished). Shipped engineering (APIs/services, data pipelines, cloud/deploy, Python+SQL through use) plus finance-adjacent / regulated-data delivery as the company differentiator — not a second track's requirements list
