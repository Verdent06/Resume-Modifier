# Northern Trust

Northern Trust (Nasdaq: NTRS) is a Chicago-founded (1889) wealth manager, asset servicer, asset manager, and bank serving institutions, corporations, and high-net-worth families. As of Q2 2026 it reported $20.0T assets under custody/administration, $15.9T AUC, and $2.0T AUM, with ~24,000 partners and offices in 24 U.S. states plus D.C. and 22 international locations. This intern req is **Technology Intern – Data Science and Analytics** (Workday **R160865**) — applied pipelines, Python-or-R, SQL, and BI exposure on wealth / asset-servicing data — **not** ML research and **not** sister Technology Intern tracks (e.g. Infrastructure and IT Management). North America campus recruiting caps applicants at **3 postings per season**.

- **Languages:** JD: programming languages such as **Python or R**, **SQL**, and/or tools like Power BI or Tableau. That is an OR, not a BI-tool must-have. Honest inventory is Python + SQL through use. Do **not** invent R, Tableau, Power BI, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, or Sentry
- **Domains:** Applied data science and analytics on wealth / asset-servicing / banking data — data pipelines, BI dashboards, statistical modeling, ML/AI *exposure*, data architecture/governance/quality. Automation via coding and some generative-AI use are program-wide technology intern notes, not a research mandate
- **What wins:** Because the bottleneck is the resume plus the 3-app cap (`companies.md`; `recruiting.md` mid-size / non-tech-tech is resume-first), a one-page PDF that shows Python + SQL through use, messy data → pipeline/ETL → served insight, applied analytics (not papers), GPA on the page, and Expected May 2028 in the graduation window. Do not lead as generic SWE, ML-research intern, or a Tableau/Power BI claim that cannot be defended
