## Screen Criteria

- **C++ through use** in bullets — lock-free / atomics / memory-pool / hot-path discipline — not a Languages-line claim (`resume.md` §2 keywords-through-use). This is the JD floor.
- Shipped application or service delivery (API, ETL, production debug, tests) so the full-stack spine is obvious: this intern still designs, implements, and tests a complete project.
- Testing / reliability evidence (unit/integration analog, defect diagnosis, release checklist) matching "rigorous testing" and "zero regressions."
- Metrics that size outcomes (latency, failure recovery, scale, hours saved) rather than vanity counts (`resume.md` §4).
- Class-year on page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies "in or after December 2027."
- Anti-patterns: Skills-only C++; invented FlashArray/Purity/Rust/Java/Go/Linux internships; notebook ML or LoRA as the lead; club-ops or GTM/deal-flow filler; a PE/search-fund or voice-AI product story that buries concurrency and C++; claiming OS/compiler/storage-engine internships the page cannot defend.
