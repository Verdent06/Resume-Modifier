# Space Dynamics Laboratory

Space Dynamics Laboratory (SDL) is a nonprofit **University Affiliated Research Center (UARC)** owned by Utah State University and sponsored by the Office of the Assistant Secretary of the Air Force for Space Acquisition and Integration (SAF/SQ). Founded **1959** (Electro-Dynamics Laboratories / Upper Air Research Laboratory lineage). HQ is on USU’s Innovation Campus in **North Logan, Utah**, with field offices including Albuquerque NM, Huntsville AL, Ogden UT, Stafford VA, and Chantilly VA. SDL builds satellites, sensors and instruments, ground systems and data processing, and autonomous systems for DoD / Intelligence Community and NASA missions. This packet is **Software Engineer Intern - Software, AI, & Machine Learning (Job 9712)** on the Strategic Military Space (SMS) Division only — not Information Security Intern **9440**, not USU year-round Student Employment (USU students are **ineligible** for SDL internships).
