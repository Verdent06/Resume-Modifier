# CACI

CACI International Inc (NYSE: CACI) is a Reston, VA federal technology and professional-services contractor focused on U.S. national security. Fiscal 2026 revenue was reported around $9.6B with a large funded backlog; the company sells engineering, intelligence, cyber, and IT on long-duration government contracts. In March 2026 CACI closed the acquisition of ARKA Group, which added space sensors and geospatial/radar heritage. This intern sits in **CACI’s Radar and Sensor Technologies (RST) Portfolio** in Ypsilanti, MI — the Willow Run / former ERIM–IST–ARKA shop that writes software for synthetic aperture radar, low observables, electro-optical techniques, and related remote sensing, including space-emerging work. It is not a generic CACI IT intern in Reston or the Sarasota software intern (separate req).
