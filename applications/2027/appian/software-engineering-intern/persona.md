- Research-only LoRA / notebook ML as the lead story when the req is SWE intern on a process-automation platform.
- AI-tool-only claims (prompting, Copilot) with no evidence of *building* AI-powered capabilities.
- Burying agentic/eval/RAG work under generic ETL or club-ops so the basic AI-platform qual never registers in 7 seconds.
- Frontend-only or data-cleaning framing with no API/deploy/debug depth.
- Invented Java, SAIL, or Appian product experience.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a strong full-stack + AI-platform spine.
- Languages or frameworks listed in Skills with zero bullet evidence.
