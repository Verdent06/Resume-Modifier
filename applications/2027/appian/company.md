# Appian

Appian (Nasdaq: APPN) sells an AI-powered Process Automation platform that automates complex workflows for large enterprises and governments. Engineering spans foundational systems that power enterprise-scale performance, AI capabilities that redefine automation, and the developer tooling that makes the platform usable. The intern program is McLean HQ, 5 days/week, production-adjacent work on that platform — not a research rotation and not a remote summer.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`, next to ServiceNow)
- **HQ / offices:** McLean, VA (this intern req: HQ onsite 5 days/week)
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (Nasdaq: APPN); 25+ years automating enterprise/government processes; low-code + AI process-automation identity
- **Product focus:** AI-powered Process Automation platform (enterprise workflow / low-code automation)
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineering Intern):** $50.00/hr ($8,666/month)
- **Work model:** Paid; full-time 40 hrs/week; 10-week summer; onsite McLean 5 days/week
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Students only; pursuing BS/MS in CS or Computer Engineering; must return to school after Summer 2027. Will not sponsor employment authorization.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD does not name a language floor. Platform engineering is historically Java/SAIL plus web; intern screen tests DS&A, OOP, full-stack components, debug, and code review — not Java-on-the-page as a knockout. Do not invent Java/Appian-platform experience on the resume.
- **Domains:** Full-stack product engineering, AI-powered platform capabilities (LLMs as product, not only as a coding assistant), process/workflow automation, developer tooling, production-adjacent debug and reviews
- **What wins:** A shippable full-stack spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) **and** visible evidence of *building* AI-powered capabilities (agentic pipelines, eval, RAG/LLM product work) because that pair is a basic qualification, not a preferred extra. AI-tool-only claims without platform-building evidence miss the bar. Resume is the binding intern gate (`companies.md`: bottleneck resume); coding exercise / Super Day behavioral follow.
