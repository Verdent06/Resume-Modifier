# USAA

USAA (United Services Automobile Association; legal hiring org on this Workday posting: **2002 United Services Automobile Asn**) is a Fortune 500 insurance and banking group whose mission is financial security for the U.S. military community and their families. Members get P&C insurance, banking, and investment products; employment does not require military service. Engineering here is regulated fintech-adjacent member platforms — not a generic AI lab and not a defense-tech shop.
