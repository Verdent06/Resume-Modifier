# Gemini

**Do not mix with Google Gemini (the LLM).** This is Gemini Space Station, Inc. (NASDAQ: **GEMI**) — the Winklevoss crypto/Web3 exchange founded in 2014. Gemini is NYDFS-licensed and offers a regulated crypto exchange plus custody to individuals and institutions in 70+ countries: buy/sell/store digital assets, bridging traditional finance with the cryptoeconomy. Engineering is the core of a bank-licensed company run like a tech startup; the intern JD names custody security, fraud, API latency, and frontend and/or backend product work — not protocol/Solidity research and not an ML intern seat.

This intern is Greenhouse **8214272** / req **1214** **Software Engineering Intern (Winter 2027)**.

- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern):** **$50/hour** in NY/CA/WA. Housing stipend for eligible relocators (`gemini.com/early-careers`).
- **Work model:** Paid **semester-long** Winter 2027 internship **Jan–Apr 2027**, **3 days/week in person** at NYC. Early-careers: three programs/year (two 16-week school-year sessions + one 12-week summer).
