Interns own meaningful projects on small fast teams, take code review (correctness, performance, security), refactor for reliability/performance/maintainability, and help diagnose production alerts/bugs. Frontend and/or backend depending on intern strengths. JD names **no programming languages**. ## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Shipped full-stack or backend software: a real frontend or API surface, a persistence decision, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12). Tests, named debug, or code-review discipline matches "high-quality code" plus production alerts/bugs.
- Keyword-through-use for frontend, backend, API, production, testing, security, reliability, performance — not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). JD languages are unnamed; do **not** invent Solidity, Go, Rust, or Kubernetes to look crypto-native.
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy–Med OA (the post-screen equalizer).
- End-to-end ownership of a scoped project (the intern's semester shape): problem → implementation → launch, not club-ops.
- Finance-adjacent or high-reliability production flavor on that spine: APIs, data integrity, regulated or financial workflows, latency/cost/security defects with a before/after — memorable here without replacing the SWE spine.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` plus currently enrolled CS satisfies the form knockout and the open JD.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Invented Solidity, Go, Rust, Kubernetes, Gemini employment, or crypto-protocol/blockchain engineering that is not on the page.
- Leaving the token **Gemini** (the LLM) in bullets — collides with the employer and reads as prior employment or stuffing.
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML as the first-pass identity because the company name is Gemini.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the lead story when the role tests frontend/backend product SWE on an exchange.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a production full-stack spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
- Skills-line technologies with zero bullet evidence where the recruiter would treat them as floor signals.
- Awards section or extracurricular ambassador titles that are not engineering evidence.
