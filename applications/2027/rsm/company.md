# RSM US LLP

RSM US LLP is the leading US middle-market assurance, tax, and consulting firm — 5th-largest US accounting firm (Accounting Today), not Big Four. HQ Chicago (30 S Wacker); this intern sits in Dallas (13155 Noel Road). The firm’s Data & Digital Solutions practice delivers enterprise data integration, migration, and modernization into client ERP/CRM/HR and other systems. This req (Workday **JR116784**) is an **Enterprise Data Integration and Migration (EDIM) Consulting Intern — Summer 2027**: client-facing ETL, data quality, migration (extract/map/transform/load), reporting, and automation — **not** Audit/Tax and **not** Pathways-gated IT Audit consulting. Work quality and SWE-vs-staffing mix vary by engagement the same way they do at Deloitte/Accenture Tech (`reference/companies.md` C-tier caveat).

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Preferred (not hard gates): SQL, Python, or other data-related technologies. Screen on languages the candidate actually has — Python and SQL through use. Do not invent R, Java, or ERP scripting stacks.
- **Domains:** Client-facing EDIM delivery: data integration and migration; analyze/validate/cleanse/transform; extract–map–transform–load between systems; data quality (accuracy, completeness, consistency); exposure to ERP, CRM, HR, and other enterprise systems; analytics, reporting, and automation. Preferred pluses: databases, systems integration, viz/reporting tools, data structures/cloud, ERP/CRM/business apps, emerging tech/AI/digital transformation, leadership, client communication.
- **What wins:** Resume-first C-tier consulting screen (`recruiting.md` intern: eligibility knockouts, then the PDF — OA is not the binding filter here). Evidence of ingest → transform/migrate → validate quality → serve/report/automate with a metric; Python/SQL in bullets not Skills-only; client- or stakeholder-facing delivery. Do not lead as generic SWE product intern, notebook ML, Audit/Tax, or Pathways IT Audit. Do not invent Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, Fusion, Sentry, Informatica, Power BI, D365, or ERP/CRM/HRIS claims.
