# RSM US LLP

RSM US LLP is the US member of RSM International and positions itself as the leading audit, tax, and consulting provider to the **middle market** (Chicago HQ; ~11,000 people in the US and Canada; network in ~120 countries). This intern sits in **Data & Digital Solutions — Application Development**: client-facing custom software, data integrations, and ERP/CRM customizations for mid-market companies — not a product-engineering seat at a software company, and **not** the sibling **Enterprise Data Integration and Migration Consulting Intern (JR116784)**. Work quality varies by engagement the same way it does at Accenture / Deloitte Tech (`reference/companies.md` C-tier caveat).

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-TIER (`reference/companies.md` — RSM US LLP; same consulting caveats as Accenture / Deloitte Tech)
- **HQ / offices:** Chicago, IL HQ. This req: **Des Moines** (USA-IA-Des Moines-400 Locust Street, Suite 640) plus **Chicago**, **Minneapolis**, **Denver Downtown**. JD asks for a **number one location preference** in the application.
- **Valuation / signal:** 5th-largest US accounting/consulting firm; middle-market brand, not Big Four and not software-product prestige.
- **Product focus:** Data & Digital Solutions Application Development — design/document data integrations; test with client users and client engineering; ERP/CRM customizations; coding technique and data-integrity / cleanup work.
- **Intern comp (2027 Application Development):** Unlisted on this req (Workday **Compensation Range: $0 – $0**). Sibling RSM intern JDs publish **$28–$41/hr** depending on practice/city **[directional]**.
- **Work model:** Paid Full time Intern (Fixed Term / Seasonal); campus hire; **travel to assigned client locations required**; reliable transportation required. Term is Summer 2027 (typical RSM intern 8–12 weeks; this JD does not name dates).
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Pursuing a bachelor's in Computer Science, MIS, or related IT field. GPA **3.0 preferred**. **No sponsorship now or in the future** (explicitly includes F-1). Travel + reliable transport. This is **not** JR116784.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Preferred examples: VB.net, C#, ASP.NET, JavaScript. Screen on languages the candidate actually has (Python, TypeScript, SQL) — do not invent C# / VB.net / ASP.NET.
- **Domains:** Client-facing application development, data integrations, database design, ERP/CRM customization (Microsoft CRM SDK / Dynamics-adjacent shop on preferred quals), SSRS, SharePoint/MOSS, Agile. Analog is shipped APIs + ETL + stakeholder-scoped delivery, not Microsoft-stack stuffing.
- **What wins:** Resume-first C-tier consulting screen (`recruiting.md` intern: knockouts, then the PDF). Evidence of ingest → integrate → serve an application with a metric; SQL/database design through use; client- or stakeholder-facing delivery; honest coding in inventory languages. Preferred Microsoft CRM/ERP tools are training-on-the-job, not fabrication targets.
