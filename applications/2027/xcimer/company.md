# Xcimer Energy

Xcimer Energy is a Denver laser inertial fusion energy (IFE) startup founded in 2022 by MIT roommates Conner Galloway (CEO/CSO) and Alexander Valys. It pairs decades of IFE research with a new laser architecture: the Phoenix prototype (Long Pulse Kinetics Platform — first private-sector e-beam-pumped excimer laser, 2025), a Vulcan ~12 MJ plant target around 2030, and grid-scale fusion power in the mid-2030s. The company is building hardware and software for commercial fusion plants, not a generic SaaS intern class. This posting is the **Computational and Software Engineering (simulations)** intern track inside a shared intern template that also lists mechanical, electrical, physics, and manufacturing seats.
