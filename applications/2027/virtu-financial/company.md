# Virtu Financial

Virtu is an electronic market maker and financial-technology firm founded in 2008. It runs proprietary trading and client-facing businesses on an in-house platform: the firm trades in 35+ countries across 235+ venues and services over 25% of US retail order flow. Engineers design, develop, and optimize proprietary low-latency trading systems, algorithms, and trading tools — the stack is built in-house, not bolted onto a vendor OMS. Culture is described as a flat mix of startup pace and disciplined financial-services process. Virtu sits in the elite S-tier quant/HFT band alongside IMC, Optiver, and HRT: early cycle, OA-gated, low-single-digit intern acceptance.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** S-Tier (quant / HFT; `reference/companies.md` — electronic market-making; proprietary low-latency C++/Python/Java trading systems)
- **HQ / offices:** New York, NY (HQ) and Austin, TX. This SWE internship is NYC or Austin only (not Chicago). Training week in New York or Austin.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public electronic market maker; in-house proprietary platform; 235+ venues; elite resume brand in the IMC / Optiver / HRT peer band
- **Product focus:** Proprietary low-latency trading systems and algorithms, plus trading tools that support a high-performance system deployed in marketplaces worldwide
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern):** $5,000–$5,800 weekly (JD; exclusive of sign-on, housing, meals, benefits). Levels.fyi directional: SWE intern ~$125/hr NYC (2026) + ~$20k sign-on + housing
- **Work model:** Paid, onsite 10-week intern; June 7 2027 – August 13 2027 (flexibility if needed); company-curated global training week; real development projects with senior mentors
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Rising juniors, or students ready for full-time employment between December 2027 and June 2028. Excellent CS/EE (or related). US work authorization — sponsorship question is on the Greenhouse form.

## Interview Process

| Stage | Format | Notes |
| ----- | ------ | ----- |
| Resume screen | Human + ATS (Greenhouse) | First filter; class-year and Python + (Java or C++) are knockout-shaped |
| OA | HackerRank, 5 questions / 75 min, Easy–Med | Arrays, loops, algorithms; time pressure and edge cases; emailed after resume pass |
| HR phone | Motivational + brainteaser | Why Virtu / why SWE; one quantitative brainteaser |
| Technical | 2–3 rounds | Project deep-dive, probability, low-latency / systems questions |

**Estimated funnel:** 4–5 stages · Easy–Med OA then Med systems/probability loop · HackerRank · Light intern sys design (low-latency, not distributed) · Bottleneck: resume + OA · ~1–2% acceptance (`reference/companies.md` S-tier; IMC / Optiver / HRT peer band)

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python **and** (Java or C++) required; JavaScript a plus; lower-level programming a plus. Honest C++ + Python depth beats inventing Java.
- **Domains:** Proprietary low-latency trading systems; scalability, performance, and efficiency; architecting for scale and massive data flow; frontend/backend variety across a polyglot shop
- **What wins:** C++ and Python proven in real systems work (latency, concurrency, memory discipline, production shipping) — not Skills-only; frontend/backend range without becoming an ML-research page; quantitative problem-solving visible for the OA and brainteaser loop. Prior trading-desk experience is not required. Per `reference/recruiting.md` Part I §1 the OA is the binding gate after the resume; Part III §11 this is general SWE at an HFT firm, not QR/trading.
