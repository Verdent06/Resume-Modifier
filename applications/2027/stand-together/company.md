# Stand Together

Stand Together Fellowships runs the Koch Internship Program (KIP): paid professional development plus a work placement at a nonprofit partner (national think tanks or state policy organizations). Programming covers individual liberty, limited government, free markets, and Principle Based Management; partners do the day-to-day intern work. This packet is the **Illinois Policy Institute Analytics Intern** seat — a Chicago-based 501(c)(3) state think tank (policy research and taxpayer-facing analysis; 300 S. Riverside Plaza, Suite 1650) — not a generic KIP policy intern and not a software-engineering intern.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C
- **HQ / offices:** Stand Together Fellowships — Arlington, VA (KIP summit). Partner for this req: Illinois Policy Institute, Chicago, IL (300 S. Riverside Plaza, Suite 1650)
- **Valuation / signal:** Philanthropic network (Charles Koch / Stand Together). IPI is a State Policy Network member; 501(c)(3) research + 501(c)(4) Illinois Policy advocacy arm. Combined ~$12M annually (IPI 2025 annual report)
- **Product focus:** Policy research / analytics at a state think tank — messy public data → insight → stakeholder report, not a product-SWE rotation
- **Intern comp (2027 KIP Analytics intern):** $7,500 stipend full-time (~32h/wk at partner) or $5,500 part-time (~24h/wk). Term stipend, not hourly
- **Work model:** Paid. Lever: **Hybrid** / Chicago. Spring 2027 program **January 28 – April 8, 2027**. Thursday 1–4pm ET online (six dates). On-site summit **February 4–5, 2027** Arlington, VA (travel Feb 3 & 6). Both full- and part-time must attend all KIP programming
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Geographically located in the US for the program; authorized to work in the US; SSN required if admitted (admin/payroll). Early-career including undergraduate/graduate/law and recent graduates. Rolling admissions through December 2026; must apply **and** accept a partner offer by December

## Interview Process

| Stage | Format | Notes |
| ----- | ------ | ----- |
| Resume screen | Lever ATS + human (STF staff) | Apply to the specific partner role. JD: interviews with both the partner and Stand Together Fellowships |
| Short-answer email | Written | Official process: after apply, STF emails short-answer questions (issues you care about; nonprofit work that fits your goals). Not on the public Lever form |
| STF matching call | Phone | Verify application, match openings, community fit |
| Partner interview | IPI-specific | Every partner sets its own loop. Analytics seat: Python/data walk + stakeholder reporting, not LC |
| Partner offer | Contingent | Placement at IPI is required for KIP participation |
| STF admissions interview | Final | Official last step after a partner offer. HireVue reported on some KIP loops **[directional]** |

**Estimated funnel:** 4–5 stages · no standard LC OA · Lever · no intern sys design · Bottleneck: resume + mission/behavioral fit + Chicago hybrid vs remote · ~15–25% **[directional, C-tier]**

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python or R (this JD). Do not invent R
- **Domains:** Messy/unstructured public or policy data, ETL, analysis, visualization, APIs/web scraping, AWS familiarity, reproducible code, stakeholder reports
- **What wins:** Python through use on messy data → ranking/KPI/report a non-builder used; honest viz analog (do not invent matplotlib/seaborn/plotly/ggplot2); APIs/scraping and AWS only if real; dual CS + Economics is on-axis for policy analytics. Mission fluency for KIP (liberty / limited government) is a later behavioral filter, not a resume keyword dump
