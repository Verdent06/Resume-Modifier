# Software Engineer Intern (Winter 2027) at Together AI

## Role Summary

Paid on-site SWE intern at Together AI HQ (San Francisco), Winter 2027 (January 4–April 9; 12–14 weeks). Placement is into Platform Engineering, Infrastructure, SRE, or Inference. Interns design scalable systems, ship product features, and optimize performance-critical code — end-to-end from scope into production — across the stack from low-level systems to customer-facing UIs. JD surface is generic full-stack SWE (Python, Go, or TypeScript; web, databases, distributed systems, cloud as bonuses). Company identity is the AI Native Cloud: inference that scales, fine-tuning/RL, pre-training, and an open-model marketplace at 400+T tokens/month.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (generic SWE requirements — Python/Go/TypeScript; shippable product; web, databases, distributed systems, cloud)
- **differentiator:** ml-infra / inference-cloud (GPU serving, inference platform, model infrastructure, systems-performance)
- **track_divergence:** true

The screen spine follows `resume.md` Part III §12 and `recruiting.md` Part III §11 — shippable product, APIs, databases, cloud deploy, CI/CD proof in bullets, breadth plus depth in at least one layer. The differentiator follows `resume.md` Part III §14 and `recruiting.md` Part III §13 — end-to-end ML workflow, model adaptation/serving, inference latency/cost, data pipelines feeding models; MLOps is a bonus at intern level, not a fake production-SRE claim. When divergence is true, lead with the full-stack spine **and** keep ml-infra / inference-adjacent / systems-performance carriers prominent and deep in the top half — do not pivot the entire page to research-only ML or to notebook `model.fit()`.

## Team & Bar

Together AI is A-tier in `companies.md` (~2–4% directional acceptance; Scale AI / Perplexity peer). Funnel: Greenhouse resume → recruiter (~30m) → tech screen (~60m LC medium or applied ML-systems coding) → 2–4 team rounds; OA for some roles **[directional]**. Bottleneck is resume + tech. Recruiter voice: interns who already ship production software, can own a feature end-to-end, and show curiosity about inference/platform systems without reading as a CUDA kernel researcher or a frontend-only product intern. Comp is $58/hr + housing stipend. Eligibility is a hard gate: degree earned or expected by Summer 2028; this intern is on-site SF for January–April. Greenhouse stores file + parsed profile (`resume.md` Part I §2) — keyword-through-use matters more than a stuffed Skills line. For Greenhouse/startup-class pipelines a human reads the PDF early (`recruiting.md` Part I §1 / §5), so the resume carries more front-end weight than at OA-gated big tech.

## Screen Criteria

- Full-stack shipping with APIs, data stores, and a deploy path demonstrated in bullets — not Skills-only (`resume.md` Part III §12).
- JD languages shown in bullets, not only the Skills line. Python or TypeScript (or Go) is the floor; listing a language with zero bullet evidence is a ding at a company that names languages as a qualification.
- Performance-critical or systems-depth evidence a screen can size (latency, throughput, cost, reliability) — JD names optimize performance-critical code and low-level-to-UI range.
- ML-infra / inference-adjacent depth beyond API wrappers: on-device or served inference, fine-tuning/eval, vector search, orchestrated pipelines, serving latency/cost — the company differentiator must register in the first pass, not only in a buried project.
- End-to-end ownership from scope to production (internships, hackathons, or projects — JD lists all three as prior-experience paths).
- Cloud, databases, web, or distributed-systems signals where present — bonuses, not fake Kubernetes/CUDA claims.
- Class-year on page must show current enrollment with `Expected May 2028` (`resume.md` Part I §2) — satisfies degree-by-Summer-2028.

## Anti-Patterns

- Screening as ML research / GPU-kernel PhD track when the req is SWE intern — lead engineering delivery, not paper abstracts or invented CUDA/vLLM.
- Agentic buzzwords without pipeline architecture, eval, or production metrics.
- Frontend-only or club-ops framing with no backend/API/data/performance depth.
- Skills listing Go (or any JD language) with zero bullet evidence — do not invent Go.
- Notebook-only `model.fit()` with no serving, eval, or delivery.
- Thin single-bullet filler entries that dilute a strong production + inference/systems spine.
- Languages or frameworks listed in Skills with zero bullet evidence.
