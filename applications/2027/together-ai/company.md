# Together AI

Together AI is a San Francisco AI-infrastructure company, founded in 2022 (CEO Vipul Ved Prakash; CTO Ce Zhang), that sells an AI Native Cloud for open-weight models: high-performance inference, fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, large-scale pre-training, and a marketplace of models that teams can run, adapt, and own. After an $800 million Series C in July 2026 led by Aramco Ventures (NVIDIA, Vista Equity, General Catalyst, and Salesforce Ventures also in the round), it is valued at $8.3 billion; named customers include Cursor, Cognition, Decagon, ElevenLabs, Salesforce, and Zoom. Research signature is FlashAttention from chief scientist Tri Dao; the product is GPU cloud and serving infrastructure, not a closed frontier lab. The Summer 2027 Software Engineer Intern (Greenhouse 5232036007) is a 12-week on-site HQ rotation across Platform Engineering, Infrastructure, or Inference at $58/hr plus a housing stipend — a generic SWE seat, not a research-paper intern.
