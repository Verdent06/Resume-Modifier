# Together AI

Together AI is an AI Native Cloud: high-performance inference that scales, fine-tuning and RL for specialized models, pre-training at massive scale, and a marketplace of leading open models. The product is GPU serving and model infrastructure that other AI companies run on — customers include Cursor, Decagon, ElevenLabs, Salesforce, and Zoom, at 400+ trillion tokens a month. HQ is San Francisco; this intern sits on-site and is placed into Platform Engineering, Infrastructure, SRE, or Inference.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A (`reference/companies.md` — Prestigious / Highly Selective)
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco, CA (this intern: on-site HQ); additional AI-infra hiring in Amsterdam, London, Bangalore
- **Valuation / signal:** $8.3B Series C July 2026 ($800M, Aramco Ventures + NVIDIA); 400+T tokens/month; inference-cloud peer to Scale AI / Perplexity
- **Product focus:** Inference / fine-tune / pre-train platform for open models (Together Cloud)
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern, Winter):** $58/hr + housing stipends + competitive benefits (JD)
- **Work model:** Paid; on-site San Francisco HQ; Winter cohort January 4–April 9 2027 (12–14 weeks)
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Degree in CS / SWE / related earned or expected by Summer 2028; legally authorized to work in the US (this posting does not list a sponsorship bar)
