# SeatGeek

SeatGeek is a New York live-events ticketing company: a fan-facing marketplace plus primary ticketing software for teams, venues, and shows. Founded in 2009 (Jack Groetzinger and Russ D'Souza). The product thesis is an open entertainment ecosystem — fans get verified tickets with a Deal Score on seats; teams and venues get a primary-ticketing stack (historically including Rally event-day experience and SeatGeek Swaps). Named partners have included the Dallas Cowboys, Brooklyn Nets, MLS, roughly half of the English Premier League, and Broadway houses. This intern req is **Analytics / Data Analyst intern**, not a software-engineer new-grad seat.
