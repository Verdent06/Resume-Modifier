# Aquatic Capital Management

Aquatic is a Chicago-based systematic hedge fund founded in 2018 by Jonathan Graham, a former Citadel partner who led its Global Quantitative Strategies business. The firm runs medium-frequency, machine-learning-driven trading strategies across global equities and other asset classes, pairing a high-performance research and development platform with disciplined risk management. With roughly $5.4B AUM (early 2026) and a fast-growing engineering org across Chicago, NYC, and London, Aquatic is still an early-stage growth shop — the engineering job is to build the distributed, low-latency systems that power research and live trading, not to ship customer-facing product.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated in `reference/companies.md`; functionally an S-tier quant peer to Citadel, Two Sigma, IMC, and HRT (Chicago quant cluster).
- **HQ / offices:** Chicago (225 W Randolph St); also NYC and London.
- **Valuation / signal:** ~$5.4B discretionary AUM (Mar 2026); ex-Citadel pedigree; ~100+ employees and hiring hard.
- **Product focus:** High-performance distributed systems for research and trading infrastructure — low-latency, high-throughput data processing.
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** Not listed; application asks for annualized total comp expectations. FT quant SWE listings advertise ~$150k–$300k; intern pay is S-tier quant scale (~$80–120+/hr peer band).
- **Work model:** Onsite, Chicago; Summer 2027 internship.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Active student in a quantitative discipline (math, stats, ML, physics, CS) with expected graduation Fall 2027–Spring 2028.

**Estimated funnel:** 4–5 rounds · Hard (quant bar) · HackerRank-style OA · sys/perf design likely · Bottleneck: tech screen + OA · acceptance estimate <1–2% (S-tier quant peer band)

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python and C++ are the named core; strong algorithms, data structures, and computer-architecture fundamentals.
- **Domains:** Distributed systems, low-latency / high-throughput data processing, research and trading infrastructure, reliability, performance optimization; ML-driven medium-frequency strategies sit above the platform.
- **What wins:** Demonstrated systems depth — concurrency, async orchestration, real-time/low-latency delivery, queueing and at-least-once delivery, performance/latency wins shown with numbers — plus genuine C++ surface and a curiosity-driven "how things work" engineering identity. Refactoring/TDD discipline and shipped, reliable systems beat keyword-matched full-stack breadth.
