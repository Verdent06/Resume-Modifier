# DoorDash

DoorDash (NASDAQ: DASH) is a technology and logistics company whose core product is a 24x7 on-demand logistics engine for a three-sided marketplace of consumers, merchants, and Dashers. The intern SWE seat is product engineering: user-facing flows and scalable backend architectures on that marketplace, not an ML-research rotation. HQ is San Francisco. `reference/companies.md` A-TIER Notes: logistics; vague problem statements are intentional; brute force is penalized.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-TIER — Excellent / Strong Signal (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco, CA (HQ). This intern: in-person at **New York, NY; San Francisco, CA; Sunnyvale, CA; Los Angeles, CA; Seattle, WA**. Applicant picks preferred location(s) in the form.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public logistics / local-commerce marketplace. Leading US food-delivery platform; intern class is selective (`companies.md`). Extern cites ~1–3% SWE intern acceptance **[directional]**; company row says Selective.

- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer Intern, US):** JD national base pay range **$107,400–$158,000 USD** (localized by work location). Extern cites ~$55/hr plus housing stipend **[directional]**, not a JD number.
- **Work model:** Paid intern, **in-person**, **12 weeks**, Summer 2027 only. **May or June 2027 start.** Hosted at a DoorDash office in NY / SF / Sunnyvale / LA / Seattle.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Pursuing B.S. or M.S. in CS or equivalent; **no more than 2 years full-time**; graduating **Fall 2027–Summer 2028**; currently enrolled; May/June 2027 start. **Must be authorized to work in the U.S.** F-1 CPT OK; **no J-1**. Knockout questions also cover sponsorship now/future, prior DoorDash employment, GPA band, location prefs, and engineering-org prefs. ## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD examples Python, Java, Kotlin — "at least 1 OOP language." Screen tests the language the candidate actually ships (**Python**). Do **not** invent Java, Kotlin, Go, Kafka, Copilot, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, or Fusion CAD.
- **Domains:** Product engineering on a three-sided logistics marketplace — consumer/merchant/Dasher surfaces, APIs, databases, reliability at scale, real-time matching/dispatch-shaped problems. Team-match orgs on the form include Marketplace, Dasher & Logistics, Merchant & Consumer Platform, DashOS, Foundations, Platform Services, and others.
- **What wins:** A full-stack intern spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) with Python (and other inventory languages) shown in bullets, a database + AWS/SQL through use, version control and unit testing, plus real-time / reliability / API evidence so the page is memorable at a logistics company. After the PDF, HackerRank then tech onsite are the binding filters (`companies.md`). Apply in this first-wave window.
