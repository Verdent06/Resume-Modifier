## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Full-stack shipping with a frontend, an API/backend, and a datastore or deploy/test path demonstrated in bullets — not Skills-only (`resume.md` §12). Breadth across the stack plus depth in at least one layer.
- At least one JD-listed language the candidate actually has (Python, TypeScript) used inside architectural decisions, not asserted on a Skills line. React through use is a plus (helpful, not required). Do not treat Copilot / Claude / Cursor as must-have inventory.
- Evidence of shipping to users or stakeholders (product, latency, scale, adoption, production ownership) over club-ops or GTM-only lead stories.
- Hands-on practice that reads as real systems work (projects, internships, shipped code) — the JD names practice as the potential signal.
- Technical collaboration / communication visible in how work is described (mentor-scoped intern; ceremonies; bugs and tradeoffs; final presentation).
- Class-year on page shows current enrollment (`Expected May 2028` matches Fall 2027 / Spring 2028; eligible).
- Fast iterate / own-a-scoped-production-project energy: end-to-end slices with before-after numbers beat feature laundry lists.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-list Python/TypeScript with zero bullet evidence at a shop where one general-purpose language in use is the floor.
- Routing the page to research-only LoRA / notebook ML as if this were an `ai-ml` intern req because the JD names AI tools.
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, Next.js, GraphQL, Starlette, EHR employment, or HIPAA implementation.
- Lead entry that is off-axis for product engineering (pure GTM, low-level DSP with no product/API spine) while the full-stack shipping story is buried.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a shippable production-product story.
- Club-ops without engineering depth (`resume.md` §8).
- Keyword stuffing mental-health / marketplace / EHR in Skills with no supporting system.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
