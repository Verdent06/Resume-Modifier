# Grow Therapy

Grow Therapy is a three-sided mental-health marketplace that connects independent providers, insurance payors, and patients so care is easier to find, cover, and start. Founded in 2020; JD-stated scale is 15 million sessions started. Engineering identity is product software on that marketplace — provider/client surfaces, production services, not a research lab and not a generic CRUD shop. Flagship surfaces include provider scheduling/billing tools and the client-facing Grow app; clinically-guided AI features exist as product, not as an intern ML-research bar.
