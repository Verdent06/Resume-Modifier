# Clarios

Clarios is a Brookfield-owned automotive energy-storage OEM (Johnson Controls Power Solutions, rebranded **2019**). It manufactures and recycles the car batteries behind OEM brands including Ford, General Motors, Toyota, Honda, and Nissan — JD copy: **18,000** employees worldwide and recovery/reuse of up to **99%** of battery materials; Built In company copy **[directional]**: ~150 million batteries per year / about one in three vehicles. Consumer brands include VARTA and OPTIMA. Global HQ is **Florist Tower, 5757 N. Green Bay Ave., Glendale, WI 53209** (LEED-certified; cafeteria + workout facility). This folder is the **Data Science Intern (Summer 2027)** packet (Workday **WD50211**): applied business data science at HQ — data preparation, EDA, feature engineering, model development; exposure to forecasting, classification, anomaly detection, and generative AI — **not** the sibling **IT Digital/AI Intern**, **not** Forward Deployed Engineering Intern, **not** ML-research.
