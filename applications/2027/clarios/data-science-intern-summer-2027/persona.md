## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract — the writer discovers which entries carry them):**
- Python and SQL demonstrated through use in bullets, not only the Skills line (`resume.md` keyword-through-use). Snowflake is named — PostgreSQL/SQL through use is the honest analog; do not invent the lakehouse.
- End-to-end applied-DS workflow: messy or multi-source data → prep/EDA/aggregate → feature or score → evaluated classification, regression/forecast analog, or ranked insight → presentation or dashboard → measured outcome. Preprocessing through evaluation beats `model.fit()` (`resume.md` §14).
- Stakeholder-facing industrial/ops-analytics signal kept visible in the lead window because of track divergence: shipped analysis for a business or research customer, not hobby ML.
- Classification, forecasting, or scoring with a real eval (held-out accuracy, precision against a business criterion, faithfulness lift) — analog to the JD's ML techniques list without claiming Clarios plant data.
- Generative-AI / open-source LLM analog through use (orchestration, eval gates), never Copilot Studio or VLMs.
- Visualizations / dashboards / ranked reports as a first-class artifact — analog to "present findings" without inventing Power BI.
- Quantitative coursework (stats, calc, econ) visible in Education; GPA on the page.

**Anti-patterns:**
- Generic SWE / full-stack product resume that never shows data pipelines, SQL, models, or a viz/report analog — wrong role.
- ML-research paper framing with no delivery, no pipeline, no measured business outcome. Classification with eval is on-axis; notebook-only `model.fit()` is not.
- Voice-AI, audio-DSP, or embedded C++ as the **lead** with no analytics analog.
- Skills-only Python/SQL/Snowflake/PowerBI with no bullet proof.
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, Fusion, Power BI, Azure Data Factory, Matillion, or VLMs (`resume.md` §8 fabrication smell).
- Club-ops or community-growth filler occupying prime slots.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact.
- Treating this as a software-engineer intern screen (OA-first, systems-design, generic SWE keywords).
- Rewriting education to say "Data Science" when the degree is CS + Economics.
