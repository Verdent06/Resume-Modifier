# Bose Corporation

Bose designs and sells premium audio — headphones, earbuds, speakers, soundbars, professional systems, and automotive audio — around the thesis that sound is the product, not a feature. Founded in 1964 by Dr. Amar Bose; privately held (Delaware corporation); HQ **100 The Mountain Road, Framingham, Massachusetts 01701**. FY2024 ESG: products sold in **64** countries; ~**5,200** employees. Majority non-voting ownership sits with **MIT**.
