# CME Group

CME Group is the world's leading derivatives marketplace (NASDAQ: CME; S&P 500): four designated contract markets (CME, CBOT, NYMEX, COMEX) plus clearing and related platforms, with flagship **CME Globex** electronic central limit order book. Technology writes and maintains ultra-low-latency electronic trading platforms, risk-management systems, and front-end trading interfaces that are central to the business. Core systems are migrating toward Google Cloud. This intern req is Workday **34821** **Software Engineering Internship - Summer 2027** — a Technology intern SWE seat (Java and/or Python; back-end or full-stack, cloud, application resiliency, automation), **not** Year-Long Technology Internship **34831**, **not** Software Engineering Fellowship **34824**.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`) — respected exchange / market-infrastructure brand; intern pay is C-band; peer of Interactive Brokers / Deutsche Bank TDI, not Citadel-class HFT prestige
- **HQ / offices:** Chicago, IL HQ at 20 S Wacker Dr; this intern Chicago hybrid. Principal US offices 20 S Wacker, Chicago 60606. ~3,875 employees YE2025 (~2,230 US)
- **Valuation / signal:** Public S&P 500 derivatives exchange operator; record-scale listed futures/options franchise; Globex is the flagship electronic CLOB
- **Product focus:** Ultra-low-latency electronic trading, risk systems, and market architecture on Globex and related platforms — derivatives marketplace infrastructure, not a consumer-app shop and not a prop-trading desk
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineering intern):** **$21.68–$36.10/hr** (this JD). Select intern benefits: comprehensive health coverage and a mental-health benefit
- **Work model:** Paid full-time intern, typically **10–12 weeks**, **hybrid**, typically Chicago (20 S Wacker). Summer program includes peer networking, Chicago excursions, trading/coding challenges, lunch-and-learn speakers
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently pursuing undergraduate or graduate CS or related. **Unable to provide employment sponsorship** (CPT, H1B, F1, L, etc.) — US work auth without assistance required. This JD has **no class-year window**; WayUp summer-2027 tech form: candidates graduating Summer 2027 are ineligible (must still be enrolled after the term). GPA unstated on this posting. Posted **2026-09-18**. Background check at hire (Early Careers: Americas)
