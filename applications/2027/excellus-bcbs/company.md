# Excellus BlueCross BlueShield

Excellus BlueCross BlueShield is a nonprofit independent Blue Cross Blue Shield licensee serving Upstate New York. It sits inside The Lifetime Healthcare Companies, a Rochester-headquartered nonprofit holding company (CEO James R. Reed) that also includes Univera Healthcare, Lifetime Benefit Solutions, and MedAmerica. Official company facts list HQ at 165 Court Street, Rochester, NY 14647; about 1.5 million members across 31 counties; a $6 billion family of companies; and more than 4,000 New York employees. This intern sits on the **Candesa Pharmacy Solutions** development team — application enhancements, conversion work, Agile delivery, and code review — not a generic IT-helpdesk intern, not Records/Information Management, and not a clinical/pharmacy technician rotation.
