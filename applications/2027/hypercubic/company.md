# Hypercubic

Hypercubic is a YC F25, three-person San Francisco startup building an AI-native platform that understands, operates, and modernizes COBOL/mainframe systems still running core operations at ~70% of the Fortune 500 (banking, insurance, airlines, utilities, government). The product stack is HyperDocs (COBOL/JCL/PL/I → living docs and dependency graphs), HyperTwin (digital twins of expert mainframe workflows), Hopper (agentic TN3270/z/OS ADE that drives ISPF, JCL, JES, CICS, VSAM), and HyperLoop (verification-driven modernization). For a software engineering intern, the screen tests general SWE fundamentals while the company identity is agentic AI plus program-analysis tooling for legacy/mainframe modernization.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated (YC F25; not in `reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco, CA; full-time in-person
- **Valuation / signal:** Founded 2025; YC Fall 2025; ~$0.5M pre-seed (JD); team size ~3; founders Sai Gurrapu (CEO) and Aayush Naik (CTO), both ex-Apple AIML
- **Product focus:** Agentic AI for COBOL/mainframe operations and modernization (HyperDocs, HyperTwin, Hopper, HyperLoop)
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** Not listed in JD (YC jobs page lists $125K–$300K + 0.50–2.00% equity for full-time/new-grad-ok roles, not this intern req)
- **Work model:** Paid internship assumed; SF onsite, full-time; term is flexible/rolling (summer, semester-long, co-op); relocation support available
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently enrolled undergrad or grad, any year/major; must already be authorized to work in the US (CPT/OPT supported; no new visa sponsorship)

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD names none as hard requirements. Founding-adjacent / product stack: Python, TypeScript, React. COBOL/mainframe is preferred domain knowledge, not a filter.
- **Domains:** Agentic workflows, evals, program-analysis tooling, backend infrastructure, user-facing features; legacy systems, compilers, HPC as pluses
- **What wins:** End-to-end shipped systems (prototype then polish), agentic pipelines and eval discipline, backend + UI evidence on one page, systems thinking under ambiguity, quantified reliability/quality — not COBOL fluency and not notebook ML. Human reads the PDF early.
