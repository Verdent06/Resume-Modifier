## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract — the writer discovers which entries carry them):**
- SQL and Python demonstrated through use in bullets, not only the Skills line (`resume.md` keyword-through-use). Both are required and both are in inventory.
- End-to-end data workflow: ingest messy or multi-source data → ETL / transform / normalize → validate or quality-check → database, warehouse analog, or API consumer → measured outcome (`resume.md` §14; `recruiting.md` §13).
- Data pipelines and infrastructure as the lead story — analog to “design, develop, and maintain data pipelines” and “expand the company’s data warehouse” without claiming Audax production ownership of Snowflake.
- Data validation, quality assessments, freshness checks, or anomaly resolution with a witness metric.
- Schema design or entity-resolution analog on structured/public data — analog to “optimal data schemas.”
- Cloud or on-prem database evidence limited to inventory (PostgreSQL, AWS EC2/S3, Docker) through use.
- Stakeholder- or business-facing delivery — analog to analytic pipelines for investment professionals and interacting with all levels of the organization.
- Quantitative coursework (stats, calc) visible in Education; class-year shows current enrollment (`Expected May 2028` is in window for a Winter 2027 co-op who returns Fall 2027). GPA on the page.

**Anti-patterns:**
- Generic SWE / full-stack product resume that never shows pipelines, ETL, SQL, or data quality — wrong req.
- Unmodified GenAI-agentic product lead with no data-engineering analog — this JD is warehouse ETL, not a product-agent persona.
- Notebook ML or `model.fit()` with no pipeline, no quality step, no measured outcome — that is AI Engineer / research, not this req.
- Skills-only Python/SQL/AWS/Docker/Snowflake with no bullet proof.
- Invented Java, Spring, Node, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, Fusion, Sentry, Alteryx, Streamlit, Prefect, Terraform, or PE/deal/LOI/coverage claims (Java and Alteryx are pluses, not exclusive gates; stuffing them is a fabrication smell — `resume.md` §8).
- Club-ops or community-growth filler occupying prime slots.
- Pure audio-DSP / voice-AI / embedded systems as the lead with no data-pipeline analog.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact.
- Claiming day-one ownership of firm-wide warehouse platforms — co-op supports the Senior Data Engineer.
