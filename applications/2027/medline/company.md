# Medline

Medline is the largest U.S. manufacturer and distributor of medical-surgical products and a supply-chain solutions company for hospitals, surgery centers, and other care sites. It is public (Nasdaq: MDLN); FY2025 net sales were $28.4B, with 45,000+ employees and operations in 100+ countries. Headquarters is Northfield, Illinois; this intern req sits at CORP Northbrook, IL (hybrid). The Software Engineering Intern works on Warehouse Management Systems and e-commerce applications across the SDLC — front-end and back-end — not device firmware.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-tier (`reference/companies.md` — legitimate healthcare supply-chain / WMS + e-commerce intern work; limited national SWE brand vs B-tier medical-device names)
- **HQ / offices:** Northfield, IL (HQ); this req CORP Northbrook, IL hybrid; other Chicagoland corporate offices include Merchandise Mart (Chicago) and Waukegan, IL
- **Valuation / signal:** Nasdaq: MDLN (IPO completed); FY2025 net sales $28.4B; 45,000+ employees, 100+ countries
- **Product focus:** Medical-surgical manufacturing/distribution plus supply-chain software — Warehouse Management Systems and e-commerce platforms for this intern seat
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE Intern):** $23.25–$33.75/hr (req R2617623)
- **Work model:** Paid full-time intern, 10-week summer program (early June through mid-August 2027), hybrid at one of four Chicagoland corporate offices; this req is Northbrook
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Rising senior or junior pursuing bachelor's in CS / Software Engineering / IT / related; 3.0 GPA preferred; unrestricted permanent U.S. work authorization. No sponsorship implied.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages (this JD):** Java and/or JavaScript (OR — JavaScript-family is enough; Java is not a knockout). Front-end: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Angular, or Next.js. SQL, Git, OOP. Plus: Spring, Hibernate, Maven, Selenium, Jasmine, Karma; AWS/Azure/GCP.
- **Domains:** Warehouse Management Systems; e-commerce / business applications; agile SDLC (design, code, test, deploy, support); defect/production debug; unit and automated tests
- **What wins:** A general SWE intern page — shipped front-end and back-end (React/Angular + API + SQL), Git, test/debug evidence — plus a readable analog to business/e-commerce or warehouse-ops software. Resume is the gate (`recruiting.md`: mid-size / non-tech-tech, human reads the PDF early). Do not invent Java, Spring, Hibernate, Maven, Next.js, Azure, GCP, or a healthcare/WMS internship.
