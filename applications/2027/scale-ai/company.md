# Scale AI

Scale AI builds the data infrastructure and full-stack tooling that frontier labs, enterprises, and governments use to train, evaluate, and oversee generative models. The Data Engine and annotation products feed RLHF, post-training, and evaluation pipelines; the intern SWE job is product engineering on those systems — contributor tooling, eval infra, fraud/quality, matching, and data-exploration UIs — not a research-paper rotation. HQ is San Francisco. `reference/companies.md` A-TIER Notes: AI infra, ~$29B valuation after Meta's 49% stake, Tier 1 Blind.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-TIER — Excellent / Strong Signal (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco, CA (this intern req: on-site SF). Also NYC and other offices on the broader board.
- **Valuation / signal:** ~$29B post Meta investment (June 2025, 49% stake, no voting control per public reporting); founder Alexandr Wang left for Meta Superintelligence Labs; Francis deSouza appointed CEO effective August 10, 2026
- **Product focus:** AI data platform — high-quality training/eval data, RLHF / post-training pipelines, contributor marketplace, model-reliability tooling for enterprises and public-sector customers
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern, Summer):** Unlisted on this JD. Third-party cycle writeups cite ~$60/hr + ~$7,000 SF housing stipend for prior intern classes — **[directional]**, not a JD number
- **Work model:** Paid Summer 2027 internship (May/June start) in San Francisco, on-site. Interns own shipped work on FTE roadmaps. Many return as full-time Scaliens.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Graduation Fall 2027 or Spring 2028, Bachelor's (or equivalent) in CS, EECS, Computer Engineering, or Statistics. Summer 2027 SF availability. 90-day wait before reapplying to the same role.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python, TypeScript, React, and/or MongoDB — "and/or," not an exclusive Mongo floor. Do not invent MongoDB.
- **Domains:** Full-stack product engineering, large-volume data pipelines, eval / quality infrastructure, agentic applications and observability, contributor-platform tooling, fraud/quality matching, data visualization/query products. Applied pipelines and shipped product, not ML-research publications.
- **What wins:** A full-stack intern spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) with Python and TypeScript/React shown in bullets, a prior CS/SWE internship, shipped product used by someone other than the author, and applied data-pipeline / eval / agentic depth so the page is memorable at an AI-infra company.
