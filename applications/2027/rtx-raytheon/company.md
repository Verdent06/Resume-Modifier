# RTX / Raytheon

Raytheon is the defense prime of RTX Corporation, building missiles, launchers, radars, and naval and fire-control systems for U.S. and allied militaries. Its software organizations engineer the real-time systems, digital signal processing (DSP), autonomy/ML, cyber, and DevOps that run on those platforms — code where stability, integration, and test rigor are mission-critical because failures have real-world consequences. The Marlborough, MA campus is a core radar and sensor software site, and the internship sits inside a development team that develops, integrates, tests, and documents hardware/software platforms in a Unix/Linux, C/C++/Java environment.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B (defense prime; `reference/companies.md` defense section)
- **HQ / offices:** RTX HQ Arlington, VA; this role onsite at Marlborough, MA (1001 Boston Post Rd, BLDG 2)
- **Valuation / signal:** Fortune 100 defense prime; traditional prime undergoing ongoing restructuring; slow-paced, process-heavy engineering culture
- **Product focus:** Missiles, launchers, radars, naval/fire-control; software spans real-time systems, DSP, ML/autonomy, cyber, DevOps
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** $17.79–$39.42/hr
- **Work model:** Paid, onsite Marlborough MA; relocation listed; Summer 2027
- **Clearance / eligibility:** U.S. citizenship required; must be able to obtain and maintain a U.S. government security clearance (interim Secret before start); enrolled Bachelor's+ in CS/CE/SE or related, at minimum sophomore standing by summer 2027
