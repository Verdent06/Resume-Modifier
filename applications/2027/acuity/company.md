# Acuity Inc.

Acuity Inc. (NYSE: **AYI**; formerly Acuity Brands, Inc.; legal name change **March 26, 2025**) is an Atlanta-headquartered industrial technology company. Two segments: **Acuity Brands Lighting (ABL)** — lighting and lighting controls — and **Acuity Intelligent Spaces (AIS)** — building management, spatial intelligence (Atrius), Distech Controls BMS, and Q-SYS A/V + control (QSC, LLC acquired; FY2025 contribution cited in the 10-K). FY2025 net sales **$4.346B** (up 13.1% YoY); ~**13,800** employees YE Aug 31 2025 (~4,300 US / ~8,200 Mexico). Careers SSO is still **AcuityBrands**. **This folder is the Finance AI Engineering Intern — Onsite packet only** (SuccessFactors RMK **1434878900** / Req **16595**).
