# Robinhood

Robinhood Markets (NASDAQ: **HOOD**) is a consumer brokerage, crypto, and payments platform whose mission is to democratize finance. Backend engineers ship the services, data systems, and infra behind brokerage, crypto, and payments for millions of retail customers — correctness and transactions at consumer scale, not a trading desk and not an ML lab. Founded 2013 (Vladimir Tenev, Baiju Bhatt); HQ 85 Willow Rd, Menlo Park, CA. Public 2021; intern identity in `companies.md` is **fintech-backend**.

Stack named as *such as*: Python/Django, Go, PostgreSQL, Kafka, Redis, Memcached, AWS, Kubernetes.
