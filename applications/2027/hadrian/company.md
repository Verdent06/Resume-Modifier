# Hadrian

Hadrian is building autonomous factories to reindustrialize U.S. aerospace and defense manufacturing — Factory-as-a-Service that combines AI, software, robotics, and full-stack production so primes can ship rockets, satellites, aircraft, ships, and other mission-critical systems faster and cheaper. The physical-AI platform **Opus** is the software layer for manufacturing and supply-chain autonomy (TDPs, digital twins, factory management, embedded automation). Day-to-day intern work on this family of reqs sits next to production: telemetry, quality records, and program data into a warehouse analog, plus models and analyses that change how the factory runs — not a robotics-only C++ seat and not ML research.

- **Tier:** A-TIER — Excellent / Strong Signal (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Torrance, CA (HQ, 20850 Normandie Ave, 90502). This intern family: **Los Angeles, CA, onsite**. Additional factories: Mesa, AZ (F3) and Muscle Shoals, AL (F4); four sites totaling just under 3 million sq ft (Series D announcement). Additional U.S. sites in development.

JD also names JPMorgan Chase, a16z, Founders Fund, Lux Capital, T. Rowe Price, Morgan Stanley. Follow-on: ~$360M revolver (Aug 2026). Founder/CEO Chris Power.
- **Product focus:** Autonomous factories / Factory-as-a-Service for aero/defense; Opus physical-AI platform; expanding into welding, casting, forging, electronics, additive, munitions, and autonomous systems
- **Intern comp (2027 intern family):** $46.15–$50.71/hr (JD). Housing support may be available based on business need
- **Work model:** Paid intern; **onsite Los Angeles**. Terms posted: Fall 2026, Spring 2027, Summer 2027. This packet defaults to **Summer 2027**

Current Bachelor's or Master's student in CS, Data Science, Statistics, Applied Math, Engineering, or related; expected graduation **December 2027–June 2028** for the Data Science/Data Engineer intern. Three published intern reqs: Software Engineer Intern, Robotics Engineer Intern, and **Data Science/Data Engineer Intern** — do not mix packets

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python and SQL required on the Data Science/Data Engineer intern. Honest inventory is Python + SQL through use; do not invent Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, Fusion, Sentry, Prefect, dbt, Airflow, or Spark.
- **Domains:** Factory data backbone — machine/production/quality/program ingest; ETL/ELT into a warehouse analog; throughput, capacity, cycle time, yield, utilization analytics; dashboards and metrics; prototype stats/ML (anomaly detection, demand forecasting, process optimization); data quality and instrumentation. Adjacent intern reqs (SWE, Robotics) are different packets.
- **What wins:** Because the bottleneck is the resume (`companies.md`; `recruiting.md` startup/Ashby human-read early), a one-page PDF that shows ingest → ETL/transform → quality → store/serve (warehouse analog / API / dashboard) → measured outcome, with Python and SQL in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §14; `recruiting.md` §13). Applied ML/stats with eval is a plus, not a research-paper lead. Telemetry analog only if it exists in the pool — do not invent FRC/Java telemetry. Do not lead as generic SWE, notebook ML, or robotics-only C++ DSP.
