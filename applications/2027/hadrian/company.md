# Hadrian

Hadrian builds autonomous factories so aerospace and defense programs can produce rockets, satellites, aircraft, ships, and other mission-critical hardware faster and cheaper than the legacy industrial base. Factories run on Opus, the internal full-stack factory-autonomy platform that interprets designs, automates manufacturing and inspection, and trains technicians in ~30 days — Hadrian sells parts and factory capacity, not a public SaaS license. After a $1.37B Series D at $7.87B (Aug 2026), the company is expanding welding, casting, forging, electronics, and additive lines while scaling Factory-as-a-Service. Intern SWE work is the factory software backbone: internal tools, dashboards, backend APIs, and data pipelines for operations, quality, and program visibility — not robotics/CNC specialist hiring.
