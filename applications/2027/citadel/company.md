# Citadel

Citadel is one of the world’s leading alternative investment managers — a multi-strategy hedge fund founded in 1990 by Ken Griffin, not the market-maker Citadel Securities. It manages capital for pensions, endowments, sovereign wealth funds, and other institutions across equities, commodities, credit, macro, and quantitative strategies. Engineers sit next to researchers and investment professionals: small teams, short cycles into production, and software that turns investment ideas into live platforms. The intern SWE job is to build the tools that bring trading strategies to life and the high-performance, large-data research platforms those strategies run on — system architecture, platforms, and web frameworks, plus distributed computing, NLP, and machine learning as techniques, not as a pivot to notebook ML.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** S-Tier (quant / hedge fund; `reference/companies.md` lists "Citadel / Citadel Securities" — algo/math heavy, top comp)
- **HQ / offices:** Miami, FL (HQ); this internship: New York, Miami, Greenwich, Houston (onsite). This application: NYC.
- **Valuation / signal:** Privately held multi-strategy hedge fund; elite prestige band on a resume; distinct legal entity from Citadel Securities (market making).
- **Product focus:** Investment-research and trading-strategy technology — high-performance large-data research platforms, production tools for investment teams, web frameworks and system architecture.
- **Intern comp (2027 SE):** $4,500–$5,800 per week (per JD); 11-week summer program; possible sign-on, housing stipend, company-sponsored travel.
- **Work model:** Paid, onsite (NYC / Miami / Greenwich / Houston); 11-week summer internship with senior-engineer mentorship, networking, and peer cohort events.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Bachelor's / master's / PhD in CS, computer engineering, or related; current student returning to school after the internship. No published class-year knockout beyond degree-in-progress.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python and C++ are the firm’s published core intern languages; the posting itself names programming and design, not a language list. Strong programming and design fundamentals matter more than a specific stack.
- **Domains:** High-performance large-data research platforms, tools that operationalize trading/investment strategies, system architecture, platforms and web frameworks, distributed computing, NLP/ML applied to markets.
- **What wins:** Exceptional programming + design fluency proven in real, defensible projects; systems/performance depth (real-time constraints, concurrency, memory, latency) that reads as investment-tech adjacent; demonstrated familiarity with probability and statistics (model evaluation, regression, quantitative analysis) via a research/data platform, not notebook toy ML; production web/API/platform shipping. Prior finance or a trading-desk internship is not required. Per `reference/recruiting.md` Part I §1 (OA is the binding gate) and Part III §11 (general SWE), the resume clears the human screen and supports a math-and-systems-first technical loop.
