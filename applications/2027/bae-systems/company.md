# BAE Systems

BAE Systems, Inc. is the U.S. subsidiary of BAE Systems plc, a global defense, aerospace, and security company. This req sits in **Electronic Systems** — the business that builds advanced defense and commercial electronics (from F-35 protection systems to spacecraft electronics). San Diego is an Electronic Systems software site. Intern work is general software engineering inside the nationwide **LEAP** (Learn, Engage, Apply, Progress) program: write new code, debug critical problems, and improve system stability and resilience on teams that may use React/Angular, AWS/Azure/Kubernetes, Spring/Gradle, and SQL/NoSQL stores. The memorable identity is mission-critical electronics software — reliability and resilience — not hardware technician work or a Java-only shop.

- **Tier:** B (traditional defense electronics prime; peer to Northrop / L3Harris in `reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** BAE Systems, Inc. HQ Falls Church, VA; Electronic Systems HQ Nashua, NH; this req onsite San Diego, CA

- **Product focus:** Defense and commercial electronics software — new code, critical-problem resolution, system stability/resilience
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE Intern III):** $50,079–$67,640 listed full-time salary range (onsite Summer 2027)

- **Clearance / eligibility:** U.S. citizenship required (Jobright: No H-1B; U.S. Citizen Only). Peer BAE SWE intern postings list Clearance Must Currently Possess: None / Must Be Able to Obtain: Secret — eligibility to obtain, not an existing-clearance knockout. Rising junior or senior (or graduate student) Summer 2027; CS/CE/SE or other coding degree; DS&OOP; proficient in one of Java, C++, C#, Python, JavaScript.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** One of Java, C++, C#, Python, JavaScript is the floor — not Java-only. Preferred: Angular, React, HTML/CSS, Docker, AWS, Git, PostgreSQL, Redis, PyTorch (ML/GIS is preferred, not required).
- **Domains:** New feature code, debugging critical problems, system stability and resilience; frontend, backend, cloud/DevOps, and databases as team-dependent.
- **What wins:** Because the bottleneck is the resume (`recruiting.md` intern funnel; B-tier defense primes), a one-page PDF that proves a listed language in bullets, shipped software, and reliability/stability work (fault tolerance, failure recovery, deterministic systems) passes this screen.
