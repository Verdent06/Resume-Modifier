# Courier Health

Courier Health (courierhealth.com; Greenhouse board `courierhealth`) is a New York patient-experience platform for life sciences. Biopharma teams use it to unify fragmented patient-access data and orchestrate specialty-therapy journeys from prescription through start and adherence — Field Access, Patient Services, and AI Intake on a purpose-built CRM, not a generic healthcare tool bolted onto a legacy stack. Founded 2021; HQ 135 Madison Ave, 3rd Floor (NoMad).

The intern identity is generic product SWE: new features, speed/reliability of existing apps, and product discovery on an AWS-native TypeScript/React/Postgres stack. Company identity is biopharma patient engagement (and a Series B push into agentic AI) — not an ML-research rotation and not a hospital-EMR intern.
