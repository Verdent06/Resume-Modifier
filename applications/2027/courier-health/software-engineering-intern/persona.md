# Software Engineering Intern (Summer 2027) at Courier Health

## Role Summary

A paid **10–12 week** Summer 2027 software engineering internship at Courier Health (Greenhouse **5258913007** / req **92**), **$45/hour**, **in-office NYC (NoMad)** with occasional remote. Interns ship features on the patient-experience platform, improve speed and reliability of existing apps, and help find product opportunities. JD *requirements* are generic SWE intern (rising senior, BS CS or related, one modern language, desire to learn AWS, independent self-starter, simple approaches). Bonus stack is TypeScript, React, GraphQL, Postgres, Terraform, and AWS (DynamoDB, API Gateway, Lambda, AppSync), plus a prior software-product internship, “particularly in healthcare.” Company identity is biopharma patient engagement — not ML-research and not an EMR/hospital intern.

## Screen Criteria

- Full-stack spine: shipped product with a backend/API layer, a data store, and a frontend — breadth plus depth in one layer (`resume.md` §12). Lead with shipping, not with a research-ML or DSP identity.
- TypeScript, React, and Postgres through use in bullets (JD bonus languages/stack the candidate actually has). Python is honest and fine; do not imply GraphQL or Terraform.
- Cloud-based delivery (AWS, Docker) tied to a ship or reliability story — the JD names AWS as “experience or desire to learn,” and bonus names DynamoDB/API Gateway/Lambda/AppSync the pool does **not** have. EC2/S3 through use is the honest AWS signal.
- Builder / self-starter evidence: owned delivery, diagnosed defects, shipped under a real stakeholder — matching “independent self-starter” and “simple approaches to complex problems.”
- Titled SWE intern/co-op visible (bonus: prior internship building software products). Do not recast data-engineering contracts as extra SWE internships.
- Quantified impact (latency, cost, reliability, accuracy, throughput, paying users) so “production” and “reliability” can be sized in ~7 seconds (`resume.md` §4).
- Class-year on page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies the intern graduation window.
- Keywords through use, not isolation (`resume.md` §2): TypeScript, React, Postgres, AWS, REST, Docker, reliability.

Anti-patterns:

- Inventing GraphQL, Terraform, DynamoDB, Lambda, API Gateway, AppSync, FHIR, HL7, Epic, or a healthcare internship.
- Notebook ML, LoRA-as-the-whole-story, or research-track framing that hides the SWE intern spine.
- Club-ops, GTM/quant sourcing, or deal-flow with no engineering the role tests.
- DSP/audio-plugin identity as the lead (hard real-time C++ is not this intern’s screen).
- Skills-list TypeScript/React/Postgres with zero bullet evidence.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a shipped-product + TypeScript/AWS spine.
- Healthcare-mission essays with no engineering the 30-second screen can size.
