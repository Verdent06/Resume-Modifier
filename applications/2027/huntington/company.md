# Huntington

Huntington Bancshares Incorporated (NYSE: HBAN) is a Columbus-headquartered super-regional bank founded in 1866. Through The Huntington National Bank it provides retail and commercial banking, payments, mortgage, capital markets, and wealth products across a multi-state Midwest-and-beyond franchise (10-Q: over 1,400 branches in 21 states as of June 30, 2026; total assets $284.0B). This req is Summer 2027 **Data and Analytics Internship** (Workday **R0075377**) — pull, scrub, analyze, and visualize data across business segments — **not** SWE, not branch IT, and not a trading-desk quant seat. Interns apply to one data requisition; specific team placement is discussed at screen. Culture signal on the JD: Can-Do Attitude, Service Heart, Forward Thinking; "Look out for people."

Housing stipend (grossed-up lump sum on first paycheck) if intern city ≠ personal residence or college (`companies.md`; 2024 intern brochure)

- **Clearance / eligibility:** HS graduate; enrolled undergrad/grad with **at least one quarter/semester after** the summer program; **no immigration sponsorship** — must be currently authorized to work in the US full-time. This JD lists no GPA floor (older campus PDFs cited 3.0; RippleMatch 2023: GPA minimum removed — do not treat 3.0 as a knockout on this req)

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Preferred Skills & Interests: SQL, Python, Tableau, PowerBI, JavaScript, R. Honest Python + SQL through use win. Do not invent Tableau, Power BI, Snowflake, Databricks, R, Copilot, or Fusion. JavaScript is preferred, not a fabrication target if the pool is TypeScript
- **Domains:** Applied data across Huntington business segments — pull/scrub datasets, analysis, visualization products for presentations, partnership with a named function's data needs. Banking interest is preferred, not invented domain experience
- **What wins:** End-to-end ingest → clean/aggregate → insight, ranking, or visualization analog → measured outcome (`resume.md` §14; `recruiting.md` §13). Stakeholder-facing delivery and a finance-adjacent / banking-operations flavor because the company is a super-regional bank, not a tech-product company.
