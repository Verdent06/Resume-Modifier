## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract — the writer discovers which entries carry them):**

- Python and SQL demonstrated through use in bullets, not only the Skills line (`resume.md` keyword-through-use). JavaScript is preferred; TypeScript/React visualization counts as a JS-adjacent analog when it is real work — do not invent JavaScript or R.
- End-to-end data workflow: ingest messy or irregular source data → scrub/normalize/aggregate → analysis, ranking, dashboard, or presentation artifact → measured outcome. Analog to "pulling and scrubbing data sets, providing data analysis, creating data visualization products for presentations."
- Visualization or reporting analog only if it exists in real work (React/report UIs, ranked outputs, stakeholder summaries). Do not invent Tableau or Power BI.
- Stakeholder-facing operational-analytics / business-delivery signal kept visible in the lead window because of track divergence: shipped analysis for a business, nonprofit, or principal customer — analog to partnering with a Huntington business segment.
- Finance-adjacent flavor on that spine: financial, regulatory, campaign-finance, entity-resolution, or scoring/shortlist work — memorable at a super-regional bank without stuffing "retail branch / commercial credit" jargon into work that is not that.
- Teamwork and leadership as engineering ownership (sole contractor, founder, scoped delivery), matching the JD basic qual — not club-ops filler.
- Quantitative coursework (stats, data analysis, calc, econ) visible in Education; class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` is a Spring 2028 grad → rising senior for Summer 2027 Enterprise. GPA 3.66 is a plus even though this JD has no floor.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Generic SWE / full-stack product resume that never shows ETL, scrubbing, SQL, analysis, or a visualization/insight analog — wrong role.
- Notebook ML or `model.fit()` with no pipeline, no insight, no measured outcome.
- Skills-only Python/SQL/Tableau/Power BI/R with no bullet proof.
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, Power BI, or R (JD names several; absence in bullets is honest; stuffing them is a fabrication smell — `resume.md` §8).
- Club-ops or community-growth filler occupying prime slots.
- Pure audio-DSP / embedded / voice-AI as the lead with no data/analytics analog.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact.
- Treating this as a software-engineer intern screen (OA-first, systems-design, generic SWE keywords) or as a trading-desk quant.
- Keyword-stuffing "Can-Do / Service Heart / Look out for people" into bullets that are not about that work.
