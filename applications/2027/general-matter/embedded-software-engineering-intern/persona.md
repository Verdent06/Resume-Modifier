# Summer 2027 Internship - Embedded Software Engineering at General Matter

## Role Summary

A paid, onsite, 12-week Summer 2027 embedded software engineering internship at General Matter's South Bay Los Angeles HQ ($32–$46/hr plus housing stipend; Greenhouse 5377131008). The intern helps develop performant, safe software that directly controls enrichment systems — code that commands actuators, processes high-speed sensor data, and executes fault-tolerant control logic where safety and accuracy are first-class. Cross-functional with EE, SWE, chemical, manufacturing, nuclear, materials, and physics. JD surface: current CS/CE/EE student; development experience in C, C++, Golang, Python, or Rust; general understanding of embedded or low-level systems. Preferred: DAQ, PCBA bring-up, DSP, protocol debugging, PLC/SCADA. Company identity is domestic uranium enrichment (LEU/HALEU) — Founders Fund–incubated, hardware-adjacent, mission-critical — so the memorable signal is systems discipline next to physical plant, not a consumer web app and not a fabricated firmware/MCU specialist page.
