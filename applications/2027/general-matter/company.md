# General Matter

General Matter enriches uranium. Founded in 2024 by Scott Nolan (former SpaceX Merlin/Dragon engineer, Founders Fund partner) and incubated inside Founders Fund — the same playbook as Anduril and Palantir — it is building domestic LEU and HALEU (high-assay low-enriched uranium) capacity so advanced reactors are not dependent on foreign fuel. Software sits next to physical enrichment hardware: high-performance services that coordinate control systems, fault-tolerant telemetry pipelines, and CI/CD/observability where a mistake has physical consequences. `reference/companies.md` has no General Matter row; nearest published peers are Anduril (Founders Fund defense-tech, A-tier, C++ for embedded/autonomy) and SpaceX (C++/Python mission-critical systems, B-tier).

- **Product focus:** Lowest-cost U.S. uranium enrichment (LEU + HALEU) for advanced reactors, AI/manufacturing power, and national fuel-cycle independence

- **Work model:** Paid, 12-week internship, onsite at LA South Bay HQ for the full term. Form also offers Fall 2026 / Spring 2027 / Summer 2027 availability checkboxes.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Current undergrad or grad in CS, Engineering, or related (no sophomore/junior gate). Form: may require eligibility to obtain/maintain a U.S. security clearance; work-authorization question; unofficial transcript required. Commute to South Bay HQ is a hard form gate.
