# Harvey

Harvey is the legal infrastructure for law firms and in-house teams: Assistant (day-to-day drafting/research), Vault (analysis across large private corpora), Knowledge (firm precedent), and workflow agents that chain associate/paralegal steps. Founded 2022 by Winston Weinberg (ex-O'Melveny securities litigator) and Gabe Pereyra (ex-DeepMind / Meta research). HQ San Francisco with New York and Toronto offices. Category-defining legal AI at **$15.5B** (Sep 2026).
