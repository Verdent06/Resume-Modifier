# Harvey

Harvey is the legal infrastructure for law firms and in-house teams: Assistant (day-to-day drafting/research), Vault (analysis across large private corpora), Knowledge (firm precedent), and workflow agents that chain associate/paralegal steps. Founded 2022 by Winston Weinberg (ex-O'Melveny securities litigator) and Gabe Pereyra (ex-DeepMind / Meta research). The Summer 2027 Software Engineering intern is a generic application-layer / GenAI product SWE seat with team-match across Frontend, Backend, Full-Stack, Infrastructure, and Applied AI — not a legal intern, not an ML-research rotation, and not the Winter 2027 Toronto twin.
