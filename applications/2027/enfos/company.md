# ENFOS, Inc.

ENFOS builds **STATERA**, a multi-tenant SaaS subledger for Environmental Obligation Management: Asset Retirement Obligations (ARO, ASC 410-20) and Environmental Remediation Obligations (ERO, ASC 410-30) under US GAAP and IFRS (IAS 37). The platform is the enterprise system of record for Finance, Legal, and Remediation teams — audit-ready 10-Q / 10-K reporting, SOX controls, ERP/procure-to-pay integration — not generic EHS software and not a spreadsheet replacement sold as a point tool. Founded 2000; privately held and (per the JD) profitable, with no named direct competitor in this category. STATERA 5.0 launched 2026-02-02 with a purpose-built ARO module unified with ERO. This intern req (Workable **CA15908E0A**, published 2026-09-04) is generic full-stack SWE on that platform (React/TypeScript frontend, Java/Spring Boot backend, PostgreSQL/Redis, Docker/GitLab CI) in **Durham, NC** (also listed Chicago, IL) — not an ML-research seat and not a document-intelligence specialist req.
