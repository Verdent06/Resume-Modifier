# Lennox International (LII)

Lennox International (NYSE: **LII**) designs, manufactures, and markets energy-efficient climate-control products for residential and commercial HVAC plus commercial refrigeration. Founded **1895**; FY2025 10-K: net sales **$5.195B**, net income **$805.8M**, ~**12,900** employees at year-end 2025 (~8,500 US / ~3,200 Mexico). HQ is **2140 Lake Park Blvd, Richardson, TX 75080**. Segments: Home Comfort Solutions and Building Climate Solutions. This intern req is an **AI Product Management & Analytics** seat — product discovery, data analysis, GenAI evaluation, and stakeholder delivery for HVAC digital/AI initiatives — **not** the Master's AI Engineering intern, **not** the Master's AI-Enabled Full-Stack SWE intern, and **not** the IoT intern.
