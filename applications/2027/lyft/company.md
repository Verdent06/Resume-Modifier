# Lyft

Lyft, Inc. (NASDAQ: LYFT) is a multimodal transportation network: a ridesharing marketplace that matches drivers and riders, plus bikes, scooters, Express Drive rentals, and Lyft Business. HQ is **185 Berry Street, Suite 400, San Francisco**. Substantially all revenue (~85%+) comes from ridesharing; bikes/stations are a smaller but real line (bike and station hardware/software sales plus shared micromobility). **Lyft Urban Solutions** is the city-partner micromobility arm — Citi Bike in New York is the flagship (Lyft Urban Solutions cites ~36,701 bikes and 2,109 smart stations). This intern req is **Data Analyst Intern on Urban Solutions** (ops/city-partner analytics for bikeshare), **not** a SWE intern and **not** an ML-research / Data Scientist intern.
