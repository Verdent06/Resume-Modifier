# Rockwell Automation

Rockwell Automation is the largest pure-play industrial automation company, building PLC/control hardware (Allen-Bradley), industrial software (FactoryTalk, Studio 5000), and digital-twin/simulation tooling for manufacturers worldwide. This internship sits inside the AI Center of Excellence (reporting to its Director), a corporate research function building agentic AI systems that read PLC code and control logic, auto-generate test/validation plans, and execute them in simulated industrial environments — a research bet on AI-driven verification for safety-critical control software, not a product-shipping SWE seat.
