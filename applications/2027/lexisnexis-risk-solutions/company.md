# LexisNexis Risk Solutions

LexisNexis Risk Solutions (LNRS; legal posting org **LexisNexis Risk Solutions Inc. Company**) is the Risk market segment of **RELX**: information-based analytics and decision tools that combine public and industry-specific content with algorithms to evaluate and predict risk. HQ is metro-Atlanta (**1000 Alderman Drive, Alpharetta, GA 30005**). RELX 2025 Risk revenues **£3,485m**; ~**11,800** employees; customers in **190+** countries. Verticals include Business Services, **Insurance**, Healthcare, specialised industry data, and Government. Flagship insurance assets: **C.L.U.E.** (claims-history exchange) and **Telematics Exchange / Telematics OnDemand** (normalized driving-behavior data for auto pricing). LNRS says it works with 99% of top U.S. personal auto and home insurers. This intern sits on the **Insurance** vertical’s data/analytics/emerging-tech work (insurance + automotive: vehicle, ownership, telematics, mobility, imagery/video, tabular/time series). **Not** LexisNexis Legal & Professional. **Do not mix** Data Science Intern **R118955** with a sibling Data Analyst Intern packet.
