# CIBC (US)

Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce is a Toronto-based Big Five bank (founded 1867; ~48,000 employees; ~CA$1.1T assets **[directional, Extern]**). CIBC US is the Chicago-headquartered commercial, personal, small-business, wealth, and cross-border banking franchise — not a bulge-bracket IB coverage desk and not Capital Markets summer-analyst recruiting. This intern sits in **Technology**: hands-on coding, testing, and debugging of applications that support banking products, with exposure to regulatory and information-security requirements.
