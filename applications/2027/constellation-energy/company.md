# Constellation Energy

Constellation Energy Generation, LLC (NYSE: CEG) is the largest U.S. private-sector power producer and the nation's largest producer of carbon-free electricity (ex-Exelon Generation spin, 2022; Calpine close January 2026). The fleet is about 55 GW across nuclear, natural gas, geothermal, hydro, wind, and solar, with generating capacity equivalent to about 27 million homes. This intern sits on the **IT Applications Team** as a remote corporate-IT data-engineering seat — pipelines and applications supporting the generation business, not plant operations or a site engineering rotation.
