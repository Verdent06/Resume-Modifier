Interns own a real production problem on a small team — not shadowing. It is **not** the ML/AI Infrastructure track (distributed training / CUDA / Ray / Spark) and **not** a computer-vision research intern. Company identity is physical AI / geospatial mapping / Visual Positioning — Pokémon GO heritage is the data corpus, not the intern job.

JD surface for all tracks: currently pursuing BS/MS in CS or related; TypeScript, Python, C++ or Go for Infrastructure and Backend; curiosity about AI in the physical world; independent debug; SF 4x/week for 12 weeks.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract — the writer discovers which entries carry them):**
- Full-stack product spine: REST/APIs/workflows that replace a manual process or serve real users, with architecture the intern can defend (`resume.md` keyword-through-use).
- JD languages in bullets, not Skills alone: Python and TypeScript through use; C++ through use if listed (Go is an or-list alternative the candidate may lack — not a knockout on Product Engineering if the Python/TS spine is real).
- Upload / processing / serving reliability: fault-tolerant data paths, not tutorial CRUD.
- Systems or applied-ML depth in the top half under track divergence (real-time constraints, lock-free/memory discipline, serving/eval) so a physical-AI company remembers the page — not a CV paper and not a PE/search-fund GTM essay.
- Cloud/containers/SQL through use when present (AWS, Docker, relational DBs) matching nice-to-haves.
- Impact metrics that size hours saved, failure rates, latency, throughput, or scale — not vanity (uptime, LOC, coverage %).
- `Expected May 2028` on the page so current-student status is obvious.

**Anti-patterns:**
- Fabricating Go, CUDA, Kubernetes, Terraform, Ray, Spark, COLMAP, OpenCV, NeRF, Gaussian Splatting, ROS, or a Niantic/Pokémon GO internship (`resume.md` §8 fabrication smell).
- Applying as ML/AI Infrastructure (distributed training / GPU optimization) or Backend Systems (Go/C++ microservices on VPS) when the page is product APIs — pick the track the evidence supports.
- Leading as notebook LoRA / `model.fit()` or as audio-DSP identity with product APIs buried.
- Skills-only TypeScript, Python, or C++ with no bullet proof.
- Club-ops or GTM/quant deal-flow occupying prime slots.
- Remote-only or spoofed San Francisco home location on the form.
- Treating this as an OA-first FAANG SWE intern screen and ignoring the PDF (Ashby + CoderByte; both matter).
