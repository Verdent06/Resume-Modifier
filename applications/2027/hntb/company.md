# HNTB

HNTB Corporation is an employee-owned infrastructure solutions firm (founded 1914 in Kansas City as the Harrington / Howard lineage; renamed HNTB in 1993; employee-owned since 2000). It plans, designs, and program-manages transportation and civil infrastructure for public and private owners — highways, bridges, transit, aviation, and related systems — not a software-product company. ENR 2026 Top 500 Design Firms ranks **HNTB Cos. #10** (was #11 in 2025). This intern sits in the **Austin office** and redesigns internal business workflows with applied AI (ChatGPT / Claude / automation platforms), bridging non-technical project teams and technical systems. It is **not** Strategic Technology Intern **R-31411** (Tallahassee/Tampa), **not** the 2026 Arlington AI/DIS intern, and **not** the 2026 Corporate IT Data & AI (no/low-code) intern.
