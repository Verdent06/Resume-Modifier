# Adobe

Adobe builds creative and marketing software used by hundreds of millions of people: Acrobat Studio, Express, Firefly, Creative Cloud, Experience Platform, Experience Manager, and GenStudio. The intern engineering brand is a solid B-tier creative-tools program — respected, not top-tier eng prestige (`reference/companies.md`). This req is applied product ML (generative AI features, agents, search/recs, production models), not Adobe Research / publication-track ML.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-tier (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** San Jose (HQ). This intern: San Jose, San Francisco, Austin, Seattle, Lehi (manager/team location).
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NASDAQ: ADBE); ~30,000 employees; flagship AI product is Firefly plus Sensei-class intelligence across Creative Cloud and Experience Cloud. Intern program is large and well-reviewed, not FAANG-SWE prestige.
- **Product focus:** AI-powered creative + marketing products; this intern ships applied ML into those surfaces (LLM apps, multimodal features, search/recs, production models).
- **Intern comp (2027 Machine Learning Engineer intern):** $45.00–$55.00/hr (US range; location-adjusted). JD listed range.
- **Work model:** Paid full-time internship between May–September 2027. All 2027 Adobe interns are co-located hybrid — assigned office + home; managers set office frequency.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently enrolled full-time, pursuing BS/MS/PhD in CS, CE, or related technical field; expected graduation December 2027 – June 2028. Ability to participate full-time May–September. Live-interview AI tools not permitted unless invited. Posted ~2026-08-31; listed end date June 11, 2027 (rolling review in practice).

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python required. Java or C++ a plus. JD also names PyTorch or TensorFlow; Hugging Face / LangChain / scikit-learn as pluses; AWS, Azure, or GCP or model-deployment/eval exposure as pluses.
- **Domains:** Applied generative AI and production ML in creative/marketing products — LLM applications, agents, multimodal features, search and retrieval, recommendations, evaluation frameworks. Not a research-lab / publications intern (that is a different Adobe Research track).
- **What wins:** End-to-end applied ML on the page — Python through use, a real LLM/ML system with eval and a delivery path (not `model.fit()` notebooks, not a paper list). Search/retrieval, agentic workflows, or production inference are on-axis. Intern funnel is OA-gated at this program's scale (`recruiting.md` §8); the resume still has to clear the ~7-second scan and hold up in tech rounds (`companies.md` bottleneck).
