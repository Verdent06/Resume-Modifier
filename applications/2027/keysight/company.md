# Keysight Technologies

Keysight Technologies (NYSE: KEYS) is an S&P 500 electronic design, simulation, prototyping, test, manufacturing, and optimization company — the Agilent/HP measurement-science spinoff (2014; HP lineage 1939). ~16,800 employees ship instruments plus software for communications, 5G, automotive, energy, quantum, aerospace/defense, and semiconductors. This intern seat is **not** R&D instrument firmware: it sits on Information Technology software engineering and automation teams doing full-stack programming, AWS, release automation, and enterprise product work. Flagship identity is measurement science (ADS, RF simulation, PathWave); intern work is internal IT full-stack.
