# Castleton Commodities International

Castleton Commodities International (CCI) is an independent global energy commodities merchant and infrastructure asset investor, founded in 2001 and headquartered in Stamford, CT. As a trader, CCI deploys capital on a proprietary basis in physical and financial commodity markets; as a strategic investor, it develops and holds energy infrastructure assets. The Global Data Science & Technology team builds the front-office analytics platform — Python services, APIs, Snowflake, AWS, and visualization — that commercial desks use to understand markets, forecast prices, and manage risk.
