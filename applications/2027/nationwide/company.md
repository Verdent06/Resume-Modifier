# Nationwide

Nationwide is a Columbus-rooted Fortune 100 mutual insurer and financial-services company (auto, home, life, farm, retirement, annuities). 2025 sales and premiums were $73.2 billion with $32.8 billion adjusted capital; S&P rates it A+. The Enterprise Analytics Office (EAO) sits in Finance and applies statistical inference and model validation across products. This intern is **applied GenAI** — LLMs, agents, and RAG on the insurance value chain — not an ML-research seat, not the catch-all Technology intern, and not Feature Engineer 100233.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-tier (`reference/companies.md` — Fortune 100 insurance/financials; legitimate intern program; limited national SWE brand vs B-tier finance-tech names; peer of State Farm / Allstate / Liberty Mutual / Principal)
- **HQ / offices:** Columbus, OH (One Nationwide Plaza). This req: Ohio - Columbus Metro, onsite
- **Valuation / signal:** Mutual; Fortune 100; ~$73.2B 2025 sales/premiums; $1.5B technology investment through 2028 with ~$100M/year tagged to AI; Allstate Benefits group-health acquisition (2025)
- **Product focus:** Multi-line insurance and financial products; EAO data scientists ship end-to-end analytics with business partners
- **Intern comp (2027 Generative AI Internship):** $23–$50/hr (national intern range on the JD)
- **Work model:** Paid intern, May–August 2027, onsite Columbus, OH; posting is Full time (internship). Sibling Technology intern is a 12-week, 40 hours/week program — treat this EAO seat as the same summer window
- **Clearance / eligibility:** No class-year exclusive on this JD. "Graduate candidates will be considered, from related majors" is additive, not undergrad-excluding. Some statistical and/or technical background preferred. Nationwide does not participate in STEM OPT Extension; sponsorship is not usually available for entry-level roles. US citizen / no sponsorship clears. Posted 2026-09-04; Workday `endDate` 2026-11-15

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python is the proficiency floor. ML libraries are listed as *e.g.* PyTorch, TensorFlow, Transformers, scikit-learn — not exclusive gates. Do not invent TensorFlow, scikit-learn, Kubernetes, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, Fusion, or Sentry.
- **Domains:** Applied GenAI in EAO: LLMs, AI agents, agentic workflows, NLP, vector databases, RAG, cloud, MLOps; Docker / Kubernetes / CI/CD for ML pipelines as a plus. Insurance value-chain efficiency with business partners. Statistical inference and robust model validation.
- **What wins:** A shipped agentic/LLM workflow with eval, plus a retrieval/embedding or ETL pipeline served to non-ML users — not notebook `model.fit()`, not a research paper. Python through use. Resume is the gate (`recruiting.md`: intern eligibility knockouts first; mid-size / non-tech-tech, human reads the PDF early).
