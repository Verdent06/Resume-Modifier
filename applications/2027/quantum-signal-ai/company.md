# Quantum Signal AI

Quantum Signal AI (QSAI) is a Ford Motor Company subsidiary in Saline, Michigan, that builds software, simulation, algorithms, and hardware prototypes for intelligent ground vehicles and mobile robotics. Founded in 1999 as Quantum Signal, it became QSAI in July 2019 to advance Ford R&D in mobility, sensing, perception, autonomy, and simulation. The shop is engineering-first: AI is in the name, but internships stress fundamentals, mentorship, and hands-on problem-solving rather than treating LLMs as the product. Historic HQ is the Union School (200 N. Ann Arbor Street). This packet is the **Software Engineering Intern – Tools and Prototypes** seat only — not the sibling Embedded and Real-Time Software Intern.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-TIER (`reference/companies.md` C-TIER row, after Ford) — Ford-sub R&D shop; resume-first mid-size / non-tech-tech (`recruiting.md` Part I §5)
- **HQ / offices:** Saline, MI — **200 N. Ann Arbor Street, 48176** (historic Union School). This intern is **in-person Saline**
- **Valuation / signal:** Wholly owned Ford subsidiary (2019). Not a separate funding round. Local Michigan mobility R&D brand; less national intern-prestige than Ford corporate
- **Product focus:** Engineering tooling and prototype software used by Ford and QSAI engineers, researchers, and designers — plus broader vehicle software, real-time sim, algorithms, and hardware prototyping
- **Intern comp (2027 Tools and Prototypes SWE):** **$29.00/hr** starting (JazzHR JD). Up to **$500** relocation assistance (terms apply). 401(k) participation; Ford AX-Plan vehicle discounts
- **Work model:** Paid; Summer 2027 (typically May/June–August); **onsite Saline**; office hours ~8am–5pm with some flexibility. Interviews may be virtual; the intern is not remote
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Current undergrad in CS / CE / SWE / related; **student status through the internship and the academic term after**. Legally authorized to work in the US. **Visa sponsorship is not available.** No GPA floor on this JD

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Compiled (C++, Go *etc.*) plus scripting (Python, JavaScript *etc.*) and web (HTML, CSS, JS). Screen on languages the candidate actually has (C++, Python, TypeScript, HTML/CSS). Do **not** invent Go, Java, GCP, IAM/Entra/MSAL, or Linux/Windows as claimed OS fluency if they are not in the pool.
- **Domains:** Internal engineering tools, prototype software for advanced vehicle features, SDLC (design → implement → test → release), CI/CD / build processes, Linux+Windows shops, REST and modern web (React / Node / TS / Flask as bonus). Company identity is vehicle autonomy / sim / perception — this *seat* is tools and prototypes, not an embedded/ROS intern.
- **What wins:** Resume-first C-tier screen (`recruiting.md` intern: knockouts, then the PDF). Compiled-language depth through use (not Skills-only C++); shipped tooling / REST / web that other engineers consume; CI/CD or a real build story; impact metrics on hours saved, failure recovery, or delivery. Local Saline/Ann Arbor commute is a logistics plus this JD cannot encode. Do not fabricate vehicle autonomy, ROS, GCP, or a Ford internship.
