# ABB

ABB Ltd is a Zurich-headquartered technology company in electrification and automation. It connects engineering and software so utilities, industry, and transport run leaner and cleaner ("Engineered to Outrun"). Continuing businesses are **Electrification**, **Motion**, and **Automation** (formerly Process Automation). **Robotics** is reported as discontinued operations (signed divestment to SoftBank). This intern sits in **USABB ABB Inc.** Information Systems under an **IS Manager** at the **U.S. corporate HQ in Cary, NC** — building an AI layer (agents, tools, data connectors) on business systems, not a robotics/PLC product-SWE seat and not a research-scientist rotation.
