# Northrop Grumman

Northrop Grumman (NYSE: NOC) is a U.S. aerospace and defense prime — stealth aircraft, space, and mission electronics — and the best-paying traditional prime on the intern market (~$40/hr class). This req sits in **Mission Systems**, **Navigation Intelligence and Connectivity (NIC)** in San Diego: software for positioning, navigation, and timing (PNT), connectivity, and intelligence products that keep platforms oriented when GPS is contested. NIC's navigation line includes assured PNT / inertial-GPS systems (EGI-M / LN-351 M-Code and the LN-family IMUs) plus comms/intel software — not Aeronautics (B-21) and not Space Systems satellites. Intern work is general SWE that may land in embedded, Agile, DevOps, integration/test, or automation on those mission systems.

Must be able to obtain and maintain a U.S. Government security clearance (U.S. citizenship prerequisite) **and** Program Special access. ## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Preferred or-list is **C, C++, Java, Python, Rust**. Honest intern floor is **C++ and Python in bullets**. Do not invent Java, C-as-language, MATLAB, LabVIEW, RTOS, Rhapsody, GreenHills, or Rust.
- **Domains:** Design/develop/document/test/debug software with logical/mathematical solutions; possible landing in Embedded SWE, Agile, DevOps, SW Integration and Test, or Software Automation; multidisciplinary work with equipment/systems engineers. Preferred tools: Git, Jira, Jenkins, Linux, containerization, DSP, real-time/embedded, Agile/SCRUM, GDB/debuggers, Visual Studio.
- **What wins:** Because the bottleneck is the resume (`recruiting.md` intern funnel; B-tier defense primes), a one-page PDF that proves C++ and Python through use, shipped software, and deterministic/real-time or production-delivery discipline (zero-allocation constraints, DSP, Git/CI/containers, test/debug) passes this screen. Do not fabricate Java, RTOS, RF lab tools, or an existing clearance. Full-stack Python delivery plus real-time C++/DSP both map; PE/search-fund GTM framing does not.
