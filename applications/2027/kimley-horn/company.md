# Kimley-Horn

Kimley-Horn is one of the largest planning, engineering, and design consulting firms in the U.S. (~10,000 employees), repeatedly named to Fortune's "100 Best Companies to Work For." Its Technology & Innovation team builds **internal full-stack software** that helps civil engineers deliver projects faster and more consistently — workflow-automation utilities, system integrations across CAD/engineering/document platforms, geospatial web tools, and increasingly applied-AI features (retrieval-augmented generation, multi-agent orchestration). The intern joins the corporate team in Raleigh/Cary, NC to support development, enhancement, and post-deployment maintenance of products in that internal portfolio.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated (not in `reference/companies.md`) — large, stable non-tech-tech engineering consultancy
- **HQ / offices:** HQ Raleigh, NC; this role is in-office at Cary / Raleigh, NC
- **Valuation / signal:** Employee-owned; Fortune "100 Best Companies to Work For" (multi-year); ~10,000-person firm
- **Product focus:** Internal full-stack web apps for civil engineering — workflow automation, system/CAD integrations, geospatial tools, applied-AI (RAG / multi-agent) features
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** Not stated in JD
- **Work model:** In-office, Summer 2027; office hours 7:30a–5:30p Mon–Thu, 7:30a–11:30a Fri
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Must be legally authorized to work in the U.S. without employer sponsorship; pursuing a Bachelor's in CS/IT/related
