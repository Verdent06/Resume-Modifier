# Pylon

Pylon is an agentic customer support platform purpose-built for B2B companies — a modern, AI-native replacement for legacy help-desks like Zendesk, Intercom, and Salesforce Service Cloud. Its core insight is that B2B support now happens in shared channels (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, WhatsApp) rather than email/ticket forms, and legacy tools were never built for that world. Pylon lets human agents and AI agents collaborate on customer work — investigating, resolving, and acting on signals across every channel — while enriching each interaction with deep account-level context and turning support into a growth driver (flagging upsell opportunities for sales and product feedback for PMs). Founded November 2022 (YC W23) by Marty Kausas (CEO), Advith Chelikani, and Robert Eng — engineers out of Airbnb, Samsara, and Affinity.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Go (Golang) backend, TypeScript/JavaScript (React frontend)
- **Domains:** AI-native SaaS, agentic workflow automation, B2B post-sales (support/success) tooling, omnichannel integrations, full-stack product engineering on AWS
- **What wins:** Product features shipped end-to-end for real users; strong full-stack fundamentals (frontend framework + backend service/API + a datastore + cloud) with defensible trade-offs; demonstrated ability to build fast, prototype, and own work under ambiguity; heavy, disciplined use of AI in the build loop; prior internship or side projects building actual product features; experience navigating a high-growth startup. Tech-stack overlap (React, Go, GraphQL, AWS) is an explicit bonus, not a filter — the binding signal is shipped full-stack product work plus AI-native building fluency.
