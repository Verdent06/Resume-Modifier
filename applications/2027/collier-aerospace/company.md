# Collier Aerospace

Collier Aerospace is a small aerospace CAE software company (founded 1995 by Craig and Ivonne Collier; NASA Langley ST-SIZE license → HyperSizer → HyperX®). HyperX is a .NET desktop application for classical aerospace failure analysis, stress reporting, and sizing optimization of metal and composite structures — space launch, reusable rockets, commercial airframes, business jets, eVTOL, plus high-performance composites. NASA, AFRL, and OEM engineers use it. This intern sits in Research Engineering in Raleigh and writes C# tools that connect emerging AI workflows (MCP servers, RAG, local LLMs) to HyperX — not Application Engineering (customer HyperX training) and not Methods & Automations (analysis-code / Fortran-adjacent).
