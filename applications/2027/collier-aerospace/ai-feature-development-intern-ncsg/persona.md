# Persona — AI Feature Development Intern - NCSG, Collier Aerospace

Recruiter lens shared by the writer and grader. Abstract bar signals only — no candidate entry names, no include/omit table.

## Role Summary

A Summer 2027 onsite internship in Raleigh, NC on Collier Aerospace's Research Engineering team. The intern collaborates with engineering and research to define AI tool use-cases, then plans, designs, prototypes, and tests C# features that make AI tools interoperable with HyperX® — Collier's .NET desktop CAE product for structural analysis and sizing optimization. Day-to-day also includes supporting existing MCP servers and RAG systems. JD surface: CS/SWE student with AI/ML interest; OOP statically typed language (C# / C++ / Java; C#/.NET preferred); large-codebase collaboration; DSA; concurrency/thread safety; databases and structured formats (JSON, XML). Preferred: custom AI workflows, local LLMs or smaller models, data science (NumPy / scikit-learn / PyTorch), context-retrieval pipelines. Company identity: a small NASA-heritage aerospace CAE ISV shipping HyperX to launch, airframe, eVTOL, and high-performance-composite customers — not a generic GenAI internship and not an application-engineering/training seat.

## Screen Criteria

Pass signals:
- Shipped AI workflows, local-model inference or fine-tuning, or context-retrieval / embeddings with a quality or latency number — not a notebook `model.fit()` or a thin API wrapper.
- An object-oriented statically typed language (C++, C#, or Java) demonstrated **in bullets**, not Skills alone; C#/.NET preferred but C++ satisfies the or-list.
- Concurrency and thread safety as an architectural decision (locks, atomics, worker isolation) with a bound (latency, FPS, failure rate).
- Databases or structured data (SQL, embeddings store, JSON/XML-class formats) through use.
- Large-codebase / product-engineering flavor: extending a real system, release artifacts, or collaboration — not a one-file script.
- Impact metrics (accuracy lift, latency, cost, throughput, scale) as closers.
- One-page, parseable layout; live GitHub.

Anti-patterns:
- Leading as a PE/search-fund, campaign-finance, or pure frontend intern with AI only in Skills.
- Claiming C#, .NET, scikit-learn, NumPy, MCP, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, or Fusion without evidence.
- C++ / concurrency buried below the fold on a HyperX desktop-product team.
- Notebook ML without delivery; metric-free architecture theater.
- Inventing an aerospace internship, HyperX, or NC State enrollment.
