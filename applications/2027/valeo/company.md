# Valeo

Valeo is a Paris-listed automotive-technology supplier (Euronext) that ships electrification, ADAS, interior-experience, and lighting systems to OEMs and new-mobility players. The group is organized as POWER (powertrain + thermal), BRAIN (vehicle automation, SDV software, in-cabin UX), LIGHT (lighting, wipers, sensor cleaning), and Valeo Service (aftermarket). This Troy, MI intern sits in Engineering Software on BRAIN-adjacent Software-Defined Vehicle work: C++ middleware, APIs, and OS components, plus backend services for AI-enhanced in-cabin experiences — not a lighting/thermal hardware seat and not an ML-training lab. The software product line is Valeo anSWer (apps + hardware-agnostic middleware such as vOS/vCORE + integration/validation services).
