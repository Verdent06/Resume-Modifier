# Schonfeld

Schonfeld is a global multi-manager hedge fund (Schonfeld Strategic Advisors) that allocates capital to internal and partner portfolio managers across four strategies: Quant, Tactical, Fundamental Equity, and Discretionary Macro & Fixed Income. The firm invests in proprietary technology, infrastructure, and risk analytics so those books can trade. This intern seat is Schonfeld Technology — not a PM/quant-research desk: design, develop, deploy, and support applications and platforms under Agile, assigned to **Treasury Technology** (securities lending, financing, repo, FX hedging, collateral, margin; interfaces with the Treasury Desk, PMs, prime brokers, OMS/EMS) or **Compliance Technology** (intraday monitoring, position limits, surveillance, trading restrictions, global regulatory reporting). Interns spend ten weeks writing software that improves functionality, performance, stability, efficiency, and scalability of the research and trading platform, then present a summer-long project. Prior finance is not required.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`) — multi-manager hedge fund; sits next to Garda / Blackstone / Bloomberg in the B table, not Citadel/HRT S-tier prestige. Intern cash is A-band ($125–145k annualized).
- **HQ / offices:** New York, NY (HQ, 590 Madison Ave). This intern is NYC onsite. Other offices include Miami, London, Hong Kong, Singapore.
- **Valuation / signal:** Multi-manager platform competing with Millennium / Citadel for PMs (Business Insider: ~$17B, Dec 2025). Form ADV (Jul 2026) lists large RAUM — do not treat RAUM as fee-paying capital. Founded 1988.
- **Product focus:** Research and trading platform software — Treasury (financing/collateral/margin) and Compliance (surveillance / regulatory reporting), not an HFT matching engine and not a consumer product.
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern):** $125,000–$145,000 annual base, prorated to the 10-week term (JD). Simplify lists $60.10–$69.71/hr. Performance bonus named on Simplify; housing unpublished on this JD.
- **Work model:** Paid, **in-person New York**, 10 weeks, manager + mentor, summer-long project with an end-of-program presentation; possible FT conversion.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Student pursuing a technical or quantitative degree (Bachelor's / Master's on Simplify). No published GPA or class-year window on the posting. Greenhouse required: US work authorization, future visa sponsorship, GPA, degree type, and **“Are you graduating in the summer or fall of 2027?”** — Expected May 2028 answers **No**.
