This intern sits in **MTS Headquarters, Eden Prairie, MN** on **Platform Realtime Software** (`02M00 - 11700 Plat Realtime Sftwre`) — not Miller Electric welding software in Appleton, not North Star Imaging X-ray/CT in Rogers, not a corporate IT rotation.
