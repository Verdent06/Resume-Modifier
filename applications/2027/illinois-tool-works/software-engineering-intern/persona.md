# Software Engineering Intern at Illinois Tool Works (MTS Systems)

## Role Summary

A paid **2027 MTS Software Engineering Internship** (Workday **JR10261**) at **MTS Headquarters, Eden Prairie, MN** — full-time, onsite, flexible summer start/end. The intern owns one project with a manager and mentor, presents to MTS business leaders, and networks across ITW Minnesota divisions. The JD *surface* is a generic intern gate: rising junior or senior, 3.0 GPA, communication, multitasking, Office, leadership/teamwork, onsite Eden Prairie; previous internship preferred. The *company* identity is test-and-simulation systems (F1 / auto / aero / materials labs) and this req's org is **Platform Realtime Software** — not Miller Electric embedded welding software, not NSI X-ray/CT in Rogers, not a corporate IT helpdesk intern.

Soft JD items (Office, "eager to learn") are not screen differentiators.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals:** GPA ≥ 3.0 visible; Expected month+year; one-page single column; shipped software in Experience; JD languages (none named) not stuffed; C++ or another systems language *through use* if the page claims real-time fit; a project the intern can present to business leaders; onsite Eden Prairie is a form gate, not a resume line.

**Anti-patterns:** notebook ML or PE/lead-gen as the lead story; Skills-only Office/C++/LabVIEW with no bullet; club membership as "leadership"; invented test-rig, motion-control, or DAQ internships; two pages; year-only graduation date.
