# Rivet Industries

Rivet builds **Integrated Task Systems** — rugged, body-worn hardware fused with software, sensors, AI, and networking — for industrial workforces and defense personnel. Flagship work sits on soldier-borne AR / Soldier Borne Mission Command (U.S. Army prime) and austere-site / sustainment tools; the San Jose engineering seat writes C++/C# that runs at the edge and talks to cameras, IMUs, and displays. Founded **2024**; CEO **Dave Marra** (ex-Palantir mixed reality; Microsoft **IVAS** program director). **Do not mix with Rivet construction comms (Oakland).** Partners named on rivet.us: Northrop Grumman (secure defense/government ITS) and Palantir (AI-powered spatial intelligence at the edge).
