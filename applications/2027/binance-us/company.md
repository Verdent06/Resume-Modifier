# Binance.US

Binance.US is a licensed and regulated U.S. cryptocurrency exchange where users buy, trade, hold, and earn digital assets. Operating as the U.S. arm of Binance, it runs a high-throughput trading and custody platform under heavy regulatory scrutiny, which makes backend correctness, auditability, and distributed-systems reliability the core engineering concerns. The engineering org is actively in a replatform effort, building product features across backend, frontend, and full-stack on a Java/Spring Boot + TypeScript/React stack deployed on AWS.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated in doctrine (closest peer: **Coinbase, B-tier** — crypto exchange, ~95% resume rejection, crypto-market volatility shapes hiring; Binance.US is smaller and carries heavier regulatory overhang)
- **HQ / offices:** Palo Alto, California, USA (role is remote, United States)
- **Valuation / signal:** Founded 2019; 201–500 employees; growth-stage; reported $200M seed (2022). Crypto-exchange brand with regulatory baggage from the broader Binance entity.
- **Product focus:** Regulated U.S. crypto exchange — buy/trade/hold/earn digital assets; trading engine, custody, and account services on distributed backend.
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** $35/hr
- **Work model:** Paid, remote (United States), internship
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's in CS/SWE or related technical field; no clearance.
