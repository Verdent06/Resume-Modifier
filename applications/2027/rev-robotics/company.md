# REV Robotics

REV Robotics designs and ships educational and competitive robotics hardware and software used by FIRST teams and STEM classrooms. Flagship products include the SPARK MAX motor controller and the Control Hub / Expansion Hub, plus student-facing libraries, example programs, and the REV Hardware Client for configure/update over USB and Wi‑Fi. Founded in 2014 in Carrollton, TX by Greg Needel and David Yanoshak (FIRST mentors; RIT mechanical / UT Austin electrical). The intern job is product software — firmware *or* end-user libraries — not a ROS/autonomy research seat and not the electrical-engineering intern twin.
