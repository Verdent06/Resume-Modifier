## Cover letter / "Why APL / why this internship?" (paste if there is a box)

I am applying for the 2027 Internship - Sensor Systems/Data Analytics on the Air and Missile Defense Sector team in Laurel (req 59958) for **Summer 2027**.

I want to spend the summer on applied data work that has to survive messy, multi-source inputs: ingest, normalize, score or fuse, and measure whether the output is actually better. That is this posting — signal and data-processing algorithms, multi-sensor fusion, and data science / statistical analysis / applied ML on large multi-dimensional datasets — not a BI dashboard internship and not a generic SWE intern.

I do not have radar or classified sensor internships. The analog I can defend is multi-source public and operational data already shipped to people who are not me:

- At Lyndbrook Capital I aggregated EPA ECHO and MassGIS into a unified PWSID entity database, delivered 800+ Day-1 acquisition targets, and built a Review Velocity score that filtered those to a 280-lead shortlist at 35% precision against the fund's revenue criteria.
- At Michigan Data Consulting I replaced portal searches and irregular Excel exports (~2 hours per committee) with a Requests + Pandas ETL and a deterministic PAC-ranking engine, eliminating ~800 hours of manual pulls across 400 tracked PACs.
- On Vylet, a live product at $1,500 MRR, I built a pure-Python triangulated consensus score (query + state registry + website crawl) and an asyncpg SQL layer with injection-safe timestamp checks that re-scrapes stale records.
- On SignalWeaver I LoRA-fine-tuned Llama 3.1 8B on 3,454 Financial PhraseBank entries and lifted sentiment accuracy from 81% to 96% on a held-out set, then combined fundamentals and sentiment in an out-of-sample regression (3.39% R²) so the score was not just fitting noise. Research assistant, not investment advice.

I have not used Tableau, Power BI, Snowflake, or Databricks. I would ramp on the team's approved stack rather than pretend I already have it.
