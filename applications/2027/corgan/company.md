# Corgan

Corgan Associates, Inc. is an employee-owned architecture and interior design firm founded in 1938 and headquartered in Dallas. It designs airports, data centers, education, healthcare, commercial, and other building types. Architectural Record's 2026 ranking lists Corgan as the No. 4 U.S. architecture firm (~$676M 2025 revenue). The intern sits on **Business Technology** (IT Pathways) — internal full-stack tools, dashboards, APIs, and applied AI that architects actually use — not a studio design intern and not a CAD drafter.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated (not in `reference/companies.md`) — large AEC firm; intern is corporate IT / Business Technology, peer of Kimley-Horn Technology & Innovation
- **HQ / offices:** Dallas, TX HQ (this intern: primarily onsite Dallas). Firm copy: 24 locations, 1,400+ team members globally (Corgan Fact Sheet, September 2026)
- **Valuation / signal:** Private / employee-owned. 2025 global revenues **over $660M** (firm fact sheet); Architectural Record 2026 Top 300: **$675.75M**, rank **#4**. ~98% repeat-client business (firm copy)
- **Product focus:** Internal web apps, data visualization, APIs/integrations, and applied AI/ML for architectural design workflows, project management, and client visualization (AEC Business Technology)
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Development Intern):** Paid; rate unlisted on this req (C-tier intern **$22–38/hr** band). **$3,000** IT Pathways scholarship is a separate, required companion submission
- **Work model:** Paid; Temporary Full-Time; **10–12 weeks Summer 2027**; primarily **onsite Dallas, TX**. IT Pathways program
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Pursuing BS/MS in CS, Software Engineering, IT, Data Science, or related. **At least one semester remaining after Summer 2027.** Scholarship PDF: legally authorized to work in the U.S. No GPA cutoff on this JD. Do not invent C#, Java, .NET, Azure, CAD/BIM internships

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD floor is **at least one** of C#, Python, JavaScript, Java, etc. Preferred web: HTML, CSS, JavaScript frameworks (**React or Angular**). Preferred: SQL, Git, cloud (Azure, AWS, or GCP), AI/ML exposure. Nice: data viz, .NET, CAD/BIM *interest*, mobile, UX/UI. Do **not** invent C#, Java, .NET, Azure, CAD/BIM, or an architecture internship.
- **Domains:** Internal full-stack for practicing architects — design-workflow tools, project-management integrations, dashboards, applied AI (CV / generative design / spatial analysis as intern *projects*, not prior must-haves).
- **What wins:** Because there is no named OA, the PDF plus GitHub is the front door (`recruiting.md` Part I §5 mid-size / non-tech-tech; Part III §11). A one-pager that shows shipped web + REST + SQL + deploy, Angular or React through use, and stakeholder delivery to non-engineers matches this intern. Do not lead as an ML-research intern, a C++ DSP intern, or a studio architecture intern.
