# Arconic

Arconic is a privately held aluminum manufacturer: sheet, plate, extrusions, and Kawneer architectural products. The lineage is Alcoa (1888) → Arconic Inc → **1 April 2020** Howmet retained engines / fasteners / structures / wheels while rolled products spun to Arconic Corp. Apollo Funds + Irenic completed the acquisition **18 August 2023** ($5.2B including debt; $30/share; delisted NYSE:**ARNC**). HQ is **201 Isabella Street, Suite 400, Pittsburgh, PA 15212**; ~**11,300** employees (2023 sustainability report). **This folder is the Davenport Works Software Developer Intern packet only** (Oracle CX **114233**). Davenport Works is the country's largest aluminum rolling mill (opened 1948; 2,000+ people; 4879 State Street, Bettendorf / Riverdale, IA — Quad Cities). The Oracle page can carry a Kawneer header; this seat is mill IT Applications, not a Kawneer architectural plant. Do not invent mill/metallurgy, CAD, or Pittsburgh ServiceNow work for the candidate.
