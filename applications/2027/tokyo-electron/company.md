# Tokyo Electron (TEL)

Tokyo Electron Limited (TSE: 8035) designs and sells semiconductor production equipment — etch, deposition, coater/developer, and cleaning tools fabs use to make chips, not the chips themselves. FY2026 net sales were a record ¥2,443.5 billion (official FY26 Q4). The JD cites a global workforce of over 20,000. U.S. operations sit under TOKYO ELECTRON AMERICA, INC.; the U.S. headquarters moved from the Grove Blvd campus to the **RiverSouth** office in Austin (West Riverside / South First / Barton Springs; Statesman 2024). This intern req is a **Business Intelligence Analyst seat supporting Service and Sales Support operations** (source-data assessment, validation, reports/dashboards, workflows) — not an ATG research/simulation intern, not a process-engineer intern, and not the Data Engineer sibling that names Snowflake/Power BI.
