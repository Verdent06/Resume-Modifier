# Midland States Bank

Midland States Bancorp, Inc. (NASDAQ: **MSBI**) is a community-based financial holding company headquartered in Effingham, Illinois, and the sole shareholder of **Midland States Bank** (Illinois state-chartered; Member FDIC; NMLS #411141; Equal Housing Lender). The bank provides commercial and consumer banking, equipment financing, merchant card services, trust and investment management, and insurance/financial planning. Q2 2026 IR: total assets approximately **$6.70B**, loans **$4.2B**, deposits **$5.7B**; Wealth Management assets under administration approximately **$4.78B**. Earnings deck: **53** branches/offices in Illinois and Missouri, with commercial growth emphasis in St. Louis and Chicago. Strategic copy names a tech-forward community-bank path: fintech services, data/analytics, and **automating back-office processes using AI and RPA**. The intern AI team (Strategic Engineering and Innovation) ships applied AI into bank workflows — the 2026 AI Engineer intern seat produced an internal RAG policy assistant (**SpotCheck**), an OpenAI-compatible FastAPI LLM proxy, eval/governance work, and an employee AI Solutions Hub. That is applied enterprise AI at a community bank, not a research lab and not generic branch IT.
