## Role Summary

A paid **Summer 2027 Intern - AI Solutions Engineer** (Workday **JR1461**, $18/hour) on Midland States Bank's AI team. Term: mid-May through early August 2027 (program start **May 17, 2027**). Full time, **40 hours**, Monday–Friday **8 a.m.–5 p.m.**, **onsite** at the assigned office in **St. Louis, MO**, **Effingham, IL**, or **Rockford, IL**. JD: must reside local and report to that office. Program apps close **2027-01-04**; this req `endDate` **2026-12-31**.

Day-to-day is **applied bank-workflow AI, not ML research and not generic SWE**: own or contribute to at least one production-focused AI project from idea through delivery; build AI agents and applications for practical business use cases; connect enterprise data/systems via APIs, Model Context Protocol (MCP), or other integrations; enhance the Midland AI platform and workflow automations; test emerging models and measure adoption/business impact; collaborate with AI/engineering and business partners under governance, security, and testing; maintain BSA / bank-regulation compliance.

**Do not mix** with Intern - Associate Solutions Engineer - Data Warehouse **JR1462**, Intern - IT Infrastructure **JR1471**, Intern - Security Analyst **JR1473**, Intern - Financial Services Cloud Admin - Salesforce **JR1470**, or the closed **2026 Intern – IT – AI Engineer** ($15/hr).

Company identity (`company.md`): NASDAQ **MSBI** community bank (~$6.7B assets). Engineering identity is **community-bank applied-AI platform and workflow automation** (SpotCheck-class RAG assistants, LLM gateway/API, eval/governance, employee adoption) — not a research lab and not branch IT.

## Screen Criteria (abstract)

Pass signals:
- Hands-on AI agents and applications (multi-step orchestration, tool/model integration, structured output) — not a thin chatbot wrapper or a notebook demo.
- Workflow automation with a before/after: pipelines or recurring workers that replace manual work, scored throughput, or hours saved — the JD's Midland AI platform / automation line.
- Integration evidence: APIs, SQL, cloud, or scheduled data wiring that connects a system a business partner uses. MCP is a JD integration noun — prove honest API/data integration; do not invent MCP.
- RAG / LLM / GenAI through use (retrieval, embeddings, or LLM-in-a-pipeline) with architecture the intern can defend (`resume.md` keyword-through-use).
- Testing / eval / governance analog: eval harnesses, quality/faithfulness lifts, fault tolerance, or measured adoption — so "AI solutions" reads as engineering, not a prompt dump. BSA is a compliance duty, not a skills-line.
- Application development: Flask/FastAPI or equivalent shipped API/app; Python + SQL demonstrated in bullets, not Skills alone.
- Impact metrics that size hours saved, quality, cost, throughput, or adoption, landed as the closer (`resume.md` §4).
- Community-bank / messy-business-process analog kept visible in the lead window because of track divergence: scored insight or automation for a non-builder stakeholder — without claiming a prior bank internship.

Anti-patterns:
- ML-research framing: publications, novel methods, LoRA/`model.fit()` as the identity with no agent, no automation, no delivery (`recruiting.md` §13 research vs applied split). This req is AI Solutions Engineer.
- Off-axis lead: pure C++ audio-DSP, club-ops, or a generic SWE identity with agents buried — wrong role.
- Agentic/LLM/RAG claims only in Skills with no supporting system.
- Inventing Microsoft Excel/Teams as engineering tools, MCP, BSA/AML platforms, banking-core, Copilot, Power Platform, Salesforce FSC, Snowflake, Databricks, or Tableau.
- Mixing this req with JR1462 / JR1471 / JR1473 / JR1470 or the 2026 $15 AI Engineer intern.
- Metric-free architecture bullets the recruiter cannot size.
- Treating this as an OA-first FAANG SWE intern screen.
- No live GitHub on an intern engineering resume (`resume.md` §3).
