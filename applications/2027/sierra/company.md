# Sierra

Sierra builds a platform for enterprise companies to deploy production AI agents that handle customer experience at scale — partnering with SoftBank, Uber, Rivian, CLEAR, Sutter Health, and a large share of Fortune 50 CX (Prudential, Cigna, BCBS, Rocket Mortgage, and one in three of the largest banks). The engineering identity is shipped customer-facing agents, not notebook LLM demos: evals, RAG, agent tooling, and prompt engineering sit next to a product stack that prefers React, TypeScript, and Go. Co-founders Bret Taylor (OpenAI board chair; former Salesforce co-CEO / Facebook CTO) and Clay Bavor (Google Labs, Lens, Workspace) signal a high-bar, in-person SF culture (Trust, Customer Obsession, Craftsmanship, Intensity, Family).

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-tier (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco, CA (primary, in-person); New York, NY; growing offices in North America, Europe, and Asia. This intern req is on-site SF or NYC.
- **Valuation / signal:** $15.8B post-money Series E (May 2026, $950M; Tiger Global + GV; Benchmark, Sequoia, Greenoaks); $150M+ ARR in eight quarters; 40%+ of Fortune 50 as customers
- **Product focus:** Enterprise customer-experience AI agents — a platform for companies to build more human CX with AI
- **Intern comp (2027 Intern, Agent Development):** Not posted as hourly/salary. Intern package names a monthly stipend for health insurance, daily lunch, and free alphorn lessons (FT benefits listed separately on the posting)
- **Work model:** Paid intern, Summer 2027, on-site San Francisco or New York; employment type Intern; department Early Career → Intern
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Intern program open to students graduating December 2027 – June 2028. No citizenship/export-control gate named on this posting.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD preferred: React, TypeScript, and/or Go. Production agent work is also Python-shaped in the industry; do not invent Go on the resume.
- **Domains:** Production AI agents, enterprise customer experience, eval frameworks, agent tooling, RAG pipelines, prompt engineering, shipping product features
- **What wins:** Evidence of *shipped* AI/LLM systems in production — not prompt-playground work. Eval/RAG/agent-tooling through use. Full-stack shipping (React/TypeScript in bullets, not Skills-only) plus a builder/high-agency signal. Club-ops and notebook ML fail this screen. Resume is a real bottleneck at A-tier Ashby, so the page must look like someone who already ships agents and product, not someone pivoting into AI.
