# Palantir Technologies

Palantir builds software platforms that integrate an organization's data, decisions, and operations — Gotham (defense/intelligence), Foundry (commercial operational data), Apollo (continuous delivery into classified and commercial environments), and AIP. Interns on this req sit in Product Development (Dev / SWE), not Forward Deployed, and ship code into those products on small PD teams. `reference/companies.md` A-TIER Notes flag Defense/Gov identity, Karat interviews, and a unique Decomposition round that is not LeetCode; recruiter screen is the intern bottleneck.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-TIER — Excellent / Strong Signal (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Doctrine row: NYC, Denver, Palo Alto. This intern req: **Palo Alto, CA, on-site**. Apply form also lists New York, Washington DC, Seattle, Denver. Corporate HQ has moved (Denver 2020; Miami announced Feb 2026) — treat Palo Alto as the engineering/intern site for this packet, not a HQ claim.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public company (NYSE/Nasdaq: PLTR); S&P 500. Doctrine does not list a valuation; web market cap is large and volatile — do not treat a snapshot figure as hiring signal. Prestige is A-tier Defense/Gov + operational data platforms.
- **Product focus:** Gotham / Foundry / Apollo (+ AIP) — intern work is platform product development, not a Palantir-product-usage internship and not FDSE customer deployment
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer Intern):** JD this posting: **$10,500/month** estimated. Doctrine row: **$60/hr**. Levels.fyi Summer 2026: ~$60.58/hr ≈ $10,501/mo (NYC/Denver/Palo Alto), plus housing/relocation (and Palo Alto reports contingent RSUs on return). Record both; JD monthly figure is the posting-specific number.
- **Work model:** Paid Dev / Internship; on-site Palo Alto for this req; not fully remote. Term not dated on JD; this packet is **Summer 2027**. Interns contribute across the product lifecycle with a mentor; may tour customer sites. Collaboration with PMs, designers, PREs, and BD (FDEs / Deployment Strategists).
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Graduating **2028**; this should be the **final internship before graduating**. Engineering background (CS, Math, SWE, Physics). Coding in Java, C++, Python, JavaScript, or similar. Active US security clearance, or eligibility and willingness to obtain one, is **valued not required**. US work authorization is an apply-form question.
