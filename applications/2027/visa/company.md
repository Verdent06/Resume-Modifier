# Visa

Visa is a publicly traded global payments network that authorizes, clears, and settles digital transactions among consumers, merchants, financial institutions, and governments in 200+ countries. Software lives in Visa Technology and Operations: network processing, authorization, fraud/risk, Visa Checkout / acceptance products, and developer-facing platforms — high-reliability, low-latency transaction systems at network scale, not a trading desk and not a consumer-app startup. The US campus Software Engineer Intern is a generic 12-week engineering seat that may land on any technology team (Kafka/K8s self-serve, chatbots, payments prototypes, testing and monitoring tools, digital-currency routing, mobile banking app factory); the company's identity is still payments-scale, high-reliability transaction systems (similar profile to Mastercard per `companies.md`).

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Intern JD names Java, C/C++, C#, .NET, Python, JavaScript, HTML (proficiency in one or more). Screen tests polyglot proof in whatever the candidate actually ships — **do not invent Java, C#, or .NET**
- **Domains:** Generic intern SWE on payments-scale systems: design/code/test, automation and testing tools (Selenium, REST, Python, Groovy named as preferred), secure coding, full software lifecycle, monitoring/testing tools, occasional Kafka/Docker/Kubernetes or chatbot/payments-prototype work depending on team match
- **What wins:** Shipped full-stack or backend software with tests, Git through use, and CS fundamentals defensible in an Easy–Med HackerRank (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11). Payments-scale, high-reliability, or finance-adjacent delivery is the company differentiator, not a second track. Resume must clear the screen; **tech rounds are the binding intern gate** after invitation
