## University of Michigan — school typeahead (do this first)

Workday's school field is **not a text box**. It searches that company's institution list. You must **click a dropdown result**. Typed text that is not selected saves as unmatched freeform — recruiter school filters will not see you (Workday education matching; campus-name variants).

### Search order (click a result each time)

1. `Michigan` — scan for **University of Michigan-Ann Arbor** or **University of Michigan - Ann Arbor**.
2. `University of Michigan-Ann Arbor` (hyphen, IPEDS form).
3. `University of Michigan Ann Arbor` (spaces, no hyphen).
4. `University of Michigan – Ann Arbor` / `University of Michigan, Ann Arbor`.
5. `Ann Arbor` — then pick the university row, not a high school.

**Do not pick:** University of Michigan-Dearborn, University of Michigan-Flint, Michigan State University, Michigan Technological University.
