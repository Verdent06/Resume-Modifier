# Saab, Inc.

Saab, Inc. is the U.S. arm of Saab AB, a Swedish defense and security company (~28,000 people globally). U.S. headquarters is Syracuse, New York, with about ten U.S. sites. The East Syracuse (Collamer) site writes **Surveillance Software** for air traffic management (ATM) systems used at today's airports — surface-movement safety, tower automation, multilateration, ADS-B, and related surveillance products for the FAA, U.S. Armed Forces, and international/commercial partners. Adjacent Syracuse teams (Saab Sensis / Traffic Management heritage) ship Aerobahn, digital-tower, and sensor products; this co-op is the software-development seat on that ATM stack, not radar-hardware technician work and not a generic defense-IT ticket shop.
