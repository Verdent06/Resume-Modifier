# Universal Health Services (UHS)

Universal Health Services, Inc. (NYSE: UHS) is a Fortune 500 hospital operator headquartered in King of Prussia, PA. Through subsidiaries it runs acute-care hospitals, behavioral-health facilities, outpatient and ambulatory sites, an insurance offering, and a physician network across 40 U.S. states, Washington, D.C., Puerto Rico, and the United Kingdom. Corporate Information Services sits at the holding-company office and implements technology to improve healthcare quality and safety, patient care, and family engagement — hospital operations systems, not a product-ML lab.

This intern req (**367247**) is titled Software Engineer Intern* but the body is **Data Analytics Intern** on Corporate IS: reporting and dashboards, analyzing data, technical writing/documentation, IS project participation, and shadowing healthcare IS roles. Applied pipelines and analytics, not ML research.
