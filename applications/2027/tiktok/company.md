# TikTok

TikTok is ByteDance's global short-video and creation platform, operating at massive scale across recommendation, ads, and content infrastructure. This role sits inside Agentic Creation — TikTok Effect Platform (TTEP), the team that runs TikTok's creator-facing creation ecosystem (creation tools, website, and in-app surfaces) and is actively building AI agents and an operation platform to improve creator acquisition, activation, retention, and operational efficiency across the creation funnel.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-Tier in `reference/companies.md` — strong brand, excellent comp, respected eng culture
- **HQ / offices:** ByteDance/TikTok global HQ in Los Angeles and Singapore; US engineering offices include San Jose, LA, Seattle, and NYC
- **Valuation / signal:** ByteDance US; rec/infra/ads at massive scale; geopolitical/regulatory overhang on the brand
- **Product focus:** TikTok Effect Platform (TTEP) — creator tools, rewards/inspiration/activity/analytics/badge modules, and an emerging AI-agent operation layer supporting creation-business workflows
- **Intern comp (2027, campus intern):** $45/hr (JD-stated hourly range)
- **Work model:** 12-week internship, Summer 2027; engineering team is fully close-looped in the US for this role
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Must be currently enrolled in an undergraduate, graduate, or master's program in CS/CE/Software Development or related technical field; must be available for the full 12 weeks
