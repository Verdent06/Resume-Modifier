# Nike

NIKE, Inc. is a consumer-technology company whose Global Technology organization ships the flagship nike.com experience, five-star mobile apps, product-development tooling, and data platforms at retail scale. Engineering sits at the confluence of tech and sport: consumer-facing web/mobile product work plus the backend services, performance, and reliability those surfaces depend on. The undergraduate SWE intern (R-91111) is a 10-week paid Summer program onsite at World Headquarters in Beaverton — not a robotics, ML-research, or embedded rotation.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`, next to Adobe / MongoDB / Instacart)
- **HQ / offices:** Beaverton, OR — World Headquarters / Phil H. Knight Campus (this intern). Additional tech presence in Memphis, NYC, Boston (Converse).
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: NKE); Fortune-class consumer brand. Strong consumer/product name; limited SWE-prestige signal vs. FAANG (`companies.md` Notes).
- **Product focus:** nike.com + mobile apps, plus product/data platforms in Global Technology
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineering Undergraduate Internship):** unpublished on JD. Tech intern analog ~$32–48/hr + $3–6.5k WHQ housing stipend **[directional]** (`companies.md`)
- **Work model:** Paid; 10-week Summer internship; in-person Beaverton (Phil H. Knight Campus); relocation assistance. Start/end dates unpublished on posting.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Enrolled in a Bachelor's with expected graduation December 2027–Spring 2028. Background in CS, mathematics, statistics, or related technical field. 0–2 years work experience. Work authorization not stated on posting.
