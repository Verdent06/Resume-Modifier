# NIKE, Inc. Software Engineering Undergraduate Internship at Nike

## Role Summary

Ten-week paid Summer SWE intern onsite at NIKE World Headquarters (Phil H. Knight Campus, Beaverton). Interns support Global Technology across the enterprise: own a project aligned to the strategic roadmap, with senior leaders and experienced managers. JD surface is generic undergraduate SWE intern — foundational programming (Java, Python, JavaScript, C#, or Swift), readable/tested code, DS&A / OOP / web fundamentals; preferred web/mobile (React, Node.js, Xcode, Android Studio), cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP), version control, APIs, databases, collaborative coding. Company identity is consumer-facing digital at scale: nike.com, five-star mobile apps, product/data platforms — not a robotics, ML-research, or embedded rotation.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (Python as the honest required-language hit; TypeScript as JS-family evidence in bullets — do not relabel TypeScript as JavaScript in Skills). Keyword-through-use, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2).
- Shipped full-stack software: web or mobile surface, REST/APIs or backend services, a persistence decision, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12). Breadth across the stack plus depth in at least one layer.
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy HackerRank/Codility OA.
- Preferred through use: React; cloud platforms; version control; APIs; databases; testing/debugging; collaborative coding or code-review discipline.
- Consumer-facing or public-product flavor on that spine: digital experiences, public-facing APIs/workflows, performance/reliability of services users hit — memorable at Nike without replacing the SWE spine.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies December 2027–Spring 2028.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-line Java, C#, Swift, Node.js, JavaScript-as-listed-skill, Xcode, or Android Studio with zero bullet evidence (fabrication smell at a polyglot floor-signal company).
- Robotics / hard-real-time C++/DSP or notebook-ML / LoRA as the first-pass identity when the role tests consumer web/mobile + backend SWE.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a shippable product story.
- Invented Sentry, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, or other tools not in inventory.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
