# DRW

DRW is a Chicago proprietary trading firm that trades its own capital across fixed income, ETFs, equities, FX, commodities, and energy, and has expanded into real estate, venture capital, and cryptoassets. Engineering is core to the desk: City Scholars on this posting join the Exchange Data / Market Data team that captures, catalogs, indexes, and presents data from 70+ (JD) / 100+ (firm blog) financial exchanges, including low-level networking, binary protocol decode, matching-engine internals, and cloud scale at 180B+ messages per day. **This folder is the Spring 2027 City Scholars Software Developer Intern packet only** (Greenhouse **8220587** / req **10978**). It is not a general DRW summer SWE intern, not Quant, and not a non-UIUC campus hire. City Scholars is a UIUC Grainger / Discovery Partners Institute co-op: students remain full-time Illinois undergraduates while working part-time in Chicago.
