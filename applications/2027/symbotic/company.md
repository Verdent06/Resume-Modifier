# Symbotic

Symbotic (NASDAQ: **SYM**) builds an AI-powered, end-to-end warehouse automation platform: dense case storage, fleets of autonomous robots, and software that orchestrates tasking, retrieval, and pallet build for large retail and wholesale DCs. Walmart is the flagship customer and a major commercial relationship; the product identity is warehouse robotics and fleet software, not a generic IT intern rotation. HQ is 200 Research Drive, Wilmington, MA.
