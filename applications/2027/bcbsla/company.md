# Blue Cross Blue Shield of Louisiana (Louisiana Blue)

Louisiana Blue is an independent Blue Cross Blue Shield licensee (legal entity: Louisiana Health Service & Indemnity Company), licensed to sell only in Louisiana, with corporate HQ in Baton Rouge. Careers copy cites 3,000+ people; LinkedIn is **[directional]** ~2,095 employees / ~$3.7B. This intern sits on **Healthcare Analytics & Data Science**: stats, predictive, and ML models for patient utilization, outcomes, provider performance, risk-adjusted revenue, and medical-cost optimization, plus GenAI tools, wrangling/feature engineering/EDA, stakeholder insights, and IT support for model deployment.
