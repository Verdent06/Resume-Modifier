# Software Engineer Intern (Summer 2027 – Chicago) at Optiver

## Role Summary

Mentored SWE intern on a Chicago technology team at a global options market maker. Interns ship real production software — performance, reliability, and scalability of systems that power trading — not hypothetical intern tasks; code is expected to reach production. The JD surface is generalist high-bar SWE: CS or CE, junior-or-higher graduating Dec 2027–June 2028, proficiency in C++, Python, or Java, CS fundamentals, clean/well-architected code, analytical problem-solving, and collaboration with engineers, traders, and business teams. The firm’s identity is technology-driven market making. This is the SWE intern role, not quantitative trading or research. AI-enabled tools appear as trading/developer tooling, not as an ML-research mandate.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (general SWE — the JD requirements literally test programming in C++/Python/Java, CS fundamentals, clean architecture, and shipping production software)
- **differentiator:** HFT / market-making identity — performance, reliability, and scalability of production trading systems (real-time constraints, concurrency, memory discipline, measured latency/throughput)
- **track_divergence:** false — the differentiator is flavor on a general-SWE spine, not a second track. The screen still reads as general SWE (`reference/recruiting.md` III §11, `reference/resume.md` III §12); the systems/performance signal is what makes a candidate memorable here, not a re-target to ai-ml, robotics, or infra-only.

The spine is general SWE: ship real, defensible software; strong DS&A/CS fundamentals for a hard OA; breadth across the stack with depth in at least one layer. The differentiator is carried by whatever on-page work demonstrates real-time / low-latency / reliability engineering — kept prominent and deep, but the resume does not become an ML-research or FPGA page.

## Team & Bar

Optiver is S-tier quant/HFT (`reference/companies.md`) — Chicago market-making peer of IMC, with an OA-gated funnel (~1–2% intern acceptance). The screener is an engineer/recruiter at a firm where programming excellence and production discipline are floors. Funnel: application → HackerRank OA (coding + CS-fundamentals MCQ + Zap-N) → recruiter behavioral → technical screen → Super Day (algorithms + low-level/systems + behavioral). Comp is $80k USD base plus housing/flights (JD). What wins the human screen: C++ and/or Python proven in real systems work, not Skills-only; clean architecture the candidate can defend; production shipping with metrics; entrepreneurial ownership; curiosity about markets without fake desk experience. Class-year on page must show Expected May 2028 (inside Dec 2027–June 2028). Prior finance is not required.

## Screen Criteria

- At least one JD language (C++, Python, or Java) proven in bullets — not Skills-only. Honest C++ + Python depth beats inventing Java.
- High-performance / reliability / scalability **systems** depth surfaced on the page: real-time constraints, concurrency, lock-free or memory-discipline, latency/throughput, production correctness — the HFT-adjacent signal that separates this firm from a generic SWE screen.
- End-to-end ownership and production impact with metrics (latency, throughput, %, scale) — soft, metric-free claims read thin at this bar.
- Clean, well-architected systems the candidate can walk decision-by-decision; a live GitHub backing the claims.
- DS&A / CS fundamentals visible (coursework + engineered projects) for a hard OA that also tests OS/networks.
- Entrepreneurial / ownership signal and collaboration with non-engineers is a plus; markets curiosity (econ, financial-data systems) helps without requiring a trading internship.
- Class-year on page shows a current student graduating Dec 2027–June 2028 (Expected May 2028 is in window).

## Anti-Patterns

- Re-targeting to an ML-research / LoRA / agent-pipeline lead because the JD names “AI-enabled tools” — this is a general-SWE role at a trading firm.
- All-CRUD / all-web-app page with no systems-performance or production-reliability signal — reads generic and off-differentiator at a market maker.
- Java named in Skills with zero bullet evidence when the candidate cannot interview in it.
- Soft, metric-free shipping claims where latency/throughput/scale numbers are expected at a performance-obsessed firm.
- Finance-jargon padding with no engineering substance (prior finance is not required, and faking it reads worse than honest strong CS).
- Thin single-bullet filler entries that dilute a strong systems + production spine.
- Treating this as a quantitative-trading or research internship (probability puzzles as the resume story) instead of SWE.
