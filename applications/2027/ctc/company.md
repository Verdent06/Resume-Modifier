# Chicago Trading Company (CTC)

Chicago Trading Company is a privately held proprietary trading firm founded in 1995 that specializes in options market making and liquidity provision across global derivatives exchanges. The firm trades exclusively with its own capital across equities, interest rates, and commodities, pairing traders, quantitative researchers, and software engineers who build proprietary pricing, trading, and risk platforms end to end. Technology is core to the desk: release cycles are measured in days, and engineers ship tools that traders and quants use in real time. CTC sits in the Chicago prop-trading cluster alongside peers like IMC — established, tech-forward, and collaborative rather than a consumer-product shop.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated in `reference/companies.md`; treat as quant/trading-tech peer to IMC / mid–S-tier Chicago prop (not Jane Street / Citadel prestige band, but same funnel family).
- **HQ / offices:** Chicago, IL (HQ); also New York, London, Austin, Amsterdam. SE intern is Chicago; most FT offers Chicago with limited NY seats.
- **Valuation / signal:** Private prop firm (~600–780 employees in public estimates); designated / lead market-maker roles on major options venues; ~30 years of options MM franchise.
- **Product focus:** Proprietary trading tools and risk applications for options market making — price, trade, and manage risk in real time.
- **Intern comp (2027 SE):** $14,500/month + signing bonus; free downtown Chicago housing (per JD).
- **Work model:** Paid 8-week onsite internship, mid-June to early August 2027, Chicago relocate required; cohort with QT interns (Basics of Options, Mock Trading, Quant Curriculum).
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Bachelor's/Master's CS/CE/similar with Java, C++, or Python experience; graduate Dec 2027–June 2028; full program participation; one application per position type (SE vs QT) recommended.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Java, C++, Python (any of these; interview matched to preferred language)
- **Domains:** Trading tools, risk applications, short-cycle production delivery, options/pricing/risk exposure during the program; AI coding assistants and agentic tooling as daily developer habit (also AI built into trading/research/engineering systems)
- **What wins:** DS&A + OOP foundation with demonstrated success shipping production software on short cycles; C++/Python (or Java) proven in bullets not Skills-only; systems/latency fluency that reads as trading-tech adjacent; interest in tech + markets without prior finance required.
