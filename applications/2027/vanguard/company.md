# Vanguard

Vanguard is a client-owned investment manager (crew members, not shareholders-first) whose products — index funds, ETFs, advice, and retirement plans — exist to lower costs and improve long-term investor outcomes. College to Corporate (C2C) is the undergraduate intern pipeline into specialty rotations (IMDP, TLP, AFP and peers); conversion into those programs is the prize, not a generic summer. Technology and data work here serves business lines that exist for client investment success, not a consumer-app or trading-desk culture.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-TIER (`reference/companies.md`) — peer to Fidelity / Charles Schwab as a large asset-manager intern brand; Vault #2 financial-services internship (Extern 2026, directional)
- **HQ / offices:** Malvern, PA (global HQ / Valley Forge area). This req is **Charlotte, NC** (hybrid; Two North Falls Plaza analog). Other C2C sites: Dallas TX, Scottsdale AZ
- **Valuation / signal:** ~$10.1T AUM (Extern 2026); second-largest asset manager after BlackRock; investor-owned mutual-fund structure
- **Product focus:** Low-cost index/ETF and advice platforms; intern data work is insights for business lines that support client investment success
- **Intern comp (2027 IT / Data Analyst C2C):** this JD posts paid, no number. Extern 2027–28 guide: C2C ~$25–$58/hr by track; technology tracks ~$39–$56/hr; general/business ~$25–$38/hr. Housing stipend analog ~$1,850 or corporate housing some programs. Treat as analog only.
- **Work model:** Full-time paid summer internship; hybrid for majority of crew (`How We Work` on this JD). Official C2C: ~10 weeks, late May–early August. This posting: start **May/June 2027**
- **Clearance / eligibility:** In pursuit of bachelor's or master's with concentration in data and analytics, statistics, math, computer science, or related technical subject matter. Cumulative GPA **3.2+ preferred**. Official C2C page: college sophomores and juniors (graduate students also considered). **Vanguard is not offering visa sponsorship for this position.** Drug screening is a common C2C contingency (sister IM postings; not restated on this DA JD).

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** This Data Analyst JD names **no** programming languages and **no** BI/cloud tools. Do not invent Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, Python-as-required, or SQL-as-required. Python/SQL/Pandas count only when they appear through real work (`resume.md` keyword-through-use).
- **Domains:** Applied data analysis for business-line and client-outcome decisions; C2C IT / data & analytics — **not** the Data Science & AI graduate-flavored track and **not** Malvern IM/tech seats
- **What wins:** End-to-end ingest → clean/aggregate/score → insight or recommendation → measured outcome, communicated to a non-builder stakeholder (`resume.md` Part III §14 / `recruiting.md` Part III §13). Leadership and collaboration are explicit JD preferreds. Resume is the binding intern gate; digital assessment and HireVue follow. Do not optimize as generic SWE or as a Data Science research intern.
