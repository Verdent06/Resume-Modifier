# Lutron Electronics

Lutron Electronics Co., Inc. is a privately held lighting-control and automated-shading company. Founder Joel Spira shipped the first solid-state electronic dimmer in **1961**; those early products sit in the Smithsonian. The company designs and manufactures dimmers, whole-home and whole-building lighting-control systems, and motorized shades for residential and commercial buildings, sold in 100+ countries. Application software is how those systems get designed, commissioned, scheduled, and operated — not a generic CRUD internship and not a firmware-only seat. HQ is Coopersburg, PA (7200 Suter Road), northwest of Philadelphia; this co-op sits at the Philadelphia engineering office (2929 Arch Street, Suite 1000), a few blocks from Drexel.
