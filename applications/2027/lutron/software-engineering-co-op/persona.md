## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- At least one real programming language demonstrated **through use in bullets**, not Skills-only — JD names none, so do not invent Java or C#.
- Full-stack / general-SWE spine: shipped applications, APIs/services, test/debug, code-review adjacency, infrastructure/deployment, Git — the literal intern filter (`resume.md` §12).
- Lighting-control / hardware-adjacent differentiator visible in the top half: real-time or deterministic constraints, concurrency/memory discipline, fault-tolerant delivery, reliability metrics — without turning the page into a hobby-audio specialist packet or inventing dimmers, shades, CAS, RA2, or HomeWorks.
- Impact metrics that size performance, reliability, hours saved, or scale — not vanity (uptime, LOC, coverage %).
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028`; GPA 3.66 satisfies the 3.20 floor if they look.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Inventing Java, C#, embedded firmware, Lutron product names, or a lighting internship the page cannot defend.
- Leading as an ML/agentic PE-deal intern or notebook-ML researcher because the parent is “hardware.”
- Skills-only language with zero bullet evidence (fabrication smell).
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + reliability spine.
- Vanity metrics standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
- Two pages or year-only graduation date.
