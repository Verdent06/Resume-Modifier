# Notion

Notion is a collaborative AI workspace where teams and agents think together — documents, databases, wikis, and product surfaces in one block-based editor. Engineering is TypeScript + React product work on a real-time collaborative editor, plus Notion AI (LLMs, embeddings, agents as product features, not a research lab). The intern program is hybrid San Francisco or New York with required relocation and in-office Anchor Days. `reference/companies.md` frames it as a good product with a small eng team and limited brand signal for SWE; the screen still tests generic full-stack intern shipping, not ML-research depth.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`, next to Pinterest / Coinbase)
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco, CA (HQ); New York, NY (this intern req: SF or NY hybrid; relocate required). Additional regional offices include Singapore, Tokyo, Seoul, Sydney.
- **Valuation / signal:** Private; ~$11B valuation via $270M secondary tender (Jan 2026, GIC / Sequoia / Index). Strong consumer/product brand; limited SWE-prestige signal vs. FAANG (`companies.md` Notes).
- **Product focus:** Block-based collaborative editor + Notion AI (connected workspace for teams and agents)
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer Intern):** $57/hr bachelor's, $61/hr master's
- **Work model:** Paid; hybrid SF or NY; Anchor Days Mon/Tue/Thu in office; two windows — May 17–August 6 or June 14–Sept 3, 2027; relocate required
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Pursuing bachelor's or master's in CS, engineering, or related; must graduate before Summer 2028; work from NY or SF office during the internship; previous internship experience required. Location is not a skip.

## Interview Process

| Stage | Format | Notes |
| ----- | ------ | ----- |
| Resume screen | Human + Ashby ATS | Human reads the PDF earlier than a Workday OA-gated big-tech funnel (`recruiting.md` §1) |
| OA | CodeSignal (primary per `companies.md`) | Medium; intern reports also describe a recruiter screen + CoderPad practical (text-editor-style stubs) rather than only CodeSignal — keep CodeSignal as the published primary, CoderPad as a reported intern variant |
| Recruiter screen | Conversational | Fit, motivation, SF/NY relocate, prior internship |
| Tech rounds | Coding / practical | Bottleneck per `companies.md`; intern reports: CoderPad text-editor implementation, then team block (coding, light product/system design, sometimes AI-tooling fluency) |
| Behavioral | Values / curiosity | Users-first, not ideological about tech, empathetic communication (`recruiting.md` §6 — filter round) |

**Estimated funnel:** 3–4 rds · Medium · CodeSignal · No sys design (interns; light product-design possible) · Bottleneck: tech rounds · ~3–5% (`reference/companies.md` Notion row)

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** TypeScript + React product engineering (JD: TypeScript, Node.js, or Python proficiency; React/Python expertise nice-to-have). Do not invent Node.js bullet proof the pool does not carry.
- **Domains:** Full-stack product engineering, collaborative editing, web services and databases, Notion AI (LLM/embeddings/ML as product features)
- **What wins:** A shippable full-stack spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) with TypeScript and/or Python demonstrated in bullets, plus visible LLM/embeddings/ML prototypes as a named qualification — product-SWE with AI features, not an ai-ml research/training resume. Prior internship is a JD requirement. Ashby means the PDF is read by a human early; CodeSignal (or a CoderPad practical) remains the binding technical filter after the screen (`companies.md`: bottleneck tech rounds).
