# Software Engineer Intern (Winter 2027) at Notion

## Role Summary

Winter 2027 Software Engineer Intern at Notion — hybrid San Francisco or New York, Anchor Days Mon/Tue/Thu in office, relocate required. Term: January 25 – April 16, 2027; must work from the NY or SF office during that window. Comp: $57/hr bachelor's / $61/hr master's. The intern writes, tests, documents, and debugs full-stack software (web services, databases, applications, tools) and designs/enhances the Notion product with an AI-first mindset. JD surface is generic SWE intern: TypeScript, Node.js, or Python; prior internship; CS/engineering student who **must graduate before December 2027**. Company identity is a collaborative AI workspace — TypeScript + React product engineering on a block-based editor, with LLMs/embeddings/ML as *product features*, not a research rotation.

The single full-stack track grounds ordering, lead entry, and skills. `lead_signal_window` is 0.

## Screen Criteria

- Full-stack shipping with web services, a datastore, and a deploy or test path demonstrated in bullets — not Skills-only (`resume.md` §12). Breadth across the stack plus depth in at least one layer.
- TypeScript and/or Python used inside architectural decisions, not asserted on a Skills line. React evidence is a plus at a TypeScript+React product shop; do not invent Node.js bullet proof.
- Prior titled internship / co-op visible — the JD names previous internship experience as a requirement.
- AI/LLM/embeddings/ML as *built or prototyped product features* (pipeline, eval, embeddings store, on-device inference) — a named qualification, not a research-track pivot and not "I use Copilot."
- Clean, tested, documented engineering: debug/reliability stories with before-after numbers beat feature laundry lists.
- Graduation window: JD requires graduation **before December 2027**. A class-year on the page after that cutoff fails the intern knockout (`recruiting.md` §8) — ineligible. Winter term is Jan 25–Apr 16, 2027; returning to school after the term does not satisfy the stated graduate-by date.
- Users-first product framing — what the system does for a person — over club-ops or GTM-only lead stories.

## Anti-Patterns

- Skills-list TypeScript/Python with zero bullet evidence at a shop where those are the floor.
- Routing the page to research-only LoRA / notebook ML as if this were an ai-ml intern req.
- AI-tool-only claims (prompting, Copilot) with no prototype or pipeline.
- Lead entry that is off-axis for product engineering (pure GTM/quant scoring, or low-level DSP) while the full-stack + AI-product spine is buried.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a shippable product story.
- Invented Node.js, Notion-block, or CRDT experience the pool does not carry.
- Rewriting the Education graduation date to clear the December 2027 knockout.
