# Hy-Vee

Hy-Vee is an employee-owned Midwest grocer (founded 1930) headquartered in West Des Moines, Iowa. This JD family: 550+ business units across nine states, about $12 billion annual sales, 70k–80k employees (wording varies by posting). Digital/IT sits at the Helpful Smiles Technology (HST) campus in Grimes; analytics, supply-chain, and corporate functions sit at Westown Parkway. Two Summer 2027 intern reqs live under this folder — **do not mix them:**

- **Digital Software Engineering Intern (Workday R250133)** — HST Grimes; Agile business applications, unit tests, SSRS, SQL Server. Grocery digital / business-application SWE. Not a store job.
- **Data Analytics Intern (Workday R250146)** — Corporate Office, Westown Pkwy, West Des Moines; applied retail analytics on in-store and digital customer trends. Not IT SWE.

Neither is the Data Integration Intern sibling (WDM corporate pipelines) nor Forecasting & Replenishment.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-tier (`reference/companies.md` — Midwest grocery / retail-tech peer of Meijer, Best Buy, Home Depot; legitimate intern program; limited national SWE brand vs Target Tech / Walmart Labs)
- **HQ / offices:** West Des Moines, IA (Corporate Office, Westown Pkwy). HST campus: Grimes, IA (~104k sq ft; Des Moines Register 2017) **[directional]**
- **Valuation / signal:** Employee-owned; ~$12B sales on these JDs; Top 10 Most Trusted Brands / America's Top 5 favorite grocery stores (JD copy)
- **Product focus:** Grocery + pharmacy + fuel + e-commerce; HST ships e-commerce, mobile, supply chain, finance, DW/BI; WDM analytics uses first- and third-party in-store/digital customer data
- **Intern comp (2027 Digital Software Engineering Intern, R250133):** Unlisted. Paid; up to 40 hours/week (possibly exceeding). C-tier intern band $22–38/hr; 2017 IT intern $20/hr is stale **[directional]**
- **Intern comp (2027 Data Analytics Intern, R250146):** Unlisted. Paid on sibling intern JDs. C-tier intern band $22–38/hr. Do not invent a number
- **Work model:** Both Workday `timeType` Part time and `remoteType` **In Person**. R250133: HST Grimes (ATS string is West Des Moines). R250146: Corporate Office WDM. Sibling Forecasting & Replenishment analog: May 17–August 13 2027, 40h, onsite WDM — analog only. Pre-employment drug screen on both
- **Clearance / eligibility:** R250133: working toward or recently acquired CS, MIS, Software Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, etc. R250146: working towards BA/BS in a STEM field (graduate degree preferred — additive). No GPA floor. No class-year exclusive. No sponsorship language on either JD. Posted 2026-09-04; Workday `endDate` **2026-10-31**

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Digital SWE (R250133):** JD names no exclusive language. Intern tasks: Microsoft SQL Server, Microsoft Reporting Services, Agile, unit tests, business applications. Do not invent C#, .NET, Java, SSRS, SQL Server, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, Fusion, or Sentry. What wins: a shipped business application (API + data plumbing) with tests and SQL through use, plus a dashboard/report analog.
- **Data Analytics (R250146):** SQL (SQL Server named; BigQuery preferred) and R and/or Python. Do not invent R, SQL Server, BigQuery, Power BI, Data Studio, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, Fusion, or Sentry. What wins: Python + SQL through use in ingest → transform → score/KPI → dashboard, plus a stakeholder analog. Not notebook `model.fit()`, not ML-research, not generic SWE.
- **Resume is the gate** for both (`recruiting.md`: intern knockouts first; mid-size / non-tech-tech, human reads the PDF early).
