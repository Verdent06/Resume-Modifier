# Transcard Payments

Transcard Payments LLC is a Chattanooga-based private payment-orchestration fintech (founded 2008; ~100–200 people). It sells **SMART Suite** to banks, fintechs, and businesses: multi-rail embedded payments (disbursements, receivables, A2A, cross-border), system-of-record integration, reconciliation, supply-chain financing, and bank-grade security. **Virtual CFO** is the agentic AP/AR / working-capital insights product on top of that rails layer. Origin story is fuel payments for U.S. Xpress; that platform was sold to U.S. Bank around 2012. This packet is the **2027 Artificial Intelligence Intern** (Paylocity **4476416**) — applied product AI on SMART Suite / Virtual CFO, **not** ML research and **not** the sibling SWE intern.
