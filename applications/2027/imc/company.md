# IMC Trading

IMC is a research-driven proprietary trading firm founded in 1989. It provides liquidity across global venues using its own capital and technology: quantitative modeling, machine learning, and engineering sit in one loop from experiment to execution. Engineers, researchers, and traders share infrastructure that has to hold under real-time market feedback. IMC sits in the elite S-tier quant/HFT band with Optiver, HRT, and Virtu — early cycle, OA-gated, low-single-digit intern acceptance. **Do not mix intern reqs:** this packet is Greenhouse **4962456101** / **REQ-04868** **Machine Learning Engineer Intern - Summer 2027** (New York) — applied ML *engineering* (systems that train and deploy large-scale models; GPU acceleration; distributed computing; open-source tooling; faster experimentation) — **not** the Chicago/Amsterdam Machine Learning *Research* intern siblings (PhD/MSc, publications, PyTorch/JAX research), **not** a generic IMC SWE intern, **not** Quant Trader.
