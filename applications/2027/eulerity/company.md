# Eulerity

Eulerity is a New York marketing-technology company building an agentic marketing operating system for small businesses, franchises, and multi-location brands. The platform unifies paid placements, organic listings, reputation, and call tracking so campaigns can be planned, bought, optimized, and reported in one stack rather than as siloed point tools. Founded in 2017 by Google, Facebook, and Yahoo alumni, it is a small NYC-HQ SaaS org (~73 employees, ~$2.3M raised). For a backend intern, the screen tests Java services, third-party APIs, and hands-on AI coding-agent fluency; the company identity is martech automation plus agentic marketing workflows.
