# Software Engineer Intern at Roblox

## Role Summary

12-week mentored internship owning a production project end-to-end across distributed systems, real-time communications, 3D, data processing, and rendering — with exposure to ML frameworks, agentic coding tools, and LLMs. Team matching spans Infra, Engine, Search & Discovery, Foundational AI, and Economy. JD surface is generic SWE intern (ship production code, CS degree enrollment); company identity is UGC platform engineering at scale — C++/Lua engine, real-time performance, distributed infra, and consumer-concurrency discipline.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (generic SWE requirements — production ownership, APIs, data, deployable services)
- **differentiator:** real-time / distributed systems at UGC scale (Engine/Infra identity — C++/Lua, latency, concurrency, rendering-adjacent systems)
- **track_divergence:** true

The screen spine follows `resume.md` Part III §12 — shippable product, APIs, databases, cloud deploy, end-to-end ownership in bullets. The differentiator follows real-time systems and low-latency engineering signals — C++ or hard real-time constraints, distributed/production scale, concurrency/latency metrics — not buried at the bottom behind generic web-only framing. When divergence is true, lead with the full-stack spine **and** keep real-time / systems-depth carriers prominent and deep in the top half.

## Team & Bar

Roblox is A-tier in `companies.md` with ~2–4% estimated acceptance; bottleneck is OA (CodeSignal + comms) plus Med–Hard tech rounds. Recruiter voice: interns who already ship production systems, can own a scoped project with mentorship, and show systems curiosity (latency, scale, real-time constraints) without reading as pure gamedev hobbyists. Hybrid San Mateo; no H1B sponsorship — work authorization is a hard knockout. Enrollment and return-to-school eligibility are binary gates.

## Screen Criteria

- Production shipping with measurable impact — APIs, pipelines, or client systems deployed to real users, not tutorial repos.
- Real-time or distributed-systems evidence in bullets — latency, throughput, concurrency, cost at scale — company differentiator must register in the first screen pass.
- C++ or hard real-time constraint proof where claimed — engine/infra companies treat language-in-Skills-only as a floor miss.
- Full-stack or backend depth (data stores, deploy paths, CI) alongside systems signal — JD spans infra through product surfaces.
- ML/agentic/LLM exposure as a plus when tied to shipped pipelines or eval — Foundational AI is a team-match lane, not a research-only pivot.
- Class-year on page must show current enrollment (`Expected May 2028` satisfies Summer 2027 internship eligibility).

## Anti-Patterns

- Gaming or club framing without engineering depth the role tests.
- Skills-list C++/Lua with zero bullet evidence at an engine-heavy company.
- Thin single-bullet filler entries that dilute a strong production + systems spine.
- Notebook ML or agentic buzzwords without pipeline architecture, metrics, or delivery.
- Leading with unrelated quant/GTM or pure frontend with no backend/systems depth.
