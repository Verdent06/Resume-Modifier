# Roblox

Roblox operates a UGC gaming platform where millions of creators ship experiences on shared real-time infrastructure — distributed systems, a custom engine (C++/Lua), rendering, and economy/search services at consumer scale. Interns own mentored production projects end-to-end across Engine, Infra, Search & Discovery, Foundational AI, and Economy teams. The engineering identity is real-time performance and distributed systems at massive concurrency, not generic web CRUD.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-tier (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** San Mateo, CA (hybrid Tue–Thu onsite for this role)
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (RBLX); UGC platform at scale; often bucketed as "gaming" but eng bar is systems-heavy
- **Product focus:** Real-time 3D UGC platform — engine, infra, economy, search, foundational AI
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern):** ~$62/hr + ~$10K housing (2025–26 Levels.fyi/careers signal)
- **Work model:** Paid 12-week summer internship; hybrid onsite Tue–Thu
- **Clearance / eligibility:** No H1B sponsorship offered; must be pursuing undergraduate or graduate CS-related degree
