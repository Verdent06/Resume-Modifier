## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**Python**, **C++**). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). **Do not invent Java.**
- Shipped full-stack or service/API software: REST, a data store or SQL, and a deploy path tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12). Flask/FastAPI, Docker, AWS through use match “write clean, maintainable, efficient code” for automation.
- Data and task automation plus data-quality / governance flavor: messy-source ingestion, normalization, freshness or quality fixes, ranked or validated outputs — memorable here without inventing Boomi or a biometric product.
- Debug / defect stories with before-after numbers (JD: debug and resolve defects).
- Prior internship or project experience in software development (preferred) shown as titled delivery, not club-ops.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies current-student plus return-to-school after Summer 2027.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Invented Java, Boomi, Microsoft Power Apps, OutSystems, or biometric/CV model work (fabrication smell — `resume.md` §8).
- Skills-only Python or C++ with zero bullet evidence (JD languages as a floor signal).
- LoRA / notebook ML / facial-recognition research as the first-pass identity (this is a low-code data-automation intern at a biometrics company, not an ML intern).
- Hard-real-time audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests automation, SQL, and data quality.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a full-stack + data-automation spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
