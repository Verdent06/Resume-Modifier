# The Exploration Company

The Exploration Company (TEC) builds **Nyx**, a reusable orbital cargo capsule with a crewed-vehicle path, and **Storm**, a high-thrust reusable rocket engine. Founded 2021 (CEO Hélène Huby); HQ Munich with a Houston Rapid Innovation Lab (Webster, opened Jul 2026) for crewed-capsule work. This intern req is **Los Angeles**, US & Future Programs / US Business Development — not Houston. Customers and partners include ESA LEO Cargo Return, Axiom, Vast, and Starlab; Bikini demo flew on Ariane 6's first flight (2024). Culture: Agile, open/collaborative, "democratising space."
