# Summer 2027 Internship (Software) at The Exploration Company

## Role Summary

A Summer 2027 (minimum 12 weeks beginning May 2027), full-time onsite software internship in Los Angeles on TEC's US & Future Programs / US Business Development team. The intern works under a mentor on crewed-vehicle development: design, develop, test, debug, and document engineering systems and prototypes; analyze data and recommend; sit in design reviews; support validation and quality assurance. The JD surface is generic intern SWE — enrolled in Engineering or a related technical field, 3+ months engineering experience, ITAR US person — with **no named language exclusive**. TEC's company identity is aerospace / crewed-vehicle / mission-critical systems (Nyx reusable cargo/crew capsule, Storm engine; real flight hardware, not coffee runs).

## Screen Criteria

- Shows shipped software with real users, a stakeholder, or a release artifact — design/develop/test/debug/document on something others depend on, not tutorial-grade coursework.
- Demonstrates test, debug, validation, or quality-assurance discipline through use in bullets (root-cause, recovery, data-driven recommendation), not a Skills-line claim.
- Makes the aerospace / crewed-vehicle / mission-critical differentiator visible in the top half: real-time constraints, C++ systems depth, or operational debug — without flipping the page to robotics-only (no ROS/controls exclusive) and without inventing aerospace tools the JD never named.
- Backs the full-stack spine with end-to-end delivery (APIs/services, data analysis, deployment, ownership) so the generic SWE screen is satisfied. JD names no exclusive language — do not treat MATLAB/C/Java/ROS as a floor; any real language through use is enough.
- Uses impact metrics (latency, failure recovery, throughput, scale) rather than vanity counts or club-ops filler.
- Anti-patterns: inventing flight software, GNC, Nyx/Storm internships, MATLAB, ROS, or clearance; leading as an ML-research or PE-deal intern; Skills-list keyword stuffing with no backing bullet; a purely web page with zero test/debug or real-time/systems evidence at a crewed-vehicle company; burying the differentiator below the fold.
