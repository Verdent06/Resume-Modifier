# Expedia Group

Expedia Group (NASDAQ: **EXPE**) is one of the world's largest online travel companies. It powers a high-volume travel marketplace across **140+ branded sites**, with core consumer brands **Expedia**, **Hotels.com**, and **Vrbo** (plus Orbitz, Travelocity, ebookers, Wotif, and majority-owned hotel metasearch **trivago**). A unified tech platform now sits under those brands: Hotels.com and Vrbo front-ends migrated onto the Brand Expedia stack, and B2B partners tap the same supply through APIs (Rapid), white-label ecommerce, and TAAP. Engineering identity is **travel e-commerce at scale** — backend supply and database functions through new UIs — not a research lab. The JVM is the primary backend (hundreds of Spring Boot services); Kotlin is used for new GraphQL / REST / gRPC work. This packet is the **US 2027 SDE intern** (Workday **R-110311**: Austin / San Jose / Seattle) — generic Software Development + AI-enabled workflows, **not** the APAC Gurgaon/Bangalore twins, **not** a named Data Engineering or ML Engineering specialization picker.
