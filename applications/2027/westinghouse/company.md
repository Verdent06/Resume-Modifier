# Westinghouse Electric Company

Westinghouse Electric Company LLC is a nuclear OEM (formed 1999 from the Westinghouse Electric Corporation nuclear division) that designs and services commercial nuclear power plants. Flagship product is the AP1000® pressurized-water reactor; the company also supplies nuclear fuel, plant services, and Instrumentation & Controls. HQ is Cranberry Township, PA. Owners: Brookfield Renewable Partners 51% and Cameco 49% (closed 2023; ~$7.9B including debt). This intern seat is Software Developer Intern on **GIC Tools & Applications** in the I&C organization — engineering tools for I&C engineers (requirements, code, test, document), not sibling intern 34373 Computer/Software Engineering, not the Cranberry HTML/CSS/JS Application Development intern, not Application Integration, and not Americas I&C intern.
