# Persona (Persona Identities)

Persona (legal/brand: **Persona Identities**; product: [withpersona.com](https://withpersona.com/)) is the configurable identity platform for verifying individuals and organizations — KYC/KYB, fraud, compliance, and trust building blocks for a digital-first world. Reddit uses it for age assurance; OpenAI uses it to keep bad actors out of ChatGPT; Coursera uses it so the right person earns each credential; Lime checks ages of scooter and bike customers. Engineering here is product SWE on identity infrastructure at customer-facing scale (schema/data modeling, CSV-scale workloads, ship-in-week-1 features) — not an ML-research lab and not a mobile-intern seat. The JD still frames a ~250-person Series C SF startup; identity is product identity *inside* full-stack (Superhuman/Fable-shaped), not a security-research rotation.
