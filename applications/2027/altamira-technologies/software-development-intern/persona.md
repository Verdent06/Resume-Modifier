# Software Development Intern - 2027 at Altamira Technologies

## Role Summary

A summer software-development internship in Altamira's Fairborn, OH office (Wright-Patterson AFB / Dayton area), working alongside an experienced software team on real-world national-security requirements. The JD surface is a broad, generalist SWE intern: the desired qualifications are *familiarity with SOME of* a wide language list (C++, JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, Java, HTML/CSS, and PostgreSQL/PostGIS/general SQL), plus working toward a BS in CS or computer engineering, the ability to work in a team and communicate, and the ability to learn new technologies and apply them. U.S. citizenship is a hard gate. The company identity is data processing and intelligence extraction — turning sensor/multi-source feeds into actionable intelligence (SIGINT, missile-warning satellite analytics, AI/ML ISR tooling, geospatial data) inside a cleared, mission-driven defense org (Parsons acquired Altamira Jan 2026).

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (general SWE requirements — polyglot familiarity across web + systems + database languages, teamwork, learn-and-apply new tech; no ML/DevOps/robotics specialization is *required*)
- **differentiator:** national-security data processing / intelligence extraction — real-time and batch data pipelines over multi-source feeds, with a geospatial (PostGIS) and regulatory/entity-data flavor
- **track_divergence:** false

The screen spine and the company identity agree. `reference/resume.md` Part III §12: a genuinely engineered, shippable full-stack product narrated decision-by-decision, with breadth across the stack and depth in at least one layer. The JD's most distinctive move is the *language breadth* it lists — so the binding on-page signal is polyglot depth demonstrated inside bullets (a web/dynamic language, a systems language, and SQL/Postgres all shown through real use), not any single framework. The differentiator does not pull to a different track; it sharpens emphasis within full-stack toward data-processing pipelines, PostgreSQL/PostGIS/SQL, and multi-source/geospatial data aggregation that rhymes with the intelligence-extraction mission. The single full-stack track grounds ordering, lead entry, and skills.

## Team & Bar

Altamira Fairborn is a small, heavily cleared engineering team inside a ~600-person national-security firm (now a Parsons unit). This is a low-volume, resume-first funnel on Jobvite: a human reads the PDF early, so resume signal carries real front-end weight rather than being gated behind a high-volume OA. The recruiter voice: a self-motivated CS/CompE student who has actually shipped working software, can pick up an unfamiliar language or technology and apply it to assigned work, and can operate on a team against real requirements — not a notebook-only hobbyist and not a one-language specialist. Because the JD explicitly wants "familiarity with SOME of" a broad list, evidence of polyglot range and demonstrated learn-a-new-stack ability is disproportionately valuable. U.S. citizenship is a binary knockout; a clearance is sponsored, not required at entry.

## Screen Criteria

- Polyglot breadth demonstrated inside bullets — a web/dynamic language, a systems language, and SQL/PostgreSQL all shown through the decision that justified them, not asserted only in a Skills line. This is the JD's dominant desired-qual signal.
- Shipped, defensible software with measurable impact — a pipeline, API, or app built end-to-end and narratable under "walk me through this," not a tutorial clone.
- Data-processing / data-pipeline evidence — ingesting, normalizing, and extracting signal from messy or multi-source data reads directly onto the company's intelligence-extraction mission; geospatial (PostGIS/maps) or regulatory/entity-data aggregation is a strong differentiator plus.
- Full-stack breadth with depth in at least one layer — backend service/API, a datastore (PostgreSQL/SQL), a frontend, and a deploy/CI path, with trade-offs the candidate can defend.
- Systems-language depth as a differentiator — real C++ (or comparable) work signals the candidate can go below the web layer, which a defense/real-time data shop values and which proves "learns and applies hard technologies."
- Learn-and-apply-new-tech signal — a from-scratch build in an unfamiliar stack, or a diagnosed-and-fixed defect, evidences the JD's "ability to learn new technologies and apply them."
- Class-year on page shows current enrollment toward a CS/CompE BS (`Expected May 2028` clears Summer 2027 intern eligibility; rising senior returning to school after).
- Live, clickable GitHub — a public repo a small team can open beats a skills list here.

## Anti-Patterns

- One-language (or Skills-line-only) breadth at a shop whose headline qualification is familiarity across many languages.
- Tutorial/notebook projects with no deploy path, no data pipeline, and no defensible trade-offs.
- Pure-frontend or pure-quant/GTM framing with no backend/data/systems depth as the lead.
- Buzzword AI/agentic claims with no pipeline architecture, metrics, or delivery behind them.
- Thin single-bullet filler entries that dilute a polyglot, shipped-software spine.
- No visible GitHub / public code for a small team that will want to click through.
