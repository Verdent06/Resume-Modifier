# Altamira Technologies

Altamira Technologies is a McLean, Virginia national-security engineering firm (founded 1999) serving the defense, intelligence, and homeland-security communities. Its core work is turning raw sensor and multi-source feeds into actionable intelligence — signals intelligence (SIGINT), missile-warning satellite data analytics, AI/ML-enabled ISR tooling, cyber, and space-based mission support — with a stated focus on common standards in architecture, data, and security. The Fairborn, OH office sits in the Wright-Patterson AFB ecosystem, where the software team builds real-time data-processing and intelligence-extraction systems against operational requirements. In January 2026 Parsons Corporation (NYSE: PSN) acquired Altamira for up to $375M, folding its ~600 employees (over 90% cleared) into Parsons' Defense & Intelligence business unit; the intern would join a heavily cleared, mission-driven engineering org.
