# ICF International

ICF (Nasdaq: **ICFI**) is a Reston-headquartered advisory and technology consulting firm (founded 1969). It sells strategy, policy, and implementation to U.S. federal/state/local government and commercial clients across energy and environment, health and social programs, disaster recovery, and **digital transformation** (IT modernization, cloud, cybersecurity, data analytics). This intern is a **generic software-developer seat** on that technology practice — design/configure/test/deploy enterprise apps, possibly on Salesforce / ServiceNow / Appian or with traditional code — not a ServiceNow-only intern sibling and not an ML-research rotation. Work quality varies by client engagement the same way it does at Accenture / Deloitte Tech / Booz Allen (`reference/companies.md` C-tier caveat).
