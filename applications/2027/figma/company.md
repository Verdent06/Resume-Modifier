# Figma

Figma (NYSE: FIG) is a collaborative design platform: a browser-based canvas where teams brainstorm, prototype, translate designs into code, and iterate with AI in real time. Engineering is product work on a multiplayer editor (FigJam, Design, Dev Mode) plus platform/infrastructure for performance, developer tooling, and security. The intern program embeds students on small teams in the San Francisco or New York Hub; this Winter 2027 req starts January 4, 2027. `reference/companies.md` frames Figma as A-tier with Byteboard replacing a traditional OA — reading speed under time pressure is the technical bottleneck, not LeetCode volume.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-TIER (`reference/companies.md`, next to Discord / Snap)
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco, CA (HQ, 760 Market Street); New York, NY Hub (this intern req: SF or NY Hub, in-person onboarding if hired)
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: FIG, IPO July 31, 2025). Priced ~$19B; first-day close pushed market cap well above the abandoned Adobe $20B deal. Design-tool category leader; intern cohort is tiny ("Very low" acceptance in `companies.md`)
- **Product focus:** Real-time collaborative design platform (Figma Design, FigJam, Dev Mode) — multiplayer canvas, not generic CRUD
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer Intern, Winter):** $55/hr + housing stipend + travel reimbursement (JD pay-transparency disclosure)
- **Work model:** Paid; Winter intern; based out of San Francisco or New York Hub; January 4, 2027 start; cameras on for video interviews; in-person onboarding if hired
- **Clearance / eligibility:** No class-year, GPA, or citizenship gate on this posting. JD asks expected graduation (dropdown includes Spring 2028) and work authorization. Grow-as-you-go: apply even if experience does not align perfectly

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD examples are C++, JavaScript, Python, Java — "at least one general-purpose language," not a named floor. Do not invent JavaScript (TypeScript is not JavaScript) or Java. C++ and Python are in inventory; prove them in bullets, not Skills alone.
- **Domains:** Full-stack product engineering (user-facing collaboration tools, workflows, growth experiments); platform/infrastructure (distributed systems, developer tooling, performance/scalability); security engineering as a named interest area. Past intern projects: FigJam diagramming, smart selection, shared fonts — product-surface shipping.
- **What wins:** A shippable full-stack spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) **and** visible real-time or user-facing product engineering (client performance, collaborative workflows, APIs that other people use). AI-assisted tools are a JD nice-to-have as *usage* (ChatGPT/Claude as examples) — do not put GitHub Copilot on the resume. Greenhouse human screen + Byteboard are the binding intern gates; resume must look like someone who already ships product, then Byteboard tests reading + implementation under time pressure.
