# Mindex

Mindex is a Rochester, NY custom-software company founded in 1994. It runs two businesses that feed each other: **Professional Services** (SOW / enterprise custom development, cloud, data, and AI for private and public clients) and an in-house **K–12 product suite** built from the ground up — **SchoolTool®** (cloud student-management for New York districts; the company cites a 400+ district cloud migration), Advanced Analytics, Integrations Platform, plus supported products MTSS Edge and ClearTrack. This co-op (Workable **84B10DB922**, published 2026-10-01) sits in department **SOW**, not a named SchoolTool product req — generic Agile delivery (design, code, test scripts, docs) next to Scrum teammates, with an AI-assisted-tools overlay. Shop familiarity is an or-list: Java, Angular, Oracle, OpenShift, Python, and/or ASP.NET.
