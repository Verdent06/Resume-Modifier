# apexanalytix

apexanalytix (stylized lowercase) is a private procure-to-pay and supplier-management software company founded in 1988 and headquartered in Greensboro, North Carolina. It sells controls, audit, and analytics software for Global 2000 procurement — supplier onboarding, risk monitoring, and recovery audit — on the flagship apexportal / supplier hub, with ERP sync named on product pages (SAP/Oracle/PeopleSoft — **do not invent those as candidate skills**). Company site and the May 2026 Gartner announcement: 400+ of the world's largest companies and more than $10 trillion in annual spend protected; named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Supplier Risk Management Solutions (Ability to Execute and Completeness of Vision; report dated 4 May 2026). **Not** Apex Space (satellite buses; that packet lives at `applications/2027/apex/`).

This intern sits in **R&D – Archimedes**, reports to the Manager, Automation Architect, and works an **on-premise data warehouse QA + applied SQL/Python analytics** rotation: source-to-target validation, regression testing after pipeline changes, SQL integrity checks, ad-hoc SQL, Linux CLI + grep, kubectl-as-plus, Data Catalog / Business Glossary, and supplier n-tier mapping. It is **not** ML research and **not** a generic SWE intern.
