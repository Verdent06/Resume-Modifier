# apexanalytix

apexanalytix (stylized lowercase) is a private procure-to-pay and supplier-management software company founded in 1988 and headquartered in Greensboro, North Carolina. It sells controls, audit, and analytics software for Global 2000 procurement — supplier onboarding, risk monitoring, and recovery audit — on the flagship apexportal / supplier hub, with ERP sync named on product pages (SAP/Oracle/PeopleSoft — **do not invent those as candidate skills**).
