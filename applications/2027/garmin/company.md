# Garmin

Garmin Ltd. (NYSE: GRMN) designs and ships GPS-enabled navigation, communication, and sensor products across aviation, marine, automotive, outdoor, and fitness. Operational HQ is Olathe, Kansas; legal domicile is Schaffhausen. Flagship aviation software/hardware is the G1000 / G3000 integrated flight-deck family (G3000 PRIME the current OEM line). This intern sits on-site in Chandler, AZ — Garmin Aviation engineering — on a catch-all Summer 2027 SWE intern req (iCIMS 19858) that can place into Embedded Software, DevOps, Mobile, or Web. The memorable identity is consumer + aviation hardware with embedded C/C++ next to comms and navigation products, not a generic SaaS web shop.
