# Amazon

Amazon (NASDAQ: AMZN) is a customer-obsessed technology and operations company whose intern SDE seat is classic product/backend engineering: design, build, and operate scalable services that ship on weekly cycles, then stay on-call for them. Flagship surfaces span e-commerce, AWS, devices, and logistics; the intern identity is **high-volume Big Tech SDE** — ownership from design through operations — not an ML-research lab even when a posting lists Machine Learning as an optional specialty interest. `reference/companies.md` A-TIER Notes: highest intern volume in Big Tech; OA equalizes non-targets; 30K+ layoffs hurt prestige but the brand holds.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-TIER — Excellent / Strong Signal (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Seattle, WA (HQ). This intern: in-person at **all US intern sites**, including AZ, CA, CO, GA, IL, MA, MD, MI (Detroit), MN, NC, NJ, NY, OR, PA, TN, TX, VA (Arlington, Herndon), WI, WA (Bellevue, Redmond, Seattle). Location prefs considered, not guaranteed.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public Big Tech; Earth's most customer-centric company brand. Highest intern volume in the A-tier; ~1–2% estimated intern acceptance on a large class (`companies.md`). Layoff-cycle prestige hit does not change the screen.
- **Product focus:** Scalable customer-facing products and services (retail, AWS, devices, logistics) — intern placement by business need, not by specialty checkbox
- **Intern comp (2027 SDE intern):** Arlington VA / Seattle WA listed **$109,395 USD annually** (starting pay). Day-1 benefits: EAP, mental health support, medical advice line, 401(k) matching.
- **Work model:** Paid intern, **in-person**, **12 weeks**, full-time ~40 hrs/week Mon–Fri typically 8am–5pm. Majority **Summer 2027** (May/June–Aug/Sep). Also possible: Winter 2027 (Jan/Feb–Apr/May), Fall 2027 (Aug/Sep–Nov/Dec) — non-summer placement not guaranteed. Candidate prefers Summer 2027; Winter 2027 OK if asked; do not select Fall 2027.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** 18+; enrolled Bachelor's or above in CS, CE, Data Science, IS, or related STEM; expected conferral **October 2027 – September 2029**; **minimum one quarter/semester/trimester remaining after the internship**; 40 hrs/week min; 12-week max. No citizenship/export-control gate on this JD. Job ID **10552937**.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD: *at least one of* Java, Python, C++, C#, Go, Rust, TypeScript. Screen tests the language the candidate actually ships (**Python**, **TypeScript**, **C++**). Do **not** invent Java, Go, Rust, Kafka, or AWS-as-deep-expert. Cloud/containers only at routine level from real work (Flask on EC2, S3, Docker, GitHub Actions).
- **Domains:** Classic SDE intern — full-stack / backend / scalable services. Optional specialty interests (ML, distributed systems, databases, quantum, networking, query processing, automated reasoning, embedded, data engineering, mobile, games) are **team-placement hints**, not ML-research requirements.
- **What wins:** A full-stack intern spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) with Python/TypeScript/C++ shown in bullets, a database + AWS/SQL through use, version control and CI, plus **ownership from design-to-operation** (shipped services, monitoring/troubleshooting flavor) so team-match after the OA is memorable. Do not lead with LoRA / LangGraph-as-research / notebook ML. After the PDF, the OA is the binding filter (`companies.md`; `recruiting.md`).
