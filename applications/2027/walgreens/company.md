# Walgreens

Walgreens (legal **Walgreen Co.**) is a US retail-pharmacy chain founded in 1901. After Sycamore Partners closed its acquisition of Walgreens Boots Alliance on **August 28, 2025**, Walgreens operates as a **private standalone** company; HQ stayed in the Chicago area at **200 Wilmot Rd, Deerfield, IL**. JD copy: nearly **9 million** customers and patients per day across about **8,500** stores in the US and Puerto Rico; about **220,000** team members including nearly **90,000** healthcare service providers. **Walgreens Tech** is the software / data-science org that builds and runs the DS and ML applications behind that pharmacy-retail scale. This folder is the **Data Science Intern Summer 2027** packet only (Brassring **jobid 1933902** / req **1886736BR**) — **not** the sibling Centralized Services AI intern, **not** community pharmacy / community management, and **not** a marketing intern even though Brassring lists Job Function as Marketing.
