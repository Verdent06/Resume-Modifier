# Cotiviti

Cotiviti is a healthcare informatics and analytics company that helps payers, providers, and health plans improve payment integrity, risk adjustment, quality measurement, and operational efficiency through large-scale clinical and financial datasets. Their platform combines advanced analytics, emerging generative-AI capabilities, and governance programs to surface fraud/waste/abuse, validate claims, and support value-based care — technology applied directly to how U.S. healthcare is paid for and delivered. The Agentic AI Research intern sits on the R&D side evaluating generative and agentic models, vector-database patterns, and AI governance for healthcare workflows rather than a generic IT helpdesk role.
