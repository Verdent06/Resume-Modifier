# Brevan Howard

Brevan Howard is a leading absolute-return / global-macro hedge fund (founded 2002; flagship strategy April 2003) managing over $34bn for institutional allocators. The firm runs discretionary directional and relative-value processes in fixed income, FX, and equities, plus BH Digital (crypto, launched 2022), with 1,050+ personnel and 400+ investment professionals across London, Jersey, Geneva, New York, Austin, Hong Kong, Singapore, Abu Dhabi, and Bengaluru. Systematic strategies reach the market through Systematic Trading Technology: the live trading platform every systematic PM uses to execute, plus the market-data stack those strategies trade on. This intern req is **STT Software Engineer** (Workday JR101597) — production SWE on that platform — not the Systematic Trading / AI&Quant research siblings.
