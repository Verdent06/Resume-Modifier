# Systematic Trading Technology Software Engineer (Summer 2027, New York) at Brevan Howard

## Role Summary

Ten-week Summer 2027 intern on Brevan Howard's Systematic Trading Technology team in New York (Workday JR101597). The team builds and operates the live trading platform every systematic portfolio manager uses to execute, and delivers the market data those strategies trade on. Day-to-day work is production software: real-time services for order execution and trading workflow; market-data capture, normalization, and delivery; tooling and interfaces PMs and traders use daily; tests, instrumentation, and monitoring; full SDLC through deployment and production support. The posting's opening paragraph names quantitative/ML skills, LLMs, retrieval, and agent-like systems as how the *industry* is being reshaped; the actual team is execution + market-data engineering, not the AI&Quant / Systematic Trading research sibling (JR101591). Requirements are generalist high-bar SWE with a Java floor, plus Python / JavaScript/TypeScript / Linux / Git / SQL, an interest in reliable low-latency systems, and a genuine interest in markets. Comp is $150k annualized, prorated for ten weeks, plus housing stipend and a completion bonus. Top performers convert to the 2028 Graduate Program. One global SIP application.

- Real-time, low-latency, or systems-under-load depth somewhere on the page (concurrency, memory discipline, measured latency/throughput, production failure recovery) — the differentiator that separates STT from a generic product-SWE intern.
- Market-data-adjacent signal: capture, normalization, messy-dataset ETL, or delivery of data other people trade or research on — without claiming an exchange feed or desk the page does not have.
- JD languages the candidate actually has (Python, TypeScript, SQL) demonstrated in **bullets**, not Skills alone. Java is a JD-required language; listing it without bullet proof is worse than omitting it.
- End-to-end ownership and production impact with metrics (latency, throughput, %, scale) — soft, metric-free claims read thin where correctness, robustness, and speed are the firm's selling point (`reference/resume.md` I.4).

- Class-year on page shows penultimate-year / junior standing and degree awarded before July 2028 (Expected May 2028 is in window).
- Interest in markets can be analytical coursework (stats, econ) or finance-adjacent data systems — prior trading-desk experience is not required.
