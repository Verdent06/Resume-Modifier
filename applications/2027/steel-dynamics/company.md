# Steel Dynamics

Steel Dynamics (NASDAQ: STLD) is an S&P 500 industrial metals company: lower-carbon carbon steel from recycled scrap, plus metals recycling, fabrication, and aluminum, serving construction, automotive, energy, and manufacturing across North America. Software Development Interns (iCIMS **2026-7994**) do **not** write mill process-control or sell product software; they build and debug **internal** web applications used by operations and corporate employees (Picnic Project analog: events, SQL, auth, Azure hosting). Official intern FAQ: C#, SQL, modern web frameworks, Agile, full SDLC. Honest candidate inventory has HTML, SQL, TypeScript, Python, C++ — not C#, Blazor, .NET, or Microsoft Azure.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-TIER (`reference/companies.md` — Fortune ~196 industrial; legitimate internal-SWE intern work; limited national SWE brand)
- **HQ / offices:** Fort Wayne, IN (Corporate Office / this intern). Mills, recycling, fabrication, and aluminum sites across the US and Mexico
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NASDAQ: STLD); S&P 500; ~13Mt steel capacity; one of the largest US carbon-steel producers
- **Product focus:** Internal enterprise web apps for a manufacturing/steel operations workforce — not external SaaS, not mill DCS/PLC
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Development Intern):** Competitive hourly (unlisted on JD; Glassdoor US intern analog ~$26/hr **[directional]**). Furnished housing if >50 miles from site; 401(k) with 10–50% match vesting immediately; intern extravaganza at HQ
- **Work model:** Paid onsite Summer 2027 internship; Fort Wayne Corporate Office; 18+; reliable transportation required (not provided). Summer internships generally 3–4 months
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Pursuing bachelor's in CS, Software Engineering, IT, or related. Preferred GPA 2.7+. No class-year gate on this posting. Actively enrolled. Apps reviewed in submit order until filled (summer postings open ~Sept 1)
