# Ecolab

Ecolab (NYSE: ECL) is a Fortune 500 water, hygiene, and infection-prevention company: chemistry, service, and digital monitoring for food, healthcare, hospitality, data centers, and heavy industry. Flagship industrial water stack is 3D TRASAR hardware plus the ECOLAB3D IIoT platform — connected chemistry, remote monitoring, and operator-facing software, not a consumer app and not an ML-research lab. Ecolab Digital & AI ships data-driven products that help customers use less water and run plants more reliably. This intern req (R00303249) is a dual-track Digital & AI seat: Business Analyst (translate business needs into products/services/apps) or Software Engineering (applications that deliver digital value to customers).
