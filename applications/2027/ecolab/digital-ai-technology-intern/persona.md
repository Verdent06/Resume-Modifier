# Persona — Digital & AI Technology Intern (Summer 2027)

Recruiter lens shared by the writer and grader. Abstract bar signals only — no candidate entry names, no include/omit table.

## Role Summary

A paid 11-week hybrid internship starting Tuesday, June 1, 2027 on Ecolab's Digital & AI team (St. Paul or Eagan, MN, or Naperville, IL). Dual flavor on one req: Business Analyst (translate business needs into new products/services/applications) or Software Engineering (applications that deliver digital value to customers). Surface work is software engineering plans and process, source-code review, requirements elicitation and documentation, user/pilot testing and launches, success metrics, and usability/UI. Company identity is industrial water/hygiene digital products (3D TRASAR / ECOLAB3D) — operator-facing software and data for water safety and conservation — not a consumer web product, not ML-research, not robotics, not SRE-as-the-job. Org name is Digital & AI; the screen is applied digital/SWE-or-BA.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** `full-stack` (general SWE / applied digital). The JD's requirements and responsibilities test software engineering plans/process/code review, requirements/BA, testing, UI, process docs, and optional Power BI/Tableau — not ML frameworks/training, not ROS, not CI/CD-as-the-job. That routes to `full-stack` per `resume.md` §12 and `recruiting.md` §11.
- **differentiator:** industrial water / hygiene digital products — ECOLAB3D / 3D TRASAR; software that plant and water-system operators actually use.
- **track_divergence:** false. Water/hygiene is domain flavor on the same full-stack spine (shipped applications, APIs, data systems, stakeholder translation). It does not imply `ai-ml`, `dev-ops`, or `robotics`. Lead with the full-stack spine; keep industrial-operations / water-adjacent digital and BA-style translation visible as within-track emphasis.

## Team & Bar

Screener is a C-tier industrial university recruiter plus a Digital & AI hiring manager reading Workday — resume-first intern funnel, not a high-volume OA gauntlet (`recruiting.md` intern: OA is the binding filter at big-tech scale; here the resume carries the front-end weight). Funnel: resume screen → recruiter (auth, location, grad window) → 2–3 Easy live rounds (behavioral STAR + project walkthrough). Bottleneck is the resume (~15–25%). Evidence that wins: a shipped API or data pipeline with ownership and impact metrics; requirements/stakeholder translation; test/debug; UI/usability; Python/SQL through use in bullets. Recruiter voice is practical — "can this person write, test, document, and ship with business partners?" — not "did they intern on a water-treatment DCS" and not "did they publish an ML paper." Graduation in the December 2027 or May/June 2028 window and no-sponsorship work auth are knockout gates, not resume lines. Pay $23–$25/hr; hybrid MN/IL.

## Screen Criteria (abstract)

Pass signals:
- Shipped application or data-system delivery (APIs, ETL, databases, test/debug, production ownership) so the Software Engineering flavor is obvious.
- Requirements / stakeholder translation: eliciting needs, documenting delivery, shipping to non-engineer users — analog to the Business Analyst flavor and "work with business, engineering and R&D."
- Testing and launch discipline: user testing, failure recovery, metrics that size success — not "we tested it" with no number.
- UI / usability evidence somewhere on the page (front-end, operator-facing interface, or visualizer) — JD names IT-intern UI work.
- Python and SQL (or equivalent application/data stack) demonstrated through use in bullets, not Skills alone. JD names no languages; orphaned AI buzzwords still fail.
- Industrial-operations or water-adjacent digital work is a plus for the differentiator, but not a fabricated plant-control or chemistry internship.
- Metrics that size impact (hours saved, failure rate, throughput, precision), landed as the closer (`resume.md` §4).

Anti-patterns:
- Leading with C++ audio DSP or a hobby synth as the identity — this req is applied digital/SWE-or-BA, not real-time audio.
- Notebook ML or `model.fit()` with no pipeline, no delivery, no stakeholder.
- Agentic/LLM claims only in Skills with no supporting system; treating the org name "Digital & AI" as an ML-research intern req.
- Club-ops filler; metric-free architecture bullets the recruiter cannot size.
- Inventing Power BI/Tableau, Java, or plant-DCS internships the page cannot defend.
- No live GitHub on an intern engineering resume (`resume.md` §3).
