# Southwest Airlines

Southwest Airlines Co. (NYSE: LUV) is a major U.S. passenger carrier headquartered in Dallas, known for low-cost scheduled service, a single-aisle Boeing 737 fleet, and a hospitality-first culture ("Heart," Warrior Spirit / Servant's Heart / Fun-LUVing Attitude). As of year-end 2025 it operated 803 aircraft serving 117 destinations. Technology is an enterprise ops function: Workday HCM/Payroll integrations, revenue-accounting platforms, Amadeus-to-AWS feed modernization, air-traffic / flight-ops tools, and Network Operations Center (NOC) Tier-1 systems (AWS Serverless, DynamoDB, Confluent Kafka). SWE intern brand is limited relative to Big Tech; the work is Fortune-class airline operations software.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-tier (`reference/companies.md` — airline, limited SWE brand, Fortune-class ops)
- **HQ / offices:** Dallas, TX (principal executive offices; Love Field / corporate campus). This req posts TX-Dallas; the intern role is remote with required travel for trainings/meetings.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public NYSE: LUV; Fortune-class U.S. airline; Campus Reach intern program (Vault travel/transportation intern ranking; Glassdoor intern reviews directional)
- **Product focus:** Scheduled passenger air transportation; Technology org ships internal enterprise integrations, revenue systems, and real-time flight/NOC operations software — not a consumer SaaS product company
- **Intern comp (2027 Spring SWE intern):** $26–$30/hour corresponding with progress towards degree (this JD). Free unlimited space-available travel for the intern. Housing/internet stipend mentioned on Campus Reach pages for some seats — not listed on this SWE JD.
- **Work model:** Paid full-time internship, Technology department; Jan 26, 2027 – Apr 23, 2027; 40 hrs/week, max 8 hrs/day, consistent weekly schedule. Remote workplace; ineligible remote states include AK, CA, CO, DE, IL, IA, MA, MD, MT, NJ, NH, NY, ND, SD, OR, PA, VT, WA, WV, WY, and Puerto Rico. Michigan is eligible. Spring Technology window posted 2026-09-01 (Campus Reach recruiter: apps Sep 1–7, 2026).
- **Clearance / eligibility:** U.S. citizenship or current U.S. work authorization; no current or future sponsorship. Age 18+; valid SSN. High school diploma/GED; progress toward a 4-year (or graduate) degree in CS/CE/IT/MIS/related. Enrolled full-time at application and during the internship. Minimum 2.5 cumulative GPA. Preferred: 1–2 semesters remaining when the internship ends; prior SWE intern/work in the past 12 months.

Culture fit is a co-equal filter after the PDF (HireVue). Do not claim Workday, Kafka, SOAP, XSLT, SIRAX, BRIM, Copilot, Java, C#, or Node if they are not in inventory.
