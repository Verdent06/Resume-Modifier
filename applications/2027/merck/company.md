# Merck (MSD)

Merck & Co., Inc. (NYSE: **MRK**; known as MSD outside the United States and Canada) is a Fortune-class biopharmaceutical company: prescription medicines, vaccines, and animal health. Flagship human-health products include Keytruda (pembrolizumab) plus a broader oncology, vaccines, and infectious-disease portfolio. This intern sits in **Merck Research Labs** on the **Robotics** and **Workflow Data Capture (WDC)** teams — they build automated laboratory systems and the software that captures, organizes, and analyzes scientific data so research sites generate high-quality data with less manual work. The project is an **AI-enabled knowledge repository** over automation scripts, GitHub, and historical troubleshooting records — not a Business Intelligence intern seat, not a bench-chemistry rotation, and not an ML-research paper.
