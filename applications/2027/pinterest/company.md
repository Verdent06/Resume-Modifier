# Pinterest

Pinterest is a visual discovery platform where people find ideas and plan things to make — inspiration as a product, not a social feed. Engineering is large-scale consumer product work (home feed, search, ads, shopping, creator tools) with a heavy ML/recommendation identity: ranking, embeddings, and visual understanding sit next to Python / Java / JavaScript / TypeScript / Go / C++ services and React surfaces. `reference/companies.md` frames it as B-tier, ML-heavy, with declining product relevance as a *brand* signal; this intern req is still a generic SWE intern (team-match after the screen), not an ML-research seat. PinFlex: some interns hybrid near an office (~1–2 days/week), some fully remote in approved US locations.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`, next to Lyft / Notion / Coinbase)
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco, CA (HQ). This intern form also offers Palo Alto, Seattle, and Remote. JD location line: San Francisco, CA, US; Remote, US.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: PINS). Strong consumer brand; `companies.md` Notes: ML-heavy, declining product relevance as a prestige marker vs. FAANG.
- **Product focus:** Visual discovery / inspiration graph — ranking, search, ads, and consumer product surfaces at Pinner scale
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer Intern, USA):** $8,250–$11,000 monthly (JD; US-based applicants only)
- **Work model:** Paid; PinFlex hybrid (~1–2 in-office days if matched to a hybrid team, commutable to an office) or fully remote in approved US locations. Two windows: May 17–August 6, 2027 or June 14–September 3, 2027. Specific site and hybrid vs remote confirmed in interview/offer.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently enrolled toward a Bachelor's or Master's in CS or related; on track to graduate by June 2029; US-based applicants only. Form also asks work authorization and sponsorship. No published GPA floor.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD or-list: Python, Java, JavaScript/TypeScript, Go, or C++, plus React or similar. Requirements vary by host team. Do not invent Java or Go.
- **Domains:** Consumer product SWE at scale, visual discovery / ranking (company identity), web + backend services, AI *as an engineering tool* on this intern JD (not an MLE training loop)
- **What wins:** A shippable full-stack spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) with JD languages in bullets, plus non-academic GitHub/product proof. Keep recommendation/ML-adjacent shipping visible as company-fit flavor — not a LoRA-research resume. Recruiter screen then CodeSignal is the real intern gate after the PDF (`recruiting.md` §8).
