# HMH

HMH Holding Inc. (NASDAQ: **HMH**) is a Houston-headquartered provider of highly engineered drilling equipment, aftermarket services, spare parts, and integrated digital solutions for oil and gas drilling. HMH Holding B.V. was formed **1 Oct 2021** by combining Baker Hughes’ Subsea Drilling Systems pressure-control business with Akastor’s MHWirth drilling-equipment business; legacy brands include **Hydril**, VetcoGray, Wirth, and Maritime Hydraulics (Hydril produced the first hydraulically operated BOP in 1937). The company completed an IPO of Class A common stock on **2 Apr 2026** at **$20**/share. **This folder is the AI & Automation Development Intern packet only** (Workday **JR102458**).
