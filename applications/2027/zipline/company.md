# Zipline

Zipline designs, manufactures, and operates the world's largest autonomous drone delivery network — aircraft, launch/landing systems, and logistics software — moving food, retail, and healthcare products on four continents. The drone is only ~15% of the system; the rest is consumer mobile/web apps, backend services, and fleet operations that have flown 140M+ commercial autonomous miles. January 2026: 2M+ commercial deliveries, $600M+ round at $7.6B (Fidelity, Baillie Gifford, Valor, Tiger Global), U.S. expansion into Houston and Phoenix.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B (next to Nuro)
- **HQ / offices:** South San Francisco, CA (this intern onsite); U.S. delivery markets expanding (Dallas–Fort Worth, Houston, Phoenix, and further metros)
- **Valuation / signal:** $7.6B (Jan 2026, $600M+); 2M+ commercial deliveries; 140M+ autonomous miles (JD); 125M+ miles / 20M+ items in the Jan 2026 newsroom note
- **Product focus:** Autonomous logistics — consumer apps + Python/Go backends + fleet ops; Platform 2 home delivery (~8 lb, ~10-mile radius) and Platform 1 long-range enterprise
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern):** $50/hour; relocation support, housing stipend, overtime, paid sick time possible
- **Work model:** Paid, full-time, in-person at South San Francisco; Summer 2027 (May/June – August/September)
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Must have completed the second year of undergraduate studies; Masters and PhD students also eligible

## Interview Process

| Stage | Format | Notes |
| ----- | ------ | ----- |
| Resume screen | Human + ATS (Greenhouse) | Mission-aligned product/SWE evidence; practical systems over puzzle-only profiles |
| Recruiter screen | Conversational | Background, interest, location, onsite availability, comp alignment |
| Technical screen | Resume deep-dive | Past projects, CS fundamentals; less LeetCode-heavy than big-tech OA gates |
| Take-home | Domain-tailored assignment | Substantial; practical engineering over abstract puzzles |
| Final loop | Panel + project presentation | Portfolio/project defense, systems discussion, behavioral; cross-functional panels |

**Estimated funnel:** 4–5 stages · practical coding + take-home · Greenhouse · no standard LC OA gate · light sys design in loop · Bottleneck: take-home + project defense · ~3–8%
