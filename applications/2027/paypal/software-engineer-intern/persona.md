## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**Python**, **C++**; TypeScript covering the JavaScript "or similar" area). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). **Do not invent Java or Node.js.**
- Shipped full-stack or backend software: REST/APIs, a real frontend or client surface, a persistence decision, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12). Debug, tests, or code-review discipline through use matches "test, debug, deploy, triage."
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy–Med HackerRank OA (the intern bottleneck).
- Modern practices through use: Git/version control, tests or CI, SDLC/stakeholder delivery.
- Payments / fintech-backend / data-integrity flavor on that spine: production APIs, financial or regulated-data workflows, reliability/latency or named production defects — memorable at PayPal without replacing the SWE spine.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies returning Fall 2027.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-line Java or Node.js with zero bullet evidence (fabrication smell at a polyglot payments shop).
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, or Sentry — banned; not in this JD and not in inventory.
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML lead as if this were an ML-research intern seat.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web/API/SQL/payments SWE.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + payments-delivery spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
