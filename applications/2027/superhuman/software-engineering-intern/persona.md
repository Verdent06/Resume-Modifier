# Software Engineering Intern (Summer 2027) at Superhuman

## Role Summary

Summer 2027 Software Engineering Intern at Superhuman (Superhuman Platform Inc; Grammarly lineage) — hybrid US hubs in San Francisco, New York, or Seattle, ≥2 days/week in office, twelve weeks. The intern ships a mentor-scoped, fully formed project spanning front-end, back-end, full-stack, security, or mobile, with code going out in the first few weeks to a product used by tens of millions of daily active users. JD surface is generic SWE intern: one programming language (stack includes Java, TypeScript, JavaScript, C#, Python, or Go — other specialties OK), CS fundamentals, enrolled bachelor's CS/related, technical collaboration. Company identity is an AI productivity suite (Grammarly writing assistance, Docs/Coda workspaces, Mail, Go proactive assistant) — product engineering at consumer scale, not an ML-research rotation.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (generic SWE intern — FE / BE / FS / security / mobile; proficiency in at least one language; CS fundamentals; `resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11)
- **differentiator:** AI productivity consumer platform at tens of millions of users — product engineering (apps, APIs, agents as product surfaces), not model training
- **track_divergence:** false

The screen spine and the company identity agree. Lead with a genuinely engineered, shippable full-stack product (frontend framework, backend/API, a datastore, deploy or test path) narrated decision-by-decision, with Python and/or TypeScript demonstrated in bullets. The differentiator does not pull toward `ai-ml` (no training/inference-research bar); it sharpens emphasis *within* full-stack toward product-SWE that ships user-facing systems, including LLM/API product features when they are wired into a shipped app — not notebook ML. The single full-stack track grounds ordering, lead entry, and skills. `lead_signal_window` is 0.

## Team & Bar

Superhuman is B-tier in `companies.md` (~8–12% estimated acceptance, Grammarly peer; bottleneck **resume**, then OA). Funnel: Ashby resume screen (human reads the PDF earlier than Workday OA-gated big-tech — `recruiting.md` §1 / §5) → coding challenge (Grammarly intern heritage: HackerRank ~3Q Easy–Med, ~2h, directional) → recruiter/behavioral + engineer tech. Recruiter voice: an intern who can ship end-to-end with a mentor, collaborate in person at a hub, and talk through APIs/frameworks/tools without needing the exact JD language if the fundamentals are real. Strong signals: shipped systems with metrics; polyglot depth in bullets not Skills alone; product framing at user scale; technical communication. Comp is $50/hr + $8K bonus + living-expense stipend. Hub hybrid (≥2 days/week) is a form/term gate, not a resume skip.

## Screen Criteria

- Full-stack shipping with a frontend, an API/backend, and a datastore or deploy/test path demonstrated in bullets — not Skills-only (`resume.md` §12). Breadth across the stack plus depth in at least one layer.
- At least one JD-listed language the candidate actually has (Python, TypeScript; JavaScript via TypeScript) used inside architectural decisions, not asserted on a Skills line. Do not treat Java / C# / Go as a must-have — the JD says specialty may differ.
- Evidence of shipping to users (product, latency, scale, adoption) over club-ops or GTM-only lead stories.
- Technical collaboration / communication visible in how work is described (mentor-scoped intern; daily learning from a strong team).
- AI/LLM as *product features wired into a shipped system* (API, eval, assistant surface) is on-axis for this company — not a research-track pivot and not "I use Copilot."
- Class-year on page shows current enrollment (`Expected May 2028` matches the form's May/June 2028 window; eligible).
- Fast iterate / ship-in-weeks energy: owned end-to-end slices with before-after numbers beat feature laundry lists.

## Anti-Patterns

- Skills-list Python/TypeScript with zero bullet evidence at a shop where one language in use is the floor.
- Routing the page to research-only LoRA / notebook ML as if this were an `ai-ml` intern req.
- Invented Java, C#, Go, Node.js, GraphQL, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, or Fusion CAD.
- Lead entry that is off-axis for product engineering (pure GTM, low-level DSP with no product spine) while the full-stack shipping story is buried.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a shippable product story.
- Club-ops without engineering depth (`resume.md` §8).
