# Superhuman

Superhuman (legal: Superhuman Platform Inc; formerly Grammarly) is the AI productivity platform that ships writing assistance, collaborative workspaces, inbox management, and a proactive assistant into the tools people already use. The suite covers Grammarly, Superhuman Docs (Coda lineage), Superhuman Mail, and Superhuman Go, integrating with over 1 million applications and websites for 40 million people, 50,000 organizations, and 3,000 educational institutions. Engineering here is consumer product work at tens of millions of daily active users — front-end, back-end, full-stack, security, and mobile — not an ML-research lab. Grammarly remains the writing product; live intern reqs route through this Superhuman row, not the legacy Grammarly listing.
