# Tesla

Tesla designs, manufactures, and sells electric vehicles, energy storage, and solar products, and writes the software that operates those products at fleet scale — from vehicle firmware and Autopilot/FSD stacks to the internal platforms that let Engineering ship them. Software Engineering teams own a wide surface: connected-vehicle systems, firmware tooling, Autopilot support software, and the infrastructure that powers vehicle development. Brand prestige is real; engineering culture is known for speed, ownership, and a high bar for production discipline. This Applications Engineering intern sits on the *support-software* side of that stack — internal apps and infra for Connected Systems, Firmware, and Autopilot teams — not writing vehicle firmware or Autopilot models.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER — Good / Solid Resume Addition (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Austin, TX headquarters; this intern is Fremont, CA (onsite). Other major sites include Palo Alto and Gigafactory locations.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public EV and energy company (NASDAQ: TSLA); household engineering brand; culture described as brutal in the company row
- **Product focus:** EVs, energy products, and the internal engineering platforms that develop and operate them (Connected Systems, Firmware, Autopilot support software)
- **Intern comp (2027 Applications Engineering intern, req 280820):** not listed on the posting. Prior-cycle same-team Fullstack AE intern (Winter/Spring 2025, Fremont) listed $36.06–$50.48/hr plus benefits — directional only, not a 280820 fact
- **Work model:** Paid; Winter/Spring 2027; start around January or February 2027 through the Spring term (ending approximately May 2027 or later if available); minimum 12 weeks; full-time 40 hours/week; on-site Fremont
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Actively enrolled students only; graduation date 2027–2028. Recent grads not returning to school are directed to full-time. CPT: must be able to work 40 hours/week on-site

- **Languages:** JD floor is one or more of Python, Go, Java, C/C++ (or other common industry languages). Nice-to-haves: React, JavaScript, Angular, CSS, MySQL, MS SQL, Oracle. Company row does not pin a single Tesla-wide intern stack — this req is generalist/full-stack plus infra/services
- **Domains:** Internal applications and infrastructure for vehicle-engineering teams (Connected Systems, Firmware, Autopilot); Business Ops services and tools that power internal software and processes
