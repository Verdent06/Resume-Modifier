# Tesla

Tesla designs and manufactures electric vehicles, energy storage, and related products (Optimus, Robotaxi, new factories) at manufacturing scale. This intern sits in Vehicle Engineering's Design Technology group — internal software that automates or streamlines the design process used by thousands of vehicle, battery, and manufacturing engineers — not Autopilot/Dojo and not Tesla Energy UI. The work is full-stack web applications for professional design engineers and program managers: React/Next.js frontends, Python or Go APIs, PostgreSQL, REST/gRPC, and CI/CD. `reference/companies.md` frames Tesla as B-tier EV with a strong brand, a brutal ownership culture, and a HackerRank-gated intern loop whose bottleneck is the technical rounds.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER — Good / Solid Resume Addition (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Austin, TX headquarters; this req is PALO ALTO, CA (also Fremont, CA and other SF Bay Area sites)
- **Valuation / signal:** Public EV / energy company; household engineering brand; intern culture is high-ownership and high-hours
- **Product focus:** Vehicle Engineering Design Technology — internal design-process automation tools for vehicle/battery/manufacturing engineers (CAD-adjacent tooling preferred, not Autopilot)
- **Intern comp (2027 Engineering Automation SWE):** Unlisted on req 279763. Same-role Fall 2026 listing posted $20–$50/hr; Tesla intern SWE often cited ~$20–50/hr in this repo. Extern's Vehicle SWE intern band is ~$36–58/hr (directional, not this req).
- **Work model:** Paid intern/apprentice; full-time on-site; expected start January 2027 through Spring (~May 2027) or continuing into Summer 2027 if available; minimum 12 weeks
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently enrolled in an academic program (CS or related). Recent graduates must apply full-time, not internships. CPT students must confirm 40 hr/week on-site with their school. No class-year cutoff on the posting.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** TypeScript, Python, Go, Java, C++, C#, Bash (JD: at least one of these). Screen stack is React / Next.js / TypeScript **and/or** Python or Go APIs; PostgreSQL is a named floor. Do not invent Next.js, Go, Kubernetes, Jenkins, Redux, Drizzle, or gRPC.
- **Domains:** Internal tools for vehicle/battery/manufacturing design engineers; full-stack web (Next.js frontends, REST/gRPC backends, PostgreSQL + migrations); data-intensive apps; live-system debugging; Docker/K8s CI/CD preferred. CAD (Catia/3DX, SolidWorks) is preferred familiarity, not the screen.
- **What wins:** A full-stack spine with React/TypeScript (or a real web UI) plus Python APIs, PostgreSQL/SQL through use, production delivery, and a live-debug story (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11). Memorable differentiator is tools other engineers actually run — not Autopilot ML, not Energy IoT UI, not generic CRUD with no user. OA (HackerRank) is the volume gate; the resume wins team-match onto Design Technology.
