# de Anda Capital

de Anda Capital is a San Francisco family office and independent sponsor that acquires and grows owner-operated businesses across sectors such as property management, payroll/payments, manufacturing, lending, and decarbonization. The firm is small and operator-led, with 140+ completed deals and a public site oriented around inbound acquisition, operator, and investor conversion. For this internship, the technical value is not classical SWE scale; it is shipping polished Framer pages and visual systems that make the firm's content and deal flow clearer.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco; remote internship in the United States
- **Valuation / signal:** Small family office / independent sponsor; 140+ completed deals
- **Product focus:** Client-facing Framer pages, landing pages, blog layouts, and visual systems for inbound marketing
- **Intern comp (2027 front-end/design):** Not listed
- **Work model:** Internship, remote; phase 2 may transition to part-time paid design role
