# RTX / Applied Signal Technology

Applied Signal Technology (AST) is RTX's signals-intelligence business. Founded in 1984 in Sunnyvale and acquired by Raytheon in 2010, AST builds strategic and tactical SIGINT solutions — high-capacity broadband communications, signal processing and analysis, tactical SIGINT, and wireless communications — for U.S. government customers. The West Valley City / Salt Lake City, UT site (Bldg 338) writes software across the full product lifecycle (architecture, design, integration, test, field support) for applications that process modern communications. This is not Marlborough radar, Tewksbury Patriot/MDS, Huntsville, or Cedar Rapids avionics.
