# Arc (Arc Boat Company)

Arc Boat Company electrifies marine craft: consumer sport boats first (Arc One / Arc Sport), then the same powertrain and software platform into tugs, ferries, and defense. Founded in 2021 in Los Angeles by Rivian / SpaceX / Tesla alumni; HQ and this intern seat are Torrance. Series C **$50M** (Mar 2026; Eclipse, a16z, Menlo, Lowercarbon) took total raise to **~$160M**, on a **$160M** Curtin Maritime hybrid-electric tug contract at the Port of Los Angeles. Software sits next to a physical boat — infotainment, mobile, backend, and firmware — not a generic CRUD intern rotation.
