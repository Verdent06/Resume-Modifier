# BNY

BNY (rebranded from BNY Mellon in 2024) is the world's largest custodian bank: securities services, market infrastructure, and investment servicing rather than bulge-bracket IB coverage. It reports working with 96% of the world's top banks and ~$2.2T AUM (firm intern page, figures as of 2026-07-15); third-party trackers cite ~$59.3T assets under custody **[directional]**. Engineering (Data Science) is a track inside the 10-week Summer Internship Program: applied analytics, feature engineering, and automated models/tools on Agile projects, with program-wide exposure to JavaScript, Python, CSS, and Java. It is **not** an ML-research seat and **not** a generic product-SWE intern.

Firm intern page also says "graduating by August 2027 with limited opportunities for those graduating August 2028" — **this req's JD is the gate**, and May 2028 is in window

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Program text lists JavaScript, Python, CSS, Java. Screen for Python (and SQL/Pandas through use) — the DS job. Do **not** invent Java, Spring, Node, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, Fusion, or Sentry
- **Domains:** Applied data science on Engineering: analysis, feature engineering, anomaly/trend detection, automating analytical processes and models, communicating results to business partners. ML / distributed computing / scalable data-driven solutions are **exposure**, not a research mandate
- **What wins:** Python + SQL shown inside real analytical work; messy multi-source data → method → measured recommendation; applied ML with eval as a plus, not a paper lead. Resume is the front door; OA follows if invited (`recruiting.md` intern funnel)
