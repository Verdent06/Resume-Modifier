# BNY

BNY (rebranded from BNY Mellon in 2024) is the world's largest custodian bank: securities services, market infrastructure, and investment/wealth platforms rather than a bulge-bracket IB coverage shop. Public materials put it at the center of global market plumbing — custody, settlement, clearing, collateral — and the firm states it influences nearly 20% of the world's investable assets. The 2027 Summer Internship Program **Engineering (Developer)** track is Agile application SWE (JavaScript, Python, CSS, Java, and more): designs, APIs, testing, CI/CD, not M&A and not ML-research.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** 240 Greenwich Street, New York, NY (this req **81253**). Sister Developer reqs: Jersey City **81251**, Lake Mary FL **81252**, Pittsburgh **81254**
- **Valuation / signal:** Public global systemically important custody bank; Vault-class financial-services intern brand, not S-tier eng prestige
- **Product focus:** Custody / asset-servicing / market-infrastructure platforms and the engineering that automates them
- **Intern comp (2027 Engineering Developer):** JD base **$52,000–$110,000 per year** at commencement (location-adjusted; intern analog ~$38–$48/hr + sign-on **[directional, Extern]**)
- **Work model:** Paid 10-week Summer Internship Program; NYC onsite/hybrid mix unpublished on 81253 (sister cycle postings were hybrid). Enterprise onboarding, mentorship, executive speaker series
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Enrolled 4-year undergrad; graduating **Dec 2027 or May 2028 (U.S.)**; GPA **3.0+**; **no visa sponsorship** now or in the future. US citizen / no sponsorship clears it. Posted **2026-08-24**

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD names JavaScript, Python, CSS, Java, and more. Screen for coding proof in languages the candidate actually has — Python + JS-family (TypeScript) + CSS. Do not invent Java, Spring, Node, Copilot, Fusion, or Sentry
- **Domains:** Generic intern SWE on bank platforms — APIs, testing, CI/CD, cloud/container concepts, Agile delivery, financial-services outcomes/risk/controls. "Enterprise-approved AI coding tools" is a practice line, not a Copilot metric
- **What wins:** Shipped engineering (APIs/services, cloud deploy, tests/CI, debug) plus finance-adjacent delivery. Per `reference/recruiting.md` Part III §11, this is general SWE with a resume-first intern front door; OA follows invitation. Custody-bank identity is the differentiator, not a second track's requirements list
