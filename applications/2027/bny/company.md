# BNY

BNY (rebranded from BNY Mellon in 2024) is the world's largest custodian bank: securities services, market infrastructure, and investment/wealth platforms rather than a bulge-bracket IB coverage shop. Public materials put it at the center of global market plumbing — custody, settlement, clearing, collateral — and the firm states it influences nearly 20% of the world's investable assets. Two 2027 Engineering intern tracks on Oracle HCM — do not mix: **Engineering (Developer)** Agile application SWE (JavaScript, Python, CSS, Java) and **Engineering (Data Science)** applied analytics / feature engineering / automated models. Neither is IB coverage. Neither is ML-research.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** 240 Greenwich Street, New York, NY. Developer NYC **81253** (sisters Jersey City **81251**, Lake Mary **81252**, Pittsburgh **81254**). Data Science NYC **81240** (sisters Jersey City **81238**, Pittsburgh **81241**, Lake Mary **81239**)
- **Valuation / signal:** Public global systemically important custody bank; Fortune World's Most Admired (29x as of 2026 intern page); Vault-class financial-services intern brand, not S-tier eng prestige
- **Product focus:** Custody / asset-servicing / market-infrastructure platforms and the engineering that automates them
- **Intern comp (2027 Engineering):** JD base **$52,000–$110,000 per year** at commencement (location-adjusted; intern analog ~$38–$48/hr + sign-on **[directional, Extern]**)
- **Work model:** Paid 10-week Summer Internship Program; NYC onsite/hybrid mix unpublished. Enterprise onboarding, mentorship, executive speaker series, in-person induction
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Enrolled 4-year undergrad; graduating **Dec 2027 or May 2028 (U.S.)**; GPA **3.0+**; **no visa sponsorship** now or in the future. Business- and/or technology-related majors preferred on the DS posting. Posted **2026-08-24**

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages (Developer 81253):** JD names JavaScript, Python, CSS, Java, and more. Screen for coding proof in languages the candidate actually has — Python + JS-family (TypeScript) + CSS. Do not invent Java, Spring, Node, Copilot, Fusion, or Sentry
- **Languages (Data Science 81240):** Program text lists JavaScript, Python, CSS, Java. Screen for Python (and SQL/Pandas through use) — the DS job. Do **not** invent Java, Spring, Node, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, Fusion, or Sentry
- **Domains:** Developer is generic intern SWE on bank platforms (APIs, testing, CI/CD, Agile). Data Science is applied analytics: analysis, feature engineering, automating analytical processes and models, communicating results to business partners. ML / distributed computing are **exposure**, not a research mandate
- **What wins:** Resume is the front door; OA follows invitation (`recruiting.md` intern funnel). Developer: shipped engineering plus finance-adjacent delivery. Data Science: Python + SQL in real analytical work; messy multi-source data → method → measured recommendation; applied ML with eval as a plus, not a paper lead
