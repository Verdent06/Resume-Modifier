## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**Python**; TypeScript covering the JavaScript area; HTML/CSS covering **CSS**). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). **Do not invent Java.**
- Shipped full-stack or backend software: REST/APIs or services, persistence, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12). Automated tests, debugging, or code-review discipline through use matches the intern "what you will do" list.
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy–Med HackerRank OA if invited.
- Modern practices through use: Git/version control, tests or CI, Agile/stakeholder delivery.
- Custody-bank / fintech-backend flavor on that spine: production APIs, financial or regulated-data workflows, reliability/controls-minded delivery — memorable here without replacing the SWE spine.
- Cloud/containers and responsible AI-tool use as plus (foundations the program says it will build), never as the lead story, and never invented Copilot/Fusion/Sentry.
- Class-year and GPA on the page: `Expected May 2028` and GPA 3.66 satisfy May 2028 + 3.0 floor.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-line Java / Spring / Node with zero bullet evidence (fabrication smell at a shop that named Java in the intern stack list).
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, or Sentry — banned; not in inventory. Do not turn "enterprise-approved AI coding tools" into a Copilot claim.
- Invented custody-platform, core-banking, or Java-microservice claims because the company is a bank.
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML lead as if this were an ML-research intern seat.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web/API/cloud/enterprise SWE.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + finance-delivery spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
