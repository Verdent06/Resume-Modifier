# Leidos

Leidos is a Reston, Virginia–headquartered government technology and mission contractor (about 47,000 employees; ~$17.2B FY ended 2026-01-02). It sells digital and mission systems to U.S. government and commercial customers. This intern sits in the **Intel Sector Cyber & Analytics Business Area (CABA)** in **Annapolis Junction, MD** — Security Engineering, Computer Network Operations (CNO), Mission Software, Analytical Methods and Modeling, SIGINT, and Cryptographic Key Management — not a generic Reston IT rotation and not an uncleared commercial product team.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-TIER (`reference/companies.md` — Gov IT; peer funnel to Peraton / Booz Allen / RTX intern SWE; resume-gated; limited national SWE brand)
- **HQ / offices:** Reston, VA (HQ). This req: **Annapolis Junction, MD** (onsite; MD in-person clearance interview at intern's own expense)
- **Valuation / signal:** Public large-cap federal contractor; ~$17.2B FY revenue / ~47k employees (About Leidos on this Workday page). Moderate SWE prestige; CABA mission software is the memorable product, not a FAANG intern brand
- **Product focus:** Agile + BDD + test automation + DEVOPS on a large, ongoing classified-adjacent mission-software program (CNO / SIGINT / crypto key management / security engineering)
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Developer Intern, this req):** **$48,100.00 – $86,950.00** listed as a Leidos job-level guideline — not a guaranteed intern salary. `companies.md` Notes cite ~$40/hr as the historical intern analog
- **Work model:** Paid intern starting **summer 2027**; full-time during summer break; potentially part-time during the school year. Posted **2026-09-09**. Workday req **R-00191713**
- **Clearance / eligibility:** **U.S. citizenship required.** **Ability to obtain TS/SCI with Polygraph before start** (investigation includes an in-person Maryland interview at the intern's own expense) and **ability to maintain** after start. Do **not** treat this as clearance-in-hand. Enrolled Bachelor's CS / Engineering / related; **going into Junior or Senior**. ≥1 of Java, C++, Python
