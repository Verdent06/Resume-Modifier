# Fifth Third Bank

Fifth Third Bank, National Association is a Cincinnati-headquartered super-regional bank (NYSE: FITB) operating commercial banking, branch banking, consumer lending, and wealth management across eleven states. The engineering work that matters on this req is **Enterprise Modernization**: the consumer mobile app (J.D. Power #1 among regional banks for mobile-app satisfaction), the "Jeanie" intelligent customer-messaging platform (serverless + AI), Angular micro-frontends, and a domain-modernization program breaking monoliths into microservices for money movement, card operations, and account management. Co-ops land on real Agile teams owning features through deploy — bank product engineering, not branch IT.
