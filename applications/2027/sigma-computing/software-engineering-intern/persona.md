## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**Python**; **TypeScript** as JavaScript analog). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). **Do not invent Java, Sigma product work, Snowflake, Tableau, or warehouse internships.**
- SPA / full-stack delivery: a real UI (React or Angular through use), services or APIs, data modeling or a pipeline, and a deploy or CI path, with at least one product defensible layer-by-layer.
- Testing and debugging through use: unit tests, CI, eval harness, or a named defect with a witness metric — the JD treats automated tests and documentation as how intern work ships.
- Data-adjacent engineering as **honest use** (ETL, SQL, dashboards, messy filings) — the company's BI identity — not a fake Tableau/Looker claim.
- Impact metrics (latency, failure-rate drop, hours saved, throughput, eval lift) at roughly one per two bullets; titles the bullets can support.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies December 2027 or later and June 2028 FT start.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Notebook ML / LoRA / LangGraph-as-research framing as the lead, as if this were an ML internship.
- Club-ops or community-growth without engineering the role tests.
- Skills-only Python (listed, zero bullet evidence) at a company where a modern language is a floor signal.
- Inventing Java, JavaScript-as-a-separate-claimed-language the inventory does not list, Sigma workbooks, Snowflake, BigQuery, Tableau, Looker, or Databricks.
- GTM / deal-sourcing as the lead at a product-analytics SWE company.
- Pure audio-DSP / embedded C++ as the identity for a SPA/backend intern.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a shipped-API + SPA spine.
- Awards section or invented campus-lead titles.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
