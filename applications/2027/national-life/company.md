# National Life Group

National Life Group is the trade name of National Life Insurance Company (Montpelier, VT, founded 1848) and Life Insurance Company of the Southwest (Addison, TX, chartered 1955), plus affiliates. It sells life insurance and annuities to ~1.6 million policyholders; FY2025 results were **$4.64B** revenue, **$65.7B** GAAP assets, and **$703M** core earnings **[AM Best / company 2025 AR, Apr 2026]**. Fortune 1000 is claimed on the JD (NLV Financial was **#775** on the 2022 list — stale rank). This intern sits on the **Inforce Applications** team: enterprise web apps, APIs, Agile SDLC, and production support for in-force policy administration — not actuarial, and not Jackson National Life.
