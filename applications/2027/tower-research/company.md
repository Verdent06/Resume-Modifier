# Tower Research Capital

Tower Research Capital is a proprietary quantitative trading firm founded in 1998. Independent portfolio-manager teams run strategies on a shared high-performance platform that combines market access, data, compute, and research infrastructure. Engineering work is electronic-trading infrastructure: low-latency systems programming, FPGA and hardware acceleration, and machine learning — this intern req is **Software Engineer Intern (Summer 2027)** (Greenhouse 8212158), not Quant Trader, Quant Developer, or Tower Research Ventures fullstack. Tower sits in the elite S-tier quant/HFT band alongside HRT, Virtu, and IMC: earliest cycle, OA-gated, low-single-digit intern acceptance.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** S-Tier (quant / HFT; `reference/companies.md` — prop quant/HFT; independent PM teams on a shared high-performance platform; low-latency, FPGA, hardware acceleration, ML)
- **HQ / offices:** New York, NY — historic Equitable Building, Financial District (this intern). 11+ global offices.
- **Valuation / signal:** Independent prop quant/HFT with a 25+ year track record; elite resume brand in the HRT / Virtu / IMC peer band
- **Product focus:** Shared high-performance electronic-trading platform — latency-sensitive systems, market-data pipelines, algo trading systems, and (AI-assisted) ops automation for independent trading teams
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern):** Anticipated weekly base $3,500–$4,200 (JD) + housing accommodation + free breakfast/lunch/snacks
- **Work model:** Paid, onsite New York; Summer 2027; mentors (including alma-mater pairing); networking/social events; casual dress / open concept
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD student majoring in CS or related. No published GPA or class-year knockout. Returns-to-school after internship is implied by intern status. JD does not state a sponsorship policy.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** C++, Golang, Java, JavaScript, Python, or Rust (JD or-list). SQL + relational databases and working Linux are named qualifications. Honest C++ + Python depth beats inventing Go, Java, or Rust. Do not invent FPGA.
- **Domains:** Low-latency / network and systems programming; market-data and reference-data pipelines; algo trading systems; large-data collect/analyze/visualize; (AI-assisted) trading-ops automation
- **What wins:** C++ and Python proven in real systems work (latency, concurrency, memory discipline, production shipping) — not Skills-only; SQL demonstrated in bullets; data-pipeline / fast-access evidence for the market-data identity; genuine markets curiosity without a fake trading-desk internship.
