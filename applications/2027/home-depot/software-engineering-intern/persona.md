## Role Summary

Eleven-week paid SWE intern (May 17–July 30 2027) at The Home Depot, primarily remote with up to three weeks at the Atlanta Store Support Center. Interns join an existing product team (Supply Chain, e-commerce, Technology, or another SSC function) and contribute to software design, development, and product lifecycle under a senior engineer — pairing with UX, engineering, and PM to ship secure, reliable, scalable production code. Also in scope: COTS configuration, automation scripts for infrastructure and tests, code review of senior work, and communities of practice. JD surface is generic intern SWE with a web-front-end preference (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, AngularJS, ReactJS, jQuery) plus Git and paired programming. Company identity is retail-tech product engineering at Home Depot scale (stores + e-commerce/SSC), not a store job, not BI/warehouse analytics, and not an ML-research seat.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Web front-end through use: HTML/CSS honestly listed if inventory has it; React and/or Angular inside bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2, §12). TypeScript covers the JD's JavaScript family honestly — do not invent vanilla JS or jQuery.
- Git / version control through use (CI, repos, collaboration), not Skills-only.
- Shipped full-stack or product software: REST/APIs or services, a real UI, a persistence or deploy decision, production delivery under a product-lifecycle story (`resume.md` §12).
- Collaboration / pairing signal: stakeholder-scoped delivery, cross-functional product work, or group engineering — the JD names paired programming as preferred.
- Automation for infra or tests (scripts, CI, Docker) tied to an outcome — maps to the delivery-and-support accountabilities.
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy CoderPad if one fires.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` — current student returning after the internship; no hard class-year filter on this req.
- GPA on the page if ≥ 3.0 (preferred floor).

**Anti-patterns:**

- Invented **jQuery, Java, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, GCP/BigQuery, Copilot, Fusion, Sentry, Kubernetes, or Go** — banned; not honest inventory and not this JD's stack.
- Skills-line React/Angular/HTML/CSS/Git with zero bullet evidence at a shop where web FE + Git are the preferred floor.
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML lead as if this were an ML intern seat.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web/product SWE.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack product spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
