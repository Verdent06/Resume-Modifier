# The Home Depot

The Home Depot is the world's largest home-improvement retailer (~2,200 stores) whose Store Support Center technology org builds product software for associates, customers, and the retail operation — e-commerce, supply chain, merchandising, and store-facing tools. This req is a generic Software Engineering intern seat on an existing product team: ship production code under a senior engineer, pair with UX/engineering/PM, write automation for infrastructure and tests, and optionally configure COTS. It is retail-tech product engineering at Home Depot scale, not a store job and not a data-warehouse or BI rotation.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Atlanta Store Support Center (SSC / 9090). This SWE intern req is **Remote/Virtual** with up to 3 weeks in Atlanta (travel covered for eligible interns).
- **Valuation / signal:** Public Fortune-class retailer; legitimate SWE work; limited national eng-brand signal vs. Big Tech. Comp sits at the C-tier intern band.
- **Product focus:** Customer- and associate-facing product software across stores, e-commerce, and SSC functional teams
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineering intern):** $24.00–$27.00/hr (Workday pay transparency for CA, CO, CT, RI, NV, NYC, Ithaca NY, Westchester County NY, WA; careers.homedepot.com lists the same range)
- **Work model:** Paid 11-week full-time internship, May 17–July 30 2027; primarily remote; up to 3 weeks onsite Atlanta SSC
- **Clearance / eligibility:** College-student summer intern program. No hard class-year gate on this posting. Preferred: pursuing a relevant advanced degree (THD intern usage includes undergrads), 0–1 years experience, GPA 3.0+. No citizenship/export-control gate named on this req.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Preferred web front-end exposure: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, AngularJS, ReactJS, jQuery; Git. Do **not** claim Tableau, Snowflake, Databricks, GCP/BigQuery, Java, Copilot, Fusion, Sentry, Kubernetes, or Go — this SWE JD does not list them.
- **Domains:** Product-team SWE — design, development, product lifecycle; pairing with UX/eng/PM; production code; automation for infra and tests; COTS configuration; retail/e-commerce/SSC tools
- **What wins:** Shipped full-stack or web-front-end software with Git through use, collaboration/pairing evidence, and production delivery (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11). Resume is the binding intern gate; Easy coding (if any) follows. Retail-scale customer-facing product flavor is memorable, not a second track.
