# The Home Depot

The Home Depot is the world's largest home-improvement retailer: ~2,300+ stores, a large e-commerce and Pro business, and a corporate Store Support Center in Atlanta that owns merchandising, supply chain, services, finance, and technology. This req is the **undergrad / applied Data Science & Analytics intern** (Workday Req191968) — translate business questions into insights for Services, e-Commerce, Merchandising, Operations, or Finance — **not** the Master's/PhD data-science intern and **not** the SWE intern (GCP/CoderPad). Retail tech at Fortune-scale, not a consumer-app product org.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-tier (`reference/companies.md` — retail tech; peer to Best Buy / Target Tech for intern brand; resume-gated, not FAANG SWE prestige)
- **HQ / offices:** Atlanta Store Support Center (this intern: STORE SUPPORT CENTER, ATLANTA - 9090). Austin tech hub exists for other tracks; this DS seat is Atlanta.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: HD); Fortune 10 retailer. Internship program named Top 20 in the US on this posting; Vault 2026 **[directional]**: #1 Best Retail Internship. ~400–500 summer interns across ~14 tracks (Extern analog).
- **Product focus:** Enterprise retail analytics — applied DS/analytics on merchandising, e-commerce, operations, services, and finance questions at store+digital scale
- **Intern comp (2027 Data Science & Analytics):** Not specified on Req191968. 2026 undergrad DS analog listed $27/hr in pay-transparency jurisdictions **[directional]**; Extern band ~$22–$35/hr across tracks, SWE/Cyber ~$24–$27/hr official
- **Work model:** Paid 11-week Summer 2027 intern, **May 17 – July 30, 2027**; **onsite 5 days/week** Monday–Friday at Atlanta Corporate Office (this posting; 2026 analog was Mon–Thu + Friday remote — do not import hybrid). Housing assistance for eligible interns.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** College-student program. This posting: preferred GPA 3.0+, excellent academic performance, Python, SQL/BigQuery, Tableau, Microsoft Office/PowerPoint, statistics/predictive modeling. No class-year gate on the posting. Extern analog **[directional]**: sophomore–grad, must return to coursework after summer; F-1 CPT accepted. No sponsorship language on this req.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD names SQL, Python, Google Cloud BigQuery (preferred query stack), Tableau (preferred viz), Microsoft Office / PowerPoint. Honest Python + SQL through use wins; do not invent BigQuery, Tableau, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, or Sentry.
- **Domains:** Applied retail analytics — Services, e-Commerce, Merchandising, Operations, Finance. Presentations to upper management. Predictive modeling / statistics as preferred methodologies, not a research-scientist seat.
- **What wins:** Because the bottleneck is the resume (`recruiting.md` intern funnel; C-tier retail tech; mid-size / non-tech-tech is resume-first), a one-page PDF that shows end-to-end ingest → query/transform → insight or model → measured outcome, Python/SQL through use, and stakeholder-facing report-outs. Club-ops, notebook ML, PhD-research framing, and generic SWE leads fail this screen.
