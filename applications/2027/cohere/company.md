# Cohere

Cohere is a security-first enterprise AI company building foundation models and end-to-end products for business customers. The firm trains and serves large language models for retrieval, generation, and agentic workflows, pairing model research with production API infrastructure that enterprises integrate into customer-facing and internal applications. HQ is Toronto with global offices (London, NYC, SF, Montreal, Paris, Berlin, Seoul); roles are remote-friendly. Cohere sits in the legitimate AI signal band — enterprise NLP with IPO expected H2 2026 — not a thin wrapper on third-party APIs.
