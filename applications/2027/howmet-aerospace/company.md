# Howmet Aerospace

Howmet Aerospace Inc. (NYSE: **HWM**) is a Pittsburgh-headquartered engineered-components OEM for aerospace and commercial transportation. The lineage is Alcoa (1888) → Arconic → **1 April 2020** Howmet, which retained Engine Products, Fastening Systems, Engineered Structures, and Forged Wheels while rolled products spun to Arconic Corp. Primary businesses: jet-engine components, aerospace fastening systems, titanium structural parts, and forged wheels. FY2025 **$8.3B** revenue; ~**25,430** employees YE2025 (2025 AR); ~1,200 patents; ~57 locations / 23 countries. **This folder is the Corporate IT Intern – Artificial Intelligence packet only** (Oracle CX **119494**). It is **not** a mechanical, process, or plant-metallurgy intern. Do not invent aerospace-domain work for the candidate.
