# TIFIN

TIFIN builds "the AI operating layer for wealth" — an agentic platform (TIFIN.AI) that orchestrates specialized AI agents across the core personas of wealth management (investors, advisors, investment teams, operations) so financial institutions can serve more clients without replacing core infrastructure. The system combines finance-native AI, specialized data, and enterprise-grade controls to deploy secure, compliant capabilities into real production environments — advisor copilots, operational automation (onboarding, ACAT transfers, monitoring), and investment workflows, coordinated with shared context across up to ~20 agents. Founded in 2018 by Dr. Vinay Nair and headquartered in Boulder, CO, TIFIN's incubator model spawned ventures including 55ip (acquired by J.P. Morgan); TIFIN.AI launched April 2026 with a ~60-person team and live deployments across 10+ enterprise wealth clients.
