# AI Engineering Intern, TIFIN.ai at TIFIN

## Role Summary

A Summer 2027, onsite (~3-month) AI engineering internship in Boulder for a vertical-AI fintech building "the AI operating layer for wealth." The intern builds production-grade AI systems and multi-agent workflows across three surfaces: an advisor copilot (multi-agent retrieval and reasoning over client data, portfolio/risk analysis, compliant client communications inside a monitored, auditable architecture), end-to-end workflow automation (discovery → research synthesis → portfolio construction → compliant meeting prep), and the underlying agent platform (orchestration engines, memory/state management, dynamic tool invocation, structured-output validation, observability, fault tolerance, automated evaluation). The JD surface is applied AI engineering in Python; the company identity is secure, compliant, agentic AI for regulated financial institutions.

## Team & Bar

A small, venture-backed AI engineering team (~60 people at TIFIN.AI launch) reading resumes directly through Greenhouse — the PDF carries real weight (not a high-volume OA-gated funnel where the resume only matters post-OA). The reviewer is an engineer or hiring manager screening for someone who can build and ship real agentic/LLM systems, not run notebooks or wire up API demos. Winning evidence: Python shown through real backend/API work; at least one genuinely engineered, defensible AI system (multi-agent or LLM pipeline) that shipped to real use; reliability/evaluation discipline (eval harnesses, structured-output/consensus validation, observability); and — as a differentiator, not a gate — any fintech, compliance, or auditability-adjacent framing. Intern-stage weighting still favors engineered projects + a live GitHub (`resume.md` §9), and math foundations in coursework/GPA are a genuine ai-ml signal (`resume.md` §14).

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract — the writer discovers which entries carry them):**
- Python demonstrated inside real backend/API/system work, not only in the Skills line.
- At least one deep, defensible agentic or LLM system: orchestration, retrieval/reasoning over data, tool use, or a multi-node pipeline — narratable decision-by-decision.
- Evaluation / reliability rigor made visible: eval harnesses, structured-output validation, deterministic gates, observability, or fault tolerance — the platform-reliability substance the JD names.
- Impact metrics on latency, accuracy/faithfulness, throughput, cost, or scale so AI work reads as measured engineering, not aspiration.
- Backend/data-pipeline and cloud/deployment fluency tied to delivery (APIs, containers, workers, data stores).
- Within-track fintech/compliance or auditability signal kept visible without turning the page into a pure research CV.

**Anti-patterns (no-pile):**
- "Notebook ML" or `model.fit()` with no delivery, no system, no production reasoning.
- Agentic/LLM claims that collapse under "walk me through this" — buzzwords without architecture.
- Off-axis entries (pure ETL/frontend/club-ops with no AI or systems depth) occupying prime slots on a page that screens on AI substance.
- Skills-line AI/ML tools with no backing bullet (orphaned frameworks).
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact.
