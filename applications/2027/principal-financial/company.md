# Principal Financial Group

Principal Financial Group is a Fortune 500 (NYSE: PFG) retirement, insurance, and asset-management firm with 140+ years of history. Engineering sits at the intersection of financial services and technology: customer-facing tools whose bar is security, scalability, and performance — fintech-backend / retirement-insurance platforms, not a trading desk and not a consumer-app startup. The Technology & Engineering intern program places 100+ SWE interns onto Agile scrum teams with a formal leader and mentor (20+ year program). This packet is the Charlotte, NC Software Engineer Internship (iCIMS 52513, Summer 2027), not the Des Moines World Headquarters SWE posting scheduled 9/14.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-tier (`reference/companies.md` — Fortune 500 retirement/insurance/AM; legitimate intern program; limited national SWE brand vs B-tier finance-tech names)
- **HQ / offices:** Des Moines, IA (HQ / World Headquarters); this req is Charlotte, NC — 401 McCullough Dr, St 200 & 300, Charlotte, NC 28262
- **Valuation / signal:** Public Fortune 500 (NYSE: PFG); purpose-led financial-security franchise; 100+ SWE interns / year in a 20+ year Tech & Engineering intern program
- **Product focus:** Retirement, insurance, and asset-management platforms — customer-centric financial tools
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer Internship):** $28–$30/hr + $4,000 lump-sum stipend (tax gross-up). Pension eligible: No
- **Work model:** Paid intern, Summer 2027; Charlotte office; **minimum 3 days per week in office**. Program length typically 10–12 weeks (careers internships page)
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently pursuing or completed undergraduate degree (STEM preferred); no junior/senior cutoff. **Not considering any immigration sponsorship now or in the future** (F-1 OPT/CPT, H-1B, TN, L-1, J-1, etc.). AI assists application review; humans make final hiring decisions. Posting window: 3 full days after Original Posting Date (9/3/2026), then may close (`validThrough` ~2026-09-11)

## Interview Process

| Stage | Format | Notes |
| ----- | ------ | ----- |
| Resume screen | Human + ATS (iCIMS) | AI may assist initial review; humans decide. 3-day posting window — apply promptly. Bottleneck is the PDF. |
| Recruiter / HR | Phone or pre-recorded (HireVue-style) | Campus intern reports: first round often pre-recorded behavioral |
| Assessment | Possible coding / employability / aptitude | Intern OA unpublished (`companies.md`). Full-time SWE loops sometimes include Java/OOP live coding — do not assume a named intern OA platform |
| Technical / team | Live interview (Teams) | Intern reports: easy coding, well below LeetCode Easy. No intern system design |
| Behavioral | Culture / leadership / teamwork | JD weights student-org involvement, communication, work ethic |

**Estimated funnel:** iCIMS resume → recruiter HR phone or pre-recorded → possible coding/employability assessment · intern OA unpublished · No intern sys design · Bottleneck: resume · ~15–25%

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages (this JD stand-out, not exclusive):** Java, Python, React, JavaScript, Git, AWS. Data-viz tools named (R, Business Objects, Informatica, PowerBI, SAS) are nice-to-haves — do not invent them. Do not invent Java, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Tableau, Fusion, Sentry, or R.
- **Domains:** Agile scrum SWE; customer-centric application design; cloud; test/deploy/maintain; security and scalability on retirement/insurance/AM platforms
- **What wins:** A general SWE intern page — shipped APIs or full-stack apps, Python/JS-family/React/Git/AWS through use, tests/automation, Agile/stakeholder delivery — plus finance-adjacent backend or regulated-data signal as the Principal differentiator. Resume is the gate (`recruiting.md`: intern funnel; mid-size / non-tech-tech, human reads the PDF early). Do not invent Java or visualization stacks.
