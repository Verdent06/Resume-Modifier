# Software Engineer Internship - Charlotte, NC (Summer 2027) at Principal Financial Group

## Role Summary

Paid Summer 2027 Software Engineer intern on Principal's Technology & Engineering program in Charlotte, NC (iCIMS 52513) — hybrid, **minimum 3 days per week in office** at 401 McCullough Dr. Interns join an Agile scrum team with a formal leader and mentor; placement is customized to expertise and interests across a 100+ intern class (20+ year program). Surface work is generic SWE: design, develop, test, deploy, maintain, and improve software; customer-centric solutions with security, scalability, and performance; cloud experience; automation tests; partnering with architecture and stakeholders. Company identity is **fintech-backend / retirement-insurance platforms** — a Fortune 500 (NYSE: PFG) retirement, insurance, and asset-management firm building customer-facing financial tools — not a trading desk, not a consumer-app startup, and not an ML-research or data-viz intern seat. This packet is Charlotte only; Des Moines World Headquarters SWE internships post 9/14. AI may assist application review; humans make the hiring decision.

Winning *kinds* of evidence: JD languages the candidate actually has, demonstrated in bullets not Skills alone; a shippable product or production API with cloud/deploy and a persistence decision; tests/automation or CI through use; Agile/stakeholder delivery to real users; finance-adjacent or regulated-data signal as flavor. Intern-stage weighting still favors engineered projects plus live GitHub (`resume.md` intern pyramid). Leadership in student/work/community orgs is a stand-out, not a substitute for shipped software.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**Python**; TypeScript covering the JavaScript/React area). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). **Do not invent Java.**
- Shipped full-stack or backend software: REST/APIs or services, a real frontend (React) or client surface, a persistence decision, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12). Automation tests, Git, and Agile/scrum-shaped shipping with stakeholders match "What You'll Do."
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an easy live coding / unpublished OA.
- Finance-adjacent or regulated-data flavor on that spine: production APIs, financial or stakeholder-scoped data workflows, reliability/security/scalability language — memorable at Principal without replacing the SWE spine.
- Student-org involvement, prior internship, or leadership as supporting evidence of the "Who You Are" bar — never as the lead story in place of shipped software.
- AI / data-viz / DevOps tooling as plus (stand-out list), never as the lead story, and never invented (no Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Tableau, Fusion, Sentry, PowerBI, Informatica, SAS, Business Objects, or R).
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies currently pursuing undergrad for a Summer 2027 internship.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-line Java with zero bullet evidence (fabrication smell at a shop that lists Java as a stand-out).
- Invented visualization or data-platform tools (R, PowerBI, Informatica, SAS, Business Objects, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau) or Copilot/Fusion/Sentry.
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML lead as if this were an ML-research intern seat.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web/API/cloud/enterprise SWE.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + fintech-backend spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
