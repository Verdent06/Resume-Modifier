A Summer 2027 exclusive-onsite software engineering internship in Aurora, CO (US-CO-AURORA-S75, 16800 E Centretech Pkwy, Bldg S75). The intern designs, develops, tests, delivers, and maintains software for satellite ground systems on an Agile team of engineers, architects, and leaders. The JD *surface* is generic SWE intern: OOP in C++ or Java, SAFe Agile, enrolled through the term, U.S. citizenship / TS/SCI eligibility.
