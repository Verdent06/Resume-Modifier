# RTX / Raytheon Air & Space Defense Systems (Aurora)

Raytheon's Air & Space Defense Systems (ASDS) strategic business unit builds multi-domain integrated air and missile defense, advanced sensors, space-based systems, hypersonics, effectors, and cyber solutions — detection and sensing, command and control, and munitions for air, space, and missile defense. The Aurora, CO campus (16800 E Centretech Pkwy, Bldg S75) is a space-missions software site: satellite ground systems (command & telemetry, flight dynamics, planning and scheduling, spacecraft operations), intelligence mission planning/processing, and cloud-native / DevSecOps modernization of those ground stacks — not Marlborough radar, Tewksbury Patriot/MDS, West Valley City SIGINT, or Cedar Rapids avionics. Same-campus FT work includes Eclipse command-and-telemetry modernization and M2C2 CTC (command, telemetry, control) product teams on classified labs.
