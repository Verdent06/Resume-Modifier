# CHS

CHS Inc. is the largest U.S. farmer-owned cooperative (Cenex + Harvest States merger **1998**; origins late 1920s), headquartered at **5500 Cenex Drive, Inver Grove Heights, MN 55077**. It sells agronomy, grain, foods, and energy (including **Cenex®**) to customers in **65** countries. FY2025 10-K (YE **2025-08-31**): revenues **$35.5B**, net income attributable to CHS **$597.9M**, **10,683** employees. Nasdaq preferred **CHSCP / CHSCO / CHSCN / CHSCM / CHSCL**. Fact sheet: **750** cooperatives, **75,000** producers, **20,000** preferred stockholders. Fortune 500 **#130** (2026) **[directional, Fortune China]**. This intern is **Transportation/Supply Chain Data Analyst Intern** (SuccessFactors RMK **1433568700** / Job ID **25111**) on a Business Intelligence Analyst team — extract/clean/integrate (Snowflake, databases, APIs, spreadsheets), Power BI / Dataiku dashboards, document BI solutions — **not** Refined & Renewable Fuels Trading Intern **25116**, **not** Strategic Sourcing Intern **25087**, **not** a SWE intern.
