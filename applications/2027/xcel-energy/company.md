# Xcel Energy

Xcel Energy (Nasdaq: XEL) is a Minneapolis-based Fortune 500 regulated electric and natural gas utility serving customers in eight Western and Midwestern states through subsidiaries including PSCo (Colorado). The company owns and operates a generation fleet (wind, natural gas, coal, nuclear, other) plus transmission and distribution. Two Summer 2027 Colorado intern reqs live under this folder — **do not mix them:**

- **Power Generation Analytics Intern- CO (Workday JR115734)** — Energy Supply **Equipment and System Analytics (ESA)** at Henderson, CO 80640 (Materials Distribution Center). Fleet O&M analytics, Asset Performance Management software, operational/financial risk tools. Not generic utility IT and not a plant-technician rotation.
- **Reliability Data Analyst Intern- CO (Workday JR115833)** — **Electric Distribution System Performance (EDSP)** at Lipan Distribution Center, 1123 W 3rd Ave, Denver, CO 80223. Outage / reliability-metric / asset-performance analytics and reporting. Not Distribution Modeling & Analytics Intern- CO, not Gas Data Intern- CO, and not an ML-research seat.
