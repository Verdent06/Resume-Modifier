# Xcel Energy

Xcel Energy (Nasdaq: XEL) is a Minneapolis-based Fortune 500 regulated electric and natural gas utility serving customers in eight Western and Midwestern states through subsidiaries including PSCo (Colorado). The company owns and operates a generation fleet (wind, natural gas, coal, nuclear, other) plus transmission and distribution. This intern sits on Energy Supply's **Equipment and System Analytics (ESA)** team — fleet O&M analytics, Asset Performance Management software, and tools that identify operational and financial risk across the generation portfolio — applied power-generation analytics, not generic utility IT and not a plant-technician rotation.
