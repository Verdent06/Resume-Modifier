# Software Engineering Intern (Summer 2027) at Cadence Solutions

## Role Summary

A paid **12-week Remote (U.S.)** Summer 2027 software engineering internship at Cadence (cadence.care; Greenhouse **4711210006** / req **658**), **$55/hour**. Interns own a defined project from design and prototype through production rollout: AI-powered features and agents for chronic-care delivery between visits, sitting on messy healthcare data and cloud/distributed systems, with reliability and patient impact as the scoreboard. Past intern examples are a scheduling agent, a device-troubleshooting agent, and a transcript analyzer. JD *requirements* are generic SWE intern (CS or equivalent, Dec 2027 or May/June 2028 grad, prior internship/project/hands-on SWE, product-first, communication, fluency with AI-assisted development tools and judgment about where AI should not decide). Company identity is supervised clinical AI in health-system EMRs — not Cadence Design Systems, not ML-research, not a prompt-playground.

## Screen Criteria

- Full-stack spine: shipped product with a backend/API layer, a data store or pipeline, and a frontend or client — breadth plus depth in one layer (`resume.md` §12). Lead with shipping, not with a research-ML identity.
- Production AI/agent systems visible in the top half: agents, evals, or judgment/consensus gates demonstrated in bullets (architectural decision + metric), not a Skills-only AI list.
- Python and TypeScript through use in bullets (candidate languages; JD names none). React through use is a plus; do not imply a React production internship if the titled frontend is a different framework.
- Cloud-based delivery (AWS, Docker, workers/queues) tied to a ship story — the JD names cloud systems that support patients and care teams at scale.
- Builder / product-first evidence: owned delivery, diagnosed defects, shipped under a real stakeholder — matching "ownership from inception to launch."
- Quantified impact (latency, cost, accuracy, eval lift, throughput, reliability, paying users) so "production" and "patient impact" can be sized in ~7 seconds (`resume.md` §4).
- Communication / stakeholder signal somewhere on the page (client delivery or owned scoping).
- Class-year on page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies the intern graduation window.
- Keywords through use, not isolation (`resume.md` §2): agents, production, cloud, APIs, evals, reliability.

Anti-patterns:

- Notebook ML, LoRA-as-the-whole-story, or research-track framing that hides the SWE intern spine.
- Club-ops, GTM/quant sourcing, or deal-flow with no engineering the role tests.
- DSP/audio-plugin identity as the lead (hard real-time C++ is not this intern's screen).
- Inventing FHIR, HL7, Epic, healthcare internships, Java, Go, Kubernetes, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau.
- Skills-list Python/TypeScript with zero bullet evidence.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a shipped-product + agentic spine.
- Healthcare-mission essays with no engineering the 30-second screen can size.
