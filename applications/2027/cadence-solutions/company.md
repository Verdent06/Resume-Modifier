# Cadence Solutions

Cadence (cadence.care; Greenhouse board `solutions`) is the clinical AI company automating chronic-disease care between visits. Clinical Intelligence monitors patients, surfaces risk, and coordinates action so clinicians keep the decisions that require them — supervised AI agents plus a dedicated medical group, embedded in partner EMRs and health-system workflows. Not Cadence Design Systems (EDA).

The intern identity is product SWE on AI-powered care-delivery features (scheduling agents, device-troubleshooting agents, transcript analyzers) sitting on messy healthcare data and cloud/distributed systems — not an ML-research rotation and not a generic CRUD shop.
