# Moog Inc.

Moog Inc. (NYSE: MOG.A / MOG.B) is a precision motion- and fluid-control company founded in 1951 and headquartered in East Aurora, New York. FY2025 revenue was about $3.86B with ~13,500 employees; four operating segments are Space and Defense, Military Aircraft, Commercial Aircraft, and Industrial. Military Aircraft designs and integrates primary/secondary flight controls and mission-critical actuation for military fixed-wing and rotorcraft. This intern sits on the **Software Engineering team in the Military Aircraft Group at Mineral Wells, TX** — the former **Genesys Aerosystems** avionics shop (acquired December 2020) that builds electronic flight instrument systems, flight displays, radios, and autopilot solutions for special-mission, military, trainer, business, and general aviation. It is not an East Aurora / Cheektowaga actuation-software seat, not Space & Defense Arvada flight software, and not a mechanical/design-engineering intern at the same site.
