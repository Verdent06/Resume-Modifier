# DV Trading

DV Trading is a North American proprietary trading firm and global liquidity provider, founded in 2006 and headquartered in Chicago with major offices in New York, London, and satellite locations worldwide. The firm deploys its own capital across futures, options, equities, fixed income, FX, and crypto using proprietary low-latency trading software and infrastructure — its DV Commodities division is among the largest liquidity providers in global energy markets. Engineering builds the production systems traders rely on: fast, reliable, scalable market access and operational tooling in a large shared codebase.

- **Tier:** Unrated (quant/prop-trading peer — funnel family near IMC, HRT, Two Sigma)
- **HQ / offices:** Chicago (HQ); NYC (395 Hudson St), London, Toronto, Miami, Houston, Dubai, Bahamas
- **Valuation / signal:** 500+ personnel worldwide; independent prop firm since 2016 spin-out from brokerage; multi-division DV Group (broker-dealers, crypto market making, commodities)
- **Product focus:** Proprietary low-latency trading platform, market-making infrastructure, risk and operational systems for desk traders
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern):** $50/hr paid (per JD)
