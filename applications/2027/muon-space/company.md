# Muon Space

Muon Space is an end-to-end space systems provider that designs, builds, and operates LEO satellite constellations for Earth Intelligence — climate monitoring and national security remote sensing. Founded in 2021, it sells a vertically integrated stack (spacecraft, payloads, software, operations) rather than a single bus SKU. Flagship programs include FireSat / Earth Fire Alliance wildfire sensing and other dual-use missions; the company reports 11 spacecraft launched and a San Jose factory sized for ~500 satellites per year by 2027. This intern sits on Engineering–Software writing **flight software** that runs on the spacecraft, plus the simulations and test/dev tools used to validate it — not a generic cloud-SWE rotation and not the Applied Science intern.
