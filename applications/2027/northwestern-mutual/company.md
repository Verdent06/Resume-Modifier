# Northwestern Mutual

Northwestern Mutual is a Milwaukee-headquartered mutual life insurer and wealth manager (est. 1857; Fortune 500). The JD copy: 160+ years; more than **$290B** assets; **$30B** revenues; more than **$1.9T** life insurance in force; **4.6M** clients. Extern 2026 directional: ~$41B mutual, Fortune 500 **#109**, highest financial-strength ratings among US life insurers for 35 years — treat as analog, not this req. HQ is **720 E Wisconsin Ave, Milwaukee**; campuses also NYC and Franklin, WI. The Investment Data & Analytics team supplies trusted data and analytic research to Northwestern Mutual **Managed Investment Business** partners via maintainable reporting. **This intern is Investment Data & Analytics (JR-46050)** — cross-asset investment reporting, dashboards, ad hoc analysis, automation of manual processes, and data migration/validation — **not** the College Financial Representative sales intern, **not** Investment Analyst Intern – Private Debt & Equity, **not** Public Investments Quantitative Analyst or Credit Investment intern, and **not** a SWE intern.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** THIS JD names **no required programming language**. **SQL and data management are desirable, not a required language floor** — still treat SQL as a through-use ATS keyword (`resume.md` keyword-through-use). Excel spreadsheets and data models are a proven-aptitude qualification; Power BI is desirable. **Do not invent** Power BI, Excel-as-claimed-tool, Tableau, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Aladdin, Bloomberg, dbt, or investment-program enrollment.
- **Domains:** Cross-asset investment reporting, whole-portfolio views, end-user dashboards, ad hoc trusted-data analysis, automation of manual processes, data-concept/relationship/structure work for migration and validation across environments. Managed Investment Business partners — not field CFR, not PDE deal analysis, not public-markets credit/quant.
- **What wins:** End-to-end ingest → transform/validate/aggregate → report, dashboard, or ranked insight → measured outcome (`resume.md` Part III §14; `recruiting.md` Part III §13 applied data). SQL shown inside real data work when the pool has it. Stakeholder-facing reporting for a non-builder (researchers, principals, investment partners). Resume is the gate (`recruiting.md`: intern knockouts first; mid-size / non-tech-tech, human reads the PDF early). This is **not** ML research and **not** SWE.
