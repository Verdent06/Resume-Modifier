# Coinbase

Coinbase (NASDAQ: COIN) is the largest U.S. regulated crypto exchange: consumer apps and wallets, Coinbase Prime for institutions, and Coinbase Developer Platform APIs (payments, wallets, trading, stablecoins), plus Base, Coinbase's Ethereum L2. The company is remote-first, not remote-only — quarterly in-person "surges" — and stores more digital assets than any other crypto platform (about page: $246B assets on platform as of 6/30/26). The US campus SWE intern is a generic Emerging Talent seat that team-matches into Product Group (SF: Base, Consumer, Developer, Institutional, Platform) or Core (NYC: Security, Enterprise Engineering). Company identity is still **web-scale crypto product and infra** — regulated exchange + L2 + custody — not an ML-research lab and not a trading desk.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD: *any language* (academic, personal, or professional). Do **not** invent Go, Rust, Solidity, or Coinbase employment. Screen tests shipped software in whatever the candidate actually uses.
- **Domains:** Generic intern SWE on crypto-forward product/infra: write tested code that ships to production, code review, own a 12-week project end-to-end, apply blockchain concepts in a production environment. Team landing is applicant-selected (Base / Consumer / Developer / Institutional / Platform / Security / EE). Generative AI copilots (LibreChat, Gemini, Glean) are a **workflow** requirement — human-in-the-loop, not an ML-research seat, and **not** permission to claim GitHub Copilot.
- **What wins:** Shipped full-stack or backend software with tests, a persistence or deploy decision, and CS fundamentals defensible in an Easy–Med CodeSignal (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11). Finance-adjacent or high-reliability production delivery is memorable here; crypto-native claims that are not on the page are fabrication. **Resume is the binding intern gate**; the OA equalizes after the page clears. Apply immediately — rolling, 95% resume rejection.
