# Coinbase

Coinbase (NASDAQ: COIN) is the largest U.S. regulated crypto exchange: consumer apps and wallets, Coinbase Advanced, Coinbase Prime for institutions, Coinbase Developer Platform APIs (payments, wallets, trading, stablecoins), plus Base, Coinbase's Ethereum L2. Founded 2012 (Brian Armstrong, Fred Ehrsam). The company is remote-first, not remote-only — quarterly in-person "surges" — and stores more digital assets than any other crypto platform (about page: $246B assets on platform as of 6/30/26). Company identity is **web-scale crypto product and infra** / **fintech-backend / exchange-platform data** — regulated exchange + L2 + custody — not an ML-research lab and not a trading desk.

Two 2027 intern reqs live under this folder:

- **Software Engineer Intern** (Greenhouse **8168315** / **GETR-PSWE00US-Q326**) — generic Emerging Talent SWE; team-matches into Product Group (SF: Base, Consumer, Developer, Institutional, Platform) or Core (NYC: Security, Enterprise Engineering). **$60/hr.**
- **Data Engineer Intern** (Greenhouse **8175459** / **P78146**) — Platform product group, partnering with analytics engineering on pipelines and infrastructure for analytics and ML use cases. **Not** a generic product-SWE seat and **not** matching-engine. **$50/hr.** Hybrid — San Francisco, CA.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **SWE intern languages:** JD: *any language* (academic, personal, or professional). Do **not** invent Go, Rust, Solidity, or Coinbase employment.
- **DE intern languages:** JD names **Python** and **SQL**. Inventory cloud is **AWS (EC2, S3)** only — do not invent GCP, Azure, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, Spark, Kafka, or Airflow.
- **SWE domains:** Generic intern SWE on crypto-forward product/infra: write tested code that ships to production, code review, own a 12-week project. Team landing is applicant-selected. Generative AI copilots (LibreChat, Gemini, Glean) are a **workflow** requirement — human-in-the-loop, not an ML-research seat, and **not** permission to claim GitHub Copilot.
- **DE domains:** Data pipelines and infrastructure for analytics and ML; multi-source ingest (internal systems + external APIs); data integrity / accessibility; data governance, security, performance; microservices / distributed / cloud-native exposure; responsible generative AI. Partner: analytics engineering on Platform.
- **What wins:** Resume is the binding intern gate (`companies.md`; `recruiting.md` Greenhouse/human-read). SWE: shipped full-stack or backend with tests. DE: ingest → pipeline/ETL → quality/governance → serve with Python and SQL through use. Finance-adjacent or high-reliability production delivery is memorable; crypto-native claims that are not on the page are fabrication. Do not burn the 3-applications-in-6-months cap on off-axis Coinbase reqs.
