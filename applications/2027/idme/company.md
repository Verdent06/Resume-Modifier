# ID.me

ID.me is a digital identity wallet: verify once, reuse login and identity proofing across partner sites. The JD cites 152 million users across 20 federal agencies, 45 state agencies, 70+ healthcare organizations, and 600+ consumer brands. The credential is approved as a NIST 800-63-3 IAL2 / AAL2 CSP (Kantara). Engineering is a platform company with an API-first development strategy — user-facing features, partner APIs, and the underlying identity/wallet stack. Intern example projects on this req are applied LLM (shop.id.me concierge chat widget; Slack agent for Shop Ops), not ML research. Founded 2010; HQ McLean, VA; Series E; ~$727M raised; 1,001–5,000 employees. Full-time in-office five days per week unless a posting says otherwise.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD names **Java, JavaScript, Go, Python, or Ruby** (demonstrable proficiency in *at least one*). Screen for proof in languages the candidate actually has — **Python / TypeScript through use; REST/APIs, Git, AWS**. Do **not** invent Java, Go, Ruby, Azure, GCP, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Copilot, Fusion, Sentry, NIST product ownership, or ID.me wallet internals
- **Domains:** API-first platform services for identity verification at government/healthcare/consumer scale; intern work may be product features, partner APIs, or applied LLM widgets/agents on shop.id.me / Shop Ops
- **What wins:** Because the intern bottleneck is the resume (`companies.md`; `recruiting.md` Greenhouse is resume-first), a one-page PDF that shows shipped REST/backend APIs, production debug, Git/CI, and AWS, with applied-LLM *pipeline* evidence as a preferred plus — without claiming Java/Go/Ruby or identity-platform experience. Honest “at least one language” polyglot (same pattern as Java at Mastercard / BNY). Applied AI stays pipelines/agents, not LoRA/research (`resume.md` §12; user GenAI rail)
