# Software Engineering Intern (Modeling & Simulation) at Hermeus

## Role Summary

A paid, on-site Los Angeles Spring (~16 weeks, Jan–Apr) or Summer (~12 weeks, May–Aug) 2027 internship writing performant modeling and simulation code for Hermeus's 6DOF Software-in-the-Loop (SITL) stack in Julia. The intern interfaces with Flight Software, HMI, and Flight Sciences; builds physics-informed models of hardware and vehicle dynamics; scales Monte Carlo; validates outputs against real flight data; and optimizes for real-time execution. The JD surface is scientific-computing SWE for aerospace simulation — not a generic product-SWE intern and not the Atlanta Hardware-in-the-Loop bench role. Hermeus's identity is hardware-iterated high-Mach unmanned aircraft for the Department of War; the memorable company-fit signal is flight-sciences-coupled 6DOF SITL, not consumer web and not HIL test-rack language.

- Anti-patterns: claiming Julia (or MATLAB, Snowflake, Fusion, etc.) without use; HIL-bench / rack / DAQ / lab-instrument framing that belongs to the Atlanta sibling; consumer-SaaS or agentic-LLM lead with no real-time/numerical signal; Skills-only scientific-computing stuffing; notebook ML without delivery; club membership without engineering.
