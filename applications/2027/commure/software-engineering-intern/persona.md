# Software Engineering Intern, Summer 2027 at Commure

## Role Summary

A paid Summer 2027 software engineering internship, on-site five days a week in Mountain View, CA ($40–$60/hr). Interns design, build, test, and ship features across frontend, backend, mobile, or infrastructure on Commure's AI operating system for healthcare — ambient documentation, agentic patient/revenue workflows, and autonomous RCM on a platform integrated with 60+ EHRs. Project placement can land in AI & Agents, Clinical, RCM, Platform & Infrastructure, or Product Engineering (React, APIs, backend services, databases used by providers daily). The JD surface is generic SWE intern: CS fundamentals, DS&A, a language/stack list, PostgreSQL/Redis/containers exposure, debugging, and shipping to real users. Company identity is a $7B healthcare-AI scale-up (General Catalyst / Sequoia) that deploys daily into clinical and billing production — not an ML-research seat and not a consumer CRUD shop.

## Screen Criteria

- JD languages the candidate actually has — Python and TypeScript — demonstrated in bullets, not Skills alone. React through use for the product-engineering example teams. Do not treat Dart/Swift/Kotlin/Flutter as a bullet requirement.
- PostgreSQL and Redis (JD "exposure") visible through real use when present in inventory — databases and queues tied to a delivery story, not a glossary.
- Shipped full-stack product: frontend framework + backend/API + datastore + deploy/CI, with trade-offs the candidate can narrate (`resume.md` §12).
- Production AI/agent/workflow signal in the top half without burying the SWE spine: eval/gates, pipelines, metrics — not prompt-demo or `model.fit()` research.
- Software used by others (customers, operators, paying clients, a real stakeholder) and a titled internship or equivalent ownership.
- Quantified reliability, latency, throughput, or cost so "ship to production" and "instrument your work" read as measured engineering.
- Class-year on page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies the 2028 graduation window.

## Anti-Patterns

- Inventing Dart, Swift, Kotlin, Flutter, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, or Tableau.
- Skills-list Python/TypeScript/React with zero bullet evidence.
- Leading with off-axis systems or GTM/deal-flow work that has no API, product, or production-pipeline signal the role tests.
- Notebook ML / LoRA-paper framing as if this were an ML-research intern seat.
- Club-ops or thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a shipped-product + agentic spine.
- Healthcare-mission essays with no engineering the 30-second screen can size.
