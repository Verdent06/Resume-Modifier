# Commure

Commure is building an AI operating system for healthcare: ambient AI and dictation at the point of care, intelligent agents for patient and revenue workflows, and autonomous revenue-cycle management on one AI-native platform integrated with 60+ EHRs. The company was incubated at General Catalyst (2017), merged with Athelas (Oct 2023), acquired Augmedix (Oct 2024), and now sells clinical documentation, AI billing/RCM, patient-facing voice agents, and related ops software to 500+ healthcare organizations (500,000+ clinicians, $25B+ annual claims, 200M+ patient interactions). Engineering identity is product software that ships daily into regulated clinical and billing workflows — not a research lab and not a generic CRUD shop.
