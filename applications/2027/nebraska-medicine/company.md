# Nebraska Medicine

Nebraska Medicine is the Omaha academic health system behind Nebraska Medical Center and Bellevue Medical Center (~800 licensed beds, ~1,400 physicians, ~70 clinics, ~9,000 employees; roots in Omaha's first hospital, 1869). It partners with the University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC) on education, research, and care. This intern sits on the **Data, Analytics, and AI Engineering** team that builds Palantir **Foundry / AIP** applications with contracted Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineers — clinical, operational, and financial workflows (capacity, OR/PACU, revenue cycle, trial enrollment), not a research-paper ML seat and not bedside nursing IT. Palantir and Nebraska Medicine announced a multi-year AIP contract in September 2024 (partnership from January 2024; company described as a ~$2.5B academic health system). Do not invent Foundry, Dataiku, DataRobot, Tableau, Power BI, Epic, or a hospital internship.
