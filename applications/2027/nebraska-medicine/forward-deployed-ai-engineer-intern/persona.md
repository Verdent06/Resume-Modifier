## Role Summary

A Summer 2027 intern on Nebraska Medicine's Data, Analytics, and AI Engineering team in **Omaha, NE** (East Campus Corp Pavilion; Workday REQ-38924). The intern works alongside hospital AI engineers **and contracted Palantir Forward Deployed Software Engineers** in Palantir Foundry: translate clinician/staff/researcher workflows into data and AI applications, build/test/improve analytics, and talk to both executives and frontline users. Surface stack is Python, SQL, TypeScript, data workflows, and AI — not a research-paper ML seat, not Palantir-the-company, and not bedside nursing IT. Workday prints **Part time**; the JD is a hybrid intern who **must live in Nebraska or Iowa for summer months** and work on campus. Comp unlisted (C-tier intern band). Resume is required.

Preferred (not exclusive): Palantir / Dataiku / DataRobot; Tableau / Power BI; project-management in IT, industrial engineering, supply chain, or healthcare quality improvement. Do not treat those as knockouts if absent; do not invent them.

## Screen Criteria

Pass signals:

- Python, SQL, and TypeScript demonstrated in bullets, not Skills alone. TypeScript/React is honest TS; do not claim JavaScript-as-claimed.
- Data workflows: ingest → transform → validate → serve (ETL, APIs, freshness/quality checks) — analog to Foundry pipelines without inventing Foundry.
- Applied AI: agents, eval, embeddings/retrieval, or LLM-adjacent systems with a metric — not a thin chatbot wrapper and not notebook `model.fit()` as the lead.
- Stakeholder / forward-deployed analog: scoped delivery with a non-engineer user (researchers, operators, paying clients) — transferable to clinicians/staff without a hospital internship.
- Data modeling / analytical techniques: entity resolution, scoring, ranked insight, or structured aggregation — not a BI-tool name.
- Metrics that size impact (hours saved, faithfulness/accuracy, throughput, precision), landed as the closer (`resume.md` §4).
- Live GitHub on an intern engineering resume (`resume.md` §3). GPA visible if ≥ 3.5.

Anti-patterns:

- Inventing Palantir Foundry, Dataiku, DataRobot, Tableau, Power BI, Epic, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, or a healthcare/hospital internship.
- ML-research framing: publications, LoRA-as-identity, notebook demos with no pipeline or delivery.
- Off-axis lead: pure audio-DSP, PE-only GTM with no data/AI system, or club-ops.
- TypeScript / SQL / Python only in Skills.
- Metric-free architecture bullets the recruiter cannot size.
- Treating this as a Palantir (PLTR) intern or a UNMC research-lab seat.
