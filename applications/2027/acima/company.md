# Acima (Upbound Group)

Acima is Upbound Group, Inc.'s (Nasdaq: **UPBD**) virtual and staffed **lease-to-own** operating unit (Acima Holdings acquired February 2021; Acceptance Now completed the platform transition in 2024). Customers lease durable goods through retailer locations and digital channels without traditional credit. FY2025 Acima segment revenue **$2.512B** of Upbound's **$4.695B**; YE2025 **12,050** employees company-wide (10-K). Upbound HQ is Plano, TX; **Acima Digital** corporate is **Draper, UT**. Founded 2013 as Acima; Workday employer on this req is **Acima Digital**. Brands in the family: Acima, Brigit, Rent-A-Center. **Do not mix with Upbound (Crossplane / Kubernetes).** This folder is Workday **R-100759** **Software Engineer Intern** on the **Acima** career site — 2027 Summer Internship Proposal: Acima Software Engineering. Interns **build full-stack software used daily in production** with PMs and engineers (git, editors, AI agents as *tools*). **Not** Plano HQ **R-100761** / twin **R-100762**. Do not invent Ruby, Java, Copilot, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Fusion, or Acima underwriting/LTO product ownership.
