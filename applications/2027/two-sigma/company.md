# Two Sigma

Two Sigma is a quantitative investment management and trading firm that treats technology as the business, not a support function. Engineers, quantitative researchers, and data scientists apply a scientific approach — experimentation, AI, data science, and rigorous inquiry — to mine markets and deliver alpha. Software engineers build the scalable tools that explore data and act on findings: distributed storage and cloud environments, the mission-critical trading system, model test/deploy infrastructure, low-latency execution, and pipelines that ingest data from 10,000+ sources daily. The intern program is general Software Engineering (team match 2–3 months before start): one project, a mentor, and a final presentation at the Soho NYC office. The firm is known for an unusually strong engineering culture among quant shops (Unix Lifetime Achievement, Putnam, ACM ICPC, IMO alumni in the peer set) and a Python/C++ core.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** S-Tier (quant; `reference/companies.md` — strong eng culture; Python/C++)
- **HQ / offices:** New York, NY (Soho office; this internship). Hybrid Work Policy with flexible in-office days; internship takes place at the Soho NYC office.
- **Valuation / signal:** Privately held systematic/quant manager; elite resume brand in the Jane Street / Citadel / HRT peer band; engineering prestige markers (Unix Lifetime Achievement, Putnam, ACM ICPC, IMO) cited on the intern JD.
- **Product focus:** Proprietary investment/trading technology — distributed storage and cloud, mission-critical trading systems, model (quant strategy) test/deploy environments, low-latency execution, and multi-source data ingestion.
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineering Internship):** $3,800/week (Bachelor's), $3,900/week (Master's), $4,200/week (PhD) — Avature JD.
- **Work model:** Paid, 10-week Summer 2027 internship at Soho NYC; Hybrid Work Policy (flexible in-office days; intern work is office-based). Hired into a general SWE role; matched to a team 2–3 months before start. Single project + mentor + final presentation. Intern events, brown bags, guest speakers.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Coursework toward a Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD in a technical or quantitative field. No published class-year, citizenship, or clearance gate on the posting. Financial experience is not required.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD names Java, C, C++, Python, Ruby, Perl, or other JVM-targeting languages. Firm Notes and official interview page center C/C++/Java/Python. Honest C++ and Python in bullets beats inventing Java/C/Ruby/Perl.
- **Domains:** Large-scale / distributed systems; public and private cloud; mission-critical trading reliability; quant-model test/deploy; low-latency high-performance execution; ingesting 10,000+ data sources daily; internal productivity tools.
- **What wins:** Exceptional programming proven in real systems (not Skills-only); large-scale or high-performance evidence (concurrency, memory discipline, latency/throughput, ingestion/pipelines); C++ and Python on the page; curiosity + pragmatism without fake trading-desk experience. Per `reference/recruiting.md` Part I §1 the OA is a high-elimination gate after the resume; Part I §5 quant/HFT is earliest-cycle / highest-bar; Part III §11 this is general SWE at a quant firm, not QR. Resume is the published bottleneck (`reference/companies.md`).
