# CMM Technology Intern - Summer 2027 at McKesson (CoverMyMeds)

## Role Summary

A paid Summer 2027 hybrid technology internship at CoverMyMeds (McKesson) in Columbus, OH — 10 weeks, CMM Main Campus. The posting is a catch-all intern pipeline, not a single team: recruiting may place the intern on Software Development, Platform, Infrastructure/SRE, Data Engineering, Security, or Technology Business Analyst. Surface work is analysis, design, documentation, engineering, and automation of partner solutions (internal and external), plus mentorship and broad exposure to CMM technology and business areas. Company identity is healthcare prior-authorization / medication-access SaaS — a partner-facing platform — not a consumer web product and not an ML research or robotics intern seat. An immediate team match is not guaranteed; the application is a routing form into anticipated intern seats.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (general SWE)
- **differentiator:** healthcare prior-auth / partner-solutions platform (CoverMyMeds) — analysis, design, documentation, engineering, and automation of partner solutions; cloud / database / SRE as program exposure, not a DevOps-only screen
- **track_divergence:** true

The literal requirements test current-student CS/CE/EE status, graduation Spring 2028 or sooner, GPA ≥ 3.0, working knowledge of software engineering architecture / best practices / platforms / stacks, willingness to work across multiple stacks, and communication of technical concepts to non-technical audiences. The JD names **no** programming languages. That is a generic SWE/tech-intern screen, so the resume spine is full-stack: shipped applications, APIs, data systems, automation, Python/SQL/JavaScript-family delivery. The company-fit signal is partner-facing delivery — analysis/design/docs/engineering/automation of solutions for real stakeholders — plus cloud and database evidence, without inventing healthcare, Ruby, Elixir, or MSSQL internships. Security and Tech BA are placement options, not the screen bar. Lead with the full-stack spine and keep partner-facing / automation / cloud-database evidence prominent and deep.

## Team & Bar

The reviewer is a C-tier healthcare-SaaS university recruiter (Workday; 2–3 rounds; Easy; no standard OA; bottleneck: resume; ~15–25%). They route a generic intern resume onto a software, platform, data, or infra team at CoverMyMeds. What wins: architecture and stacks shown inside bullets, not Skills alone; a shipped API or data pipeline with ownership and impact metrics; partner/client-facing delivery and the ability to explain technical choices to non-engineers; enough cloud/database/automation to be believable next to a partner-solutions platform. Recruiter voice is practical — "can this person analyze, build, document, and ship with a team?" — not "did they intern on prior-auth." Prestige is a tiebreaker. Graduation by Spring 2028 and GPA ≥ 3.0 are knockout gates, not resume lines. No housing, relocation, or visa help.

## Screen Criteria

- Demonstrates software engineering architecture, platforms, and stacks through use in bullets (APIs, databases, cloud, automation, CI) — not Skills-list only. JD names no languages; Python/SQL/JS-family through use is enough; do not invent Ruby, Elixir, MSSQL, Splunk, or Sidekiq.
- Shows shipped application or data-system delivery (REST, ETL, PostgreSQL/Redis, Docker/AWS, test/debug, production ownership) so Software Development / Platform / Data Engineering routing is obvious.
- Keeps partner-facing analysis, design, documentation, engineering, and automation evidence visible in the top half as the CoverMyMeds differentiator — real stakeholders and shipped solutions, never a fabricated healthcare or prior-auth internship.
- Uses impact metrics (latency, cost, failure recovery, scale, hours saved) rather than vanity counts.
- Anti-patterns: claiming healthcare, Ruby/Elixir/MSSQL, or SRE-only internships the page cannot defend; a pure agentic/LLM product resume with no application delivery; notebook ML; club-ops filler; burying partner-facing and cloud/database work under off-axis hobby systems.
