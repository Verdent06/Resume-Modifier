# McKesson (CoverMyMeds)

CoverMyMeds is McKesson's prior-authorization and medication-access SaaS: partner-facing coverage-determination workflows for pharmacies, providers, and payers. Engineering identity is a healthcare partner-solutions platform — analysis, design, documentation, engineering, and automation of internal and external partner systems — plus platform-first modernization (cloud, reusable services, databases). The Technology Intern posting is a catch-all seat, not a single team: placement can land in Software Development, Platform, Infrastructure/SRE, Data Engineering, Security, or Technology Business Analyst.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-tier (`reference/companies.md` — Fortune 10 parent, legitimate healthcare SaaS intern work, limited national SWE brand vs B-tier medical-device names)
- **HQ / offices:** Columbus, OH — 910 John Street, CMM Main Campus (C317) for this req
- **Valuation / signal:** McKesson is a public Fortune 10 healthcare distributor/services company; CoverMyMeds is the medication-access / prior-auth software subsidiary
- **Product focus:** Prior-authorization and medication-access healthcare SaaS for internal and external partners
- **Intern comp (2027 Technology Intern):** $16.50–$27.50/hr + possible bonus/LTI; no company-sponsored housing, relocation, student visa, or Green Card assistance
- **Work model:** Paid full-time intern, 10 weeks, Summer 2027; hybrid at Columbus CMM campus
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently enrolled bachelor's in CE / CS / EE (or similar); graduate Spring 2028 or sooner; GPA ≥ 3.0. No student-visa or Green Card assistance. Apply by posted close (this req: 17 Aug 2026).
