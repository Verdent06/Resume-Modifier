# Booz Allen Hamilton

Booz Allen Hamilton (NYSE: **BAH**) is a McLean, Virginia–headquartered advanced technology and management-consulting firm. It builds commercial-grade products and solutions for U.S. defense, civil, and national security customers. The intern seat here is **not** a generic account intern on a client billable team: it is the **Summer Games** — a 10-week, full-time, startup-accelerator internship in which teams prototype a breakthrough for a customer problem, take mentorship from senior leaders, and pitch at a Challenge Cup. Past teams shipped accessibility navigation, military open-source situation-awareness apps, and similar mission software. `companies.md` caveat still applies: advertised intern SWE at Booz Allen is often consulting-shaped, not a product-SWE rotation at a software company.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Required is an **or-list**: Java, JavaScript, C, C++, Assembly, Python, Perl, R, **or** Bash. Honest intern floor is **Python and C++ in bullets**, plus TypeScript/JS-family through use. Do **not** invent Java, Assembly, Perl, R, or Bash
- **Domains:** Summer Games team prototype — research, write software, technical documentation / design artifacts, ML **or** data mining **or** statistics **or** graph algorithms (academic or internship), independent + team delivery, Secret-eligible mission software
- **What wins:** Because there is no named OA, the PDF is the gate (`recruiting.md` intern funnel; C-tier gov consulting). A one-pager that shows shipped general SWE (JD language in bullets, APIs/services, Git, test/CI) **and** research/data-mining or applied-ML through use **and** client/team delivery matches Summer Games. Java is on the or-list, not a fabrication target. Do not lead as a notebook-ML intern or a generic Reston IT intern. Citizenship and **ability to obtain Secret** are apply-form knockouts, not resume lines. McLean 10 weeks with **no housing stipend** is a logistics yes/no
