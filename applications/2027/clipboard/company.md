# Clipboard

Clipboard is a remote-first healthcare labor marketplace connecting healthcare professionals with facilities that need shifts filled. The intern role is product-engineering oriented: building real features that affect how professionals and facilities connect, embedded with engineers, PMs, and designers from day one. Current company materials emphasize remote-first execution, written decision-making, marketplace scale, and a TypeScript/React/Node-style product stack.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco; remote-first with offices in Los Angeles, New York, San Francisco, and Seattle
- **Valuation / signal:** Series C marketplace; over $90M raised and a reported $1.3B valuation in 2022, with Sequoia Capital and IVP as lead investors
- **Product focus:** App-based marketplace for healthcare, dental, childcare, and adjacent labor supply-demand matching
- **Intern comp (2027 engineering):** $6,000/mo - $9,000/mo from the Jobright listing
- **Work model:** Jobright lists hybrid in Los Angeles; Clipboard engineering materials describe remote-first work
- **Clearance / eligibility:** JD requires internship status, junior-and-above school year, and US citizen/visa eligibility

## Interview Process

| Stage | Format | Notes |
| ----- | ------ | ----- |
| Resume screen | Human + ATS likely | Resume must read as product engineering with shipped user-facing features, not a generic project list |
| OA | Unknown | Full-stack intern listings emphasize practical programming and web-development exposure; prepare for DS&A plus application-building depth |
| Technical screen | Likely coding / project deep-dive | Expect TypeScript/JavaScript, backend/API, database, and product trade-off questions if the process follows the role surface |
| Behavioral | Written, autonomous, remote-first culture screen | Clipboard emphasizes high-trust, written communication, autonomy, and moving fast |

**Estimated funnel:** Unknown round count · Easy-Med to Medium practical SWE · OA platform unknown · no intern system design assumed · Bottleneck: resume/project relevance + technical screen · acceptance estimate unavailable.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** TypeScript/JavaScript preferred in public internship listings; candidate-compatible languages include JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, Java, Go, SQL
- **Domains:** Full-stack product engineering, marketplace systems, healthcare workforce operations, web apps, databases, real-user feature delivery
- **What wins:** A resume that leads with shipped product systems, real users, backend/API and database decisions, and enough frontend/client evidence to satisfy a full-stack screen. For this company, marketplace/product impact is the differentiator; generic robotics or research depth should support the page, not lead it.
