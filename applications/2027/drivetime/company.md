# DriveTime (Family of Brands)

DriveTime Family of Brands is the largest privately owned used-car **sales, finance, and servicing** company in the U.S. (Ugly Duckling → DriveTime in 2002; Ernest Garcia II). HQ in Tempe, AZ and Dallas, TX. Operating brands: **DriveTime**, **Bridgecrest** (servicing), **SilverRock** (protection), and **GoFi**. Engineering builds proprietary tools for purchasing, financing, and protecting vehicles — an **Angular / Ionic / .NET/C# / Node / Azure** shop. The Summer 2027 SWE intern req (Workday **R16294**) is still generic full-stack exposure (HTML/CSS/JS/TS/Python/Node/Java/GraphQL/C#/.NET/Azure) on business-critical Agile projects — not a C#-only knockout.
