# Datadog

Datadog is a public (DDOG) observability and security platform for cloud-scale applications: metrics, logs, traces, and security signals unified so operators can detect and fix issues before customers feel them. Interns ship production work across backend, frontend, infrastructure, data engineering, and developer tooling — including Kubernetes, distributed systems, and cloud-native services that ingest and analyze telemetry in real time. The engineering identity is high-scale telemetry and cloud monitoring, not generic CRUD; the intern loop is short and the project deep-dive is the round that actually tests whether the resume's systems claims survive follow-up.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-tier (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** New York, NY (HQ); Boston, MA (this intern req is hybrid NYC or Boston office)
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: DDOG); Fortune 500 + high-growth AI customers; observability category leader
- **Product focus:** Unified observability and security platform (metrics, logs, APM/traces, security) plus AI products such as Bits AI
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern, Winter):** $100,000–$110,000 USD reasonably estimated yearly salary (JD); Levels.fyi-class intern signal ~$48–$57/hr plus NYC/Boston housing stipend in other cycles
- **Work model:** Paid full-time Winter intern, January 4 – April 23, 2027 (~16 weeks); hybrid, based in NYC or Boston offices
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Targeting a 2028 full-time start date; some departments may require US export-control authorization; English CV; personal email preferred over university email

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD names none. Shipping stack (engineering brand, not this posting): Go (agent/backend), Python, Java, JavaScript/TypeScript; C++ appears in systems-adjacent teams. Do not invent JD-required languages.
- **Domains:** Observability, high-scale telemetry (metrics/logs/traces), distributed systems, Kubernetes / cloud-native infra, real-time data pipelines, developer platforms, reliability/performance
- **What wins:** Production systems with latency/throughput/reliability proof; data pipelines or real-time processing the candidate can defend for 45 minutes; cloud-native/deploy evidence in bullets — not Skills-only Kubernetes. Club-ops and notebook ML fail the deep-dive. Resume screen is the bottleneck, so the page must look like someone who already ships systems Datadog would probe.
