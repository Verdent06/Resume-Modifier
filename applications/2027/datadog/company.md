# Datadog

Datadog is a public observability and security SaaS platform (NASDAQ: DDOG) that unifies metrics, traces, logs, security signals, and user sessions from a single agent and 1,000+ integrations. Founded in 2010 (Olivier Pomel, Alexis Lê-Quôc), it started as infrastructure monitoring and now spans APM, log management, cloud security, service management, and AI products such as Bits AI. The intern signal is production systems that ingest and analyze high-volume telemetry in real time — distributed backend, cloud-native infra, and developer platforms — not notebook ML because Bits AI is mentioned.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-tier (`reference/companies.md` — excellent brand, strong eng culture; primary target)
- **HQ / offices:** New York, NY (HQ, 620 8th Avenue). This req: Boston, MA and New York, NY. Other engineering hubs include Paris (major R&D), Dublin, Denver, Madrid.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public NASDAQ: DDOG; S&P 500 / Nasdaq-100; ~$88B market cap (Aug 2026). Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader for Observability Platforms, 2026 (sixth consecutive year).
- **Product focus:** Unified observability and security — metrics, logs, traces, APM, cloud security; intern work is production telemetry systems at customer scale
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE Winter):** $100,000–$110,000 USD yearly (posted range). Relocation and travel benefits where eligible.
- **Work model:** Paid intern, hybrid workplace culture, full-time in the New York or Boston office during program dates January 4 – April 23, 2027
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Targeting a 2028 full-time start date. Pursuing CS, SWE, or related (or equivalent practical experience). Some departments may require US export-control authorization. Apply with a personal email; resume in English.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD names none ("one or more programming languages"). Reported intern/product stack is Go and Python; polyglot is fine if demonstrated in bullets.
- **Domains:** Observability (metrics/logs/traces), distributed systems, Kubernetes / cloud-native infrastructure, backend services, developer platforms, reliability/performance at customer scale.
