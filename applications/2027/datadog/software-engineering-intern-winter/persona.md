# Software Engineering Intern (Winter) at Datadog

## Role Summary

Winter 2027 intern (Jan 4 – Apr 23) in Boston or New York, contributing to production systems that process large-scale observability and application data in real time. JD surface is generic SWE intern: backend, frontend, infrastructure, data engineering, and developer tooling; own a project from design through deployment with mentors. Company identity is the observability/security platform — metrics, logs, traces, distributed ingestion, reliability and scale for a global customer base — not an ML lab because Bits AI or AI coding tools are named.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (generic SWE requirements — CS fundamentals, one or more languages, ship production software; Kubernetes/cloud-native are “develop expertise,” not hard filters; Bits AI / AI-assisted tools are interest signals, not ML-framework requirements)
- **differentiator:** observability / large-scale metrics-logs-traces / distributed systems (A-tier Datadog identity + JD mission)
- **track_divergence:** true

The screen spine follows `resume.md` Part III §12 — shippable product, APIs, data stores, cloud deploy, end-to-end ownership in bullets. The differentiator follows systems and telemetry signals — real-time constraints, latency/throughput/reliability, production pipelines that move or analyze data at scale — kept prominent in the top half, not buried behind generic web-only framing. Do not pivot the spine to `ai-ml` or `dev-ops`.

## Team & Bar

Datadog is A-tier in `companies.md`; intern loop is short (2 rounds, CoderPad medium, no intern system design) with the bottleneck at pre-tech screening. Recruiter voice: a Greenhouse human+ATS pass looking for current students targeting 2028 FT, CS fundamentals on the page, and evidence of shipped systems plus curiosity about reliability/scale — not Kubernetes listed without use. Project deep-dive later means every lead bullet must survive “walk me through this.” Hybrid office culture; winter cohort is in-person NY or Boston.

## Screen Criteria

- Production shipping with measurable impact — APIs, pipelines, or client systems delivered to real users, not tutorial repos (`resume.md` intern + full-stack).
- Systems or real-time evidence in bullets — latency, throughput, reliability, concurrency, cost at scale — so the observability differentiator registers on a 7-second pass.
- CS fundamentals visible (DS&A coursework, software-design decisions in bullets); languages demonstrated through use, not Skills alone.
- Full-stack or backend depth (data stores, deploy paths, CI) alongside systems signal — JD spans backend through developer platforms.
- AI-assisted / agentic exposure as a plus when tied to shipped pipelines or eval — not a research-only or notebook-ML pivot.
- Class-year on page must show current enrollment targeting 2028 FT (`Expected May 2028` matches this winter intern window).

## Anti-Patterns

- Club-ops or community-growth framing with no engineering the role tests.
- Skills-list Kubernetes / cloud-native / “observability” with zero bullet evidence.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a production + systems spine.
- Notebook ML or Bits-AI buzzwords without pipeline architecture, metrics, or delivery.
- Leading with unrelated quant/GTM or pure frontend with no backend/systems depth.
- Treating this as an SRE/platform screen and dropping the full-stack shippable-product spine.
