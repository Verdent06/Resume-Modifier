# Mastercard

Mastercard is a publicly traded payments network that powers digital transactions in 200+ countries: authorization, clearing, and settlement at global scale, plus adjacent APIs, fraud/intelligence, identity, and developer platforms used by banks, merchants, and fintechs. Software Engineering teams design, build, and optimize high-performing, highly scalable products on cloud platforms, with AI frameworks and secure blockchain called out as current stack flavor — not a trading desk and not a consumer-app startup. The US campus SWE intern is a generic engineering seat that may land on any team; the company's identity is still payments-scale, high-reliability transaction systems (similar profile to Visa per `companies.md`).

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Purchase, NY (HQ). This req: **O'Fallon, Missouri** (primary; St. Louis-area tech hub); additional US sites Arlington, VA; Atlanta, GA (675 Ponce De Leon Ave NE); New York City, NY (150 5th Avenue)
- **Valuation / signal:** Public global payments network; large campus intern class; respected finance-tech brand, not S-tier eng prestige. Similar profile to Visa.
- **Product focus:** Digital payments network and adjacent software — scalable, high-performance, security-sensitive transaction systems and products
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer intern):** National salary range **$35–$46/hr** regardless of location (this JD). Extern (Aug 2026) third-party analog ~$29–$44/hr + reported $2k sign-on — treat JD range as authoritative
- **Work model:** Paid full-time intern (JD). Term length / hybrid vs onsite not stated on this posting; Extern directional: 10–12 weeks, typically late May–mid-August, hybrid 2–3 days onsite. Locations are named offices
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Bachelor's in CS, Engineering, or related; graduating **December 2027 and beyond**. **Not eligible for work-authorization sponsorship**; must work in the US now and in the future without employer sponsorship. **F-1 (including CPT/OPT) not eligible** — stricter than some Mastercard intern postings. Unofficial transcript required at submit. End date on req: 2027-08-21. Posted 2026-08-21 (Workday CXS, CampusApplyOnly, R-287618)

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Intern JD names Java, Python, C++, JavaScript "or similar." Screen tests polyglot proof in whatever the candidate actually ships — **do not invent Java**. TypeScript is the honest JavaScript-family analog when present
- **Domains:** Enterprise SWE on globally used payment and product systems; design/dev/test; automated tests; secure coding; code reviews; Agile; collaboration with PMs/designers/engineers. Cloud / AI frameworks / blockchain are team-paragraph flavor, not the requirements list
- **What wins:** Shipped full-stack or backend software with tests, Git through use, and CS fundamentals defensible in an Easy–Med HackerRank (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11). Payments-scale, high-reliability, or finance-adjacent delivery is the company differentiator, not a second track. Resume is the binding intern gate; the OA equalizes after the page clears
