## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**Python**, **C++**; TypeScript covering the JavaScript "or similar" area). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). **Do not invent Java.**
- Shipped full-stack or backend software: REST/APIs or services, persistence, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12). Automated tests, debugging, or code-review discipline through use matches the intern "what you'll do" list.
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy–Med HackerRank OA.
- Modern practices through use: Git/version control, tests or CI, Agile/stakeholder delivery.
- Payments / fintech-backend / high-scale secure flavor on that spine: production APIs, financial or regulated-data workflows, reliability/latency discipline — memorable at Mastercard without replacing the SWE spine.
- Cloud / AI / blockchain as plus (team-paragraph flavor), never as the lead story for a generic SWE intern req, and never invented.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies graduating December 2027 and beyond.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-line Java with zero bullet evidence (fabrication smell at a polyglot floor-signal payments shop).
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, or Sentry — banned; not in this JD and not in inventory.
- Invented blockchain, Java-only stack claims, or Copilot metrics because the team paragraph named AI/blockchain.
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML lead as if this were an ML-research intern seat.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web/API/cloud/enterprise SWE.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + payments-delivery spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
