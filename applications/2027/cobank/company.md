# CoBank

CoBank is a Farm Credit System cooperative bank (ACB; founded 1989; OTCMKTS: **CBKLP**) headquartered at 6340 S. Fiddlers Green Circle, Greenwood Village, CO, with U.S. regional banking centers and a Singapore representative office. YE2025 IR: assets **$223.5B**, loans **$166.9B**, net income **$1.667B**, patronage **$1.079B**.
