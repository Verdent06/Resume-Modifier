# Software Engineering Intern at CTGT

## Role Summary

Summer 2027 onsite software engineering internship in San Francisco (10–12 weeks, May/June–August/September; $8K–$12K/month; US visa sponsorship). CTGT is a ~10-person YC F24 company building a deterministic governance layer for AI workflows — a model-agnostic control plane (Policy Engine) that enforces policy, prevents drift, and produces auditable decisions under load. The JD surface is general software engineering: ship features from backend services to product surfaces, write code that holds up under real load with correctness and clarity, own a project from design through delivery, and make progress through ambiguity. Company identity is reliable AI infrastructure, not ML research hiring: intern work sits next to the Policy Engine and platform, in high-stakes environments where governance, auditability, and performance are non-negotiable.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack
- **differentiator:** AI workflow governance / reliable AI infrastructure (Policy Engine, auditability, correctness under load)
- **track_divergence:** true

The requirements literally test general SWE: pursuing CS or related, fluency in at least one general-purpose language (Python and JavaScript named as central to the stack), Git, basic AWS or GCP workflows, clear communication, and self-directed ownership. They do not test model training, papers, or robotics. The resume spine is therefore full-stack / product-backend. The company-fit signal is reliable-AI systems: deterministic gates, eval/audit boundaries, production AI pipelines, and measured correctness — visible in the top half without turning the page into an ML-research packet.

## Team & Bar

The reviewer is a founder/senior engineer at a 10-person venture-backed startup (Gradient Ventures, General Catalyst, Y Combinator) reading a resume-first funnel (`recruiting.md` intern + full-stack: startups weight shipped product over OA-gated volume). Interns own a real slice of the Policy Engine / platform, not a side project. The bar is evidence of clean, correct, maintainable software across the stack without constant scaffolding: Python (and JS-family language) shown in product/backend work, services that survive load, Git + cloud/deployment in delivery stories, and AI-adjacent reliability that can survive a technical deep dive. School year on the posting is Any; the degree gate is pursuing a Bachelor's in CS or related while enrolled.

## Screen Criteria

- JD languages demonstrated in bullets, not Skills alone: Python in shipped backend/product work; JavaScript-family language through actual product surfaces when present on the page.
- Shipped full-stack or backend systems: APIs, data stores, client surfaces, deployment, operational ownership — not tutorial CRUD or club-ops without engineering.
- Git and basic cloud (AWS or GCP) tied to real delivery (provisioning, Docker, CI, production deploy), not a tools list.
- Differentiator visible in the top half: reliable-AI / eval / deterministic gates / production AI systems — without burying the full-stack spine or converting the page to notebook ML.
- Metrics for latency, throughput, scale, reliability, or measured correctness so "real load" reads as engineering, not aspiration.
- Anti-patterns: vague AI claims, Skills-only Python/JS, frontend-only polish, DSP/C++ as the lead identity, club leadership without shipped systems, intern scope that reads invented.
