# CTGT

CTGT builds deterministic governance layers for AI workflows, focused on making model behavior more reliable, auditable, and performant in high-stakes enterprise settings. The company is early-stage and venture-backed, with investors including Google's Gradient Ventures, General Catalyst, and Y Combinator. For a software intern, the screen is broad product/backend engineering, but the technical identity is AI workflow governance: shipping systems where correctness, auditability, and performance matter.

- **Tier:** Unrated

- **Valuation / signal:** Early-stage AI infrastructure startup; Jobright lists $7.2M total funding and seed backing from Gradient Ventures, General Catalyst, and Y Combinator
