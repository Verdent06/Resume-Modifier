# Anduril Industries

Anduril is a defense technology company building autonomous systems for military and allied customers. Its product family runs on Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that fuses thousands of sensor streams into a real-time 3D command-and-control layer. The engineering bar skews toward C++ for embedded and autonomy stacks, practical systems work, and mission-critical delivery — not generic web CRUD.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A (defense tech; $14B+ val; CNBC #1 Disruptor)
- **HQ / offices:** Costa Mesa HQ; Seattle, Reston, Irvine, Costa Mesa, Boston, Atlanta for this intern cohort
- **Valuation / signal:** $14B+; defense-tech prestige; strong brand for autonomy/CV hiring
- **Product focus:** Lattice OS — autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, networking for military systems
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** $40–55/hr (JD)
- **Work model:** Paid, 12-week, in-person summer internship
