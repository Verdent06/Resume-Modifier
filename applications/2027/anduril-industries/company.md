# Anduril Industries

Anduril is a defense-technology company building autonomous systems for U.S. and allied militaries — drones, sensors, and command software sold on commercial timelines rather than traditional defense procurement cycles. Its flagship Lattice OS is an AI-powered operating system that fuses thousands of sensor streams into a real-time 3D command-and-control picture. Engineering culture skews toward shipped autonomy, computer vision, sensor fusion, and C++ on embedded and field systems, even when individual intern reqs read like generic software engineering.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Tier 2 (high-bar defense tech; CNBC #1 Disruptor 2025)
- **HQ / offices:** Costa Mesa, CA (HQ); intern locations include Seattle, Boston, Atlanta, Irvine, Reston
- **Valuation / signal:** $14B+ valuation; defense-tech prestige alongside Palantir; mission-driven hiring
- **Product focus:** Lattice OS + family of autonomous defense systems (drones, towers, maritime, space)
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** $40–55/hr (JD)
- **Work model:** Paid, in-person, 12-week summer internship
- **Clearance / eligibility:** U.S. Person required (export-controlled data); rising senior; review begins August 2026

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** C++ (embedded/autonomy), Go, Python, Rust, Java — breadth on the JD, depth in autonomy stack on the floor
- **Domains:** Autonomy, computer vision, sensor fusion, real-time systems, cloud infrastructure, metrics/monitoring
- **What wins:** A strong generalist SWE spine (algorithms, storage, cloud, shipping code) plus visible autonomy or perception signal — FRC, ROS2/Gazebo, CV projects, or defense-adjacent research. Generic full-stack with no robotics/autonomy thread reads as a pass at Anduril even when the JD looks generic.
