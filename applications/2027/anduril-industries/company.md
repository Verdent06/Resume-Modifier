# Anduril Industries

Anduril Industries is a defense technology company building autonomous systems and command-and-control software for U.S. and allied militaries. Its Lattice OS platform fuses sensor streams into a real-time 3D operating picture; product families span drones, counter-UAS, maritime systems, and soldier-worn tech. The engineering org ships C++-heavy autonomy, perception, and networking stacks on aggressive timelines — months, not years — with export-controlled work throughout.
