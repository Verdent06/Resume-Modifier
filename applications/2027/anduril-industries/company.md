# Anduril Industries

Anduril is a defense technology company building autonomous systems and command-and-control software for U.S. and allied militaries. Its product family runs on Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that fuses thousands of sensor streams into a real-time 3D C2 picture. The engineering org ships autonomy, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking in months—not years—and hires heavily for C++ on embedded and autonomy paths alongside production backend and infrastructure work.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Tier 1 (defense tech)
- **HQ / offices:** Costa Mesa, CA (HQ); Atlanta, Boston, Irvine, Reston, Seattle for this role
- **Valuation / signal:** $14B+ valuation; CNBC #1 Disruptor
- **Product focus:** Lattice OS — AI-powered defense C2; autonomous systems (drones, sensors, mesh networking)
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** $40–55/hr (paid, 12-week summer)
- **Work model:** Paid, in-person, 12 weeks; review begins August 2026 for Summer 2027 starts
- **Clearance / eligibility:** U.S. Person required (export-controlled data); rising senior; returning to school after internship
