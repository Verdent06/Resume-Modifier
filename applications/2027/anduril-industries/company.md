# Anduril Industries

Anduril builds autonomous systems for the U.S. and allied militaries — air, land, and maritime platforms — and ties them together with Lattice OS, an AI-powered command-and-control layer for sensor fusion, networking, and real-time decisioning. Software is how fielded systems get to more customers and stay debuggable under operational constraints, not a sidecar to hardware. `reference/companies.md` A-TIER Notes flag C++ for embedded/autonomy as the intern-relevant stack signal; the 2027 SWE intern posting still screens as generic polyglot SWE (algorithms, storage, cloud, frontend), not a ROS/perception specialist req.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** A-TIER — Excellent / Strong Signal (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Costa Mesa, CA (HQ); Seattle. This intern req also lists Atlanta, Boston, Irvine, and Reston.
- **Valuation / signal:** Doctrine row: $14B+ valuation; CNBC #1 Disruptor. Web (May 2026): $5B Series H at $61B post-money (TechCrunch); ~7,000 employees; multi-year U.S. Army enterprise contract reported up to $20B. Lead with the doctrine figure; treat Series H as a 2026 update.
- **Product focus:** Lattice OS (AI C2) plus fielded autonomy, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer Intern):** $40–$55/hr (JD estimate)
- **Work model:** Paid, in-person, 12-week Summer 2027 internship. Review begins August 2026 for 2027 starts; apply early — recruiter outreach when a matching team role opens.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** U.S. Person required (export-controlled data). Pursuing a bachelor's in CS, Computer/Software Engineering, Mathematics, Physics, or related. Must return to school after the internship for at least one quarter/semester.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** C++ for embedded/autonomy (`companies.md` Notes); JD names C++, Go, Rust, Java, and Python as the proficiency set, plus frontend frameworks and cloud/storage tools
- **Domains:** Deployed customer-facing software, autonomy, real-time / mission-critical systems, sensor fusion, networking, cloud infrastructure, metrics/debugging/monitoring
- **What wins:** A full-stack intern spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) with JD languages shown in bullets, plus visible C++ / real-time / operational-debugging depth so the page is memorable at an autonomy company (`recruiting.md` §14: DS&A still gates; robotics/autonomy depth wins the loop). Greenhouse means the PDF is read; HackerRank then the 4-hr practical onsite are the binding filters (`companies.md`: bottleneck 4-hr onsite).
