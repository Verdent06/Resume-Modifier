# Western National Insurance

Western National Insurance Group (WNIG; Paylocity employer **Western National Group & Umialik Insurance**) is a private mutual property-and-casualty carrier headquartered at 4700 West 77th Street, Edina, Minnesota. The group writes personal and commercial premium through independent agents in 20 states across the Northern, Midwestern, and Western U.S. plus Alaska, and surety in 43 states, with affiliated companies including Umialik (Alaska) and Michigan Millers. This intern is **IT Data Engineering Intern** (Paylocity **4537849**, jobCode **I02227**) on the **Data & Integrations** team — enterprise data warehousing and ETL/ELT (SQL Server, SSIS, Snowflake, Python), with selected workloads moving from SSIS/SQL Server toward Snowflake — not the IT Application Administration intern sibling and not an underwriting intern.
