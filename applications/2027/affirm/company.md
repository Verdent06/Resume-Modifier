# Affirm

Affirm Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ: **AFRM**) is a San Francisco consumer-credit fintech that sells transparent installment loans at checkout — buy now, pay later with no hidden fees and no compounding interest. Founded **2012** by Max Levchin (PayPal co-founder) with Nathan Gettings, Jeffrey Kaditz, and Alex Rampell. Flagship surfaces: Adaptive Checkout, Affirm Card, Affirm Money™ (partner-bank deposits). FY2026 (ended Jun 30): revenue **$4.26B**, GMV **$50.2B**, **27.8M** customers, **571k** merchants, **2,358** employees **[Wikipedia/IR directional]**. HQ **650 California Street, San Francisco, CA 94108**. Remote-first; this intern posting is San Francisco.
