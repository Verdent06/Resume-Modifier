# Momentive Performance Materials

Momentive is a global advanced-materials company focused on high-performance silicones and specialty products for automotive, electronics, healthcare, construction, energy, and industrial markets. Headquarters is Niskayuna, NY; KCC Corporation took 100% ownership in 2024. About 40 sites across 20 countries. This intern sits on **Enterprise Reporting & Analytics (ER&A)** — applied data science for a manufacturing business (Python/SQL, EDA, predictive models, stakeholder requirements) — not an ML-research lab and not plant-floor process engineering.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-tier (`reference/companies.md` — specialty chemicals / advanced materials; legitimate applied-DS intern work; limited national SWE brand. Peer to Dow Chemical / Ecolab industrial intern signal.)
- **HQ / offices:** Niskayuna, NY (HQ). Waterford, NY and other US plants. This req: **US-NY-Niskayuna**, onsite office intern.
- **Valuation / signal:** Private (KCC Corporation 100%, 2024). ~$2–3B revenue class; 40+ locations / 20+ countries (`reference/companies.md` Notes).
- **Product focus:** Specialty silicones and advanced materials. This team owns enterprise reporting, analytics, and applied DS for the business — not R&D polymer chemistry.
- **Intern comp (2027 ER&A Data Science):** $22.00–$26.00/hr (Workday R9807).
- **Work model:** Paid full-time intern; 40 hrs/week; 10–12 weeks May–August; onsite Niskayuna office (sedentary office physicals per careers Hiring Process tab — not plant/lab).
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Actively enrolled in undergrad or grad program at apply **and** for at least one semester after the internship. Pursuing BS/MS in DS, DA, CS, Stats, Math, Engineering, or related. Legal working age; offer may be conditioned on pre-employment checks. **JD is silent on visa/sponsorship** — do not invent a sponsorship knockout. Posted 2026-09-03.
