# SoloPulse

SoloPulse Corp is an Atlanta dual-use radar company spun out of Georgia Tech. It builds RF sensing around novel wave-theory algorithms (the JD names special relativity as the underpinning) aimed at resolution and latency that existing radar stacks do not hit, then sells that sensing into civilian safety and military autonomy. Software is not a sidecar: intern SWE work spans algorithm development, frontend-backend integration, and low-latency compute on proprietary operations. The company is early, venture-backed, and hiring from Peachtree Corners — not a generic SaaS intern class.
