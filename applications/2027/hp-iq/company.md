# HP IQ

HP IQ is HP's AI innovation lab — a distinct entity from HP/HPE campus SWE programs — combining startup-sized teams with HP's device footprint. It builds the intelligence layer at the center of HP's data and devices: cloud services that extend hardware products (AI assistants, conference-room devices) rather than a general corporate SWE rotation. The Cloud Services intern team owns that cloud foundation and works with hardware, ML, and platform engineering; a sibling AML Platform intern req (Greenhouse 6114781004) is PyTorch/on-device inference and is a different screen.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B (`reference/companies.md` B-tier)
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco, CA (this intern req); HP IQ also lists Palo Alto in some listings
- **Valuation / signal:** HP Inc. AI innovation lab (not a standalone startup); brand is HP-adjacent with a smaller, lab-style intern cohort than HP/HPE campus SWE
- **Product focus:** Device-cloud backend — integrated cloud services that support and extend HP IQ hardware (AI assistants, conference-room devices)
- **Intern comp (2027 Cloud Services SWE):** $45.00–$51.00 per hour
- **Work model:** Paid internship; San Francisco, CA; Summer 2027 dates May/June–August/September (final dates by student schedule)
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Essential qualification is currently pursuing a Master's or PhD in CS or related. Form also asks whether the candidate graduates before September 2027. Undergrad B.S. is a listed knockout. Distinct from HP/HPE campus SWE class-year norms.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Unpublished on this Cloud Services JD. Preferred qualification names Spring Boot (Java implied, not required). Do not treat PyTorch/vLLM as this req — that is the AML Platform sibling.
- **Domains:** Cloud services that extend devices; hardware-software integration; automation and infrastructure; services at scale; AI assistants and conference-room device cloud.
- **What wins:** A full-stack intern spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) with shipped backend/cloud services, APIs, deploy/automation, and visible hardware-software or on-device adjacency so the page is memorable at a device-cloud lab. Spring Boot is preferred, not a floor. Greenhouse + no published OA means the resume carries front-end weight (`recruiting.md` §1). Master's/PhD on the JD is the knockout; a strong undergrad PDF does not clear it. Club-ops filler, notebook ML, and AML-platform (fine-tuning / vLLM) framing fail this screen.
