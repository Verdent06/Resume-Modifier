# Smith+Nephew

Smith+Nephew (LSE:SN, NYSE:SNN) is a global medical-technology company in orthopaedic reconstruction, sports medicine, trauma, and advanced wound management. Pittsburgh is the surgical-robotics R&D hub — Blue Belt Technologies (NAVIO; CMU Robotics Institute spin-out) was acquired in 2016 for $275M, then expanded to a ~65k sq ft center on Robotics Row (The Stacks / Strip District) for CORI handheld robotics and Real Intelligence software. Two Summer 2027 Pittsburgh intern reqs live under this folder — **do not mix them:**

- **Intern Robotics Software Engineering (Workday R92482)** — Robotics New Product Development: requirements → design → C++ implementation → unit/integration test, plus Robotics QMS documentation and a senior-leadership readout. Not a ROS/autonomy research seat.
- **Intern AI Center of Excellence Data Science (Workday R92480)** — AI CoE proofs of concept for Robotics and Enabling Technology: research models, clean training data, feature encodings, ML methods, statistical validation, rapid prototypes, AI interfaces. Not a robotics-software intern and not System Verification **R92481**.
