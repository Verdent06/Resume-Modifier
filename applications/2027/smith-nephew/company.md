# Smith+Nephew

Smith+Nephew (LSE:SN, NYSE:SNN) is a global medical-technology company in orthopaedic reconstruction, sports medicine, trauma, and advanced wound management. Pittsburgh is the surgical-robotics R&D hub — Blue Belt Technologies (NAVIO; CMU Robotics Institute spin-out) was acquired in 2016 for $275M, then expanded to a ~65k sq ft center on Robotics Row (The Stacks / Strip District) for CORI handheld robotics and Real Intelligence software. This intern sits on Robotics New Product Development: requirements → design → C++ implementation → unit/integration test, plus Robotics QMS documentation and a senior-leadership readout. Not a ROS/autonomy research seat and not a catch-all IT intern pipeline.
