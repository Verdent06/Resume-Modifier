# Entergy

Entergy Corporation (NYSE: ETR) is a New Orleans–based Fortune 500 regulated electric utility founded in 1913. It serves about 3 million customers across Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, and Texas, generated **$12.95B** in FY2025 revenue, employs ~12,200 people, and operates roughly 25–30 GW including a large nuclear fleet. Intern hiring runs through the **JumpStart** program (~150 interns/year; 550+ intern→full-time conversions in five years). This folder’s live req is SuccessFactors **124050** **Student Intern AI Summer 2027** — HR and Shared Services (ESS) **AI Agent Development Intern**: design, test, and document process-automation agents. **Not** ML research. **Not** sibling AI Governance intern **124681**. **Not** sibling AI Enablement intern **124218**.
