# Collins Aerospace (RTX)

Collins Aerospace is the avionics-and-aerostructures segment of RTX and a worldwide leader in cockpit avionics for commercial and military customers. This Cedar Rapids, IA team builds command-and-control software for the CDU 7000 Avionics Suite — the Central Display Unit (CDU) control pages that pilots use to drive complex mission sensors (Aircraft Survivability, Weather Radar, camera sensors) and weapons systems (Hellfire, cannons, rockets) embedded in military helicopter cockpits. It supports the US Army's 160th SOAR, US Marines, Coast Guard, and similar helicopter programs. Intern software work here is modeling-and-simulation engineering on a real avionics display product: building CDU control-page simulations under a mentor and Collins engineering processes, with advanced object-oriented programming and (preferred) threaded application development as the memorable technical bar — not greenfield product invention or ML research.
