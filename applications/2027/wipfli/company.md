# Wipfli

Wipfli is a national mid-market advisory and accounting firm (Accounting Today 2026 Top 100 Firms **#21**), founded 1930, ~2,900 associates, HQ Milwaukee. It sells assurance, tax, advisory, outsourcing, and technology services into ambitious middle-market clients — not a software-product company and not Big Four. This req sits in the **Consulting Practice**: client-facing Business Intelligence (analysis, design, implementation), dashboards, dataset organization, and data management, with industry software exposure in Insurance, Health Care, Construction, and Manufacturing. Work quality varies by engagement the same way it does at Accenture/Deloitte Tech (`reference/companies.md` C-tier consulting caveat). **Not** the CRM Tech Consulting intern (iCIMS 8291) and **not** the ERP Tech Consulting intern (iCIMS 8287).

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python, R, or SQL *preferred* (not exclusive). Screen on languages the candidate actually has — Python and SQL through use — not invented R.
- **Domains:** Business Intelligence systems (analysis, design, implementation); dashboards and data visualization; collecting/organizing datasets; client data management; business analysis / light PM on industry software (Insurance, Health Care, Construction, Manufacturing). Named viz tools (Tableau, Power BI, Qlik) are *e.g. preferred*, not a hard exclusive.
- **What wins:** Resume-first C-tier consulting screen (`recruiting.md` intern: eligibility knockouts, then the PDF). Evidence of ingest → transform → dashboard/KPI/report → measured outcome; Python/SQL in bullets not Skills-only; client- or stakeholder-facing delivery. Honest absence of Tableau / Power BI / Qlik / R / Snowflake / Databricks is correct — stuffing them is a fabrication smell (`resume.md` §8). GPA ≥ 3.5 is a real ai-ml coursework signal (`resume.md` §14).
