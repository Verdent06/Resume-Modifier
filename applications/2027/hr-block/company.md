# H&R Block

H&R Block, Inc. (NYSE: **HRB**) is a consumer tax-preparation and financial-products company founded in **1955** in Kansas City by brothers Henry and Richard Bloch. FY2025 (ended June 30, 2025) total revenue **$3.761B** (+4.2% YoY); ~**4,300** regular full-time associates and a seasonal peak of ~**70,100** (FY2025 10-K). HQ: **One H&R Block Way, Kansas City, MO 64105**. Flagship is assisted + DIY U.S. tax prep; **Block Next** blends human tax expertise with digital products. **Spruce** (`sprucemoney.com`) is the emerging financial-health / mobile-banking platform; FY2025 financial-services revenue (Emerald Card + Spruce, plus Emerald Advance interest/fees) was **$101.8M**. Small-business line: Block Advisors + Wave. This packet is the Embark **Financial Services Data Analytics Intern** on the Financial Services product team supporting Spruce — **not** the Embark Software Engineering Intern, **not** Machine Learning Intern, **not** Accounting Intern, **not** Product Management Intern, and **not** a seasonal tax-preparer job.
