# Wedge

Wedge is a Y Combinator Summer 2025 startup building the operating system for healthcare AI agents — deploying, governing, and scaling agentic AI across hospitals, health plans, and multi-physician organizations. The company forward-deploys engineers into health institutions to build custom agent solutions for back-office workflows (records retrieval, claims management, payment reconciliation) and maintains them as a long-lived trust and compliance layer. For a software engineering intern, the screen tests general backend/full-stack fundamentals while the company identity is agentic AI in regulated healthcare.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** Unrated (YC S25; not in reference/companies.md)
- **HQ / offices:** San Francisco, CA; on-site team with remote leeway per JD
- **Valuation / signal:** YC S25 batch; ~$500K pre-seed (Berkeley Frontier Fund, J20 Ventures, Pioneer); piloting at 60+ locations per YC launch post
- **Product focus:** Healthcare AI agent deployment, governance, and monitoring platform
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** Not listed in JD
- **Work model:** Paid internship assumed; SF-based with remote flexibility; collaborative on-site team environment
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Current enrollment or recent completion of CS/Engineering degree; no citizenship gate in JD

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Python primary (JD names Python, Java, Go, TypeScript); Git required; cloud/containerization beneficial
- **Domains:** Back-end services, APIs, databases, AI agent deployment, healthcare workflow automation, reliability, security
- **What wins:** Shipped backend/API work with database evidence, Python in bullets not Skills alone, credible agentic-AI or production pipeline depth, Git/cloud/Docker discipline, and metrics that prove performance or reliability — not notebook ML or pure frontend.
