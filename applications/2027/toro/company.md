# The Toro Company (TTC)

The Toro Company (NYSE: **TTC**) designs and manufactures outdoor-environment equipment: turf maintenance and irrigation, landscape and lighting, snow and ice management, agricultural irrigation, rental/specialty/underground construction, and residential turf and snow. Founded 1914; FY2025 net sales **$4.51B** (10-K YE 2025-10-31); ~9,227 employees at year-end 2025 (careers/JD copy says 11,000+). Headquartered at **8111 Lyndale Avenue South, Bloomington, MN**. Brands on this posting include TORO, Boss Snowplow, Exmark, Ventrac, Tornado, SPARTAN, Subsite, Radius, Hayter, Unique Lighting Systems, Irritrol, Lawn Boy, and American Augers (portfolio also includes Ditch Witch). Sells worldwide through distributors, dealers, mass/hardware retailers, rental centers, home centers, and online. This intern req is an **Enterprise Insights Analyst** seat — market, customer, and economic-trend analysis plus AI-enabled research and stakeholder dashboards for marketing, product management, finance, and strategy. **Not** Embedded Software & Telematics SWE intern, **not** IT intern, **not** Engineering/Robotics intern.
