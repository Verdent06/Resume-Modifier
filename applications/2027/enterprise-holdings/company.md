# Enterprise Holdings (Enterprise Fleet Management)

Enterprise Fleet Management (EFM) is a privately held, full-service fleet-management affiliate of **Enterprise Mobility** (the Taylor-family parent formerly branded Enterprise Holdings). EFM serves companies, government agencies, and organizations with medium-sized fleets — customized or full-service programs — from **50+ fully staffed offices** nationwide. This JD prints a North American EFM fleet of **730,000 vehicles**, growing at over **7 percent**. Parent-level FY24 press: Enterprise Mobility + EFM **~$38B** revenue and a combined **~2.4 million** vehicles across **9,500+** neighborhood and airport locations in **90+** countries **[directional, Enterprise Mobility intern JD / FY24 fleet press]**. Flagship identity for *this* intern is **EFM IT** — a smaller shop than Enterprise Mobility corporate IT, progressive architecture, many hats — not rental-branch operations, not a generic Mobility SWE intern, and not the EFM Support Analyst intern sibling.
