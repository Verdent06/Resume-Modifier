# American Century Investments

American Century Investments is a privately owned, independent asset manager founded in 1958. It manages global equities and fixed income, multi-asset strategies, ETFs, and private investments for advisors, institutions, and individuals. Newsroom figures: more than **$325B** AUM as of **02/27/2026** and about **1,400** employees across Kansas City, New York, Los Angeles, Santa Clara, Portland, London, Frankfurt, Hong Kong, and Sydney. Distinctive ownership: **40%** of dividends — over **$2B** since 2000 — fund the Stowers Institute for Medical Research. This intern is Workday **R0005751** **Enterprise Data Intern** on the **IT** data bench (Data Engineering, Data Analytics, and Data/AI paths) — not Quant Research, not Software Developer Intern, and not Investment Operations.
