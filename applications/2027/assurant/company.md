# Assurant

Assurant, Inc. (NYSE: **AIZ**) is a Fortune 500 specialty insurer and global business services company that protects connected devices, homes, automobiles, and commercial equipment **through partner brands** rather than a direct-to-consumer agency force. Two segments: **Global Lifestyle** (mobile device protection, extended service contracts, vehicle protection) and **Global Housing** (renters insurance, lender-placed products). Engineering for this intern is **enterprise Agile application development** inside Information Technology — plan/develop/support apps, integrations, code quality, and automation — not actuarial, not Global Finance intern, and not a trading desk.
