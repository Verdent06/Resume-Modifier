# Belvedere Trading

Belvedere Trading is a proprietary trading firm headquartered in downtown Chicago that provides liquidity to markets through market-making in commodities, interest rates, ETFs, and equity index options. From its founding it has built its trading models and software systems in-house from the ground up, continually re-engineering and optimizing them to stay competitive. Engineering is the source of the firm's edge: technology teams build and maintain the low-latency, high-performance proprietary trading systems traders rely on, using a team-based, holistic development approach across the full software development lifecycle.
