# Software Engineer Intern at Belvedere Trading

## Role Summary

SE Intern working directly with full-time Software Engineers and other interns to build Belvedere's low-latency, high-performance proprietary trading systems, participating across the full SDLC (design → test → deploy). JD surface is generalist trading-tech SWE: efficient, extensible programs in an object-oriented language (C++, C#, or Java preferred), collaboration to design/implement/debug complex trading applications, and communication with project managers and team leadership. Company identity is Chicago prop-trading / options market-making: in-house proprietary systems where performance and correctness matter at scale. "Don't know finance? No sweat" — Belvedere Trading University (BTU) teaches trading fundamentals on the job, so demonstrated interest in financial markets is a plus, not a knockout. The JD's "AI Fluency" line is developer-tooling fluency — use AI tools to ship efficiently while keeping independent judgement and critical thinking — not an ML-systems internship.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (general SWE)
- **differentiator:** trading-tech / low-latency high-performance systems (OOP/C++ systems depth) + demonstrated interest in financial markets; AI tools as a developer habit (NOT ai-ml track)
- **track_divergence:** false

The screen spine is general SWE per `recruiting.md` Part III §11 and `resume.md` Part III §12 — ship efficient, extensible software, DS&A/OOP foundation, an object-oriented language proven in bullets. The differentiator is flavor on that spine (high-performance/low-latency systems, real-time constraints, financial-markets interest), not a second track. Do not pivot the resume to ai-ml research, LoRA fine-tuning, or agentic-product lead framing because the JD mentions AI tools.

## Team & Bar

Belvedere is unrated in `companies.md` but sits in the Chicago quant/prop-trading peer set (funnel family near IMC Trading, CTC, DV Trading — OA-gated, C++/OOP heavy, low-single-digit acceptance, brutal-early cycle per `recruiting.md` Part I §1 and Part II §8). Recruiter voice: high-performing interns who already demonstrate CS fundamentals, build efficient/extensible programs in an OOP language, ship real systems, communicate with engineering and non-engineering stakeholders (PMs, team leadership), and show performance-sensitive engineering depth. Comp is a headline $130K/yr for the intern — selective; the bar expects OOP/systems depth on the page. Sponsorship is available (not a knockout). Class targeting is college juniors with graduation December 2027 / Spring 2028 — Expected May 2028 is in window.

## Screen Criteria

- An object-oriented language (C++ ideal; C#/Java also named) proven in bullets — not Skills-only. Deep, defensible OOP/systems work beats a padded language list; never claim a language absent from real work.
- Low-latency, real-time, high-performance, or performance-engineering depth that reads as trading-tech adjacent (concurrency, hot loops, zero-allocation constraints, lock-free data structures, sub-millisecond wins).
- Efficient, extensible, well-structured programs: architecture decisions, module boundaries, and design → implement → debug ownership across a real build.
- Shipped-software signal: deployed services, release-ready binaries, production APIs, measurable reliability/cost/performance outcomes.
- DS&A / OOP foundation visible via coursework and engineered projects defensible in a coding interview (the OA gates entry at Chicago prop peers).
- Communication / collaboration signal: cross-functional stakeholder work, team delivery, updates to non-engineering partners.
- Demonstrated interest in financial markets is a plus (BTU backfills fundamentals) — surface it where it is genuine, do not manufacture it.
- AI-assisted development as a habit with independent judgement is a plus layered on the SWE bar — absence is not automatic reject; leading with ML research pipelines as if this were an ai-ml internship is an anti-pattern.
- Class-year on page must fall in the December 2027 / Spring 2028 window (Expected May 2028 is in window).

## Anti-Patterns

- Screening as an AI/ML systems engineer because the JD mentions AI tools.
- An OOP language named in Skills with zero bullet evidence at a firm where the language and OOP fluency are the floor signal.
- Club-ops or community-growth framing with no engineering depth.
- Soft, metric-free shipping claims where high-performance production impact is the firm's selling point.
- Thin single-bullet filler diluting a strong systems + shipping spine.
- Finance-jargon padding without engineering substance (BTU teaches finance; the firm hires for engineering).
