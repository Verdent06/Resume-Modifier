## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**Python**, **TypeScript**; HTML/CSS/JS familiarity through use). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). **Do not invent Java, C#, or Go.**
- Shipped full-stack or backend software: REST/APIs or services, a real frontend or client surface (HTML/CSS/JS or TS), a persistence decision, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) or automated testing tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12).
- Integrations / stakeholder delivery: work that talks to other systems or to non-engineer partners (BAs, designers, clients) — maps to fintech-partner integrations and agile collaboration without inventing bank-core claims.
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy coding screen if invited.
- Modern practices through use: Git/version control, unit tests or CI, code-review or collaborative delivery.
- Fintech-backend / digital small-business-banking flavor on that spine: production APIs, financial or messy-regulated data workflows, reliability-minded delivery — memorable here without replacing the SWE spine.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies currently-pursuing; no JD class-year window to miss.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-line Java / C# / Go with zero bullet evidence (fabrication smell at an or-list language floor).
- Invented Copilot, Fusion, Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, or "AI-assisted development" as inventory because the JD says learn-and-use.
- Invented SBA 7(a), BSA/AML, core-banking, or Live Oak platform claims because the company is a digital bank.
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML lead as if this were the AI Enablement intern sibling.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web, APIs, AWS, and bank-tech SWE.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + fintech-delivery spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
