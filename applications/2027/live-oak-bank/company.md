Live Oak Bank is a digital small-business bank (Live Oak Banking Company founded **2008**; holding-company IPO **2015**, NYSE: **LOB**) headquartered at **1741 Tiburon Drive, Wilmington, NC 28403**. Nationwide SBA **7(a)** / USDA guaranteed lending plus deposits; self-described America's Small Business Bank. Q2 2026 IR: assets **$16.04B**, loans **$13.14B**, deposits **$14.55B**. YE2025: **1,027** FT / 13 PT / 37 contractors. Engineering work is bank technology — cloud apps, APIs, and integrations with internal systems and external fintech partners — in a BSA/AML/CIP/OFAC regulated SDLC, not a consumer-app startup and not an ML-research lab.
