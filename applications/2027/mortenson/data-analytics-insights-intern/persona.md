The intern is the junior seat on a real analytics team (full-time analog: Data Analyst – Finance uses Python/R, statistical analysis, Power BI), not help-desk IT and not a software-engineer intern.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract — the writer discovers which entries carry them):**
- Python and SQL demonstrated through use in bullets, not only the Skills line (`resume.md` keyword-through-use).
- End-to-end data or applied-ML workflow: messy or operational source data → transform/pipeline → KPI, dashboard, report, or predictive score → measured outcome. Preprocessing through evaluation beats `model.fit()` (`resume.md` §14; `recruiting.md` §13).
- Customized KPIs, scoring, or ranked metrics as a first-class artifact — analog to "visualize customized KPIs" and "dashboards, reports, and analysis for internal customers."
- Predictive or applied-ML evidence when it is a real system (held-out eval, regression/forecast, scoring model), not a buzzword.
- Stakeholder-facing operational-analytics / business-delivery signal kept visible in the lead window because of track divergence: shipped analysis for a business or internal customer, not hobby ML.
- Quantitative coursework (stats, calc, econ) visible in Education as math-foundation signal.
- Prior related internship / titled data-engineering delivery (preferred on the JD).

**Anti-patterns:**
- Generic SWE / full-stack product resume that never shows data pipelines, SQL, KPIs, or an insight/report analog — wrong role.
- Notebook ML or `model.fit()` with no delivery, no pipeline, no measured outcome.
- Skills-only Python/SQL/Excel/R with no bullet proof.
- Invented Power BI, Tableau, Snowflake, Databricks, R, or construction-domain claims (JD names several; absence in bullets is honest; stuffing them is a fabrication smell).
- Club-ops or community-growth filler occupying prime slots.
- Pure audio-DSP / embedded / voice-AI as the lead with no data/analytics analog.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact.
- Treating this as a software-engineer intern screen (OA-first, systems-design, generic SWE keywords).
