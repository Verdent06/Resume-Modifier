# Mortenson

Mortenson is a top-25 US builder, developer, and EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) — privately held, 10,000+ employees, 12 regional offices, HQ Minneapolis / Robbinsdale, MN. Markets include sports, renewable energy, data centers, healthcare, and commercial. This req is **Data Analytics & Insights intern**: administrative and analytical support to an internal BI team that builds dashboards, reports, customized KPIs, and predictive-analysis tools to maximize business and financial performance — not SWE, not help-desk IT. The intern is the junior seat on a real analytics team (full-time analog: Data Analyst – Finance uses Python/R, statistical analysis, Power BI). The college intern program is designed to convert to full-time.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** D-Tier in `reference/companies.md`
- **HQ / offices:** HQ Minneapolis / Robbinsdale, MN; 12 regional offices. This intern seat is **in person, Robbinsdale, Minnesota**.
- **Valuation / signal:** Privately held; top-25 US builder; 10,000+ employees; internships convert to full-time (`mortenson.com/careers/college`)
- **Product focus:** Construction/EPC operational analytics and business intelligence for internal customers (business + financial performance)
- **Intern comp (2027 Data Analytics & Insights):** $18/hr starting; higher by year in school
- **Work model:** Paid summer intern program, May–August 2027, onsite Robbinsdale MN
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Must be legally authorized to work in the U.S. without sponsorship (no H-1B, O-1, TN, CPT, OPT). Undergraduate four-year degree in Data Science, Statistics, Economics, Math, or related. Preferred: prior related internship; currently enrolled junior.
