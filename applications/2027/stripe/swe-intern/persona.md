TypeScript/JS-family (Angular/RxJS/React) is the honest Javascript-adjacent signal. **Do not invent Java, Ruby, Go, Scala, or Javascript-as-a-listed-skill.** JD says new languages can be learned.

- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in a Med–Hard tech loop (and, if the intern-cycle HackerRank report materializes, implementation-heavy data processing — not DP contest problems).
- Payments / fintech-backend / high-reliability flavor on that spine: production APIs, financial or regulated-data workflows, reliability/latency discipline — memorable at Stripe without replacing the SWE spine.
- AI tooling as critical review of outputs (plus), never as the lead story for a generic SWE intern req, and never invented Copilot metrics.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` (junior; never sophomore; never 2029).

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-line Java, Ruby, Go, Scala, or Javascript with zero inventory/bullet evidence (fabrication smell at a polyglot floor-signal payments shop).
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau — banned.

- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web/API/cloud/payments SWE.

- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + payments-infra spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
- Graduation 2029 or sophomore standing on the page.
