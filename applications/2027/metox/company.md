# MetOx International, Inc.

MetOx International, Inc. (MetOx) is a Houston-based manufacturer of high-temperature superconducting (HTS) wire. Flagship product is **Xeus™** HTS tape (REBCO via MOCVD), aimed at fusion magnets, high-field magnets, next-gen grids, and hyperscale / AI data-center load. Technology originated at the University of Houston (founder Alex Ignatiev). Global HQ is **11331 Tanner Road, Houston, TX 77041** (`metoxtech.com`). This folder is the **Data Science Intern (Spring 2027)** packet (Greenhouse **5427064008** / internal **4549708008** / req **175**): applied operations analytics on process and production data, reporting to the **Chief Information Officer** — **not** a generic SWE intern, **not** a research-scientist seat, **not** a remote DS internship.

- **Languages / tools (this JD):** **Python or R** and **SQL** (PostgreSQL or MySQL; SELECT / JOIN / WHERE) are the floor to prove through use. Also named: Pandas, NumPy, EDA, statistical/ML models (predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, process optimization), gen-AI / LLMs, dashboards/visualizations, TCP/IP / DNS / HTTP(S), MQTT, OPC-UA, process historians (OSIsoft PI, InfluxDB), SCADA / industrial IoT. **Do not invent** MQTT, OPC-UA, OSIsoft PI, InfluxDB, SCADA, MySQL, R, NumPy, Tableau, Power BI, Snowflake, or Databricks. - **Domains:** Applied data science on HTS manufacturing operations: collect/clean process and sensor data; EDA; models that improve production, resource allocation, and product performance; reports and dashboards for non-technical stakeholders; CIO-org data access troubleshooting.
