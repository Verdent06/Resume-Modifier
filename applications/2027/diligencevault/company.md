# DiligenceVault

DiligenceVault is a privately held, cloud-based digital diligence ecosystem for institutional investors and asset managers. The two-sided DV platform turns messy diligence documents, fragmented data, market intelligence, and manual workflows into automations, faster analysis, and better decisions. Flagship surfaces include DDQs/RFPs, document intelligence, DV Assist (governed AI with an audit trail), and Blaze source-direct manager profiles. Founded in 2014 by Monel Amin; career-page mission is 21,000 → 100,000 global users.
