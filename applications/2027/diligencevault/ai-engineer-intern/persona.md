# Persona — AI Engineer Intern at DiligenceVault

Recruiter lens shared by the writer and grader. Abstract bar signals only — no candidate entry names, no include/omit table, no depth quotas.

## Role Summary

A 3-month, paid-unlisted, NYC hybrid (8 hours/week in office) AI Engineer Intern seat (JazzHR **Yo3RhxiDyM**) on DiligenceVault's diligence / decision-intelligence SaaS. The intern is a curious, hands-on builder who learns how fund diligence and investment research actually work, then ships practical AI inside a scaled enterprise product: unstructured documents, semantic search, RAG, workflow automation, market intelligence, and AI evaluation. Cadence is idea → PoC → test against real workflows → improve — not a research rotation and not a generic full-stack internship.

JD surface: Python + software-engineering fundamentals; familiarity with LLMs, embeddings, RAG, NLP, or modern AI frameworks; messy real-world data; user empathy and a bias toward experimentation. Curious about financial markets and investment research is a plus; prior investment-industry experience is **not** required.

Company identity (`company.md` / `companies.md` C-tier): two-sided DV platform for allocators, investors, and asset managers (DDQs/RFPs, document intelligence, DV Assist, Blaze). Goldman-backed Series A. Applied AI for investment diligence / document intelligence is **within-track domain flavor**, not a second engineering track.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** `ai-ml` (applied AI / product / agent engineering). The JD's requirements literally test Python, LLMs/embeddings/RAG/NLP/modern AI frameworks, semantic search, AI agents, document intelligence, messy data, and workflow automation. That routes to `ai-ml` per `resume.md` §14 and `recruiting.md` §13 applied ML / GenAI layer (RAG, agents, eval) — **not** ML research, **not** notebook `model.fit()`, **not** generic full-stack as the spine.
- **differentiator:** applied AI for investment diligence / document intelligence (enterprise B2B SaaS; messy unstructured docs → RAG/agents/automations).
- **track_divergence:** false. Company identity agrees with `screen_track`. Domain (diligence docs, market/regulatory intelligence, allocator/manager workflows) is within-track emphasis. Spine stays `ai-ml`.

## Team & Bar

Screener is a JazzHR human + ATS read at a C-tier SaaS — resume-first intern funnel, not a high-volume OA gauntlet (`recruiting.md` Part I §5: mid-size / non-tech-tech, a human reads the PDF early; Part II §8: intern eligibility knockouts first, prestige is a tiebreaker). Funnel: resume + application essays (something built with AI; GitHub; three why-intern bullets; availability; stipend/hr) → unpublished intern loop. No published OA or intern sys design. Bottleneck is the resume (~15–25%, JazzHR peer of Innovative Systems).

Evidence that wins: a shipped LLM/agent/RAG or document-intelligence system with an eval or iterate-to-production loop; Python through use, not Skills alone; messy unstructured-data plumbing; metrics that size impact. Recruiter voice is practical — "can this person prototype AI on real diligence workflows and move a PoC toward production?" — not "did they publish an NLP paper" and not "did they intern on a buy-side desk."

GPA is unstated. Class-year is unstated (pursuing BS/MS in a related field is the only academic gate). Intern-stage weighting still favors engineered projects + a live GitHub (`resume.md` §9). Stats coursework is a genuine ai-ml signal (`resume.md` §14) but not a substitute for agent/RAG/document-intelligence evidence.

## Screen Criteria (abstract)

Pass signals:
- Hands-on LLMs, embeddings, RAG, NLP, semantic search, or AI agents — a system with orchestration, retrieval, or document-intelligence substance, not a thin API wrapper or a notebook demo.
- Messy / unstructured data work: irregular documents, extraction, freshness, or retrieval that feeds a tool — analog to diligence docs and fragmented data.
- Workflow automation or PoC→production discipline: eval harnesses, held-out tests, faithfulness/accuracy lifts, or an iterate-against-real-workflows loop.
- Python demonstrated through use in bullets, not Skills alone. JD names Python as a floor; orphaned AI buzzwords still fail.
- Container + cloud evidence as delivery proof for applied AI-agent/product work (JD-aligned abstract signal). Do not invent Kubernetes, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, or Tableau.
- Large messy datasets handled with engineering (ETL, serving APIs, stores that feed a model/tool).
- Metrics that size impact (accuracy/faithfulness, latency, hours saved, throughput), landed as the closer (`resume.md` §4).
- Live GitHub on an intern engineering resume (`resume.md` §3).

Anti-patterns:
- ML-research framing: publications, novel methods, notebook `model.fit()` with no agent, no pipeline, no delivery (`recruiting.md` §13 research vs applied split). This req is applied AI product/agent engineering.
- Invented research depth or invented stacks: Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, TensorFlow-as-used, Kubernetes.
- Off-axis lead: pure frontend, pure audio-DSP, or club-ops fronting an applied-AI intern req.
- LLM/RAG/agent/embedding claims only in Skills with no supporting system.
- Claiming buy-side, allocator, or DiligenceVault-platform experience the page cannot defend.
- Metric-free architecture bullets the recruiter cannot size.
