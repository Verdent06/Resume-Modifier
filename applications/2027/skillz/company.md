# Skillz Inc

Skillz is a real-money, skill-based mobile tournament platform: patented matchmaking, identity, payments, fraud protection, and developer tools that let game studios run fair, real-stakes competition at scale. Founded in 2012 by Andrew Paradise; the JD cites 800,000+ daily tournaments, $7.5 billion in lifetime prizes paid, and 90 million registered users. Parent holding company is **FIRY** (RZR ads + Beamable LiveOps). `reference/companies.md` still lists **NYSE: SKLZ**; the public ticker completed a rebrand to **FIRY** in June 2026 (web update — do not treat SKLZ as current). This intern req (Greenhouse **8168006** / **TAH-296**) is **Co-op, Software Engineer** on backend services and APIs for payment infrastructure and deposit/withdrawal/payout player flows — not a game-client seat, not RZR ads, and not Beamable LiveOps.
