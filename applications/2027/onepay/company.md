# OnePay

OnePay is a Walmart-majority consumer fintech (formerly ONE / One Finance; rebranded 2024) that ships an all-in-one super app: FDIC-insured banking via partner banks, high-yield savings, debit/credit cards, point-of-sale lending, investing, and crypto, plus embedded finance for employers, HCM providers, and gig platforms. Distribution rides Walmart's shopper and associate base; Semafor (2026-07) cites ~7 million customers and CEO Omer Ismail (ex-Goldman Marcus). Engineering identity is money-movement and sensitive financial data at consumer scale — not a trading desk and not an ML-research lab. No `reference/companies.md` row; treat as Unrated.
