# Regeneron Pharmaceuticals

Regeneron (Nasdaq: **REGN**) is a fully integrated biotechnology company founded **1988** and headquartered in Tarrytown, NY. Physician-scientist-led; it invents, develops, manufactures, and commercializes medicines for serious disease — eye, allergic/inflammatory, oncology, cardiovascular/metabolic, hematologic, infectious, rare. FY2025 total revenues **$14.343B**; **15,410** colleagues YE2025. Flagship products: Dupixent® (dupilumab; **$17.807B** 2025 global net sales, recorded by collaborator Sanofi), EYLEA HD® / EYLEA® (aflibercept; **$7.9B** 2025 global retinal franchise), Libtayo® (cemiplimab). This req is a **~30-week Data Science & Digital Innovation co-op** on **Preclinical Manufacturing & Research Information Technology (IT)** — tools over manufacturing and research data (custom apps/dashboards/automation, AI/ML bioreactor-performance models, CFD as a named workstream, data-platform migrations, scientific data solutions) — **not** a 10-week summer intern, **not** an R&D bench/biology co-op, **not** MBA BuiLD, **not** the PharmD rotational program.
