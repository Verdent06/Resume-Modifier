# Garda Capital Partners

Garda Capital Partners is a privately held alternative investment firm that deploys one strategy: fixed-income relative value for institutional investors. The firm site lists $12B+ AUM, 220+ professionals, and 22 years of track record through rate-regime shifts; primary offices include Wayzata (HQ), New York, Palm Beach Gardens, Scottsdale, Geneva, Zug, Copenhagen, and Singapore. Engineering sits in Research and Technology (R&T): enterprise Python/SQL software for new lines of business, front-office and risk systems, and support of in-house plus third-party applications — not a customer-facing product org and not an HFT matching-engine shop. Interns join the NYC R&T team for Summer 2027 and work next to senior staff on complete solutions, not isolated tutorial tickets.
