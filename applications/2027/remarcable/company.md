# Remarcable

Remarcable is a Series A construction-tech SaaS / B2B marketplace that unifies electrical-contractor procurement, tool management, inventory, and prefab workflows into a single source of truth between field teams, office staff, and distributors. Founded by people who worked the jobsite (foremen, purchasers, PMs, and engineers), the platform is an award-winning SOC 2 Type II marketplace moving billions in material purchases — contractors buy ~$900M gross material value per month through a catalog of 600k+ products and 1,300+ distributors. The intern screen is general full-stack product engineering (Python/Django, AngularJS, REST, PostgreSQL, AWS); the company identity is vertical ERP-style B2B for construction procurement, not generic CRUD.
