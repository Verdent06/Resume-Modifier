# Software Engineering Intern (Summer 2027) at Dropbox

Recruiter lens shared by the writer and grader. Abstract bar signals only — no candidate entry names, no include/omit table.

## Role Summary

Campus SWE intern on a Dropbox Emerging Talent host team (Greenhouse 8106224 / req 609). Interns ship a high-priority project end-to-end on mission-critical software, then team-match into Product Engineering, Infrastructure (Magic Pocket / storage at scale), CX, Mobile, or Security. JD surface is generic intern SWE: Python / Go / JavaScript or similar, shipped software from coursework/projects/internships, CS or related degree, graduating Fall/Winter 2027 or Spring 2028 as the **final internship before graduating**, full-time 12-week Summer 2027, remote US. Responsibilities name user-centric design, agile iteration, multidisciplinary collaboration, and AI-tool fluency (Copilot is an *example*, not a skill to invent). Company identity (`company.md` / `companies.md`) is **collaboration / file-sync product engineering and distributed storage at scale** — Virtual First, Dash + Magic Pocket, not a trading desk and not an ML-research seat.

- **track_divergence:** true

That identity is distinct from generic product-SWE, so divergence is true: the resume leads with the `full-stack` spine **and** keeps user-centric shipping, data integrity, and production APIs prominent in the lead window. Do not lead as notebook ML, LoRA-research, or hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**Python**; TypeScript covering the JavaScript "or similar" area). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). **Do not invent Go or Copilot.**
- Shipped full-stack or backend software: REST/APIs, a real frontend or client surface, a persistence decision, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12). Debug, tests, or code-review discipline through use matches "ship a high-priority project" and OA-style refactor/maintain.
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in a progressive CodeSignal OA (the intern bottleneck).
- Modern practices through use: Git/version control, tests or CI, stakeholder or user-facing delivery.
- Collaboration-product / data-integrity flavor on that spine: production APIs, reliability/latency or named production defects, systems that other people used — memorable at Dropbox without replacing the SWE spine.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies Spring 2028 / final-internship window.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-line Go or Copilot with zero bullet evidence (fabrication smell).
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, or Sentry — banned; Copilot is a JD *example tool*, not inventory.
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML lead as if this were an ML-research intern seat.
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests web/API/user-centric SWE (infra host-match is flavor, not the screen).
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + shipped-product spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
