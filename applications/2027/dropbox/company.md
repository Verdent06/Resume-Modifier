# Dropbox

Dropbox is a publicly traded file-sync and collaboration company (NASDAQ: DBX) whose product is the client, the sync protocol, and the storage plane behind hundreds of millions of users — not a thin wrapper on object storage. Flagship surfaces include Dropbox files/sharing, Dash (universal search across files and apps), Paper, Transfer, DocSend, and HelloSign. Infrastructure owns Magic Pocket (exabyte-scale storage, billions of files, hundreds of petabytes). The US campus SWE intern is a generic Emerging Talent seat that team-matches into Product, Infrastructure, CX, Mobile, or Security; the company's identity is still high-scale collaboration and storage with a Virtual First work model.

## Stack & Hiring Signal
