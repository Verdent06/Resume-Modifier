# Engines Engineering Intern – Computer or Software Engineering – US – Summer 2027 at GE Aerospace

## Role Summary

A Summer 2027 onsite internship in Evendale/Cincinnati, OH or Lynn, MA on a GE Aerospace Aviation T&O Product Safety and Quality team. Placement is into Engines Computer/Software Engineering: engine controls, gas-turbine sensors, electrical power, vehicle health and data management, data collection and computing systems, real-time data analytics, embedded controls, and engine test systems and integration. Day-to-day may include design, analysis, testing, software requirements, software testing, simulation, tool development, and product/program support. The JD surface is a CS/CE/SE intern req — 3.0 GPA, accredited CS/CE/SE (or related) program, one programming language, willingness to work onsite in the U.S. The company's memorable identity is mission-critical jet-engine software: safety and quality on commercial and military engines, not mechanical design and not an ML-research internship.

Strong signals: a programming language demonstrated inside bullets (not Skills alone); shipped software with real users or a release artifact; fault-tolerant or real-time work with metrics (latency, failure rate, throughput, scale); data collection / computing / health-style pipelines; clean one-page parseable layout. Prestige is a tiebreaker. U.S. Person status and no-sponsorship are binary apply-form knockouts, not resume lines — do not claim citizenship or clearance.

## Screen Criteria

- Demonstrates at least one programming language **through use in bullets**, not only in the Skills line.
- Shows shipped software with real users, a stakeholder, or a release artifact — not tutorial-grade coursework.
- Makes mission-critical / real-time / embedded-adjacent discipline visible in the top half: deterministic constraints, lock-free or concurrent design, fault tolerance, test/integration, or hard latency bounds — without turning the page into a hobby DSP resume.
- Backs the full-stack spine with end-to-end delivery (APIs/services, data pipelines, deployment, ownership) so the CS/SWE screen is satisfied.
- Uses impact metrics (latency, failure rate, throughput, scale, hours saved) rather than vanity counts or club-ops filler.
- Anti-patterns: inventing aviation internships, citizenship, or tools not evidenced; leading as an ML-research or PE-deal-sourcing intern; Skills-list keyword stuffing with no backing bullet; notebook ML without delivery; a resume that reads purely agentic-LLM product with no real-time/systems or data-systems evidence; burying the real-time/embedded-adjacent signal below the fold.
