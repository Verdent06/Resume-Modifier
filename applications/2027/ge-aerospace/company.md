# GE Aerospace

GE Aerospace is GE's 2024 aviation spinoff: a Fortune-class public company (NYSE: GE) that designs and builds commercial and military jet engines, components, and integrated systems. Flagship products include the CFM LEAP (with Safran) and the GE9X; the installed base is supported through the full service life. This intern sits in Aviation T&O Product Safety and Quality on an Engines Computer/Software Engineering rotation — engine controls, sensors, electrical power, vehicle health and data systems, real-time analytics, embedded controls, and engine test/integration — not mechanical design or CAD.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-tier (`reference/companies.md` — Fortune-class industrial aviation peer to GE Vernova / Medtronic / WD for intern brand; solid program, not FAANG SWE prestige)
- **HQ / offices:** Evendale / Cincinnati, OH (HQ). This req: primarily Cincinnati, OH or Lynn, MA (onsite). Other sites include Niskayuna, NY (Research).
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: GE) after the 2024 GE Aerospace / Vernova / HealthCare split; ~$35B+ aerospace revenue class; intern pipeline into leadership programs or direct hire
- **Product focus:** Jet engines and integrated aircraft systems; this req is CS/SWE on engine controls, health/data, real-time analytics, embedded, and test systems
- **Intern comp (2027 Engines CS/SWE):** begins at $20/hour and increases for each undergraduate year completed; relocation support and housing assistance for those who relocate
- **Work model:** Paid Summer 2027 intern (typically 10–12 weeks, late May–mid-August); onsite Evendale/Cincinnati OH or Lynn MA
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Minimum 3.0 cumulative GPA (4.0 scale, no rounding). Full-time accredited baccalaureate or graduate program in computer engineering, computer science, software engineering, or related. Legal U.S. work authorization; no visa sponsorship now or in the future. U.S. Person status required (export-controlled): U.S. citizen, LPR, asylee, or refugee. Background investigation and drug screen. Reliable transportation (many sites lack public transit). Upperclassmen preferred, not a hard class-year filter. Posted 2026-08-17; rolling (no listed deadline).
