# Software Engineer Intern, 2027 Summer U.S. at Atlassian

## Role Summary

Summer 2027 Software Engineer Intern at Atlassian, Seattle or San Francisco, 12 weeks full-time (40 hrs/week), May/June–August/September 2027. Interns sit on a development team, report to Senior Engineers, and ship features into production products from day one — full lifecycle product development on the cloud collaboration suite (Jira, Confluence, Trello, Bitbucket) while customers move to cloud. JD surface is generic SWE intern: OOP languages (Java, Python, Golang, TypeScript or other), DS&A, prior shipping preferred. Company identity is team-collaboration SaaS / cloud product platform. Not an ML-research intern, not an infra-only intern, not a Java-or-nothing filter.

## Track Decision

- **screen_track:** full-stack (generic SWE intern — OOP languages, DS&A, ship product features; `resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11)
- **differentiator:** team-collaboration SaaS / cloud product platform (Jira, Confluence, Trello, Bitbucket; cloud migration of the suite)
- **track_divergence:** false

The screen spine and the company identity agree. Both are full-stack product engineering. Lead with a genuinely engineered, shippable product (frontend framework, backend API, a database, cloud/deploy, ideally a CI step) narrated decision-by-decision, with Python and TypeScript demonstrated in bullets — those are the JD languages the candidate can actually claim. The differentiator sharpens emphasis *within* full-stack toward cloud product shipping (APIs, clients, data, deploy), not a pivot to `ai-ml` or `dev-ops`. Preferred AI/ML / agentic interest is a plus *on* that spine (built pipelines, eval, ML frameworks), not a track change and not MCP invention. `lead_signal_window` is 0. Do not invent Java, Golang, Jira, Confluence, or MCP experience.

## Screen Criteria

- Full-stack shipping with APIs, a datastore, and a deploy or CI path demonstrated in bullets — not Skills-only (`resume.md` §12). Breadth across the stack plus depth in at least one layer.
- Python and TypeScript used inside architectural decisions, not asserted on a Skills line. Java and Golang are JD terms; absence on the page is not a knockout when other OOP languages are proven in use.
- Prior titled internship / co-op or comparable production delivery visible — preferred software-engineering experience.
- DS&A / OOP implied by engineered systems (data models, pipelines, concurrency, APIs), not a coursework dump. Coursework listing DS&A is supporting, not the proof.
- Preferred AI/ML: agentic pipelines, eval, or ML frameworks as *built* product work — not Copilot-as-skill, not invented MCP.
- Class-year on page shows current enrollment and a grad date at or before August 2028 (`Expected May 2028` satisfies Summer 2027 return-to-school).
- Cloud-product framing: what shipped for a user or stakeholder — over club-ops or GTM-only lead stories.

## Anti-Patterns

- Skills-list Python/TypeScript with zero bullet evidence at a shop where those are the floor the candidate can hit.
- Invented Java, Golang, MCP, Jira, or Confluence experience.
- Routing the page to research-only LoRA / notebook ML as if this were an ai-ml intern req, or to C++ DSP as if this were an embedded intern req.
- AI-tool-only claims (prompting, Copilot) with no pipeline or product.
- Lead entry that is off-axis for product engineering (pure GTM scoring, club-ops) while the full-stack spine is buried.
- Thin single-bullet filler that dilutes a shippable product story.
- Languages or frameworks listed in Skills with zero bullet evidence (except baseline Languages/VCS).
