# Atlassian

Atlassian (Nasdaq: TEAM) builds team-collaboration software used to plan, track, and ship work — flagship products are Jira and Confluence, with Trello, Bitbucket, and adjacent cloud tools in the same suite. Engineering’s current intern mandate is product work on that suite while customers move to cloud: ship features into production from day one, not a research rotation. The company is remote-friendly globally, but this U.S. intern req is Seattle or San Francisco only.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`, next to ServiceNow / Appian)
- **HQ / offices:** Sydney, Australia (global HQ); this intern req: Seattle, WA **or** San Francisco, CA
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (Nasdaq: TEAM); ~$47.6B market cap (Yahoo Finance, 2026-09); collaboration-SaaS franchise (Jira / Confluence)
- **Product focus:** Team-collaboration / work-management cloud platform (Jira, Confluence, Trello, Bitbucket) plus cloud migration of the suite
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Engineer Intern, U.S.):** Zone A $59–67/hr; Zone B $54–60/hr; Zone C $49–56/hr (go.atlassian.com/payzones); may include benefits, bonuses, commissions, equity
- **Work model:** Paid; 12 weeks full-time (40 hrs/week), May/June–August/September 2027; this role must work from Seattle or San Francisco (company-wide office/home/hybrid policy does not override that)
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Not eligible for F1 or J1; no work sponsorship. Must be enrolled full-time in a BS/MS CS (or related) program, return after the internship, and graduate by August 2028

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD floor is Java, Python, Golang, TypeScript, or other OOP languages via coursework or projects. Screen on languages the candidate actually has (Python + TypeScript). Do not invent Java, Golang, or MCP on the page.
- **Domains:** Full-stack product engineering on cloud collaboration SaaS; APIs, web clients, data, deploy/CI; preferred extras are AI/ML exposure (agentic AI, ML frameworks) and basic design-pattern awareness — not an ML-research intern track
- **What wins:** A shippable full-stack spine (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11) with Python and/or TypeScript in bullets, production-shaped APIs/deploy, and optional agentic/ML *product* evidence as a preferred signal. Resume must clear iCIMS; binding intern filter after that is HackerRank then tech rounds (`companies.md`: bottleneck tech rounds)
