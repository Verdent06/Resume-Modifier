# RTX / Raytheon (Tewksbury)

Raytheon is RTX's missile-and-defense segment. The Tewksbury, MA campus at 50 Apple Hill Drive (Concord building) is a radar and air-defense software site: Patriot, Missile Defense Sensors (MDS) programs such as Ground Based Radar Digitization, and related radar baselines (RDPL, UEWR, Cobra Dane, SPY-6 / GhostEye families). Software work here is product development on complex, high-performance systems — real-time C/C++ radar and air-defense applications, integration, debug, and code review — not manufacturing/avionics *test* software and not a returning-intern-only req.
