# General Motors

General Motors is a Detroit-headquartered OEM and a top Michigan employer. Software identity is the **Ultifi** vehicle-software platform plus connected-vehicle digital products — real platform work, not captive auto-finance and not a hardware-only seat. This folder is Workday **JR-202620546** **2027 Summer Intern - Digital Product: Software Engineering** (General Motors LLC). Digital Products owns mobile apps, back-office platforms, and connected services; it combines OnStar & Customer Experience Platforms with Digital Vehicle Experience and partners with Vehicle Products. **Do not mix with GM Financial** (Arlington SDE intern under `applications/2027/gm-financial/`), the MBA Digital Product PM intern **JR-202620538**, or manufacturing intern reqs.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-TIER (`reference/companies.md`) — Michigan auto OEM peer of Ford / Stellantis; stronger local SWE signal than most C-tier non-tech
- **HQ / offices:** Detroit / Warren, MI. This intern: primary **Warren, Michigan — GM Global Technical Center, Sloan Engineering Center**. Additional: Austin TX; Milford MI; Mountain View CA. Assignment follows team placement
- **Valuation / signal:** Public OEM; Student Corps intern program at scale; Ultifi + OnStar/CXP + Digital Vehicle Experience as the software brand. Limited national SWE-intern prestige vs FAANG; Michigan location is a real pipeline advantage
- **Product focus:** Ultifi / vehicle digital platforms / connected services — consumer mobile and commerce, OnStar SAE (carrier/vehicle experience, contact centers, OnStar AI Engineering), fleet (ESB), virtual/physical test platforms, vehicle software integrity and delivery (calibration, Greylock, wired programming, VCAM)
- **Intern comp (2027 Digital Product SWE, JR-202620546):** **$6,400–$10,300 per month** + one-time taxable stipend for eligible students (apply using the permanent address you would move from). Paid US GM holidays; GM Family First vehicle discount
- **Work model:** Paid full-time intern; **12 weeks**, 40h; start dates **May 24 or June 14, 2027**; May–August or June–September 2027. **Hybrid**, report **3 days/week** at the assigned GM location. Posted **2026-09-21**; Workday `endDate` **2027-01-15**
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently pursuing Bachelor's in CS, CE, Data Science, IS, Mechanical Engineering, or related. **At least one additional quarter/semester remaining after the internship.** Full-time 40h for the 12-week term. Ability to work onsite at the assigned GM location. **This JD body does not print** a GM-wide “no immigration sponsorship” paragraph and **does not print a GPA cutoff**.
