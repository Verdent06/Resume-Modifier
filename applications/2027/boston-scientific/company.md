# Boston Scientific

Boston Scientific (NYSE: **BSX**) is a global medical-technology company that designs and manufactures devices and therapies for cardiovascular, respiratory, digestive, oncological, neurological, and urological disease. 2025 net sales **$20B**; ~59,000 employees; Forbes 2026 #2 medtech employer for engineers **[directional, Extern]**. Maple Grove, MN is a major cardiovascular campus (new $170M / 400,000 sq ft facility, 1,500+ seats; Peripheral Interventions and Interventional Cardiology). This req is a **Data Science Co-op** on manufacturing process development — experimental/production process data, predictive models, data pipelines, and genAI/LLM documentation — **not** the 12-week Software Engineering Internship, **not** R&D Research Algorithm & Data Science Intern, **not** Process Development Intern/Co-op (non-DS), **not** WATCHMAN R&D Engineering Co-op.
