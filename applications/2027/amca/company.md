# Amca (Advanced Manufacturing Company of America)

Amca reconstitutes the U.S. aerospace and defense component supply chain: it designs and qualifies critical parts, then manufactures them in certified factories instead of leaving a gap between engineering and production. Flagship software is **RAPID**, an AI-enabled product-development platform used to engineer, qualify, and produce components (F-35 and commercial airframes named in press). This intern writes software that turns decades of factory engineering knowledge into structured data (drawings, CAD, documents) and internal tools for factories and **Protoshop** — not an electrical-design intern and not a shop-floor CNC seat.
