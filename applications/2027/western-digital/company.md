# Western Digital (WD)

Western Digital builds the storage systems behind hyperscale data centers, cloud platforms, and enterprise infrastructure — the HDD and related storage stack that keeps AI-scale data durable and accessible. After the SanDisk flash spin-off, WD's engineering identity is storage hardware plus the system software, firmware, tools, and applications that make drives and arrays work at scale. Intern SWE work is routed from a catch-all early-career posting into one of several tracks (applications, system tools, data analytics, firmware, systems integration), not a single greenfield product team.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-tier (`reference/companies.md` — hardware/storage peer to Intel/AMD/Qualcomm; solid intern brand, not FAANG SWE prestige)
- **HQ / offices:** San Jose, CA (Great Oaks Headquarters); intern sites also listed: Colorado Springs CO, Irvine CA, Longmont CO, Fremont CA, Rochester MN. This application targets San Jose onsite.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public storage company (NYSE: WDC); Fortune-class manufacturing + systems engineering; H1B history at the company does not imply intern sponsorship.
- **Product focus:** Hyperscale/enterprise storage systems — HDD and the software/firmware/tools that integrate them with host systems and data-center ops.
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE):** $34.52–46.00/hr (CA non-exempt range on the posting)
- **Work model:** Paid 12-week (typical) Summer 2027 internship; San Jose Great Oaks onsite (site-based / hybrid collaboration days per WD early-talent FAQ); relocation at hiring-manager discretion
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's in CS, SWE, CompE, Systems, Data Science, EE, or related. Must remain enrolled with at least one term after the internship (WD students-and-grads FAQ). Posting is 0–2+ years early-career; "an immediate opening or interview is not guaranteed." 2026 firmware intern reqs sometimes listed "right to work without sponsorship"; this 2027 catch-all posting does not.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** C, C++, Python, Java, Go, SQL, JavaScript (JD: one or more via coursework, internships, research, or personal projects)
- **Domains:** System software and engineering tools; commercial/internal applications; data pipelines/analytics; embedded firmware (C/C++/assembly); hardware–software integration. Company identity is storage, semiconductors, embedded, cloud, AI, and data analytics interest — not a web-only shop.
- **What wins:** Shipped applications or data systems in a listed language, plus defensible C++/systems depth (performance, memory, debugging, hardware-adjacent real-time) without inventing OS/compiler internships. Recruiter routes the catch-all into Applications or Data Analytics for a full-stack/Python candidate; System Tools/Firmware only if the page shows real low-level work. Git/Linux/IDE familiarity is a plus. Resume is the gate on the posted US funnel.
