# Mercury

Mercury (legal: Mercury Technologies, Inc.) is banking software for startups and growing businesses — accounts, cards, invoicing, bill pay, spend, and treasury-style tooling in one product — serving **300,000+** businesses (JD; official Series D: one in three U.S. startups). It is **not** a chartered bank today (partner banks; FDIC disclaimer on product pages) and **not** Mercury Insurance. May 20 2026 **$200M Series D at $5.2B** (TCV; returning a16z, Sequoia, Coatue, CRV, Sapphire, Spark) after April 2026 **conditional OCC approval** to form Mercury Bank, N.A. Engineering identity is a **production Haskell backend** plus a large **TypeScript** frontend (~650K lines on this JD) with a high UI bar for founder customers. This intern is a **generic product-SWE seat** that team-matches frontend, backend, or full-stack — **not** ML research, **not** a trading desk.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** TypeScript (frontend required; React plus). Haskell **or a strong desire to learn it** plus SQL familiarity (backend/full-stack). Screen tests languages the candidate actually ships — **do not invent Haskell**. TypeScript + React + SQL through use is the honest frontend/full-stack frame.
- **Domains:** Product SWE on a regulated-adjacent banking UI and/or Haskell services (transaction search, wires, admin/fraud tools, third-party APIs). Typed correctness and UI quality beat notebook ML.
- **What wins:** Shipped TypeScript/React (or honest TS-family frontend) with a persistence/SQL decision and a deploy path; named debug; communication of technical trade-offs (`resume.md` Part III §12 / `recruiting.md` Part III §11). Finance-adjacent APIs and data-integrity flavor is memorable here. Resume carries more of the front-end weight than an OA-gated FAANG intern (`recruiting.md` Part I §1 vs Greenhouse). Apply before **2026-09-25**.
