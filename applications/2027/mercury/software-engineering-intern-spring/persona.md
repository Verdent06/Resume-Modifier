## Role Summary

Campus product-SWE intern on a Mercury engineering team (Greenhouse 6199367004 / req pipelineintern2026). Interns team-match **frontend** (TypeScript required; React a plus), **backend** (Haskell or a strong desire to learn it, plus SQL familiarity), or **full-stack** (Haskell-or-learn + SQL + TypeScript). Company identity (`company.md` / `companies.md`) is **fintech-backend / banking-for-startups / typed product quality** — not a trading desk, not a consumer social app, and **not** an ML-research seat.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Polyglot proof for languages the candidate actually has that the JD names (**TypeScript**; SQL through use). Keyword-through-use in bullets, not Skills-only (`resume.md` §2). **Do not invent Haskell.**
- Shipped full-stack or frontend software: TypeScript/React (or honest TS-family UI), REST/APIs, a persistence decision, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12). Debug, tests, or code-review discipline through use matches "quality" and "explain technical decisions."
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in a practical coding session / SQL PR review (the intern bottleneck after the PDF).
- Modern practices through use: Git/version control, tests or CI, stakeholder delivery.
- Fintech-backend / data-integrity flavor on that spine: production APIs, financial or regulated-data workflows, reliability/latency or named production defects — memorable at Mercury without replacing the SWE spine.
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028` satisfies enrolled-student for Spring 2027.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Skills-line Haskell with zero bullet evidence (fabrication smell at a Haskell shop).
- Invented Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, or Sentry — banned; not in this JD and not in inventory.
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML lead as if this were an ML-research intern seat (the AI essay is a form question, not the role).
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests TypeScript/React/SQL/product SWE.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + banking-delivery spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
