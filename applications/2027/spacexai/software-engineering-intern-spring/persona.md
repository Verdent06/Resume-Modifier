**Anti-patterns:**

- Inventing C, C#, Java, CUDA, JAX, Kubernetes, or SpaceXAI/xAI employment.
- Treating this as the SpaceX B-tier vehicle/Starlink intern (embedded/avionics narrative, LangGraph omitted as off-axis).
