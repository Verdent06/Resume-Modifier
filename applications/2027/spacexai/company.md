# SpaceXAI

SpaceXAI is the legal/branding name on the **xAI** Greenhouse board (`job-boards.greenhouse.io/xai`) — the same S-tier Musk AI lab as the `companies.md` **xAI** row, not the SpaceX B-tier vehicle/Starlink intern. The lab's mission is AI systems that accurately understand the universe: **Grok** (frontier model trained on the world's largest supercluster / Colossus) and **Starmind** (a satellite constellation capturing solar energy in space to power low-cost, high-performance AI compute for Earth). The org is small, flat, and hands-on; this intern (Greenhouse **5255111007** / req **1308**) is a generic SWE rotation in Palo Alto, not a CUDA/kernel posting and not a SpaceX flight-software seat.
