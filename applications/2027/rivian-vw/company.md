# Rivian and Volkswagen Group Technologies (RV Tech)

Rivian and Volkswagen Group Technologies is the 2024 joint venture that builds the shared software-defined vehicle stack for Rivian and Volkswagen Group brands — operating systems, zonal controllers, cloud, connectivity, and in-vehicle software — rather than a Rivian-OEM vehicle program. Flagship work is the zonal architecture (fewer ECUs, less wiring, central computers, OTA) that Volkswagen Group intends to carry across Western-hemisphere EVs (ID.EVERY1 / Scout / Audi reference vehicles completed winter testing in Phoenix and Arjeplog, March 2026). This intern req is **Infotainment & Mobile applications**: customer-facing in-vehicle and companion software (Android Automotive apps, Android platform/HAL, wireless/telematics, 3D/graphics, test automation) — **not** Rivian OEM internships on `careers.rivian.com`, **not** the sibling Connected Systems intern (`13eeb795-a5dd-4b5a-9b98-808e5ec357c2`), and **not** the Canada Infotainment intern (`bc8e38eb-b68c-4234-a06a-99875df7d600`).
