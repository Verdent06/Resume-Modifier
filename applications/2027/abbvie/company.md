# AbbVie

AbbVie is a Fortune-class biopharmaceutical company: immunology, oncology, and neuroscience medicines plus Allergan Aesthetics. Flagship products include Humira, Skyrizi, and Rinvoq. Business Technology Solutions (BTS) is the enterprise IT organization that builds data platforms and internal software so scientific and commercial teams can operate — not an R&D/ML-research lab and not a consumer product org. The undergraduate BTS intern is dual-track: Data Engineering or Software/Application Engineering.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-tier (`reference/companies.md` — Fortune-class pharma; solid intern brand, not FAANG SWE prestige)
- **HQ / offices:** North Chicago, IL (HQ / Lake County). Majority of BTS interns sit in Lake County; other U.S. AbbVie and Allergan Aesthetics sites possible. Location preferences are considered; placement follows business need.
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NYSE: ABBV); large-cap pharma; ~$7B+ R&D spend class (2025, directional). Intern program is structured and rolling; Extern estimates ~45–60% intern return-offer (pharma-sector directional, not BTS-specific).
- **Product focus:** Medicines and aesthetics brands; this intern builds enterprise applications and data infrastructure that answer scientific or business questions
- **Intern comp (2027 BTS Data & Software Engineering):** $21–37.80/hr (posted). Relocation support for eligible students; select wellness benefits and paid holiday / sick time
- **Work model:** Paid 11-week onsite Summer 2027 internship (majority Lake County, IL)
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Bachelor's in CS, Computer Engineering, AI, or related (minor/concentration in those fields accepted). Graduation December 2027 – June 2028. Must be enrolled the semester after the internship and graduate within one year post-internship. Cumulative GPA 3.00+ preferred. SmartRecruiters ATS; rolling.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** Software/Application track prefers Front End (NodeJS, Vue.js, HTML), Back End (Java, JavaScript), Full Stack (Java, SQL, TypeScript; APEX or Python), plus generative AI on custom software and a public GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket. Data Engineering prefers Java, Python, R, SQL; APIs to extract data; LLMs or ML algorithms. Python/SQL/TypeScript/APIs are the realistic screen languages when Java/Vue/R/APEX are absent.
- **Domains:** Enterprise applications (UI, APIs, microservices, full-stack ownership); data infrastructure (pipelines, warehousing, integration, visualization, secure collection/storage); foundations for analytics and data science — not model-research internships.
- **What wins:** Because the bottleneck is the resume (`recruiting.md` intern funnel; B-tier non-tech-tech), a one-page PDF that proves Python/SQL (and TypeScript where real) inside shipped apps and data pipelines, plus GitHub, passes this screen. Do not invent Java, Spring Boot, Vue.js, NodeJS, R, or APEX. Lead as a full-stack intern who can also show data-infrastructure-for-science-or-business, not as an ML researcher.
