# Commerce Bank (Commerce Bancshares)

Commerce Bancshares, Inc. (NASDAQ: **CBSH**) is a Kansas City–based regional bank holding company; the lead bank is **Commerce Bank**. IR: total assets **$35.3B** as of June 30, 2026; Q2 2026 net income **$159.8M**. Founded 1865-era (JD: 160+ years). Midwest banking centers plus commercial offices in 11 states and payment solutions nationwide. Jan 1, 2026 closed FineMark Holdings (Florida / Arizona / South Carolina private banking). Enterprise Analytics and Business Intelligence (**EABI**) and **Data Management** support consumer and other lines of business with reporting, dashboards, and queries — not a consumer-app SWE intern and not an ML-research lab.
