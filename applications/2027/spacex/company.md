# SpaceX

SpaceX designs, manufactures, and launches orbital rockets and spacecraft — Falcon, Dragon, Starship — and operates the Starlink / Starshield satellite constellation at global scale. Software sits across vehicle flight and ground systems, satellite and constellation operations, manufacturing and test tooling, and (via SpaceXAI) autonomy / ML adjacent to Starlink and Grok / Starmind. The engineering culture is known for brutal pace, extreme ownership, and C++/Python systems work on mission-critical hardware; brand prestige is undeniable and the intern bar is correspondingly high.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B
- **HQ / offices:** Hawthorne (Los Angeles), CA; sites also include Bastrop TX, Brownsville TX, Cape Canaveral FL, Irvine CA, McGregor (Waco) TX, Palo Alto CA, Redmond (Seattle) WA
- **Valuation / signal:** Privately held aerospace leader; Falcon/Dragon operational; Starship + Starlink as primary growth engines; SpaceXAI (Grok / Starmind) as adjacent ML brand
- **Product focus:** Launch vehicles, crewed/cargo spacecraft, satellite constellation (Starlink/Starshield), and supporting ground/ops software
- **Intern comp (2027 SWE intern/co-op):** Freshman/Sophomore $30/hr; Junior/Senior $34/hr; Completed Bachelor's $36; Master's $38; PhD $40 — relocation stipend possible; medical; 401(k); paid holidays
- **Work model:** Paid; full-time onsite; minimum 12 consecutive weeks beginning May or June 2027; flexible across any SpaceX site
- **Clearance / eligibility:** ITAR — U.S. citizen/national, LPR, refugee, or asylee; must be enrolled in a bachelor's or graduate program; own transportation required for Brownsville, Cape Canaveral, McGregor

**Estimated funnel:** 3–4 rounds · Med–Hard · HackerRank · Yes (embedded) · Bottleneck: tech rounds · ~5–8%

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** C, C++, C#, Java, JavaScript, Python (JD); company Notes emphasize C++/Python for systems work
- **Domains:** Vehicle / flight and ground software; satellite / constellation ops; manufacturing and test tooling; SpaceXAI (Starlink / Starshield / Grok / Starmind) as optional program preference — not the sole intern track
- **What wins:** Evidence of real software shipped and used (not club-ops filler); JD languages demonstrated in bullets; debugging, performance, and testing discipline; computer-architecture / networking fluency; adjacency to real-time, embedded, or mission-critical systems without forcing a pure ML or pure web narrative
