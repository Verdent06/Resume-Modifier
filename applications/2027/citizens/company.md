# Citizens

Citizens Financial Group (NYSE: **CFG**; legal: Citizens Financial Group, Inc.) is a Providence-headquartered super-regional bank (ex-RBS US / RBS Citizens). Consumer Banking and Commercial Banking cover retail deposits and lending, private bank/wealth, and commercial/corporate products — including custodial-adjacent client-asset and custody/recordkeeping services named in the 10-Q. Enterprise Technology & Security (ETS), led at the executive layer by the CIO / Head of Enterprise Security & Technology, runs the intern program this Data Engineer seat sits in. This req (Oracle CX **49285**) is applied data engineering on AWS batch/realtime pipelines — **not** ML research and **not** a generic ETS SWE/cloud sibling. Honest candidate inventory does not include Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, Spark, Kafka, Hadoop, Beam, Flink, or Java — do not invent them.
