# Citizens

Citizens Financial Group (NYSE: **CFG**; consumer brand **Citizens Bank**; legal: Citizens Financial Group, Inc.) is a Providence-headquartered super-regional bank (ex-RBS US / RBS Citizens). YE2025 10-K: **$226.4B** assets, **$183.3B** deposits, 17,398 FTE; ~1,000 branches / ~3,000 ATMs across 14 states + DC. 10-Q at 2026-06-30: assets **$233.8B**, deposits **$185.6B**, equity **$26.2B**. Market cap ~$29.8B as of 2026-09-04 (stockanalysis) **[directional]**. Enterprise Technology & Security (ETS) runs the shared Summer Internship Program. Two 2027 intern reqs live under this folder: **Software Engineer** (Oracle CX **49286**) — generic ETS application/API/microservices SWE — and **Data Engineer** (Oracle CX **49285**) — applied AWS batch/realtime pipelines. Neither is the Cloud Engineer intern sibling, and neither is ETSDP full-time (that FT twin lists Spring 2027 grad). Do not invent Java, Swift, .NET, Go, iOS/Android ownership, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, Spark, Kafka, Hadoop, Beam, Flink, Kubernetes, Terraform, CircleCI, or Jenkins.
