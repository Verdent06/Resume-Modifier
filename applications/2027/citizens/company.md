# Citizens

Citizens Financial Group (NYSE: **CFG**; consumer brand **Citizens Bank**) is a Providence-headquartered super-regional bank. YE2025 10-K: **$226.4B** assets, **$183.3B** deposits, 17,398 FTE; ~1,000 branches across 14 states + DC. Q2 2026 assets **$233.8B**. The engineering work that matters on this packet is **Enterprise Technology & Security (ETS)**: intern SWE on APIs, microservices, and application delivery that supports the bank — not branch IT, not the Cloud Engineer intern sibling, and not the full-time ETSDP Software Engineer seat (that FT twin lists Spring 2027 grad). ETS also rotates cloud, data, network, technology risk, and cyber; this req is the software-engineer intern.
