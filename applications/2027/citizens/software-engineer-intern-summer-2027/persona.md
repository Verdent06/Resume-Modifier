## Role Summary

Paid **Software Engineer** intern in Citizens' **Enterprise Technology & Security Summer Internship Program** (Oracle CX job **49286**). 10 weeks from **late May 2027**, 40 hours/week, M–F 8:30am–5pm, onsite at one headquarters: **Johnston, RI**, **Phoenix, AZ**, or **Plano, TX**. Comp unlisted on this req (sibling intern analog **$27/hr** is directional only). Day-to-day is **generic intern SWE** inside ETS: design, develop, and implement technology solutions; contribute to applications, components, and tools; API services that support multiple data formats; microservices-framework exposure; iOS/Android *exposure* (not a mobile-ownership seat); Agile delivery; business/technical analysis and documentation; quality/SOP improvements; learn-and-apply AI-assisted development tools.

Company identity (`company.md` / `companies.md`): super-regional bank (NYSE: CFG) ETS org — APIs and enterprise application delivery that support banking, not a Cloud Engineer intern rotation, not ETSDP full-time (that twin lists Spring 2027 grad), and not an ML-training seat.

## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- At least one JD or-list language through use in bullets (`resume.md` §2 keyword-through-use). Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, React, or C++ on the page beats a Skills-only claim.
- Shipped full-stack or backend software: REST/APIs or services, persistence, and cloud/deploy (AWS/Docker/CI) tied to an outcome (`resume.md` §12). Microservices *flavor* (composed services, workers, multi-component deploy) without inventing a named bank framework.
- Professional SWE exposure as a plus (preferred): a titled engineering role with a production debug or delivery story, not club-ops.
- CS fundamentals visible: DS&A coursework plus engineered work defensible in an Easy–Med HackerRank (coding + a database question on the 2026 analog).
- Bank ETS / fintech-backend flavor on that spine: production APIs, financial or messy-data workflows, reliability-minded delivery — memorable here without replacing the SWE spine.
- AI-assisted development or applied-agent work as a **plus** (preferred / learn-and-apply), never as the lead story for a generic SWE intern, and never invented Copilot/Fusion/Databricks.
- CI/CD through use (GitHub Actions is the honest CircleCI/Jenkins analog). Cloud through use (AWS) without inventing Azure/GCP/Kubernetes/Terraform.
- Honest class year, GPA, and US-auth status on the page. Expected May 2028 and GPA ≥ 3.0 match this req.

**Anti-patterns:**

- Invented Java, Swift, .NET, Go, iOS/Android app ownership, Snowflake, Databricks, Copilot, Fusion, Tableau, Kubernetes, Terraform, CircleCI, or Jenkins.
- Invented core-banking, mobile-banking, or Citizens-platform claims because the company is a bank.
- Agentic-AI / LoRA / notebook-ML lead as if this were an ML-research intern seat (AI tools are preferred, not the job).
- Hard-real-time C++/audio-DSP as the first-pass identity when the role tests APIs, services, and enterprise application delivery.
- Cloud/SRE-only framing (this is not the Cloud Engineer intern sibling).
- Club-ops, community-growth, or coursework-only framing with no shipped application.
- Thin single-bullet entries that dilute a full-stack + finance-delivery spine.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact (`resume.md` §4).
- Missing the HireVue 4-day SLA after apply — a process fail, not a resume fail, but it auto-withdraws the packet.
