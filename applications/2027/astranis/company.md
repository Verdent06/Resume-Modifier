# Astranis

Astranis builds micro-GEO communications satellites that sell dedicated, secure bandwidth to enterprises, sovereign governments, and the US military — small spacecraft in geostationary orbit instead of a shared wide-beam transponder. The company reports five satellites on orbit, more than $1B in commercial backlog, $750M+ raised (a16z, Blackrock, Fidelity), and ~450 people, with a large Northern California HQ footprint. This intern sits on the Platform team: backend and infrastructure services that autonomously command satellites, monitor telemetry for anomalies, and give operators real-time situational awareness, plus core services the rest of the software org consumes. Distinct from the Flight Software intern twin.

- **Languages:** Python is the intern floor. Databases (Postgres) and pub/sub / streaming (RabbitMQ, Flink *examples*) are required understanding. Bonus: Kubernetes, fleet-management systems. Do not invent Kubernetes, RabbitMQ, Flink, or a fleet-ops internship.
- **Domains:** Satellite command-and-control services; telemetry / anomaly monitoring; real-time situational awareness; org-wide backend and infrastructure; commercial + US government programs.
- **What wins:** Python demonstrated in backend/service bullets (not Skills-only); relational data (Postgres) and async/queue/streaming-shaped work through use; full-stack intern spine (APIs, data, deploy) plus visible real-time / mission-critical reliability so the GEO-satellite identity sticks.
