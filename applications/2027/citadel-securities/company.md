# Citadel Securities

Citadel Securities is one of the world's largest quantitative market makers, providing liquidity and executing trades across equities, options, fixed income, currencies, and commodities in markets worldwide. Founded in 2002 and headquartered in Miami with a major New York presence, it is a technology company as much as a trading firm: engineers work in small teams on short development cycles to build the pricing engines, low-latency execution platforms, and large-scale research systems that turn trading strategies into production code. The firm pairs software engineers with quantitative researchers and traders, and applies distributed computing, NLP, and machine learning to problems where microseconds and statistical edge both matter. It sits in the elite S-tier quant/HFT band alongside Jane Street, HRT, and Two Sigma — top-of-market comp, a math-and-systems-first bar, and one of the most selective SWE internship funnels in the industry.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** S-Tier (quant / HFT; `reference/companies.md` lists "Citadel / Citadel Securities" — algo/math heavy, top comp)
- **HQ / offices:** Miami, FL (HQ) and New York, NY (this internship: NY or Miami, onsite); global offices in Chicago, London, Dublin, and Asia.
- **Valuation / signal:** Privately held; dominant global market maker (a large share of US retail equity volume routes through it); elite prestige band on a resume.
- **Product focus:** Proprietary pricing, low-latency execution, and large-scale quantitative research platforms across multiple asset classes.
- **Intern comp (2027 SE):** $4,500–$5,800 per week (per JD); 11-week summer program.
- **Work model:** Paid, onsite (New York, NY or Miami, FL); 11-week summer internship with senior-engineer mentorship, networking, and peer cohort events.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Bachelor's / master's / PhD in CS, computer engineering, or related; current student returning to school after the internship. No published class-year knockout beyond degree-in-progress.

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** C++ (low-latency core), Python (research / tooling); strong programming and design fundamentals matter more than a specific language.
- **Domains:** Low-latency execution and pricing systems, large-scale data research platforms, distributed computing, NLP/ML applied to markets, real-time systems.
- **What wins:** Exceptional programming + design fluency proven in real, defensible projects; systems/performance depth (real-time constraints, concurrency, memory, latency) that reads HFT-adjacent; demonstrated familiarity with probability and statistics (model evaluation, regression, quantitative analysis) via a research/data platform, not notebook toy ML; strong CS fundamentals for a hard DS&A OA.
