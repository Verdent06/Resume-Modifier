# IBM

IBM is a public enterprise technology company (founded 1911) selling hybrid-cloud software, infrastructure, and consulting. IBM Software infuses client operations with machine learning and generative AI across digital labor, IT automation, application modernization, security, and sustainability; the hybrid-cloud platform (IBM Cloud plus Red Hat) is the delivery layer for training, tuning, and deploying AI across on-prem, multi-cloud, and edge data. Red Hat is the cloud-relevance signal on an otherwise large, bureaucratic intern program.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** B-TIER (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Armonk, NY HQ; this intern req: Lowell MA, Durham NC, Bellevue WA, San Jose CA, Austin TX
- **Valuation / signal:** Public; Fortune 500 hybrid-cloud + AI vendor; Red Hat acquisition is the modern cloud credibility
- **Product focus:** IBM Software — AI-powered, cloud-native products on a hybrid-cloud platform (IBM Cloud / Red Hat)
- **Intern comp (2027 Software Developer Intern):** $75,600–$166,320/yr listed (projected min/max; location-dependent)
- **Work model:** Paid internship / co-op (fixed term); official posting **Hybrid**; location flexibility across the listed sites (candidate must accept assignment to any of them). Jobright listed Lowell, MA as onsite — treat Lowell as the posted site, hybrid as the Avature work arrangement.
- **Clearance / eligibility:** High school diploma/GED required; bachelor's preferred. No class-year knockout on this posting. Title is **Software Developer Intern 2027** (Summer 2027 intern class), area of work Software Engineering — not Extreme Blue and not an ML-research req. Some travel may be required.
