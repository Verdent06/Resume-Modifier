# United Launch Alliance

United Launch Alliance is the Boeing/Lockheed Martin 50/50 launch joint venture (2006): ~2,500 people flying national-security, NASA, and commercial payloads from Cape Canaveral and Vandenberg. The JD cites 150+ consecutive successful missions; Vulcan Centaur is replacing Atlas V and Delta IV. This intern (req **2144**) is **Software Engineering Internship Summer 2027** on the **ULA–Denver / Centennial CO 80112** site — applications environments, engineering simulation, **embedded launch-vehicle flight and ground control**, lab CI/CD, cloud, optional AI engineering. It is not a mechanical or avionics intern, not SpaceX, and not an ML-research rotation. ITAR / U.S. Person applies; ULA does not offer F-1 or H-1B.
