# Acxiom

Acxiom LLC (pronounced "ax-ee-um") is a Conway, Arkansas-based customer-intelligence and identity company: it collects, unifies, and sells consumer and business data used for targeted advertising and marketing. Founded 1969 as Demographics, Inc.; the current LLC is the former Acxiom Marketing Services (AMS) division, which Interpublic Group of Companies (IPG) acquired in 2018 for **$2.3B** (LiveRamp was excluded and kept the old Acxiom Corp. ticker). Omnicom (NYSE: **OMC**) completed its acquisition of IPG on **November 26, 2025**; Acxiom is now the identity/data layer of Omni (Acxiom Real ID / RealID — ~2.6B global audience / verified IDs). Flagship products: **Audience Cloud**, **Real Identity / Real ID**, **Unified Data Layer**. Privacy/governance is a named company posture (GDPR/CCPA analog on this intern JD). **Not** LiveRamp (RAMP), **not** Ensono (former ITO spin).
