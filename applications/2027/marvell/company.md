# Marvell

Marvell designs the data-infrastructure silicon that sits under cloud, AI, enterprise, and carrier networks — custom compute, interconnects, and switching rather than a consumer GPU brand. Fiscal 2025 revenue was $5.77B with 7,042 employees (10-K as of February 1, 2025); HQ is Santa Clara. This intern seat is **not** chip design: it sits on the Engineering Applications team in **Marvell IT**, which runs DevOps/CI/CD, custom engineering apps, a governed AI platform, and an agentic SDLC path from requirements to deployment on AWS. Do not collapse it with firmware or application-engineer intern reqs that hire MS EE/optical.

- **Tier:** B-TIER — Good / Solid Resume Addition (`reference/companies.md`)
- **HQ / offices:** Santa Clara, CA (HQ / this intern). Global intern footprint: 29 offices in 12 countries in FY25 (sustainability report)
- **Valuation / signal:** Public (NASDAQ: **MRVL**). FY2025 revenue **$5.77B**; data center ~75% of Q4 FY25 mix; AI-related ~50% of data-center revenue (DEF 14A 2025). Official intern program: **381** interns FY25, **>65%** converted to full-time

- **Intern comp (2027 AI-Native Development Platform Engineer Intern, req 2603848):** **$27–$54/hour** (JD). Sibling MS firmware / application-engineer intern reqs print **$34–$68/hour** — do not import that band onto this IT seat
- **Work model:** Paid full-time; Santa Clara, CA. Term: Summer 2027 (exact start/end unpublished on this JD). Intern benefits named: medical/dental/vision, perks, mental-health resources, paid holidays
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently pursuing a **Bachelor's or Master's** in CS, CE, SWE, or related; expected graduation **Fall 2027 – Summer 2028**. Title says **MS**; the JD still accepts Bachelor's. EAR export-control paragraph: US citizens, LPRs, and 8 U.S.C. 1324b(a)(3) protected individuals skip the extra license-review path. Interview Integrity: no AI tools during interviews (disqualification)
