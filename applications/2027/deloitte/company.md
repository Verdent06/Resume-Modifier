# Deloitte

Deloitte is a Big Four professional-services firm. In the US, Deloitte Consulting LLP sells strategy, technology, and operations work into enterprise and public-sector clients; **Consultative Offerings** packages that delivery as repeatable solutions rather than one-off custom build. This Summer Scholar seat sits in **Data & AI Solutions Engineering**: AI engineering, data infrastructure, and forward-deployed client activation — agentic workflows, pipelines that fuel generative models, and operationalized intelligence — not a research lab and not product-engineering at a software company. Work quality and SWE-vs-staffing mix vary by team the same way they do at Accenture (`reference/companies.md` C-tier caveat).

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-TIER (`reference/companies.md` — Deloitte (Tech); same caveats as Accenture)
- **HQ / offices:** Deloitte US network; this req is posted in 18 cities (Atlanta, Austin, Boston, Charlotte, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, Detroit, Houston, Los Angeles, McLean, Minneapolis, New York, Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, San Diego, San Francisco, Seattle). **This application targets Atlanta, GA.**
- **Valuation / signal:** Big Four; consulting brand, not a software-product prestige signal. Intern work can be real SWE/data/AI or staffed delivery depending on engagement.
- **Product focus:** Consultative Offerings — Data & AI Solutions Engineering (applied data/AI delivery into client environments)
- **Intern comp (2027 Data & AI Solutions Engineering Summer Scholar):** $48/hour (JD wage estimate)
- **Work model:** Paid Summer Scholar intern; **onsite** at assigned office; travel up to 50%; must reside within a commutable distance of the assigned office during the internship (candidate is willing to relocate to Atlanta for the term)
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Currently enrolled; expected to graduate by Spring/Summer 2028 **or** completed bachelor's+ in CS, Data Science, Statistics, Applied Math, Data Analytics, MIS, Economics, Finance, Business Analytics, Mathematics, Engineering, or related. Minimum GPA 3.0 (3.2 preferred). Legally authorized to work in the US **without employer sponsorship, now or in the future**. Age 18+ at employment. Recruiting ends **11/01/2026**. Req **363475**. This is **not** Forensic Analytics and **not** GPS AI and Data Engineering (req 362479).

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages:** JD required examples: SQL, Python, R (plus Power BI, Informatica). Preferred also names Java. Screen on languages the candidate actually has — Python and SQL through use — not invented R/Java.
- **Domains:** Applied data/AI client delivery: ETL and data pipelines, analytics/viz/big-data familiarity (Tableau, Hadoop, Spark, AWS/Azure/GCP named as examples), LLMs / vector DBs / genAI APIs (OpenAI, Claude, Cohere), MLOps/CI-CD as preferred, agentic workflows, forward-deployed stakeholder work. Possible engagement types: AI & Engineering; Advertising, Marketing & Commerce; Regulatory Risk & Forensic (project type, not the Forensic Analytics scholar posting); Strategy & Transactions.
- **What wins:** Resume-first C-tier consulting screen (`recruiting.md` intern: eligibility knockouts, then the PDF). Evidence of ingest → transform → serve/activate with a metric, Python/SQL in bullets not Skills-only, client- or stakeholder-facing delivery, and agentic/LLM or pipeline work that operationalizes intelligence — not notebook ML, not a generic SWE product resume, not invented Tableau/Informatica/Hadoop/Spark/Power BI/R/Java claims. GPA ≥ 3.5 is a real ai-ml coursework signal (`resume.md` §14).
