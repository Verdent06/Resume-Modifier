# Software Engineer Intern, Full Stack — Summer 2027 at Primer

## Role Summary

A paid, full-time, 5-days/week onsite internship in Primer's Mission District San Francisco office. The intern builds **PrimerOS**, the operating system that runs Primer's K–8 microschools, shipping features teachers, students, and families rely on every day. The JD surface is Full Stack SWE intern: TypeScript, Node, GraphQL, Prisma, Postgres, Next, React, Relay; DevEx and an agent stack; code reviews and a high quality bar; daily work with engineering, product, design, and academic program. Company identity is mission-first K–12: fix American education by building schools from the ground up and running them on a single OS nobody has built before. Work is broad (frontend, backend, systems, hardware-adjacent). Term may begin January, May, or June with a *preferred* 4–8 month commitment — preference, not a class-year knockout. This req is Summer 2027 (May/June start); returning to school after the intern term is eligible. Comp unlisted; J-1/F-1 sponsorship available.

## Screen Criteria

- TypeScript, React, and Postgres/PostgreSQL demonstrated **through use in bullets**, not asserted on a Skills line alone (`resume.md` Part I keyword-through-use; Part III §12).
- Shipped, defensible full-stack product: frontend framework + backend/API + datastore + deploy/CI, with trade-offs the candidate can narrate — not a tutorial clone (`resume.md` §12; `recruiting.md` §11).
- Production users — features or systems used by people other than the author (teachers, students, families, operators, or analogous stakeholders).
- Craft / code-review signal: quality bar, reviews, production discipline — matching the JD's "culture of excellence through code reviews."
- Education-adjacent or stakeholder-facing product shipping as mission fit — software used by non-engineers in an operational setting — without requiring an education-industry employer.
- Engineering fundamentals visible on the page (APIs, data model, production, full-stack) so the 7-second scan reads "can ship PrimerOS features," not "ML researcher" or "club ops."
- Class-year on page shows current enrollment (`Expected May 2028` clears intern-returning-to-school). Availability length (4–8 months) is a form/essay question, not a resume claim.

## Anti-Patterns

- Inventing Next, GraphQL, Prisma, Relay, or Node on the page to match the JD stack list.
- Skills-list TypeScript (or React/Postgres) with no bullet evidence — a floor-signal miss at a TypeScript product shop.
- Notebook ML, quant-only, or GTM/finance lead with no shipped user-facing product.
- Claiming 4–8 month availability or a January start the candidate does not have — that lives off-resume.
- Club-ops or membership padding with no engineering the role tests (`resume.md` §8).
- Backend-only or data-pipeline spine with no user-facing product a recruiter can picture as a PrimerOS feature.
- Two-page or ATS-broken formatting (`resume.md` Part I).
