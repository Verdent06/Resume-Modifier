# Primer

Primer is a K–8 microschool network (founded 2019, San Francisco HQ) that builds schools from the ground up and runs them on **PrimerOS** — a single school operating system covering teacher, student, and family experience plus the academic and back-office stack. Engineering is TypeScript/React product work on Postgres, with GraphQL services and real-time classroom ops named on the talent-pool posting; intern work ships features teachers, students, and families use daily. Founders Fund Seed $3.7M (2020) and Khosla/Founders Fund Series A $15M (Apr 2022, Not Boring); ~$18.8M total; ~104 employees (LinkedIn); 2024 Yass Prize. **Not** Primer.io (UK payments). Mission-first: fix K–12 education in America; keep work on mission execution, not unrelated activism.
