# SAS Institute

SAS Institute Inc. is the world’s largest privately held analytics and AI software company. Founded **1976** (Jim Goodnight et al.) and still headquartered on the Cary, NC campus, it ships **SAS Viya** — a cloud-native data, AI, and analytics platform — plus industry solutions in fraud, risk, marketing, IoT, and public sector. Engineering for this intern is **product software development and software testing** on new and existing SAS products (front-end, back-end, DevOps, or testing, team-matched after apply) — not a statistics-only intern, not CareerStart EMEA/AP, and not a SAS-programming certification class.

## Quick Facts

- **Tier:** C-tier (`reference/companies.md` — legitimate product-SWE intern; analytics brand stronger than typical C; intern pay is the C band)
- **HQ / offices:** HQ **Cary, NC** (**100 SAS Campus Drive**). This intern: **Cary HQ hybrid** (minimum **3 days in office per week**). 400+ offices worldwide **[directional, N&O]**; more than half of sales outside the US (2025 AR)
- **Valuation / signal:** Privately held. 2025 AR: **>$3B** annual sales, uninterrupted profitability, **20%** SAS Viya sales growth and **22%** cloud-sales growth (sixth consecutive year of double-digit cloud growth). ~**12k** employees **[directional, Revelio ~12,327 Mar 2026]**. JD: 90% of Fortune 100 as customers is company-copy on other SAS materials, not this intern JD. WayUp Top 100 Internship Program (careers site)
- **Product focus:** SAS Viya data/AI platform + industry analytics. This intern is **Software Development and Testing** (iCIMS **2026-42964**) — design/dev/test of SAS software — **not** Applied AI/Modeling intern, **not** Statistics intern, **not** Business (Finance/Sales/HR) intern
- **Intern comp (2027 bachelor’s-level):** **$15–$30/hour** by year-in-school and role (this JD pay-transparency band). Master’s/Ph.D intern analog **$29–$49/hour**. Not bonus-eligible
- **Work model:** Paid **12-week** Summer 2027 intern. **May 18, 2027 – August 6, 2027** (**dates not flexible**; must start in person at Cary HQ on **May 18, 2027**). Hybrid, ≥3 days/week on campus. Free SAS programming training/certification. Apps **open Oct 1, 2026 · close Oct 21, 2026**; interviews rolling
- **Clearance / eligibility:** Undergraduate enrolled in an accredited program, **not graduating before December 2027**. Pursuing CS / Software Development / Computer Engineering / IT / related. **Legally authorized to work in the US; no visa sponsorship now or in the future.** Export/trade-law nationality collection possible. No GPA floor on this JD. Posted as iCIMS req **2026-42964** (applications open **2026-10-01**)

## Interview Process

| Stage | Format | Notes |
| ----- | ------ | ----- |
| Resume screen | Human + iCIMS (`careers-sas.icims.com`) | Short application: personal info, resume, a few short questions. **No cover letter** (SAS Life intern hiring post). Rolling; resumes may be considered in order received. Bottleneck is the PDF |
| HireVue Virtual | One-way recorded | General + behavioral; SAS values (curious, passionate, authentic, accountable) **[SAS Life intern edition, 2019; still cited on internships FAQ]** |
| HireVue Live | Real-time video with hiring manager | Technical + communication + job fit; prior work, projects, coursework. Recruiter names the team in advance. Glassdoor intern **[directional]**: some loops add a panel with basic technical questions |
| Behavioral | Filter round | Values + initiative — not a culture-fit differentiator (`recruiting.md` §6) |

**Estimated funnel:** iCIMS resume → recruiter → HireVue on-demand → HireVue live HM · intern OA unpublished — do **not** invent HackerRank/CodeSignal · No intern sys design · Bottleneck: **resume** + Cary hybrid relocate · ~15–25% **[directional, C-tier peer of Ibotta / Patch My PC / Q2 product-SWE intern]**

## Stack & Hiring Signal

- **Languages the JD names (preferred or-list, not exclusive must-haves):** Java, Python, C, C#, HTML, CSS, SAS, R, JavaScript, Go, React, Angular, or other. Honest inventory overlap: **Python, C++, HTML/CSS, TypeScript, React, Angular**. Do **not** invent Java, C#, SAS programming, R, Go, Jenkins, Kubernetes, Azure, or GCP
- **Also named:** cloud (AWS, GCP, Azure), containers (Docker, Kubernetes), APIs, source management (Git, Jenkins). Honest overlap: **AWS, Docker, Git, GitHub Actions, REST APIs**. Kubernetes / Jenkins / GCP / Azure are not in inventory
- **What wins:** shipped application code (frontend + API + persist/deploy), a named defect or test/CI analog, Python/React/Angular through use, Docker/Git. Recruiter voice: “can this person contribute on a SAS product team as a developer *or* tester and walk a production bug?” — not “did they intern in Base SAS”
