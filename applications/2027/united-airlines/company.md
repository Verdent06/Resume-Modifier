# United Airlines

United Airlines (NYSE: UAL) is a Fortune-class US legacy carrier operating a global route network from Chicago WHQ (Willis Tower) and major hubs nationwide. Technical Operations (division 66) owns fleet maintenance: planning, supply chain, aircraft engineering, execution, and programs. This req is **Tech Ops Analytics & Business Intelligence Intern WHQ00026447** (Summer 2027, Chicago onsite, $20/hr) — applied analytics that turns operational data into decisions so the right work hits the right aircraft at the right location at the right time. It is **not** Digital Technology SWE and **not** a maintenance-base wrench intern.
