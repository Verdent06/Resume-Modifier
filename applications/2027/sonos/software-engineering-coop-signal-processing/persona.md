## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract):**

- Shipped software the intern could own on an internal-tools team: APIs, pipelines, tests, or CI through use — the SDLC/CI the co-op is hired to learn (`resume.md` Part III §12).
- **Audio / DSP / musical application through use**, not a Skills-line or coursework-only claim. Preferred is audio hobby or academic *projects*; academic "filtering, aliasing" without a project reads as a class, not a builder.
- Embedded-adjacent discipline visible as architecture (real-time constraints, allocation, boards, lock-free or hot-path care) — not invented firmware or ROS.
- Teachable/curious evidence as researched problems and defended trade-offs, not club-ops fluff.
- Metrics that size outcomes (latency, cost, failure recovery, scale) rather than vanity counts (`resume.md` §4).
- Class-year on the page: `Expected May 2028`. GPA 3.66 is a plus (`resume.md` I.3); this JD prints no GPA floor.

**Anti-patterns:**

- **PE / search-fund / GTM lead** that buries audio-DSP — this co-op is Signal Processing at a speaker company.
- Coursework-only DSP (no audio project) or Skills-only "signal processing."
- Invented Linux, Java, Spring Boot, MATLAB, ROS, or a Sonos internship.
- Treating ML research as the screen — notebook ML without delivery, or an `ai-ml` page that hides tooling/SWE.
- Club-ops, community-growth, or thin single-bullet entries that dilute the audio + shipped-software pair.
- Frontend-visualizer-as-the-story with no DSP or tooling behind it.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %).
