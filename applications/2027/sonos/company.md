# Sonos, Inc.

Sonos designs, develops, and sells wireless speakers, headphones, soundbars, and components on a proprietary software platform that streams audio over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth (Nasdaq: **SONO**; IPO August 2018). The FY2025 10-K cites 17M+ households in 60+ countries and ~1,404 full-time employees as of 2025-09-27. Engineering identity is **consumer-audio product development** — acoustics, DSP, and embedded-adjacent software that ships in speakers — not generic SaaS CRUD and not an ML-research lab. This packet is Workday **R2821** **Software Engineering Co-op (Signal Processing)** in Boston; do not mix with IT Data Engineering intern or Cloud/Service SWE intern siblings.
