# AutoZone

AutoZone, Inc. (NYSE: **AZO**) is the largest US aftermarket auto-parts retailer. Founded 1979 in Forrest City, Arkansas as Auto Shack; rebranded AutoZone in 1987; Store Support Center (SSC) at **123 S Front St, Memphis, TN 38103** since 1995. Q3 FY2026 (12 weeks ended May 9, 2026, 8-K): net sales **$4.841B**; YTD 36-week **$13.744B**; **7,856** stores (6,766 US / 933 Mexico / 157 Brazil). Fortune 500 **#270** / ~$18.9B **[directional, Extern]**. **This folder is the Top 100 Data Science intern packet only** (Oracle CX **155461**). It is **not** the sibling Information Technology intern, **not** merchandising/store-ops, and **not** ML research. Do not invent Snowflake, Databricks, Tableau, Alteryx, R, or Java.
