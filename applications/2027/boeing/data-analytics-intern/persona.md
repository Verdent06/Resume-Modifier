## Screen Criteria

**Pass signals (abstract — the writer discovers which entries carry them):**
- Python and SQL demonstrated through use in bullets, not only the Skills line (`resume.md` keyword-through-use). JD names no languages; program-level Python/SQL still fail if orphaned.
- End-to-end data or applied-ML workflow: ingest/integrate messy or diverse data → transform/pipeline or feature work → model, scoring, or insight → measured outcome. Preprocessing through evaluation beats `model.fit()` (`resume.md` §14; `recruiting.md` §13).
- Experiments or business-tied metrics (accuracy, precision, hours saved, throughput, cost) as the closer (`resume.md` DRAW/AR).
- Production-shaped or stakeholder-scoped delivery: APIs, cloud, pipelines, or data products shipped to people who are not the builder — analog to Boeing Analytics serving business/partners/customers.
- Applied ML / AI evidence when it is a real system (eval, deployment, data integration), not a research paper or a buzzword.
- Quantitative coursework (stats, calc) visible in Education as math-foundation signal.
- Industrial/enterprise delivery discipline (messy public or operational data, fixed engagement, measured operational impact) as the aerospace-analytics analog — not invented airframe or flight-ops work.

**Anti-patterns:**
- Generic SWE / full-stack product resume that never shows data pipelines, SQL, or analytics workflow — wrong program family.
- Notebook ML or `model.fit()` with no delivery, no pipeline, no measured outcome.
- Audio-DSP / robotics / embedded systems as the lead with no data/analytics analog.
- Skills-only Python/SQL/ML with no bullet proof.
- Invented aerospace, Tableau, Power BI, Snowflake, Databricks, or clearance-in-hand claims (JD/program may imply tools; stuffing them is a fabrication smell).
- Club-ops or community-growth filler occupying prime slots.
- Vanity metrics (uptime, LOC, coverage %) standing in for impact.
- Treating this as the SWE/embedded Engineering intern track.
