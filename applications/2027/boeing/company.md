# Boeing

Boeing is the world's largest aerospace OEM: commercial jetliners, defense, space and security systems, and aftermarket support. Boeing Analytics delivers technological solutions to the business, partners, and customers — data science, data analytics, machine learning, AI, and software applied to enterprise decision-making, not a consumer product org. This req is the paid Summer intern program's **IT / Data & Analytics** family (`jobs.boeing.com/it-internships`), not the SWE/embedded Engineering track.
