# Plastipak Packaging Inc.

Plastipak is a family-owned rigid plastic packaging and recycling manufacturer (founded 1967; Plastipak Holdings majority William C. Young). Flagship work is PET/HDPE containers, in-house recycling (Clean Tech), and proprietary packaging tech (EcoPreform, SprayPET, PPK Natura, O2blox) sold into beverage, food, household, automotive, and aerosol markets. 2024 Sustainability Report: **$3.4B** revenue, **5,300** associates; JD copy cites 60+ sites / 6,000+ employees. HQ and this intern sit at the **Global Business Technology Center (GBTC)** in Plymouth, MI (41605 Ann Arbor Road). This req (**REQ24512**) is intern SWE on the **Technical Data Integration Team**: manufacturing software, cross-facility applications, SCADA *support*, and optional ML/AI exploration — **not** an OT/PLC technician seat and **not** a mechanical/process-tech intern sibling.
