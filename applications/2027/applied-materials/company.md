# Applied Materials

Applied Materials (NASDAQ: AMAT) designs, builds, and services manufacturing equipment for the semiconductor and display industries — the tools fabs use to produce virtually every new chip and advanced display, not the chips themselves. Fiscal-2025 revenue was $28.37B with $3.57B in R&D (Extern 2027–28 intern guide). The Albany, NY **Global Technical Learning Center (GTLC)** is an Applied Global Services training site: certified instructors train employees and customers to operate and maintain Applied semiconductor systems. This intern req is a **data analyst seat supporting GTLC operations** (clean data, trends, KPIs, recurring reports) — not a process-engineer co-op, not Physics & AI Modeling, and not a generic SWE intern.
