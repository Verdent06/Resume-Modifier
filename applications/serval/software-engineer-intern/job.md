## Role Summary
This intern will work directly with Serval's CTO and founding engineering team on the backend platform that powers AI-driven IT automation — the core product loop of receiving Slack-based requests, routing them through AI agents, and executing automations like access provisioning and onboarding. Day-to-day work involves building and extending backend services in Go, contributing to the gRPC-based microservice architecture, and shipping features that reach real enterprise customers. At 3 months, success looks like having shipped at least one production feature with measurable impact and a working understanding of the platform's automation graph.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Backend ownership above all else.** "Work closely with our CTO" means Ankur will be given tasks with minimal hand-holding. They want someone who can read a codebase, identify the right place to add a feature, and ship it.
- **Go fluency is a strong differentiator.** The preferred stack leads with Go + gRPC. A candidate who can hit the ground running in Go skips the ramp-up period a Python or Java candidate requires.
- **Startup signal matters.** "Excitement about startups, ownership, and building from the ground up" is code for: don't expect a spec. They want someone who can scope, build, and iterate without waiting for requirements.
- **Systems fundamentals over framework knowledge.** Strong CS fundamentals listed as required is real here — they're building distributed automation systems where understanding concurrency and service communication matters more than knowing a specific web framework.
- **Cloud infra literacy is valued.** Kubernetes, Terraform, AWS listed in preferred means they want someone who at minimum understands the deployment environment — doesn't need to be the infra lead, but can't be afraid of it.
- **Real project experience > coursework.** The company is 14 months old. They have no patience for resume padding. Projects with measurable outcomes signal the candidate can actually build.
