# Serval

## What They Do
Serval is an AI-powered IT Service Management (ITSM) platform that automates help desk tasks for modern IT teams. It deploys AI agents to handle employee requests — access provisioning, onboarding/offboarding, password resets, and group management — through conversational interfaces like Slack. The platform integrates with knowledge bases (Notion, Google Drive, Confluence) and targets enterprise IT teams looking to replace legacy systems like ServiceNow with agent-driven automation.

## Why This Company
I want to work on software that eliminates toil at scale — and Serval is doing exactly that for enterprise IT. The technical problem is genuinely interesting: routing natural-language requests to the right automation while knowing when to escalate to a human requires real systems thinking. I'm drawn to the fact that Serval is building a general-purpose workflow automation platform, not just a narrow ITSM tool — the expansion into security, HR, and legal ops means the architecture has to be flexible and composable from day one. Working directly with the CTO and founding engineering team at this stage means I'd be making real architectural decisions, not just wiring up CRUD endpoints.

## Engineering Culture
Serval was founded in 2024 and has moved from zero to Series B ($127M total funding) in roughly two years — the engineering pace is extremely fast. The stack is Go-first on the backend with gRPC for internal service communication, Kubernetes for orchestration, and Terraform for infra-as-code. The team is small (51-200), which means every engineer owns large surface areas and ships to production regularly. This is a company where "startup engineering" is literal — processes are being invented as the product grows.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs
The JD explicitly routes to the **founding engineering team**, reporting to the CTO. This is the core platform team responsible for automation workflows, AI agent routing, and backend service infrastructure — the highest-leverage team at the company right now.
