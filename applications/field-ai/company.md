# Field AI

## What They Do
Field AI builds a hardware-agnostic AI platform for autonomous robots. Their Field Foundation Models (FFMs) are risk-aware, physics-first foundation models for embodied intelligence—vision, LiDAR, and text inputs drive perception, planning, and control that run on the edge without GPS or pre-mapped routes. The same "brain" scales across quadrupeds, humanoids, rovers, and aerial vehicles in construction, industrial & energy, manufacturing, mining, and utilities. They have raised over $405M (Khosla, NVIDIA, Bezos Expeditions) and deploy at scale across diverse robot embodiments and real-world, unstructured environments.

## Why This Company

Field AI's focus on embodied AI, edge deployment, and hardware-agnostic FFMs lines up with my interest in building systems that run in production under real constraints. The Field Application Engineer role is a chance to support customers integrating perception, planning, and control into their robots while learning how autonomy is deployed in construction and industrial settings. I'm drawn to the technical depth of the platform (multimodal inputs, dynamics and multiagent foundation models) and the chance to see how different teams use one shared AI core across many robot types.
