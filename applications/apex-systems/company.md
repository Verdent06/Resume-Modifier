# Apex Systems

## What They Do
Apex Systems is a global technology services and staffing firm that delivers enterprise IT solutions across strategy, innovation, and technology foundations. Operating in over 70 markets across North America, Europe, and India, they serve clients across industries including healthcare, financial services, and government. Apex is a subsidiary of ASGN Incorporated (NYSE: ASGN) and is particularly known for embedding consultants and engineers directly into client delivery teams.

## Why This Company
Apex's AI Practice is one of the more interesting places to do applied AI work at the intern level — rather than being siloed in a research function, I'd be embedded alongside consultants and engineers actually shipping Generative AI and predictive AI solutions for enterprise clients. The RAG work and AI accelerator development are exactly the kind of applied ML problems I've been building toward with Mira, where I implemented a hybrid BM25 + Sentence-BERT retrieval pipeline. Getting enterprise exposure to how AI governance and responsible AI practices get operationalized across industries would directly complement the model-building side of my projects.

## Engineering Culture
Apex's internal engineering is oriented around delivery and consulting rather than product development — their teams build reusable accelerators and solution artifacts that get deployed across client engagements. Agile delivery practices (scrum, backlog management, sprint cadences) are central to how teams operate. Their AI Practice works across the enterprise transformation and cloud practices, meaning engineers engage with data, cloud (Azure/AWS/GCP), and cyber teams on integrated solution delivery. GitHub and SharePoint are standard tooling; the work is more applied-AI-in-enterprise than pure research.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs
The AI Practice team is the target org — it sits within Apex's broader Innovation and Productivity practice area and focuses on Generative AI, predictive AI, and AI governance deliverables for clients. Cross-functional collaboration with the Data and Cloud practices is common, particularly for RAG pipelines and cloud-hosted inference deployments.
