## Role Summary
This is an applied AI consulting internship embedded within Apex's AI Practice team. Day-to-day involves building and refining AI accelerators (reusable solution templates and toolkits used across client engagements), contributing to Generative AI and RAG use cases, and producing documentation and PowerPoint artifacts that explain AI architectures to non-technical stakeholders. Success at 3 months looks like: a tangible accelerator or POC you shipped, clean documentation of a governance framework or AI architecture, and positive feedback from the delivery team on your ability to communicate technical AI concepts clearly. This is not a research role — it's delivery-oriented applied AI at enterprise scale.

- **RAG and GenAI in practice, not just in coursework** — "familiarity with RAG concepts" signals they want someone who has actually built or debugged a retrieval pipeline, not just read about one. The Mira RAG pipeline is directly relevant here.

- **Agile fluency** — Standups, backlog refinement, sprint planning. They want someone who won't need to be taught Scrum. Showing you've lived in agile delivery (not just read about it) is a differentiator.

- **Cross-functional collaboration comfort** — The role explicitly spans Data, Cloud, Cyber, and Enterprise Transformation. They're looking for someone who can operate at the intersection without needing to be the expert in every area.
- **AI governance curiosity** — Listed as preferred, but the fact it's listed at all signals the team is starting to formalize governance practices. Candidates who can engage with responsible AI concepts thoughtfully will stand out over pure coders.
