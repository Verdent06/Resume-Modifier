## Role Summary

Interns build full-stack features and support integrations with major EHR systems (Epic, Cerner, Meditech), implement healthcare interoperability standards (HL7, FHIR), and develop scalable backend and frontend components. The role is in HealthTech Engineering at a startup in the Blackstone LaunchPad program (via DivInc). Success at 10 weeks means having shipped or contributed to real EHR integration work, data-exchange pipelines, or UI/backend pieces that connect to those systems. The environment is startup-paced with the added constraint of healthcare standards and production-ready healthcare software.

## What They're Really Looking For

- **Full-stack ownership** — Ability to build and ship features end-to-end (backend + frontend), not just one layer.
- **Integration mindset** — Comfort connecting systems via APIs and standards (HL7, FHIR) rather than greenfield only.
- **Healthcare domain curiosity** — Willingness to learn EHR ecosystems and interoperability; no prior Epic/Cerner experience required but must be able to ramp.
- **Scalable, production-oriented code** — Code that can run in real systems with real data; CI/CD and testing matter.
- **Student status** — Pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field; the program targets first-generation and untapped talent.
- **Remote discipline** — Delivering on 35 hrs/week remotely with clear communication.
