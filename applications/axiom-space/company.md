# Axiom Space

## What They Do

Axiom Space provides end-to-end human spaceflight services and is building the world's first commercial space station (Axiom Station) in low-Earth orbit. It conducts missions to the International Space Station (ISS), designs and develops next-generation spacesuits (AxEMU), and offers full mission services—training, launch, stay, and return—for private and national astronauts. The company operates from a 22-acre campus at Houston Spaceport and collaborates with NASA and Thales Alenia Space. Revenue comes from mission fees and future sales of spacesuits and station infrastructure.

## Why This Company

I want to build software that directly supports human spaceflight—requirements gathering with mission and operations teams, databases and APIs that ground systems rely on, and SDLC rigor where quality affects real missions. Axiom’s mix of enterprise delivery (SDL, Agile, relational DBs, REST APIs) and mission-critical stakes matches my experience leading full-stack delivery with PostgreSQL, CI/CD, and cross-functional collaboration. Working in Houston alongside NASA alumni and mission-focused engineers would let me apply my backend and full-stack background to systems that enable the next phase of commercial space.

## Engineering Culture

Axiom attracts talent from NASA, industry, and academia and emphasizes agility and rapid development. The company uses Siemens Xcelerator (Teamcenter, Opcenter, Simcenter) for design, manufacturing, and digital twins; AWS for enterprise IT, engineering, and operations; and promotes collaborative, mission-driven work with strong emphasis on execution discipline and technical rigor.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs

The role partners with **MI&O (Mission Integration & Operations)** and cross-functional teams to gather requirements, design and maintain software that interacts with databases and external programs via APIs, and support the full software development lifecycle. This is enterprise application development in a space-mission context—databases, APIs, query optimization, and application support within established timelines and SDL practices.
