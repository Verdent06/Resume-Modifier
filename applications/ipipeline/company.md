# iPipeline

## What They Do

iPipeline is a leading global provider of digital solutions for the life insurance, annuities, and financial services industries (North America) and life insurance and pensions (UK). Founded in 1995 and operating as a business unit of Roper Technologies (Nasdaq: ROP), they couple one of the industry's largest data libraries with an expansive digital and automated platform to accelerate and simplify applications, workflows, and processes from quote to commission. They serve 100+ carriers, 2,500+ distribution partners, and 500,000+ agents and advisors, processing tens of billions in premiums. Their vision is lasting financial security through solutions that connect, simplify, and transform the industry.

## Why This Company

I want to build production software in a domain where correctness and reliability directly affect people's financial security. iPipeline's focus on digitizing and automating paper-heavy, manual operations—from proposal to commission—aligns with the kind of full-stack, integration-heavy work I've done on real systems: automating onboarding, replacing manual processes with scalable tooling, and shipping via CI/CD. Their recent AI-first platform (e.g., iGO Evolve®, CHARLi) and emphasis on seamless integration and security match my interest in both traditional software engineering and modern tooling. Contributing to a team that helps advisors and agents serve clients more efficiently is a concrete way to work on systems that matter at scale.

## Engineering Culture

iPipeline runs agile teams, emphasizes coding standards and technical management, and supports evening/weekend deployments—signaling a disciplined, release-oriented culture. They value collaboration, continuous learning, and adoption of new technologies. Their stack and product set (e.g., LifePipe, Resonant®, DocFast®, InsureSight®) suggest a mix of enterprise integration, data pipelines, and secure, compliant software. They have been recognized as a top workplace in the US and UK and stress inclusion and diverse perspectives.
