## Role Summary
OneTech Summer Interns work within Toyota's IT organization to align technology with business units: Consumer, Dealer, Corporate, Supply Chain & Logistics, R&D Solutions, and Manufacturing Technology. Interns gain experience in the "Toyota Way" and are placed in one or more of: software development (Agile, DevSecOps, customer-centric solutions); business requirements and process re-engineering; competitive/IT strategy; technology and manufacturing R&D; architecture, data science, and analytics; business operations (vendors, contracts, IT finance); or cybersecurity and risk management. Success at three months means delivering concrete contributions to corporate goals, innovative solutions, and strong collaboration with IT and business stakeholders. The role is onsite at Toyota's R&D headquarters in Saline, MI.

## What They're Really Looking For
- **Generalist IT/software intern** who can slot into multiple possible tracks (dev, data/analytics, security, business operations) — resume should show breadth plus depth in at least one area.
- **Business–tech alignment** — they want someone who can work with stakeholders and "determine business requirements," not just code in isolation; evidence of cross-functional or product-minded work helps.
- **Agile/DevSecOps culture fit** — OneTech has invested in DevOps; CI/CD, automation, and quality practices are strong signals.
- **Strong fundamentals** — CS/CE/Software Engineering degree, GPA 2.7+, and "strong technical expertise, business sense and communication skills" — the bar is clarity of impact and professionalism.
- **Work authorization** — lawful, unlimited U.S. work authorization with no future sponsorship need (candidate meets this).
- **Previous Toyota or IS/IT experience** is a plus but not required; first-time interns are expected.

## Required Skills
- Currently enrolled in a full-time, accredited bachelor's or above degree program in one of the following disciplines or related field: Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Software Engineering
- Cumulative GPA of 2.7 or higher
- Possess strong technical expertise, business sense and communication skills
- Must have lawful, unlimited authorization to work in the U.S. without sponsorship, both now and in the future

## Preferred Skills
- Previous Toyota internship or co-op highly preferred
- Previous IS/IT related work experience is a plus
