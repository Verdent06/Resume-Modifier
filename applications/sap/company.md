# SAP

## What They Do

SAP is the market leader in enterprise resource planning (ERP) and end-to-end business application software. They serve 400,000+ customers and 200M+ cloud users. SAP SuccessFactors is their multi-tenant SaaS HR platform — employee lifecycle, learning, recruiting, performance — with 10,000+ customers, 100M+ users, and peak daily transaction rates of 1B+ per day. The Learning product line delivers continuous cloud innovation for training and development at scale.

## Why This Company

I want to build production systems that touch millions of users and ship in an agile, cloud-native environment. SuccessFactors Learning is exactly that: a high-energy team building HR tech at massive scale with a clear DevOps and quality bar. My background — Spring Boot + Angular full-stack, CI/CD with coverage gates, and distributed systems (Redis/Celery, Lambda) — maps directly to their stack (Java, microservices, React/Angular, Docker, Kubernetes). I’m drawn to the chance to work with Gen AI and LLMs in a real product context and to grow leadership and presentation skills inside a global engineering org.

## Engineering Culture

SAP engineering emphasizes cloud-native development, microservices, continuous delivery, and ownership across the dev–deploy lifecycle. SuccessFactors runs lean/agile (stand-ups, sprint planning, reviews, retrospectives). Stack includes Spring Boot, Spring Reactor, Docker, Kubernetes, REST APIs, and modern frontends (React, Angular, Vue). The Learning team values collaboration with Product and Design, automated testing, and a DevOps mindset. Palo Alto hub works with teams across geographies in a hybrid model.

## Relevant Teams / Orgs

**SAP SuccessFactors Learning** — The team for this role. They own the Learning product within the SuccessFactors HCM suite: continuous innovation in cloud for training, upskilling, and learning content. They look for full-stack or backend-leaning interns who can design, develop, test, and ship features; participate in agile ceremonies; and collaborate with Product, Design, and other engineering teams. Experience with Gen AI, foundation models, or LLMs is a growth opportunity, not a hard requirement.
