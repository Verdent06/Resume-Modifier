# Clearwater Analytics (CWAN)

## What They Do

CWAN (Clearwater Analytics) is a public company (NYSE: CWAN) that provides a unified front-to-back investment management platform for institutional investors. Their cloud-native software powers portfolio management, risk analytics, and investment operations for insurance companies, asset managers, hedge funds, corporations, endowments, and pension funds. They serve 2,400+ global clients managing over $10 trillion in assets across 24 offices in 12 countries. The platform delivers a single pane of glass for investment data, replacing legacy and fragmented systems.
