# Avnet

## What They Do
Avnet is a global technology distributor and solutions provider (10,001+ employees, HQ Phoenix) that sources electronic components and IT solutions from suppliers and delivers design-in support, supply chain management, logistics, and systems integration to help customers bring products to market. They operate a major integration center in Chandler, AZ, with a 22,000 sq ft integration lab. Avnet Embedded provides software development (BIOS, BSPs, firmware, IoT, cloud connectivity, GUI/app design); the Software Technology Lab builds applications, proof-of-concepts, and demos; subsidiaries include Softweb (cloud/IoT) and Witekio (edge-to-cloud full-stack). Design Hub is a self-service platform giving engineers access to 70,000+ reference designs.

## Why This Company
I want to work on the software that powers distribution and integration at scale — APIs, internal tools, and applications that connect suppliers, inventory, and customers. Avnet’s mix of backend systems, REST APIs, and hybrid Phoenix work fits my experience with Python, JavaScript, SQL, and collaborative delivery. The Application Developer Intern role is a chance to ship hands-on projects in a team setting and see how a global distributor runs its tech stack.

## Engineering Culture
Backend and application development; emphasis on PC productivity (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint), REST APIs, Python, JavaScript, Git, SQL. Software work spans embedded, IoT, cloud, and internal applications. Chandler facility focuses on design, integration, and deployment; culture values self-driven work with clear direction and collaboration.
